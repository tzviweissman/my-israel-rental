# Playbooks by kind of business

What a visitor is deciding, what leads the page, the proof that matters, the
action, and the mistakes to avoid. Examples are illustrations, never text to reuse.

## Shops and products (bakeries, food, boards, gifts)
- **Deciding:** what it looks like, what it costs, is it kosher, how to get it.
- **Lead:** the products with photos and prices straight after one photo and
  one line, three to eight of them in the first screens, under a plain heading.
  A photo gallery leads only with 3+ real photos.
- **Proof:** kosher certificate (food only), reviews.
- **Action:** order, else message.
- **Avoid:** "fresh daily", "free delivery" unless they said so; kosher without
  a certificate; hiding prices they gave you.
- *Example headline:* "Sourdough and challah in Beit Shemesh".

## Personal care (hair, beauty, spa, therapy)
- **Deciding:** results, trust with their body, who does it, how easy to book.
- **Lead:** each treatment as a compact row with duration and price, or their own photos.
- **Proof:** reviews, years, verified; the rating or credential touching the Book button.
- **Action:** book. Calm, airy layout, quiet prices.
- **Avoid:** medical or result promises; before/after images that aren't theirs.

## Home services and repair (handymen, electricians, plumbers)
- **Deciding:** can they fix my problem, how soon, my area, licensed.
- **Lead:** the jobs they do as a list, or the facts (areas, hours, licence).
  With no photos, the facts carry the page and it still reads as deliberate.
- **Proof:** licence number, years, reviews.
- **Action:** message for a quote, or book.
- **Avoid:** "24/7", "emergency", "same day" unless they said so; "licensed"
  without a number; "insured".

## Events and catering
- **Deciding:** the food, the kosher level, my size of event, the date.
- **Lead:** their food photos (3+) or the menus. The headline names the food, not a mission.
- **Proof:** kosher certificate as its own small band after the lead, reviews.
- **Action:** message with the date, or book.
- **Avoid:** "any size event", guest counts, kosher level beyond the certificate.

## Cleaning
- **Deciding:** what's included, price, reliability, area.
- **Lead:** services with prices, or facts. **Action:** book.
- **Avoid:** "insured", "background-checked", "eco products" unless they said so.

## Health and fitness (trainers, classes)
- **Deciding:** who the trainer is, kind of sessions, where, when, price.
- **Lead:** services or their own photos. **Action:** book.
- **Avoid:** weight-loss or health promises, medical words, body-image pressure.

## Travel and tourism (guides, tours)
- **Deciding:** route, language, length, price per person, licensed guide.
- **Lead:** photos (3+) or the tours; show what's included and the route as steps.
- **Proof:** licence for "licensed guide", the languages, reviews.
- **Avoid:** landmark photos that aren't theirs; "best views" or group-size claims.

## Rentals and stays
- **Deciding:** photos, price per night or month, location, availability, rules.
- **Lead:** the units (photo, price with its period, rating), then the host,
  with the host's proof directly above "Message".
- **Avoid:** distances ("2 minutes from the Kotel") and "luxury" unless the owner wrote them.

## Anything else
Lead with services, facts or a gallery. All other rules apply unchanged.
