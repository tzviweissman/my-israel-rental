# What every AI-made site of each kind does (rules 3aj.3)

Tzvi, 22 Nov 2026: "All 7". This came from a video testing design skills: the UI UX Pro Max skill keeps a list of
anti-patterns for each kind of product, and reads it before designing. Telling a model "don't look AI-made" only swaps
one default for another, so the list names the actual defaults.

Read the part for the business's kind (the `site-category` of rules 3t) before writing any idea. CONCEPT.md gets
`- avoids: <kind>: <how this site avoids each>`, and check-concept refuses it without that line. A new kind gets its
own part here the first time it is built. The parts the rules ask of every site stay (footer 3v.1, gallery place 3m.1,
product cards 3ag.2, the category's ground 3t); the mistake is the same order and the default form of each.

The first part is for every kind. It comes from our own 21 sites, studied on 17 Nov 2026
(`docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-17/README.md`).

## every kind
- The same order after the opening: product cards, three numbered "how it works" steps, a three-column "good to know",
  a gallery, FAQ, the business name set huge to close, a four-column footer.
- Three feature cards with an icon and two lines each.
- A gradient or glow behind the headline, and white cards with rounded corners everywhere.
- One display face on everything, including small numbers and prices, where it is hard to read.
- "01 / 02 / 03" counters, and a small label above every section.
- Copy that could be any business: "quality", "passion", "experience", "we care".

## food-shop
- A hero photo of the product on a dark slate, with steam or flying crumbs.
- A menu grid of identical square cards, so a 30-item menu reads as a wall.
- "Fresh, homemade, made with love" in the headline.
- The order button the same colour as everything else, instead of the product's own colour (3g.19).
- No price on the first screen, though the price is what a buyer looks for.

## place
- A full-screen interior photo with a dark shade and a centred name.
- "Book a table" on a site where nobody books.
- Opening hours hidden in the footer.
- Food photos without the room, so it could be any restaurant.

## fashion-shop
- A beige ground with a thin serif and a model on white, like every Shopify starter theme.
- A "New arrivals / Best sellers / Sale" row of three tiles.
- Size and colour choices hidden until the product page.
- Lifestyle photos with no product price beside them.

## beauty
- Pastel pink with gold script and a stock spa photo.
- A price list as a long table with no photo of the work.
- "Relax, refresh, renew".
- The booking link only in the menu bar.

## events
- Balloons or flowers floating as decoration over every section.
- A gallery as one long masonry grid with no event named.
- "Make your day unforgettable".
- No price or starting price anywhere.

## trainer
- A black page with a neon accent and an athlete on white.
- "Transform your body, transform your life".
- Three pricing tiers with the middle one "most popular".
- Class times as a dense table that breaks on a phone.

## service
- A smiling stock worker in a uniform, and a blue-and-white trust palette.
- Three steps: "Call us, we come, done".
- Fake trust badges and numbers ("500+ happy clients") that the business never gave us (rules 1).
- The phone number only at the bottom.

## rental
- A drone photo of the view in place of the rooms.
- An amenity list of 30 icons.
- "Your home away from home".
- The price only after dates are chosen.
