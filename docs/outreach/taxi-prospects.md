# Taxi and driver prospects

Businesses to approach about a free MyIsraelRental listing. Built 18 Oct 2026 from
public directories and company sites. **Every number here is unverified** and some
source pages were years old, so confirm on the call. Best-fit prospects first:
small operators who already advertise to English speakers and tourists have the
most to gain from a page, and are the easiest yes.

## WhatsApp

**WA ✅** means the business advertises WhatsApp itself. **WA ?** is an `05x` mobile,
which in Israel is on WhatsApp in the great majority of cases but is not confirmed
for this number. A `02` / `03` / `072` line is a landline or virtual number and will
not be on WhatsApp, so those get a phone call. Nothing here was probed by sending
a message. The `wa.me` links below open a chat so you can see for yourself before
you type anything.

## Where to get hundreds more

For volume, work a directory city by city rather than searching one business at a
time:

- **Yellow Pages (d.co.il)**, taxi category 24060: 154 stations listed in Tel Aviv,
  605 across Tel Aviv and the centre. Filterable by city and by 24-hour service.
- **moniot.org.il** — 48 Tel Aviv / Jaffa numbers, kept current.
- **easy.co.il** — per-city taxi pages, with languages listed per driver.
- **Janglo transportation category** — the English-speaking community board. Small
  operators advertising here are the warmest leads on this page.
- **shemesh.co.il business directory** — Beit Shemesh.
- **Google Maps**, search `מוניות` plus a city, then sort by the ones with no
  website. Those are the easiest sell.

## The pitch

Keep it to one message. No commission, free to list, and they reach customers who
are already looking for a ride.

**English**

> Hi, I'm from MyIsraelRental, an Israeli site for rentals, local businesses and
> services. You can add your business, free, with no commission, and reach more
> customers, including tourists and English speakers looking for a driver or an
> airport transfer. Want me to set up a page for you?

**Hebrew**

> שלום, אני מ-MyIsraelRental, אתר ישראלי לדירות, לעסקים מקומיים ולשירותים.
> אפשר להוסיף את העסק שלכם בחינם, בלי עמלות, ולהגיע ליותר לקוחות, כולל תיירים
> ודוברי אנגלית שמחפשים נהג או הסעה לנתב"ג. רוצים שאכין לכם עמוד?
