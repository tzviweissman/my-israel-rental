## EFFECTS (seen frame by frame)
1. Page-change curtain: click a link, a lime block wipes in as stepped slabs (about 6-8 rectangles in a staircase, under 1 s, strip 1 frames 1-3), the new page loads behind it, the slabs retreat to reveal it. Plain JS fetch + swap of the document, no framework, not the View Transitions API. Trigger: link click. NEW. Suits every multi-page site (salon, bakery, trainer); use one flat brand colour and a single simple panel; links must still work with JS off.
2. Cursor depth on the opening portrait: photo shifts a few pixels against the cursor, from a depth map (white = near) in WebGL, plus alpha cut-out and roughness maps. Trigger: mouse position. NEW idea, but our rules discourage decorative parallax (2022, 2065). A cheap 2-layer version (cut-out person over background shifting 6-10 px) could suit trainer, hair or nail artist; mouse only, nothing on phones.
3. Fluid-blob cursor mask: moving the mouse paints blobs revealing a second image; size follows cursor speed. Custom shader. Skip: costly, nothing on phones.
4. Scroll-driven 3D helmet: real 3D model spins as you scroll. He contrasts with Apple's scrubbed video. Our scrubbed video ALREADY-HAVE covers the small-business equivalent.
5. Odd-shaped images by CSS mask-image pointing to an SVG shape (blob/star) so tiles are not rectangles. NEW, cheap. Suits salon, decorator, bakery galleries; check the shape never crops the product (our fit rules).
6. Hover reveal by clip-path: an ellipse clip-path grows on hover so a second photo opens like an iris over the first (helmets grid). NEW, cheap CSS; phones need a tap or always-visible version (rules 398).
7. Hover letter roll: each letter in a span, translateY up 100%, per-letter transition-delay steps, word rolls on hover. NEW, cheap; suits menu links and buttons; keep the full word in aria-label.
8. Signature drawn by scroll progress (stroke painted as you scroll). Scroll-scrub is ALREADY-HAVE; a hand-drawn name or logo stroke is a NEW small touch (trainer, decorator, baker's own name) if the owner supplies it.
9. Lenis smooth scroll also drives horizontal sections and sticky entries; space bar and find-in-page still work. See video 1 item 2.
10. One accent-coloured phrase in a paragraph. ALREADY-HAVE (single accent word).

## PERFORMANCE LESSONS (ALREADY-HAVE in spirit, rules 709)
Flat: no drop shadows, few filters/blurs/gradients, little position:absolute; animate only transform; result is no dropped frames. Note: keep soft shadows on static cards only, never on animated elements.

## STRUCTURE (Lando site, as seen)
Opening: small wordmark top-left, tiny Store button and icon top-right (no big nav), full-screen portrait with cursor effect. Then: "On track" (dark, helmet pinned and spinning while text scrolls, stats block), "Off track" (light page, photo, carousel), "Helmets hall of fame" (grid of shaped tiles, hover shows photo), calendar, sponsor logo row, footer with huge name type and a stacked social list (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch). Dark/light alternates by topic (dark = racing, white = personal), not as stripes. All inner pages share the same top bar; page changes use the curtain. No hide-on-scroll or colour change of the header seen in the clips. Huge type used as section header, cut off at the top edge. Mostly NEW as rhythm, but it is a personal-brand site; take: one huge-type header per section, social list in footer, light/dark by topic only if the business has two moods (e.g. decorator: weddings vs kids).
