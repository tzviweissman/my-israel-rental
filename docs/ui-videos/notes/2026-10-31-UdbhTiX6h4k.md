# Obys: Storytelling in web design (UdbhTiX6h4k), 5 min

**What it is.** A talking-head lesson from the Obys agency with screen clips of award-style sites. Almost no sales pitch (one free PDF checklist offer, ignore). No fake reviews or counters. The idea is a film structure: **Hook (main screen / preloader) -> Set-up (intro text) -> Chapters (info + "breakers") -> Resolution (outro/credits)**. Content must be shaped around one hero object (their Atmos lamp), "not the other way round".

## EFFECTS (seen in frames, 4 fps strips)
1. **Preloader as the opening theme** (New Layer Capital / Odin's Crow clips, frames/2026-10-31/UdbhTiX6h4k-2.jpg). A tiny coloured box (about 40 px, layered purple/orange/magenta frames) sits on a white screen, then scales up in about 1 s to fill the screen, the headline "New Layer Capital" rises inside it, a thin line draws under it, then the page shows. Odin's Crow: cream page with "Loading" and black bars that thicken line by line, then the layout appears. Trigger: page load, plays once. Built with a GSAP timeline (scale plus clip). Suits any category as a 1.5 to 2 s title card with the business name or product (bakery box opening, salon logo growing). Wrong where the order button must show at once (food shop on a phone; keep it under 2 s and skippable). **ALREADY-HAVE in spirit (3h film opening, 3g.13 opening is an event). The "box that grows into the page" form is NEW as a no-video opening for sites with no film budget.**
2. **Product swap with a slice wipe** (Ayocin lamp, frames/2026-10-31/UdbhTiX6h4k-1.jpg). On scroll, the red-light lamp is replaced by the air purifier: a horizontal mask edge sweeps down through the image (about 0.5 s; old image cut away from the top while the new one shows), and the right-hand text card swaps its title ("Red light therapy" to "Air purification") with a small icon. A round cursor ring with a dot follows the mouse on the stage. Trigger: scroll step on a pinned stage, one product per step. Built with pinned section plus clip-path/mask. Suits food shop (flavour or size swap), fashion, event packages. **ALREADY-HAVE: pinned scenes and per-product colour rooms (3h, 3t food-shop). The follow-cursor ring is against line ~2021 (no cursor trails for decoration); NEW only as a small "View" ring on gallery photos.**
3. **Breaker section**: a pause between content blocks. Clips show a row of blank grey cards jumping up with "DON'T BE AFRAID" in giant type below, a split photo "WE ARE LAXER", a red "44 ADD TO BAG" huge-type strip. One giant statement plus one moving graphic, full width. Trigger: scroll arrives. **NEW as a named idea: one deliberate breaker band after every 2 to 3 content blocks so a long page is not a list.** Suits events, trainers, holiday rentals, salons. Wrong for menu and price-list pages (keep dense and still).
4. **On-scroll animation as the play button**: scroll drives the story; smooth transitions between sections and pages. **ALREADY-HAVE** (3h scroll-scrubbed film; multi-page sites, 3j).
5. Editorial micro-type at the screen edges ("Intro / Grids / Books", on/off toggles) with huge headline. **ALREADY-HAVE (chapter lines 3h.9, 3i).**

## STRUCTURE
- Named order: Hook -> Setup -> Chapters (info + breakers) -> Resolution. Mapping to our sites: opening = hook; one short who/what line = set-up; products, services, gallery, reviews = chapters with a breaker between; final big line + Order button + footer = resolution. **ALREADY-HAVE (3t "a last big line, the button, link columns, contact"; 3g.13); the "breakers between chapters" wording is NEW.**
- Header on Ayocin: dark full-width bar, logo left, 5 text links, round "Pre-Order Now" pill with arrow at the right; stays on top, slightly translucent over the product. **ALREADY-HAVE (menu bar, line 1073).**
- Grid habit (Obys Grids clip): golden-canon grid, 80 px margins, 6 "power lines"; blocks snap to the lines; scale the design only after choosing the lines. **NEW as a rule of thumb: pick 4 or 6 column lines first and put every block edge on one.**
- Footer and inner pages: not shown.
