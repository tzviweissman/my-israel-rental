## Candidate rules for us (one-page local business sites)

All SHOWN on screen unless noted.

1. **Lead the hero with the benefit, then the name.** H1 = biggest customer outcome, then business or offer name; one supporting line; one primary button. (03:20-05:30, 16:00) Test: the H1 contains a customer outcome (e.g. "Fresh challah for Shabbat, delivered") and the name sits beside or under it.
2. **A trust marker in the first screen, but only a real one.** Rating line with real count above the headline (05:00, 16:00). Render it only when the owner supplies a source (Google rating, customer count). Otherwise show a plain true fact (area, kosher certificate, years). Never invent numbers.
3. **Three benefit icons with one-line labels directly under the intro.** (05:00, 16:00) Test: three items, each under 8 words, each a concrete fact (delivery area, ingredients, certificate), not slogans.
4. **Strict three-level text hierarchy per section.** Big headline, one medium line, small body. (15:00 slide; every sample block 09:40-12:50, 30:10-31:50) Test: max one headline, one subline, one short paragraph or 3 bullets per section.
5. **One idea per section; every section has a stated job and ends with the same single action.** (03:00-03:50 job labels; 29:10-30:00 repeated CTA) For us: repeat the same contact action (WhatsApp / call / form) after each section, never two competing primary actions.
6. **Put the contact or order button directly above three small, true reassurance facts.** (18:50-23:40) E.g. "Reply within the day, free quote, no deposit" only if the owner confirms them.
7. **Make choices easy: show prices, show a running total, name the popular option.** (18:20-22:00) For us: package cards (cleaning 2h / 4h / deep clean) with prices; mark "Most popular" only if the owner says it truly is.
8. **Social proof as a wall of short real quotes with first names, plus a photo or video from a real customer, after the main offer.** (26:50-28:50) Only reviews the owner can show with permission. With none, omit the section; no placeholders.
9. **Answer objections in an FAQ near the end, 6-8 plain questions.** (34:30-34:40, 25:30) Price, area served, cancellation, kosher status, how to book.
10. **Gallery images carry meaning, not decoration.** Lifestyle, close-up, before/after, comparison, each with a short headline. (06:00-12:50) For us: real photos of real work; before/after only if truthful and unretouched.
11. **Short plain headlines.** (31:20 source on screen; all samples under about 8 words) Test: no headline over 10 words, no jargon.
12. **One marker-highlighted phrase per block.** (30:10-31:50) Limit to one per section.
13. **Mobile first: single column, full-width primary button, repeated or sticky CTA.** (throughout) A sticky bar must not cover content.
14. **A guarantee only if the business really offers one,** worded exactly as the owner commits. (10:50, 23:40) Do not borrow "100 nights".

### Rejected on honesty grounds

- **"SELLING FAST! Over 1,200 orders in the last 24 hours"** (22:30, frame saved) and the "Limited inventory at this price remaining" line (23:40-24:00). Rejected: fake or unverifiable scarcity and urgency. A local business has no live order counter; do not build one.
- **"Limited Time Offer / Offer ends soon" on the bonus and the "HURRY! LAST CHANCE" promo bar** (21:50, 30:10-31:50). Rejected unless there is a real dated deadline the owner states; then show the real date.
- **Giant counters like "427,873+ Miracles Delivered" or "1M+"** (24:00, 28:00). Rejected unless the owner can prove the number. "Since 2016" or "over 200 events" only if real.
- **"Sweepstakes entries" and bonus checkbox nudges** (21:50). Rejected as gamified pressure. Any add-on must be unchecked by default.
- **Fake "As seen on" logos or invented Trustpilot-style marks.** Rejected unless real.
- **Strike-through "was" price with "You Save X%"** (05:00, 22:30). Rejected unless the higher price was genuinely charged recently; consumer-protection rules require a true reference price. Prefer plain current prices.
