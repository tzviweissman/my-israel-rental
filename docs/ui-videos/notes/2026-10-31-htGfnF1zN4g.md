# Hyperplexed: multi-card spotlight hover (htGfnF1zN4g), notes 2026-10-31

**What it is:** a 5-minute code-along recreating the Linear features grid (6 dark cards, 3x2). Almost no sales pitch, nothing invented. The only site shown is linear.app (its Features cards), as the thing being copied.

## EFFECTS
1. **Cursor spotlight on a card grid (glow follows the mouse)**
   - Saw (4 fps strip): dark cards; with the cursor over the grid a soft round light sits under the cursor and slides with it, fading in over about 0.5 s on entry and out on leave. No jumps.
   - Built: `.card::before`, absolutely positioned, same size as the card, `radial-gradient(800px circle at var(--mouse-x) var(--mouse-y), rgba(255,255,255,.06), transparent 40%)`; opacity 0 by default with `transition: opacity 500ms`; JS `mousemove` sets `--mouse-x/--mouse-y` from `clientX - getBoundingClientRect().left` (and top).
   - Suits: price cards, treatment/class menu cards, "what we do" tiles for trainers, salons, home services, decorators. Wrong for bakery/food photo menus and holiday-rental photo grids (the photo must carry; the light is invisible on light grounds). Only reads on dark or tinted card grounds.
   - Grep of page-generation-rules.md: glow/cursor/spotlight only hit "glowing line", the cloud-for-a-mouse-pointer touch (3q.5) and "no cursor trails" (section 6). **NEW**, but it is a stock library effect: keep it a quiet card hover on a dark-grounded site, never the AI purple-glow look.
2. **Neighbour-lit borders (the trick)**
   - Saw: the border of the hovered card AND the cards next to it light up where the cursor is, so the light seems to travel across the gaps and is cut by the card edges.
   - Built: card has a 1px-inset opaque `.card-content` (z 2) over a `.card-border` layer (z 1) holding a tighter 400px radial gradient, so only the 1px rim shows it. The mouse listener is on the WRAPPER and loops over all cards setting each card's `--mouse-x/y` relative to that card; border opacity switches on `.cards:hover`, not `.card:hover`.
   - Suits: a row of 3 package or price cards. **NEW.**
3. Details: 8px gap, radius about 10px, `cursor:pointer` on cards.
