## STRUCTURE / LAYOUT
1. Two columns: text and button one side, image the other (1A image right, 1B left). Basic; do not flip it all the way down the page. ALREADY-HAVE (3p no two alike).
2. Three columns: icon or picture, heading, short text (2A); 2B is 2 rows by 3. Cap: 3 across, six is the most, never 9. NEW (cap on points per grid). Services, trainers, shops "why us".
3. Centred vertical stack: heading, text, button, image above or below. ALREADY-HAVE (CTA section).
4. Heading block left, slider of cards right with the last card cut off by the right edge so people know to slide; the card leaving on the left must fade or it bumps the heading; needs arrows, a timer or a drag cursor. NEW as a layout ("peek card"). Events (past events), bakeries (flavours), rentals (rooms).
5. Product in the centre with icon plus text callouts left, right and one below; built as three aligned columns, not free positioning. NEW. Food products, gifts, boards (4 to 6 true facts: ingredients, size, kosher, shelf life); needs a clean cut-out product shot (section 8).
6. Tabs by bullets: media left, three bullets right, active one dark with a coloured dot, the others faded; click or timer swaps the media; if timed, a progress line runs down the active bullet. PARTLY-HAVE (progress only with true numbers, rules line 429; steps that light up on scroll are discouraged, so keep it click-driven). Trainers, salons (treatment tabs), home services.
7. Two columns with a 2x2 of boxed icon plus text in one column, heading and social row beside it. NEW (combo). Services.
8. Nested stacks (his favourite, the Stripe style): left column = vertical stack (heading, text) over a horizontal pair of small icon plus text items; right column a tall image. Rule: alternate vertical, horizontal, vertical, horizontal down to the smallest element. NEW as a named technique. All kinds.
9. Image left; right side heading over a 2x2 of small text blocks (label, line, short text), four perks. NEW. Holiday rentals (perks), salons, trainers.
10. Staggered steps: heading top-left; three steps, each a picture, a tiny "Step n" label, a title and a line; rows shifted (1 right, 2 left, 3 right) in two aligned lanes (first and third share an edge, second sits back). Looks unaligned but is aligned. NEW; needs a note against our one-left-edge rule (two lanes allowed here). Home services (the three steps), events, rentals (how booking works).
11. Bento grid: boxes of different sizes in one frame; the most important is the largest, left, coloured; images positioned differently in each box; next section can flip which is largest. NEW (no bento in our rules). Shops (hero product plus two small), beauty, events.

Spacing numbers: none given beyond the 3x3 and six-point caps.

## EFFECTS
- Slider card fade at the left edge (layout 4): drag or timer. NEW but we avoid sliders.
- Active-bullet tabs with timed advance and progress line (layout 6): keep click-driven only.

## Verdict
Value is the layout vocabulary (2B cap, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11) to stop every section looking like a two-column template. Layouts 5, 8, 10 and 11 are worth offering per category, with real photos only.
