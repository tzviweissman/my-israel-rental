## EFFECTS (frames at 4 fps)
1. **Colour curtain, then video** (Double Play): a solid blue screen holds a small logo about 2 s, then the blue panel slides up like a curtain over about 1 s, revealing a dark video with the headline "Game-changing Websites" already bottom-left; the top bar fades in last (links left, logo centre, one link right). Trigger: load, plays once. Built with a translate on a cover div. Suits any business as a 1 s brand-colour opening. **NEW as the simple opening when there is no film.**
2. **Persistent order card over a dark hero** (Opal Tadpole): black screen with logo, a hand fades up out of the dark and turns while the left headline "A new species of webcam" sits still; a small white card top-right (product name, price, Overview / Comparison, solid black Order now button) is there from the first second. **NEW as a layout: one small white order card top-right with price and button, nothing else competes.** Suits food shop, fashion.
3. **Scroll-spun product with giant type** (Hungry Tiger jar, frames/2026-10-31/aLZCG8XMM_s-1.jpg): "BOLD FLAVOR / TIKKA MASALA" in huge yellow caps, jar centred and cut at the bottom; scrolling lifts the type away and spins the jar through four poses (front, tilted, lid on top, label side); a new line "A NEW ANGLE OF FLAVOR" rises while the jar passes in front of the letters. Scroll-scrubbed frames. **ALREADY-HAVE (3h scroll-scrubbed film, 3h.18, product hero shot section 8).** The giant word sitting BEHIND a cut-out subject (Baikal: "BAIKAL" behind the climber) is **NEW as a depth trick**; wrong where the subject cannot be cut out cleanly.
4. **Hand-drawn outline and arrow around the headline** (Pipely, Tadpole "A new species of webcam" with a drawn curved arrow pointing at the product): sticker-style annotation drawn on as a line over about 0.6 s. **NEW, small touch** for events and salons. Not a cursor trail (line 2021 bans those).
5. **Headline lines rising from a mask, one after another** (Irene, Allies): about 0.4 s each. **ALREADY-HAVE (3i headline reveal).**
6. GSAP letter scatter with little flowers: decorative, wrong for small business sites.

## STRUCTURE (hero-only video)
- Header: thin bar, logo left or centre, 4 to 6 small links, one button at the right (black pill on Opal, blue on Double Play); the Double Play bar fades in AFTER the opening. Sticks. Mostly **ALREADY-HAVE (line 1073)**. **NEW: the bar fades in after the opening, never during it.**
- First screen holds only headline, one line, one button; corner meta text and thumbnails sit at the edges, left margin about 40 to 60 px. **ALREADY-HAVE (3g.13).**
- Footers, inner pages, section order below the hero: not shown. Host's closing advice: subtle parallax or 3D, not too much.
