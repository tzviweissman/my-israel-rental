## Candidate rules for us
1. **One accent colour, used on ONE word or ONE object per screen, never two.** SHOWN 0:00, 0:20, 2:40, 4:00 (green word in every headline, grey/white elsewhere). SAID 2:40 (style.md "One accent"). For us: a bakery hero headline gets exactly one accent-coloured word; the rest ink on white, per the flow theme.
2. **Write the look down as a fixed style file and reuse it on every section; fix a rule once, not per round.** SHOWN 2:40-3:05, 3:00 (with/without file comparison). SAID 3:00 "Fix a rule once, fixed for good". For us: every client page gets a short style note (accent, ink, type pair, motion limits) that all sections read.
3. **Pair roman and italic type for emphasis instead of bolder weights.** SHOWN 2:00-2:30 (sans line + italic serif red word), 2:25 note "Roman + italic pairing is the emphasis, not weight". For us: heading in Playfair roman with one italic accent word; in Hebrew use Frank Ruhl Libre italics only if the face supports it, otherwise use colour.
4. **One headline per scene or section, about six words, no sub-copy stuck under it.** SHOWN 4:00-4:30. SAID 4:15 (style.md line). For us: each scroll section = one short line plus one image; detail goes in a separate block lower down.
5. **No eyebrow pills, mono chip labels or small pill buttons.** SHOWN 4:15 (before/after note card). SAID 4:00 "buttons look like AI slop". For us: drop the tiny uppercase labels above headings; buttons are normal-size black solids per theme.
6. **Use only real images: screenshots, the owner's own photos, their logo. No stock, no generated art passed as theirs.** SHOWN 2:40 ("Real only"). SAID 2:40. For us: bakeries and trainers use the owner's own photos; if none, a plain type-led section rather than a filler photo.
7. **Motion is spring-like and settles: tiny overshoot at most, text arrives blurred and snaps sharp on the beat.** SHOWN 2:40 (style.md), 4:30 blur-to-sharp headline. SAID 2:40. For us: reveal text with a 400-600 ms opacity+blur(6px to 0) ease-out, no bounce, respect prefers-reduced-motion.
8. **Keep things alive: every hold has a slow drift or camera push, but never a hard stop.** SHOWN 2:40 (style.md), 1:20 shoe hero. SAID 2:40. For us: hero photo gets a slow 1.0 to 1.05 scale over ~12 s; stop it under reduced-motion.
9. **Continuity between sections: the next section starts at the zoom/position the last ended on.** SHOWN 2:40 (style.md). For us: a pinned hero image scales to full-bleed and the next block begins from that same crop, with no jump cut.
10. **Colour carries meaning (one hue per meaning), and connectors/lines are thin (about 1.5 px) with one travelling dot.** SHOWN 2:25 notes. For us: in a kashering or catering "how it works" strip, a thin line with one dot moving step to step; green stays for status only.
11. **Review stills before animating: approve one poster per scene at full size before building the motion.** SHOWN 3:15 (grid of six approved stills), 3:30 (dark-gray-on-dark logo fault). SAID 3:15. For us: screenshot each section at laptop and phone first, fix contrast, then add motion.
12. **Give feedback as short plain notes (what you see, what you like), one round at a time: Note, Watch, Next note.** SHOWN 4:00-4:30. For us: show the owner one section at a time; keep a rule for each approved note.
13. **Check legibility in the first draft: logos or text on near-same dark tone fail.** SHOWN 3:35 ("Dark gray on dark"). For us: any grey-on-dark text or logo must read at 4.5:1; check with the site's contrast script.
14. **Mix scene types so every screen differs: a type card, a product still, a grid, a number card.** SHOWN 0:00, 1:20, 3:15. For us: alternate photo-led, text-led and list-led sections down the page.

### Rejected or restricted for honesty
- "1,000,000 monthly visitors after 8 months" scene (1:25): the video itself strikes it out as "Not true for this brand". Never invent traffic, customer or sales counts, or money counters (also on his own leave-out list at 2:15).
- Logo wall "Our team has worked with Y Combinator, Forbes, Apple, Google..." (0:20, 3:35): only with written permission and a real relationship. Local businesses should show only clients who agreed, or skip it.
- Review screenshots and "8,000+ builders" counter (4:50-5:39): promotional for his club; reuse only with real, consented reviews and real counts.
- Confetti, orange/violet/pink accents, made-up numbers: his own "leave out" list; also off-brand for us.
- Dark neon-green launch-film look: wrong for our flow theme (white, accent blue, black buttons). Take the discipline, not the palette.
- Per-scene full-video rendering and the Claude-to-video pipeline: does not apply to a static or scroll page.
