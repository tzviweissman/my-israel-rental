## Candidate rules for us

1. **One display word per heading gets a different treatment (italic serif in colour); the rest stays plain.** SHOWN 1:05, 1:20, 8:05 (italic crimson "bottle,"), 10:00-12:00 (every heading). SAID none. For us: each section heading has exactly one accent word (our accent blue, italic Playfair or Frank Ruhl in Hebrew), never two.
2. **Hero = full-bleed real media with the brand name huge and centred, one tiny tagline, nothing else competing.** SHOWN 7:20, 8:50, 8:35. SAID 6:20-7:00 (scroll story). For us: a bakery hero is the bakery's own photo or clip under the business name; tagline one line at 12-14 px; one button only.
3. **A hero name that fades to translucent as you scroll hands the screen to the next scene.** SHOWN 7:25 (PULP goes ghost), 8:05. SAID none. For us: on scroll-film pages, fade the hero title by 50-60% scroll rather than sliding it off; keep reduced-motion = static.

### Rejected or restricted for honesty

- Fake product names, "fruit flavors", slogans and fictional brands (PULP, Isohel, Nocturne, Alpenglow) are inventions made in "imagination mode"; never copy their copy, only the layout and motion.
- "100% fruit. 0% apologies", "900 vials", "the water is already warm", and the "174" counter are made-up claims; we show only facts the owner confirms.
- "Cinematic AI footage" of food, a cabin or a perfume is generated, so it cannot stand for a real bakery, kitchen or property. Use it only for abstract or illustrative scenes, labelled, or as between-stills for the owner's own images. Never as the product itself.
- The "$10,000 website" and "sold sites for $10k" claims, and credit/cost numbers (about 250 credits, about $10) are sales claims and vendor-specific; not for our pages.
- Course promos, "Fable 5 is the best design model", model rankings bar chart: marketing for the presenter, no design rule to take.
- Heavy 3D/flythrough effects and large video backgrounds do not suit slow mobile data or Hebrew copy in many small businesses; use one scrub scene at most per page.
