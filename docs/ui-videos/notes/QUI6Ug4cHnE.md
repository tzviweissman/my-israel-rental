## The one transferable idea
Interview first with four-to-eight multiple-choice questions, each with a recommended answer and an "Other" box, one question about what the visitor must BELIEVE at the end (one sentence), one about the page's single "signature move", one about the energy curve (where calm, where intense), one real-world vibe reference. Then build, then screenshot your own scroll at many positions and look at them before showing anyone. The questions make every site come out different; the self-check catches defects.

Ideas his skill shows that ours does NOT have:

1. Energy curve question (where calm, where one surge): add to section 0b build order or 3n wow gate as one concept-sheet field (3q); options: quiet then one surge, slow build, strong open then substance, even.
2. "What must the visitor believe by the end, one sentence" as a required line of the concept sheet (3q) and checked against the closing section.
3. Vibe reference from outside the web (annual report, printed issue, flight board...) as a concept-sheet field; ties to 3t "site follows its category" but from a non-web source.
4. Multiple-choice interview with a Recommended answer, each option carrying a one-line effect, plus "Other"; ours says "interview-me only when no brief". Add to 0b: when owner answers are needed, ask in this form so questions go out fast (see memory "build while asking").
5. Signature move offered as 3-4 concrete options with a mechanism each (counter that fills a margin as you scroll, page as a printed issue). Ours has "unique touches" (small pointer touch) but not a required page-level signature chosen from options; goes in 3p.
6. Own-scroll self-review at three sizes plus reduced motion (his: desktop, 375x667, reduced motion) followed by a SECOND contact sheet of every scroll position, "the part the harness can't judge". Ours does screenshot checks (3r, section 6); check whether it names a full-scroll contact sheet and phone plus reduced-motion runs in the same pass; if not, add to section 6.
7. Named defect classes his log looked for: nowrap that breaks prose, a transparent margin rail letting a pan run through it, empty first screen on the peak, dead space after the cover, an empty screen before the close, copy overflowing the top of a pinned stage. Our check-site has many, but these specific ones (empty screen before the close, overflow at the top of a pinned stage) could become check-site items; confirm against scripts/check-site.mjs before adding.
8. Draw data graphics from real data and label them ("Illustration. The lines are routes between real cities, not a member map"): a graphic must not imply data the page does not have. Fits the honesty rules in section 1; ours has facts-only but not this graphic-specific line.
9. Claude lists "two things worth your call" (its own judgement calls, e.g. two peaks) at the end of the build. Fits section 6 reporting to the owner.
10. Feedback loop shape: praise-first message, then section by section complaints with a direction each (slower, more exaggerated, remove), one regeneration. Ours has the review loop but not a stated owner-feedback format; low priority.
11. Counter that rises as sections are passed ("sourced 0 of 9" in the margin, each claim footnoted with a source) as a trust device for proof-heavy sites; idea only, goes in docs/inspo.
12. Port clash guard: his harness ran against another session's server on port 4512 and checked the wrong site; check-site should verify the server it hits is this build (e.g. title or a marker) before trusting a pass. Not in ours by grep (no "wrong server" hit); worth one line in section 6.
