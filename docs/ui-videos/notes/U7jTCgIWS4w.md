## Candidate rules for us
1. The lead image of a service/product card is large, sized to create appetite, never a 56 px thumbnail or a logo. Fail if the image is under about 25% of card width. (0:55-2:20 SHOWN)
2. Do not cram things above the fold; make the first screen worth scrolling for. (1:19-1:30 SAID, before/after SHOWN)
3. Every card or block has one visible reading order: a clearly larger name, one emphasised fact (rating or price), the rest smaller and lighter. Squint test: one thing wins. (1:43-2:40 SHOWN)
4. One corner radius and one shape language per page. (1:58-2:20 SHOWN)
5. Put text on a plain strip below photos, not over them, so any owner-supplied photo stays legible. Relevant because owners upload their own photos. (4:07-4:15 SHOWN)
6. Search and form fields say what they accept ("cake, event, area"). (3:52 SHOWN)
7. Primary button states the real outcome ("Show 359 homes"); for us, "Send my request" with the owner's real reply time, only if true. Options show their consequence before the click. (10:15, 14:17 SHOWN)
8. If category icons sit near photos, make them a quiet icon set so they do not compete with real photos. (2:51-3:05 SHOWN)
9. Where no single default suits everyone (menu as cards vs compact list), offer a toggle. (3:29-3:45 SHOWN)

Rejected or not applicable:
- "Best match" style labels: allowed only if based on a real signal; an invented "best match / most popular" badge is rejected as made-up ranking.
- Mortgage calculator, histogram, stepper and filter-sheet advice: app search specific, not for one-page small business sites (parked).
- Mobbin sponsorship and discount code: rejected as a source of rules, it is paid promotion.
