## Real sites checked in the browser (structure only)

These were checked against five of ours (Blazin' Boards, The Brown Box, The Gift Boutique, Michal Yodaiken, KasherMyBnb).

- **Tartine** (bakery):
  - A thin announcement line above the menu, and a sticky white bar.
  - Menu: Locations, About Us, Order, Join, Press, National Shipping, with Order repeated.
  - The home page runs: welcome, news, special-order cakes, pre-order, gift cards, story, who we are, at home.
  - Footer: socials, the brand line "Rise each day. Warm every table.", privacy, terms, site map.
- **Blue Bottle** (cafe and shop):
  - An announcement line on top.
  - Footer in three link columns (Company, Learn, More), a newsletter, then legal.
- **Sally Hershberger** (salon):
  - The menu bar slides away as you scroll down.
  - Footer: about line, newsletter, social, customer care (FAQs, contact, terms, privacy).
  - It also has a 10%-off pop-up on arrival. Not for us: it covers the first screen.
- **Olive & June** and **Barry's**: big multi-category menus. Too big for a small business, so we take nothing from them.

**Ours, for comparison:**
- **Menu bar:** in-page jump links plus one button. Real sites link to their other pages: About, Order, Locations or Visit.
- **Footer:** one line ("The Gift Boutique, Jerusalem ... Listed on MyIsraelRental"), and Blazin' Boards' home page has none. Every real site has a full footer: visit (address, hours), pages, contact, social, the brand line, then privacy and terms.
- **Announcement line:** none of ours has one. Real ones use it for a true current notice (shipping, a new item, a cut-off time).
