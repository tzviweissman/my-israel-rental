# Flux Academy: perfect landing page structure (fGCtS6D6tr0), studied 31 Oct 2026

**What it is.** A 16 minute talking-head video (Izzy, Flux) retelling one marketing article (marketingexamples.com, "step-by-step guide to landing pages that convert"). Almost all talk; the site shots are short screen grabs. Examples are software and product-brand sites (Muzzle, Dormeo tea, Zencaster, Privy, Smile Direct Club, Bite toothpaste, We Are Nudes, Neuro gum). Ignore the SaaS framing (download, free trial, app screenshots). Keep the order.

## STRUCTURE: the ten parts, in order
On the first screen: 1 headline (what you get), 2 subhead (how you deliver it), 3 call to action, 4 social proof (a rating plus review count, right under the subhead), 5 visual (the product in use, not a fancy illustration).
Below: 6 features and objections (justify the headline, show more of it in use), 7 more social proof (testimonials, success stories, media), 8 FAQ (accordion; only objections not answered in 6), 9 second call to action near the footer, 10 founder's note (story, photo or video of the people behind it; may live on its own page).
Frame: frames/2026-10-31/fGCtS6D6tr0-1.jpg (the numbered list as drawn in the video).

## Ideas, checked against docs/page-generation-rules.md
- Headline says the result, subhead says how: ALREADY-HAVE (`hero` row, 3g.13).
- Call to value ("Start learning") and objection handling in the button ("try for free, no risk"): ALREADY-HAVE (item 6 "The button says what happens", ~line 304 and 389). Objection text only when true (claims row).
- A small rating line right under the subhead on the first screen: PARTLY-HAVE (rating beside the proof, ~line 232; real reviews only). NEW as a fixed slot: "4.9 from 31 reviews" under the hero line if real reviews exist, nothing otherwise.
- Product in use as the hero visual: ALREADY-HAVE (product hero shot, 3t, section 8).
- FAQ accordion after proof, only unanswered objections: ALREADY-HAVE (lines 123-136).
- Second call to action just before the footer: ALREADY-HAVE (3t: footer has "a last big line, the button").
- Founder's note with a real photo: ALREADY-HAVE for food-shop (3t "founder or kitchen story band"); NEW as a standard short note near the end for every category, plus an "Our story" page in the 3k website (owner's own words and photo only).
- Testimonials below the fold, not in the hero: ALREADY-HAVE (real reviews only).
