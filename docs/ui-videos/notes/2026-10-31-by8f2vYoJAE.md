# Payton Clark Smith: how to properly layout a website (by8f2vYoJAE, 8 min)

A clean explainer with a few "subscribe" asks (ignore). Aimed at software sites, but the order of blocks is general. No live sites named; the screens are template-style sites (Eupay and Notion footers; Linear, Semflow and Saasie navs). ## STRUCTURE / LAYOUT
1. Four blocks in order: nav, hero, body, footer. ALREADY-HAVE (section 3 cont. "sections every page has"; 3k one footer on every page).
2. Nav: logo (links home) left; only the few necessary page links; one call-to-action button top right. Every extra link is a "bleed point" pulling the visitor away. PARTLY-HAVE (3k nav and footer; 5b.2 one action identical everywhere). NEW: "no nav link unless necessary; the rest go in the footer".
3. Hero must have: a headline saying what you do and who for; one strong call to action (service: Book a call); social proof directly under the button (stars, a count, logos, faces); optionally a picture or demo that peeks up from the bottom edge of the first screen to invite scrolling. ALREADY-HAVE (section 3 cont. item 1, 3g.13, 5c). The peek-up picture is a small NEW cue (shops, trainers, rentals).
4. Body checklist: overview, features and benefits, examples of work, social proof, blog, FAQ, CTA section last. ALREADY-HAVE (section 3 cont.). Blog does not fit our small businesses.
5. Three variation tips for the body:
   - Layout variation: never the same two-column block in every section. ALREADY-HAVE in spirit (3p no two alike).
   - Colour variation: background changes now and then. CONFLICTS with the flow theme (white plus one alternate, hairlines) and 3t (colour per product for food shops). Not adopted.
   - Section height variation: short banner, tall feature, thin divider strip, so the scroll has rhythm (frame 2: 3-col facts, a thin strip of small dashes, picture-left and picture-right pair, a bento grid, a short centred CTA). NEW as a written rule. All kinds.
6. CTA section: a full section of its own: thin banner (heading plus button, or an email field) or pricing plans. ALREADY-HAVE (section 3 cont. item 3).
7. Footer: link columns to all core pages (including those not in the nav), logo linking home, social icons, optional newsletter field, copyright. Frame 1: dark footer with four columns (Main pages, Solutions, Utility, Newsletter field) and a bottom row (logo left, credit centre, social icons right); light footer with logo plus socials left and four link columns right. ALREADY-HAVE (3k.3, 3t real footer). Newsletter field NEW, only for businesses that really send something.
8. Attention falls down the page: put the key facts and proof in the first one to two scrolls. ALREADY-HAVE in spirit (3g.13 four questions on the first screen); the "two scrolls" wording is NEW and small.

## EFFECTS
None in the sites shown.

## Suits
All kinds for nav, hero and footer anatomy; height variation suits shops and events most.
