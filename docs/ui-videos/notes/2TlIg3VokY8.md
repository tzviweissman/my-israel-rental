## The one transferable idea
Before a customer has to do or pay anything, hand them something real: pre-filled sensible choices, a visible head start, and a useful free result. Then ask. The strongest honest rows are the pre-filled form with a button that states its result ("Search 12 Available Tables") and "show a real partial result before asking for sign-up".

## What to discount or flag as dishonest for us
- 8:59 "Your Files Will Be Deleted in 3 days" with "I'll Risk It": a deadline and confirm-shaming. Only honest if the deadline is a real fact about the product; for our sites there is nothing like it. Commenters also call it a dark pattern ("a robber at a bank..."). Do not use. Keep only the honest half: say plainly what the customer already has or what a missed slot means, when true (e.g. "Saturday slots fill; Friday 14:00 is free" only if the owner's calendar says so).
- 2:32 20% progress for "sign up" already counted, and the closing line "progress, even a fake one": an invented head start is deceptive. Honest version: count only steps really done, and show the real total up front.
- 9:57 "Just 2.6%": $49 is a monthly charge set against a one-time $1,899 price. Per year it is $588, about 31% of the laptop. The comparison is apples to oranges and is exactly the anchoring trick that makes a price look smaller than it is. Do not copy; our percentages (3j) are like-for-like only.
- "Recommended for you" label on the add-on: fine only if we mean it; "Most popular" stays owner-confirmed (3j.6).
- 6:44 Duolingo "10 minutes already invested so you won't leave": sunk-cost lock-in. The presenter calls it "not manipulation"; ours is only: let people look and choose before any account.
- All statistics (3% / 30% jam, 70-90% defaults, 2,000% samples, nearly double stamps) are quoted by voice with no source on screen. The jam figures are from the well known Iyengar and Lepper study, which later replications found weak, so do not build a rule on "fewer is always better". Not verified here.
- Sponsor reads: Mobbin (3:15-3:47) and uxpeak Plus with code (7:03-8:18). Ignored.
- "Search 12 Available Tables" and "Save My Report — Free" are mock copy; the number must be real in ours.

## Ours? (rows we do NOT already do)
Already covered, not repeated: guarantee beside the button, one add-on, give first (free taster), come-back stamp card, middle option, real saving as percent, never fake scarcity (3j); fewer choices first and button names the consequence (3s.16, 3s.17); the button naming the result "Search 12 homes" style was candidate 7 in U7jTCgIWS4w.

1. **Pre-selected sensible defaults in every order/booking field** (earliest real slot or the owner's usual quantity ticked, date "Tomorrow"/next free day, time chips with one chosen, guests stepper starting at the usual number, a plain "View all times" row below): extends 3j.6 and 3s.16 (those pick a middle option and ask a question first, they do not say every field arrives filled, nor use chips for time). Must stay editable; never pre-tick paid extras (3j.2 already).
2. **Order button states the live real count of what is waiting** ("See 12 open slots on Sunday", "Send my order, ₪186") only when the number is read from the owner's real data: extends 3s.17 (names the consequence) with a live count.
3. **A head start on progress, only for real done steps** (multi-step order or quote: "Step 2 of 3", first step ticked once the item and quantity are chosen; show the full count from the start): extends 3k (basket and flow) and 3s. No invented percentage.
4. **A useful partial result before any request for contact details**: a price or availability estimate shown first, then "Send this to the owner" (we already never require sign-in to order, memory 2026-10-07); extends 3j.3 (Give first) to the quote flow: show the real estimate or available slots before asking name and phone, and the ask sits below it, button says what it does ("Save my quote"), not "Sign up".
5. **Let people build before they commit**: the customer's own choices (box contents, colours, date) visible as a live summary card that they edit before the final button, which says "Continue to confirm" not "Sign up": extends 3k (basket) and 3l (choices per item). Honest because it is their own selection, no sunk-cost wording.
6. **A price shown next to the thing it relates to** (add-on price beside the main item with the real per-unit comparison, e.g. "₪75, about ₪6 a portion"): extends 3j "Show the deal" like-for-like rules; only per same unit, never a monthly figure against a one-time price.
7. **Say what the customer would miss, in plain true words, only when it is true** ("Friday slots are booked; next free is Sunday 10:00", "This box serves 8 and your group is 12"): extends 3j "Never" list as the allowed honest form of loss framing. Never a countdown, never a shaming dismiss button; the dismiss stays neutral ("No thanks", per 3j.2).
