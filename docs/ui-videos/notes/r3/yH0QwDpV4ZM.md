## For our pages

- Press feedback on every tappable card, pack tile and button: scale to about 0.97 (and dim slightly) the instant it is pressed, return on release, and cancel (no navigation) when the pointer or finger leaves the element before release.
- Fade the page content in once on first load: short (about 200 ms), opacity only, no movement.
- Keep every animation purposeful and fast (150-300 ms); no bouncy entrance animation on every card, list or section; each animation should answer something the user just did.
- Cross-fade state changes instead of snapping: when a pack or option is chosen, fade the empty circle into a tick circle and move a 1px accent outline to the chosen row; unselected rows keep a pale grey filled circle and no outline.
- Show each pack or option row as: bold title, dimmer price line under it, and a right-aligned price on one common unit (for example per item or per session) only where that unit is true.
- Show a saving as the old price struck through beside the new one plus a short "Save ₪X" tag sitting on the row's top-right border; no sentence, no percentage.
- When the state changes, change the badge: "Save ₪X" before choosing becomes "Selected" or "Current" (solid accent pill, dark text, same position) once chosen or booked.
- Make the button label follow state ("Book" becomes "Manage booking"; "Continue" becomes a disabled grey "Processing..." while the request runs).
- Confirm success with a small toast at the top (dark bar, tick in a circle, one short line such as "Booking confirmed") that slides in and leaves by itself; no OK button.
- Keep a primary button greyed and disabled until its required field has content, then turn it solid.
- On phones keep the primary action visible above the keyboard (about 12px gap) using the visual viewport, so it is never hidden behind it.
- Give a focused text input a soft, pale glow (about 8px blur) rather than a hard focus ring.
- Open a step with its first field focused so the keyboard or cursor is ready without an extra tap.
- Let a message or notes box grow up to 4 lines, then scroll inside; shrink it back smoothly when cleared.
- Before asking for any browser permission (location, notifications, camera, photo upload), show our own short screen first: icon, one line saying why in plain words, then a "Continue" that triggers the real prompt; name the exact use in the prompt text.
- For photos and notifications say what the person gets ("show your photos on your listing", "tell you when someone replies"), never a generic line.
- Write empty states that say what is missing, what to do and where to tap ("You have no bookings yet. Pick a service and your bookings will show here.").
- Replace a bare spinner with plain status lines that change about every 2 seconds with a cross-fade and a light shimmer sweep; keep the lines true to what is happening (for example "Checking the calendar...", "Holding your slot...").
- Fade images in quickly (about 200 ms) when they arrive; dim an image to about 0.7 while pressed.
- Offer suggestion chips above a free-text box (for example quick request types on the Requests board): two scrolling rows of pills, some with a small round photo.
