## For our pages

- Show a deal as the old price struck through beside the new one plus a short "Save ₪X" tag; no sentence.
- Keep the shop's order of items varied only where the change is real: add a genuinely new product or pack to the top of the shop and label it "New"; do not shuffle prices to tease.
- Put a small honest count badge on the basket icon (accent or red circle ~35 px, white numeral, top-right) showing the real number of items in it.
- On the booking page show a visible progress cue (for example "Step 2 of 3") so people know how much is left; fill it truthfully.
- Add one honest pause before payment: a plain "Ready to book?" summary step with price, date, and a clear "Back" and "Change" beside the confirm button.
- Keep Back, close and "Continue shopping" always visible; never hide the exit.
- Quote real client praise as a chat-message style card: grey bubbles (~#E9E9EB), ~14-16 px, radius ~18 px, exact words, only from a real message and with permission.
- Make follow/add/book buttons one style repeated: same size, radius and colour on every row, hairline dividers between rows.
- Offer one-tap reactions (a few fixed emoji choices ~28 px) for feedback on a delivered order or a review, before any text box.
- Write a booking or order step as the customer's feeling in a short italic quote under the step title ("It's only five minutes.") only where it is true.
- Use a fixed colour code for status pills (one colour per meaning, black ~17 px bold text, fully rounded ~28 px high) and keep it identical on every page.
- Show a customer's own progress honestly where it exists (an order tracker "3 of 4 done", or loyalty stamps they really earned) as a ring or pill bars: done = solid accent, to-come = grey.
- Give a real timestamp on updates ("4h ago", "Today 14:30") so a message reads as recent.
- Draw a "how booking works" explainer as four labelled steps clockwise around a hand-drawn chalk-style arrow, labels bold, one idea per label.
- Open a section with a single lowercase bold geometric phrase centred on a calm ground, with thin lines drawn in above it (lines ~470 px, 2 px, diamond end caps).
- Use a single accent colour on a near-black card for a stats strip (neon-green style) with grey for what is not done yet.
- Say exactly what an offer includes at the moment of decision (one line under the button), not further down the page.

## Not for us (dishonest or not applicable)

- "Watch it before it ends!" urgency in banners or notifications: only fine for a real live event; invented urgency is dishonest.
- Removing stopping cues or building endless scroll: manipulative; we add pauses instead.
- Randomised, unpredictable rewards or prizes (variable ratio) to keep people returning: gambling-style hook.
- Streaks that punish missing a day (Snapchat, Duolingo): loss aversion with no benefit to the customer.
- "Streak about to end" or sunk-cost messages ("300 days, can't lose that now"): pressure, not service.
- Leaving loops open on purpose (unwatched stories, red badges with nothing behind them): fake unread counts are dishonest.
- Invented follower, like, reaction or review counts: never fake social proof.
- Using a likes feed to drive compulsion: not applicable to a booking or shop page.
- Promoting the presenter's Blockwalk app and QR code: his sponsorship, not a page change.
- B-roll, memes and stock footage: video-edit choices, not site changes.
