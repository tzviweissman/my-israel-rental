## For our pages

- Put a small status pill directly above the page title (cream fill, brown text, tiny star, uppercase ~11px, ~8px gap), only when true ("Best seller" if it is, "New" for a new item).
- Lead the hero with the product in use (the item beside the finished drink, dish, set-up or result), not alone on a blank ground; first gallery thumb is this shot.
- Make the gallery show use: a close-up in hand, in a real setting, then the ingredients or process, not only packshots.
- Show the real rating as the exact number: "4.9" bold then "(221 Reviews)" in lighter weight, same line; only with the real count.
- Keep a 1px pale grey vertical divider (~18px tall, ~14px each side) between facts on one line (rating | sold or booked count), only if the count is real.
- Replace dropdowns for 2 to 5 choices (flavour, size, service, time) with equal-width option cards in a row: ~40-48px tall, 1px pale border, ~10px radius, ~12px gap.
- Put a small round photo (~24-28px) left of each option name.
- Selected option state: pale mint fill, 1.5px green border, solid green circle with white tick at the right end.
- Swap the main photo and label when an option is chosen.
- Show a one-line tooltip above an option on hover: pale green bubble, ~13px, notch pointing at the card, fades in, answers the doubt in plain words ("Light, tart, and not overly sweet"); for touch, show it when selected.
- Put the plan choice (subscribe, pack, session) in a warm panel as side-by-side cards: small caps label, big bold price (~26-32px), one muted line; the pre-chosen card gets the mint fill + green border.
- Tag the best-value card with a solid green "MOST POPULAR"-style pill on its top border (~11px white caps, half overlapping), only if it really is the most chosen.
- Under the cards, put three tick lines inside the same panel that answer fears at the moment of choice ("Cancel anytime, no fees", "Free shipping", dispatch/ready time), each a green tick circle + ~15px text.
- Reveal pack sizes (1, 2, 3 months/units) only after the one-time card is chosen; panel grows smoothly; chosen tile syncs the price on the top card.
- Show a pack saving as money: old price struck through beside the new one, plus a short "Save ₪X" tag (pale green pill, ~11px caps); no sentence; compute from real prices.
- Make the main button full width inside the panel, sentence case, outline cart icon, ~12px radius, ~44px tall, dark fill; label as a step ("Add to basket, start your order") not "Add to cart".
- Drop the quantity stepper beside the button on single-item buys.
- Replace a generic grey list ("Free shipping, money back") with three icon columns: ~46px cream circle, thin brown line icon, bold headline + muted sub-line ("100% vegan / No fillers", "60-day guarantee / Full refund"), each a true, specific claim about safety or quality.
- Keep accordions (Serving info, Ingredients) below the trust row with 1px hairlines and a plus.
- Use one warm palette: green only for selection, saving and ticks; one solid button; gold only for stars.
- Check every percentage against the real prices before showing it, or show money saved instead.
- Keep the saving figure in a label, the radio label and the tick list the same number.
- Tint shadows to the surface colour instead of grey (e.g. Y 19, blur 56, #CFC9DD on a light purple ground).

## Not for us (dishonest or not applicable)

- "500+ sold this week" with fire icon: dishonest unless it is a real, current count.
- "4.9 (221 Reviews)": swapping a round number for an invented specific one is faking; only show the real count.
- "Best seller" / "Most popular" tags: only if true; never decorative.
- "Save 10% / 20%" tiles and "Save 15%" with prices that do not give those percentages: percentage-off framing, and the video's own numbers do not add up (13% and 22% actual; 35% vs 15% on the same page).
- Claimed 2% to 7% conversion and "3.5x revenue": unverified, on-screen 2.4x disagrees; do not quote.
- Subscribe pre-selected to push the plan: fine only if the cancel promise is real and the one-time option stays equally visible.
- Sponsor reads and codes (Mobbin 20% off, PLAYBOOK15 vs spoken "playbook50"): ads, not design changes.
- Subscribe, priority dispatch, 60-day guarantee, heavy-metals testing claims: only for businesses that really offer them.
