## For our pages

- Show a photo of the actual product or place on every card, not the business logo.
- Give photos real space: wide rectangle, at least three times the area of a thumbnail, biggest thing on the card; never a 56 px thumbnail.
- Do not cram items above the fold; make the first screen worth scrolling from. Let the next card peek in at the bottom edge so the list visibly continues (no more than two full cards in view on a phone).
- Use one corner radius family across a screen (about 12-14 px on photos, full pills on chips and search) instead of mixing 4 px boxes with circles.
- Give each card three levels: name bold and larger, rating with star and weight, everything else in lighter grey; separate details with middle dots, not hyphens.
- Highlight "Free" or a good price in bold green; keep other details grey.
- Show filters as visible tappable chips (solid black when active, white with 1px pale border otherwise), row scrolling sideways with the last chip cut off; no 17-option dropdowns.
- Put a round filter button and a round sort button before the quick chips; filter button opens a bottom sheet with groups.
- In the filter sheet print a count beside every option, grey out options with 0 results, and make the button read "Show N places" (live count); never just "Show".
- Show a result count next to the list heading ("640 places") in small grey.
- Offer a card / compact-list switch (two icons in a pale pill, active on white) instead of choosing one layout for everybody.
- Replace stock-photo category circles with one custom icon set on quiet, near-neutral rounded tiles so the product photos stand out.
- Make the search placeholder say what can be searched ("Search places, dishes or cuisines").
- Put name and numbers under the photo, not on it, so any uploaded photo works (name bold ~17 px left, time bold right, rating line below, "Free" in green under the time).
- Use a "BEST MATCH"-style label only if it is true for that visitor (small caps cream pill, top-left of the photo, dark-green text).
- On a booking page, make the button say what will happen ("Book Sat 11:30") and offer three real time chips with the chosen one outlined.
- Mark a listing that fits the visitor's search with a small "Fits your search" tick pill, only when it really fits.
- Show the price with a per-month figure beside it where that is the real cost ("$935,000" with "$5,216/mo").
- Open filters as a sheet over the results (grabber, X left, "Filters" centre, "Reset" right), never a full page; keep every filter in one scroll.
- Use a range slider with typed Minimum / Maximum boxes beside it, a tooltip above the thumb while dragging, and a "No max" link.
- Show a histogram of real prices behind the price slider, selected range solid and the rest pale, with a one-line subtitle ("3+ bed homes . median $568K"); only with real data.
- Replace plus/minus steppers with all options visible in a row (Studio, 1+, 2+, 3+, 4+), with "3+" meaning at least 3 and an "Exact number" checkbox beneath.
- Give each type (House, Townhouse, Condo ...) a small line icon above its label in a 2x3 grid; add the real count under each label.
- Show "N of total" fit in the price header ("459 of 1,557") in the same sheet.
- Ask for what people know (deposit, monthly budget) and work out the price for them, with a "Use as my maximum" button and an empty-state line "Add both and I'll work out the price you can shop at." (only with a correct formula).
- Show the "what it costs per month" breakdown as plain lines (principal, tax, insurance, total) with a footnote of what is not included.
- On the histogram mark "Your reach" for the visitor's budget and a line "N a month over your budget, M of these fit it. Show those".
- Show the effect of a stricter choice before the tap as a small "-230 homes" pill, and let two taps make a range across a joined segmented bar with a "Clear" link.
- Keep the sheet short: show only information that helps the decision and leave out the rest.
- Count taps: aim to set two filters in two taps, not seven; keep chosen values visible on the sheet, not on separate pages.
- When a request has no right default, give the visitor the control (toggle, "Clear", "Reset") rather than deciding for them.

## Not for us (dishonest or not applicable)

- "BEST MATCH" or "Fits your search" labels when nothing real backs them: dishonest unless computed from what the visitor entered.
- Showing "$1,500 a month over your budget" style guesses or a "Your reach" line from invented rates: only valid with a correct, shown formula.
- Counts, "640 places", "+1,332 more worth opening": only with real numbers; never invented.
- The mortgage calculator and house-price histogram: property sales only, not applicable to shops, bookings or services.
- "COMPLETELY EXCESSIVE" stamp, soil/trash/filing-cabinet/scale metaphors, count-up animation: video decoration, not page content.
- Sponsor segments (Mobbin MCP, 20% off, UXPeak Plus plug): advertising, not a design rule.
- Third-party delivery apps' promo tricks seen in passing (FLAT 50% OFF, "Free delivery on your first order", percentage-off banners): percentage framing is banned for us.
