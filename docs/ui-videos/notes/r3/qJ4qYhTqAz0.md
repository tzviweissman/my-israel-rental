## For our pages

- Show a saving as the old price struck through beside the new one, plus a short pale-green "Save ₪X" tag; no sentence, and only when the old price was really charged.
- Put the plan or pack you want to lead with first and make it the pre-selected one (purple outline and corner tab in the video; our accent outline and a small corner tab).
- On a shop or booking page, keep the choices to the few that matter; if a side option competes with the main purchase, move it behind a small grey link ("View all options") instead of showing it beside it.
- Keep the other option reachable: a ~12px grey centred link under the main button ("View all plans"), demoted, never removed.
- For a free trial, free first visit or pay-later booking, lay it out as a short timeline above the price: three steps with icons, real dates in small pills ("Today", "Day 12", "Day 14"), one grey caption each saying exactly what happens and when any charge comes.
- Add a reassurance line directly between the price box and the button, with a green check, bold, ~13px: "No charge for 14 days, cancel anytime" (a comma, not a long dash).
- Put "No payment due now" with a tick right above the main button when that is true.
- Price box layout: small grey lead-in ("14-day trial, then"), the price bold with "/month" and "(billed annually)" in grey, the regular price struck through below, all centred in a white rounded box.
- Primary button: flat solid fill, uppercase bold condensed text, ~3px corners, ~250x56px, no shadow (ours stays black with white text per the flow theme).
- Make the price follow the customer's own need: a stepper (minus, big number, plus) for quantity, with the per-unit price and the total under it (Undertone: 2 sessions, "$12.5 / session", "$25 per month").
- Monthly/annual switch as a segmented control, with a small pill on the cheaper option showing the true saving in money.
- Plan comparison table when tiers differ: rows of features, tick circles, dashes where a tier lacks it, the recommended column tinted pale.
- Say what the first step costs in the button's own sub line ("First 3 days free. Cancel anytime"), only if true.
- Do not blur or lock content the person already made or owns behind a wall; show the full product and ask one question instead.
- Let people try the full product before paying where we can (free trial beat a discounted intro price by 140% revenue in their test); do not lead with a discount to get them in.
- Test one structural change at a time with the price unchanged (hide one option, change which plan comes first) and judge it over about a week.
- Hide extra options beside the main offer (Mojo removed credit packs from the first screen and kept them for existing subscribers) when they pull people into a smaller, cheaper choice.
- Show proof of a result as one big number in mono with a small label, and a thin bar for before and after, only with real figures we hold.
- Underline the one price that matters and fade the rest when a single number is the point of a slide or section.
- Use a yellow highlighter mark on the single key phrase of a quote or result, with earlier lines pale and the current one dark.

## Not for us (dishonest or not applicable)

- "SAVE 73%" / "20% OFF" / "Save 44%" / "SAVE 50%" tabs: percentage-off framing, banned by our rules; show money saved instead.
- A struck "regular price" that was never really charged ("$37.49/month" beside a "20% OFF" pill): fake reference price, only use a real previous price.
- Making a monthly price look small next to a long plan: our rule is real savings in money, never how much more the other costs.
- Pre-selecting the long plan to steer people (default effect): fine only when it is genuinely the better deal and the other choice stays one tap away.
- Hiding the monthly option completely: keep it reachable, never hidden.
- "No charge for 14 days" billing flow: only applies if we really run a trial with a later charge; do not copy it for free listings.
- Statistics ("60% to 75-80%", "+14%", "+140%", "80 million downloads"): their data, never quote or imitate as ours.
- Superwall / RevenueCat / Adapty article cards, jPOS, Spotify and app-store montage: third-party material and app-store specific.
- App paywall tooling (credit packs, ARPPU, A/B tests at app scale): subscription-app concepts, not our shop or booking flow.
- Typed monospace explainer slides with cursor: video style, not a page pattern for a small business site.
- Tim's thumbnails, facecam and Zipsnap call promotion: self-promotion, not applicable.
