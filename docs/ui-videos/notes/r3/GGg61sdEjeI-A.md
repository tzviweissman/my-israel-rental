## Easy to miss

- 0:37-1:21: test the page with several different photos (pineapple, ice, pizza, farmer) before trusting an overlay icon; the fix is a white circle with a thin outline, not a darker photo.
- 3:06-3:32: pick one photo style and one background for every product (cut-out on the same pale warm white), so the grid never looks messy; the photo shows the product the way it is sold (a basket for something sold by weight, not one berry).
- 5:22: small uppercase label needs a little letter spacing and a muted (olive) colour, not neon.
- 5:34-5:44: badge text cut from "20% OFF DISCOUNT" + icon to "20% off", sentence case, no icon (the percentage is only honest if the real price was higher).
- 6:23-6:37: body text gets looser leading (~1.5) and slightly softer grey, and the copy itself was rewritten shorter and friendlier (4 lines to 3).
- 8:49: feature list turned into one row of four centred line icons with sentence-case labels under them, all one olive colour.
- 9:01: dividers from black ~2 px to pale grey ~1 px; tighten gaps to ~24 px equal steps.
- 10:21-10:35: remove "- 1 kg" from the title, show the unit next to the price ("$6.20 $7.20 per kg"), two decimals.
- 11:27-11:33: unit inside the stepper ("1 kg") and the total inside the button ("Add to cart · $6.20") that updates live.
- 12:09: the title moves up into the top bar and stays sticky once the photo scrolls away; 12:25-12:35 the cart icon flies and the button turns into a green check circle.
