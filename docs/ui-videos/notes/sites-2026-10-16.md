## 8-12 buildable techniques

1. **Light the subject, not the room.** Use a dark-to-mid background with a radial gradient behind the product (`radial-gradient(circle at 60% 50%, #2a2d33, #0e0f11)`). For bright pastry or acai, invert it: a warm, bright background with a soft spotlight. Suits all four kinds of business. Never ship a hero where the product sits below about 60% of the brightest pixel.
2. **Rim light or glow on the product.** Use a CSS `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 40px rgba(255,106,26,.5))` in the brand colour, or have it baked into the generated image. Suits balloons, gifts and acai.
3. **A light sweep through the headline.** Run a gradient `mask-position` animation of 1.2-1.8 s across the text and product. Suits any business.
4. **Cut-out hero object overlapping the headline.** Use a transparent PNG with the headline `z-index` behind it, and the product bleeding off the frame edge. Suits bakery and gift shop.
5. **Line-by-line masked headline reveal.** Use `clip-path` with `translateY(100%)` per line, about 80 ms stagger over 700 ms. Suits all.
6. **One accent word.** Use one colour change in the headline, either the "OUT" or the green phrase. Never use more than one. Suits all.
7. **Mix of a heavy sans and an italic serif.** Use about 8vw for the hero, tight leading of about 0.95, and uppercase for the sans. Suits balloons and gifts.
8. **Tilted 3D tile grid with a camera push-in.** Use `perspective: 1200px` and `rotateX/rotateY`, then scale in on scroll. Use it for a menu or gallery. Suits bakery, acai and balloons.
9. **Tiny mono eyebrow plus tiny grey nav.** Use 11px, letter-spaced, uppercase labels. It adds a premium feel, so use it sparingly. Suits all.
10. **Generated video loop for the hero (inferred).** Use a 4-6 s clip between a before still and an after still. The video's own generated-video approach is the likely source of the glow trails. Suits acai (pour) and balloons (inflate).
11. **Monochrome logo and proof row.** Use a white or grey logo row with even spacing. Suits businesses with press, venue or partner logos.
12. **Do not do these (inferred from the presenter's "leave out" list):** no confetti, no invented numbers, no counters.

## Lighting rule, ready to paste

Add to `docs/page-generation-rules.md` (I did not edit any files):

> **Lighting.** The subject must be the brightest thing in frame. Before showing a page, check the hero at laptop and phone size: the product has to read in the first second at full opacity. No ghosted, dimmed, or under-exposed product images, no heavy dark overlay on top of a photo. Dark pages need a spotlight or rim light on the subject; bright pages need warm, soft light with no muddy shadows. A dim first frame is a failure even if the final frame is good.

**Buildable techniques**
1. Light the subject first. Prompt for bright, soft light on a mid or light ground. Check with `scripts/brightness.py`. Suits all four of your kinds of business.
2. Add a glow floor line. A 2px gradient bar plus a blurred box-shadow in the accent colour under the product. Suits acai and gift shops.
3. Keep one accent colour for the CTA and the active state only, for example a lime or brand pill at 14px, on one button per screen.
4. Build a tab bar that changes the hero state. Swap generated stills with a 300ms crossfade, with the tabs styled as a frosted pill (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). Examples: bowl size, balloon colours, gift-box styles.
5. Add a colour or option tray under the hero. Clicking a swatch swaps the image. This suits balloons and bakeries.
6. Place a 3-4 line headline top-left at 3-6vw, with the subject centred below it and not covered.
7. Add hotspot dots on the product that open a short caption (CSS, positioned in percent). This suits menus and gift items.
8. Alternate bright and dark bands, but keep the photo bright inside the dark one. Use a hairline-ruled numbered list beside a tall photo.
9. Use a full-bleed photo section with a short caption below it, not over it.
10. Run an object through two sections, for example a cut-out glass or fruit image that sits across the join.
11. Add a "Back to" pill for any deeper view. This is inferred as a pattern, not seen in detail.
12. Use one serif paragraph with italic emphasis next to a hero object (the Ascend look). This suits bakeries and gift shops.

**Buildable techniques to copy**
1. **Lit object on a mid-tone ground.** Use bright, even front light and a coloured ground such as olive, cream or sky. Keep the lit subject bright and make a dark ground the exception. This is the lighting fix. It suits acai bowls and bakeries.

3. **Floating cut-out tiles.** Rounded-square tiles (radius 24px) with transparent PNG cut-outs, rotated ±10–15° around the hero, plus a 20px parallax drift (CSS transform on scroll). It suits bakeries and gift shops.
4. **One rim light or glass chip.** Add a white 1px glow or a frosted chip (backdrop-filter: blur(12px) with a white 12% fill) over a dark hero. This is optional.
5. **Poster typography.** Use one oversized, tight, bold sans headline at 8–10vw and a lowercase serif italic accent word. It suits all four business types.
6. **One section equals one screen.** Use min-height:100vh sections that alternate text and art, with the text column at about 35% width. It is easy to build and never overlaps text on imagery.
7. **Deliberate hard colour breaks between sections.** After a calm cream section, drop in one bold block (green or navy band) to reset attention. Keep it to one or two.
8. **Pressable buttons.** Use solid saturated fills with a darker 4px bottom edge that moves down on :active. It suits every small business.
9. **Lowercase kicker plus short headline plus 2-line subline.** Use a coloured kicker in the accent, then 2-line body text at 16–18px. It suits every business.
10. **One consistent illustrated or photographic cast.** Generate all assets in one session with the same light and palette. It suits balloons and gift shops.
11. **Sticky category bar.** Use an icon-plus-label chip row under the nav (flavours or product types), with horizontal scroll on phones. It suits menus and shops.
12. **Interactive proof card.** Build a small working widget in a section (a mini order card or quiz). Inferred as a general pattern from the Neuro quiz.

**Lighting rule (for the user's request, to add to the rules)**

In every finished page I saw, the pages that feel inviting are bright. The subject is lit from above with soft, warm, diffuse light. Shadows are tinted by the background colour, never black. The only dark page (Glaido, and the navy deck) keeps one bright subject and one accent colour. A builder should follow these rules:

- Default to a high-key, bright hero, with the main subject clearly lighter than its background.
- If the hero is dark, light the subject separately and give it a warm highlight. Do not accept a mid-dark overall frame.
- Tint shadows with the background colour. Use no pure black shadows under food or products.
- Check the hero's average brightness before showing the page, and re-render if it looks dim.
- Generated video or stills must specify "soft overhead daylight, bright, warm, high key" in the prompt.

**Common thread on light.** In every site the subject is the lit thing and the ground is dark. The subject is brighter than its surround. The light is warm or has a rim light, and the type sits on empty ground. Contrast comes from lit subject versus dark ground. Dark alone does not do it. None of them is dim overall.

**Buildable techniques**
1. **Subject is the brightest thing on screen.** Prompt the film for a lit subject with a rim light. For a bakery or gift shop, use a warm key and a light cream ground. Suits all.
2. **One word per beat.** A centred serif word at 40–48 px and a 14–16 px sub, one idea per scroll step. Use CSS opacity and translate on scroll. Suits gifts and balloons.
3. **Scroll-scrubbed video.** Extract frames to webp, draw them on a canvas with `currentFrame = scrollProgress × n` inside a pinned section. Suits acai (the pour) and a bakery (frosting or dough).
4. **Live readout.** A mono 11–12 px counter bottom left, such as "PULL 12.2 s", driven by scroll progress. Suits any process: baking time, balloon count.
5. **Accent word in the heading.** One italic or colour-shifted word in the accent, with the filled CTA in the same colour. Suits all.
6. **Colour-pick from the subject.** Take the CTA and accent from the hero image (the copper, the neon green). Suits all.
7. **Rail of type.** Keep captions at the edge opposite the subject, at most 30% of the width. Suits all (inferred from the frames).
8. **Raking-light texture macro.** An opening macro of the material (dough, wrapping, fabric) with side light. Generate it as a video. Suits bakery and gift shop.
9. **Interactive moment.** A press-and-hold or chip picker that changes the picture and words. Suits a bowl builder or a balloon colour picker.
10. **Bright variant.** Use the cream serum layout for light brands: soft daylight, a dark serif headline, product on stone or wood (seen in the mock-up).
11. **Pill CTA, pinned.** A pill CTA in the nav and a pair of filled and outline CTAs at the end of the film. Suits all.
12. **Brightness check.** Render frames and measure the lighting before showing the page, then fix it (inferred from the author's legibility battery).
