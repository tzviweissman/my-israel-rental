## Candidate rules for us
1. **One scroll-driven hero film per page, with the product itself as the subject (pour, drip, stitch).** SHOWN 0:10-0:55, 16:00-16:30 / SAID 0:20-1:10. For us: a bakery pours or slices, a kasher steams a pot; the film must be of their real work, or clearly generated and marked as such.
2. **Hero text is short, in one fixed place (bottom-left or centred), and swaps per scroll beat, 3-4 captions at most.** SHOWN 16:00-16:30, 0:30-0:55 / SAID 7:30. For us: max about 8 words per caption, each tied to a visible moment in the film.
3. **Keep the nav tiny: wordmark, 3 links, one filled pill CTA.** SHOWN 0:10, 16:00. For us: the pill is "Order" or "WhatsApp us" and is the only filled element in the nav.
4. **Dark full-bleed film with one warm accent colour, and that accent is also the CTA colour.** SHOWN 0:10, 0:30, 0:50, 16:30. For us: take the accent from the owner's real product or brand colour, not invented per page.
5. **End the film on the payoff plus CTA, never a dead frame (full cup plus two buttons).** SHOWN 16:10 / SAID 8:00. For us: last frame shows the finished product with "Order" and a secondary link.
6. **One interactive toy section per page (hold to pull a shot, with a live timer).** SHOWN 16:35 / SAID 12:00. For us: a box or order-size picker, only if it ends in a real order or WhatsApp message.
7. **Below the film, three photo cards with name, one-line description and real price.** SHOWN 16:30. For us: the owner's real products and prices only.
8. **Mono micro-labels for specs and hours, small caps for kickers.** SHOWN 16:30-16:50, 0:10. For us: use sparingly for hours, hechsher line, allergens; check Hebrew/RTL falls back cleanly.
9. **FAQ as a plain accordion in an "answered plainly" tone; headlines are one confident sentence.** SHOWN 16:45. For us: 4 to 6 questions the owner really gets (delivery area, kosher, lead time).
10. **Run a review pass after every build: caption legibility against the brightest frame, phone sizes, reduced motion, console errors.** SHOWN 17:30 (Claude's on-screen summary) / SAID 11:00-14:00. For us: make "worst-case contrast on the brightest frame, at least 4.5:1" an explicit measured check, with heavier caption weight.
11. **Walk the whole page once, write every change down, send them in one batch.** SAID 11:00-12:30. For us: matches the owner review pass; one list, one revision round.
12. **Ask real-versus-generated first (real shop with photos / without / new idea).** SHOWN 9:50 / SAID 8:30-10:00. For us: first question is "do you have your own photos and logo?", and real ones always win.
13. **Offer 3 hero concepts with a recommended default, and approve the still frame before spending video credits.** SHOWN 10:30-11:30 / SAID 9:00-10:30. For us: approve the still before the clip; state the credit cost.
14. **The page must read without the video (poster frame, reduced motion).** SAID 17:30. For us: poster frame carries headline and CTA; the video never holds the only copy of the offer.

### Rejected or restricted for honesty
- "Heard at the counter" quote cards, "Roasted Tuesday. Pulled today." and the batch ticker are invented on a demo brand (he admits the order form sends nowhere). Never use invented quotes, names, roast dates, batch numbers or freshness claims. Only real customer quotes with permission.
- Brand-new "idea" businesses with fully generated product shots and a made-up shop ("one long counter and six seats") passed off as real. For us: generated imagery only where the owner asks, logged in the build notes, never as pictures of their real shop or food.
- "$10K website" framing and the affiliate links (Hostinger, Higgsfield) are sales content; ignore. His price and plan facts are not ours.
- Neon particle-tunnel and space-travel concepts (Sift) do not fit a bakery or trainer.
- Hostinger connector hosting: not our stack (Railway).
