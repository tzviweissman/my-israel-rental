## What most share (structure for our sites)
- First screen: ONE big line, one short sentence, ONE button; the same button repeats in the top bar (filled pill, right side).
- One row of trust right under the button (logos, or three short chips).
- The real product or work shown early.
- A "how it works" in about three steps, then bento or picture-and-text rows.
- One accent colour for the button and one key word; the rest calm.
- Simple nav: 4 to 6 text links plus one filled button.

## Ideas, checked against page-generation-rules.md
- One word in the headline drawn by hand (crossed out then replaced, in the button colour), pulling the eye to the button: NEW. Suits events, trainers, food shops. One word only, still on phones.
- Hand-drawn squiggle or arrow pointing at the main button: NEW (line 2021 bans motion without a reason; this one points at the action). Suits trainers, salons, food shops.
- Hero selector chips that swap the hero picture ("Cakes / Boxes / Platters"; "Hair / Nails"; room 1 / 2 / 3): NEW. Suits food shops, salons, events, holiday rentals. Close cousin ALREADY-HAVE: one master per product (one-look-per-product).
- Three short trust chips under the button ("Delivery Friday", "Kosher", "Cancel any time"): NEW as a standing slot; owner-confirmed facts only (claims row).
- A soft light that follows the visitor down the page and sits on each section's key word: NEW; kin to the cloud pointer and shade sweep (~line 1279). Suits places, trainers, salons. Counts toward the 3h.20 effect limit; one per site.
- Hover tile that deepens, zooms its photo and dims siblings: ALREADY-HAVE (hover lift, 3h.25 / 3s).
- Mouse-following robot, Spline objects, emoji on the cursor, floating glass cards, text moving at different scroll speeds: SKIP (decorative, tech look; line 2021 bans cursor trails and parallax).
- A line that draws down the page linking steps: ALREADY-HAVE as light-up steps, and the rule says it is overused; do not repeat.
- Soft radial gradients with grain: ALREADY-HAVE gradient (line 1800); grain is NEW and small. Suits food shops, salons.
- Photo rising about 20 px inside its box on scroll: NEW but tiny; touches the "no parallax for decoration" rule (line 2065), so at most one story photo.
- Logo walls: SKIP (banned, line 447).
