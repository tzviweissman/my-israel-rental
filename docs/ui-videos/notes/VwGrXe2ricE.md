## Candidate rules for us
1. **Make each section one screen with one point and one button.** SHOWN 9:00-10:10, 12:00-12:45, 21:25-21:45 / SAID 18:55-19:20 ("one page, one thought").
   For us: a bakery home page is a hero (what and where) plus 3 to 5 sections, each filling about one laptop height with one headline (max about 8 words), 2 to 3 lines, one action. Testable: no section needs more than one scroll-height at 1440x900.
2. **Do not use the template tells: one default font, indigo-purple gradient button, always exactly three cards, frosted-glass blobs, vague "Supercharge..." headlines.** SHOWN 0:25-1:20 / SAID 0:40-1:22.
   For us: audit each page against these six; use a deliberate font pair, a flat brand colour, as many cards as the business really has, and headlines that name the product.
3. **Choose the typeface on purpose and look at a shortlist before building.** SHOWN 13:00-15:20 (five named font personalities, brand name in 10 faces) / SAID 12:59-15:26.
   For us: 3 to 5 candidate faces per business (warm serif for a bakery, tight grotesque for a trainer) that cover Hebrew, with the licence checked.
4. **Borrow structure from a strong same-category site, then re-skin with the business's own look.** SHOWN 3:20-6:40, 9:00-10:10 / SAID 1:22-2:05, 4:34-5:00.
   For us: take section order and rhythm only; swap colour, type, imagery and copy. Never copy their artwork or wording.
5. **One accent colour on a white or warm ground; alternate section backgrounds sparingly (white, one pale tint, one dark band).** SHOWN 9:00-10:10, 12:20-12:45.
   For us: inside the locked brand tokens, one tinted band and one dark band per page for rhythm; a wavy or angled edge on at most one transition.
6. **Use one illustration or photo style across the whole page, written down as DO/DON'T lines.** SHOWN 14:15-14:45 / SAID 14:22-14:43.
   For us: same crop, light and background for every product photo; list the rules in the page brief (real photos only, no mixed stock).
7. **Hero: headline, one CTA, one big visual, with a proof row directly below.** SHOWN 11:50-12:10, 15:40-15:55 / SAID 15:47-16:08.
   For us: what you sell and where, one button (WhatsApp or Order), the owner's real photo, and a small row of REAL facts underneath (years open, hechsher, true Google rating).
8. **Show the offer working in a small interactive card.** SHOWN 12:10-12:20 and strip at 12:18, 21:25-21:35.
   For us: a mini order or booking card (cake size, price, pickup day; workout type, schedule) built on the business's real options only.
9. **Headlines in short plain words, sentence case, naming the benefit.** SHOWN 9:00-9:40, 12:10-12:40 / SAID 15:26-18:55.
   For us: 3 to 7 words, one claim the owner can back up; the banned-words list in brand/voice.md applies.
10. **Name the customer's pain first, then the fix.** SHOWN 19:35-20:15 / SAID 18:35-18:55.
    For us: a question opener only if the owner confirms the pain is real; no invented statistics.
11. **Specific or silent: cut any line that could sit on a competitor's page.** SHOWN 19:35-20:15 / SAID 18:35-18:55.
    For us: replace "high quality, great service" with a fact the owner gave (bakes from 4 a.m., delivers in Rehovot).
12. **Reduce thinking: fewer numbers, short sentences, one ask per screen.** SHOWN 18:25-18:40 / SAID 18:13-18:55.
    For us: prices as single numbers, no sums for the reader, one button per screen.
13. **Show the owner a before/after copy table.** SHOWN 19:35-20:15 / SAID 17:09-17:30.
    For us: when rewriting a page, give each old line beside the new one so the owner approves line by line.
14. **Mascots or characters only if the business wants one, and only with a locked model sheet.** SHOWN 11:50-12:45, 14:15-14:45 / SAID 9:10-9:31, 11:15-11:35.
    For us: optional for a kids' bakery or gift shop; one cast, one palette, labelled as illustration.
15. **Review against a reference with a yes/no list instead of "make it better".** SHOWN 10:20-10:45 title cards / SAID 10:13-10:53.
    For us: check each section against its reference on type, spacing, CTA, copy; stop when all pass.

### Rejected or restricted for honesty
- "Trusted by" logo row and the "4.8 out of 5" stars under the Neuro hero (12:00, 15:40): only with the business's real current rating and real named clients who gave permission; never placeholders.
- Streak, XP and heart counters in the quiz card (12:10-12:20): invented activity and urgency on a small business page; use only real data (real slots left, real hours).
- "2 seconds away from buying it" (15:47) and the "$10,000 websites" claim: sales talk, not usable.
- Re-skinning Duolingo too literally (mascot art, wording, "Super Duolingo" layout): copyright and trademark risk; take structure only.
- Higgsfield/Seedance character art and clips (9:53, 11:15): fine as clearly illustrative art, never as photos of the owner's products or staff, never passed off as theirs.
- Fonts: check every licence; "free" lists often mean personal use only (said at 15:03).
- Copy tools must never invent testimonials, counts or social proof.
- SaaS-specific ideas (codified design system, login card, XP) do not fit most small local business pages.
