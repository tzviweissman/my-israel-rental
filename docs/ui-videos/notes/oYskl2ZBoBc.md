## Do not copy

- The 2% and 7% conversion numbers and "three and a half times more revenue" (SAID, unsourced, a hook).
- "4.9, 221 reviews" and "500+ sold this week": invented example data, and a sold-this-week count is fake urgency unless real and checkable.
- "MOST POPULAR", "SAVE 10%" and "SAVE 20%" tags without the owner's real prices and real popularity. Percent savings break our money-only rule.
- The Mobbin sponsor segment (3:36-4:36) and the UI/UX Playbook plug with its discount code (6:09-7:02) are sales pitches. "Start my journey" is a health-brand voice, not for local shops.
- Subscription framing (default to subscribe, 35% off) does not fit a bakery or nail salon; use it only if the owner truly runs a standing order.

## For our pages (small businesses)

- Bakeries, caterers, meat boards: flavour or size tiles, each with its own small photo that swaps the hero photo; then one bundle row showing the real saving in money; then a button with the next step in plain words.
- Coffee and gift shops: tiles instead of a dropdown, with a one-line taste or use note per tile, and a three-icon row of the owner's real guarantees under the button.
- Nail salons and gyms: two selection cards side by side (single visit and a pack), the better value pre-selected only if the owner agrees, "cancel or reschedule free" inside the card if true; long detail (what is included, aftercare) in collapsed rows.
- Everywhere: show the finished result in use in the hero, exact real review figures or none, and make the buy block fit one laptop screen so it feels like a finished, high-end shop rather than a form.
