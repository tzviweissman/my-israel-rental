## Dead-end promises: destination lacks the affordance

1. **Overview "Waiting on you" card, link "Open"** (`frontend/src/components/dashboard/OverviewTab.jsx:166`).
   Goes to `bookings` if any booking awaits a reply, otherwise `job-requests`. `Dashboard.js:607` renders `job-requests` only when `showGigTabs`, with no empty state. Every renter and every lister with no business sees "0 / Nothing needs a reply" plus "Open", and Open gives a blank pane (verified in code).
   *Fix (change the promise):* hide the "Open" cta when `waiting === 0` or when not `showGigTabs`; or add an EmptyPane for `job-requests`.

2. **Feature pages, host features "Try it"** (`frontend/src/data/featureLibrary.js:117,131,145`, rendered at `FeatureDetail.jsx:102`).
   `one-calendar`, `instant-or-ask`, `what-to-charge` link to `/dashboard?tab=properties`. `Dashboard.js:492` renders that tab only for `isPropertyLister` (owner/manager/admin), with no fallback. A renter or provider who reads the public feature page and clicks gets a blank pane. `ctaHost` is only used by two features.
   *Fix (build the affordance):* add an EmptyPane for `properties` like the `bulk-manager` one (`Dashboard.js:524`, points to `/why-host`). This also covers item 5 below.

3. **Setup checklist `biz.areas`, "Set your service areas"** (`backend/routes/onboarding.py:174-178`).
   `done` is `any(bool(b.get("areas")))`, and the projection (line 116) omits `serves_nationwide`. The details form's `ServiceAreaPicker` can save nationwide with `areas=[]`; `BusinessCompleteness.jsx` counts that as done, the dashboard checklist does not. A nationwide business sees the item for the life of the checklist. (Checklist side verified; picker behaviour from sub-audit.)
   *Fix:* add `serves_nationwide` to the projection and `or b.get("serves_nationwide")` to `done`.

4. **Setup checklist `prop.availability`, "Set your availability"** (`onboarding.py:205,232-236`).
   Ticks on `available_from` or the singular `ical_url`. Every iCal write goes to `ical_urls` (`routes/ical.py`), so an owner who adds a calendar feed (what tour step `h.calendar` pushes) never gets the tick.
   *Fix:* test `ical_urls` (non-empty) and add it to the projection.
