## Part B. One improvement

**Mechanism: operational transparency, the labour illusion** (Buell and Norton, 2011: people value a service more, and come back more, when they can see the work being done for them).
Surface: the order card, `frontend/src/components/dashboard/OrderCard.jsx:285` to `:313`. The new "Send the customer a photo" button is the exact tool for this, but it sits behind the card's small chevron, among Edit, Repeat and Back.
Change: while an order is "Preparing", show the photo button in the card's main row beside "Ready" (the same button, no new feature); leave it in the extra actions for other states.
What would show it worked: the share of preparing orders with `making_photo` set rises from its first-week level, and orders with a photo are reordered more often than those without (the tracking page's "Order again" link and repeat orders from the same phone are already recorded). Honest by design: it is the business's own photo of the real order.
