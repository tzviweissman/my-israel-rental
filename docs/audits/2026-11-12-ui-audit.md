## Part B: one improvement idea (proposal only)
- Mechanism: information scent (people keep going when a link says plainly what is on the other side; Pirolli and Card). Used before on the Restaurants tile on /businesses, not on business sites.
- Surface: the new "Back to businesses" pill, `frontend/public/pages/mir-back.js:20`, which always says "Businesses" even when the visitor came from a filtered list (for example `/services?cat=food`, which the pill already remembers).
- Change: name the list they will return to, from the stored path: "← Restaurants", "← Cleaners", "← Search results". Fall back to "Businesses". Real labels only, from the category they actually filtered by.
- Expected if it works: more visitors open a second business after the first. The visits the app already records (`designed_site_visit`, by source) would show more second-business visits per session from the businesses list, two weeks before against two weeks after.
