# UI / UX audit, 11 Nov 2026

## In short
- The new business sign-up shows two progress signals that disagree on the first screen: the words say "Your page is 0% ready" while the bar under them is already a third full. A business owner starting out sees "0%" first.
- On phones, the new "+ Add a photo" style shortcuts on the sign-up are small to tap (26px tall, the comfortable size is 44px), and so are "Start over" and the language switch.
- On the L.A. Cholent page, in a laptop window about 1000 by 700, the kosher line ("Kosher, hechsher from Rabbi Weiner") is cut in half at the bottom of the opening screen. That is the trust line customers look for.
- Fixed tonight: the "Your page is live!" heading lost its exclamation mark, in English and Hebrew (branch `claude/sweet-knuth-9ul4q2`).
- Needs your decision: none. One improvement idea below (start the readiness bar from what is honestly already done).

## New findings

1. **Sign-up readiness label and step bar disagree** (Low, business owners).
   `frontend/src/pages/CreateGig.jsx:1157` prints "Your page is {{pct}}% ready" from the seven readiness items; `:1183` fills the step bar by step number (`n <= step`), so step 1 of 3 is a third full while the label says 0%.
   Proof: `docs/audits/shots/2026-11-11/signup-en-1280.png`, `signup-he-375.png` (Hebrew: "הדף שלכם מוכן ב-0%" over a bar a third full).
   Fix: pick one signal. Either fill the bar with `readyPct`, or label the bar "Step 1 of 3" and keep the percentage for the chips.

2. **Sign-up tap targets under 44px on phones** (Low, business owners).
   Measured at 375 wide: the seven readiness chips `wizard-todo-*` are 26px tall (`CreateGig.jsx:1198`, `px-2.5 py-1 text-xs`), "Start over" is 16px (`:1176`, plain underlined `text-xs`), and the bare sign-up header's language switch is 34px (`Navigation.js:354`, `py-1.5`). The goal chips are 40px.
   Proof: measurements from the rendered page (Playwright, `getBoundingClientRect`), screenshots `signup-en-375.png`, `signup-he-375.png`.
   Fix: `min-h-[44px]` (or `py-2.5`) on the chips and the language switch; give "Start over" `py-3` padding.

3. **L.A. Cholent home: kosher line cut at 1000x700** (Low, customers).
   `clipped.mjs` reported "Kosher, hechsher from Rabbi Weiner (cut by 11px by div.sc-stage) at scroll 0". Confirmed: the line wraps to two lines at this width and its box ends at 713px in a 700px window, and it stays there while the opening holds (scroll 0, 60, 150 and 300 all give top 674, bottom 713).
   Proof: `docs/audits/shots/2026-11-11/cholent-index-1000x700-top.png` ("WEINER" cut at the bottom edge).
   Fix (in `scrollcraft/builds/la-cholent/`, then republish): shorten the fact to "Hechsher: Rabbi Weiner", or lift the fact list so the opening's bottom pad covers two lines at heights under 720.

## Part B: one improvement idea (proposal only)
- Mechanism: endowed progress (people work harder to finish a goal they feel they have already started; Nunes and Dreze, 2006). Used before on the dashboard leads board, not on the sign-up.
- Surface: the business sign-up readiness line, `frontend/src/pages/CreateGig.jsx:1157`, which opens at "Your page is 0% ready".
- Change: count only what is honestly already true before the first keystroke: the listing type is preselected (Deliverable service), and when they arrive from the Google lookup the name, area and category are filled. Add "Choose your type" as a done item, so a new owner sees "1 of 8 done, 13%" and a Google-filled one sees about half. Real items only, no padding.
- Expected if it works: more drafts reaching step 2. Compare `signup_drafts` that reach step 2 against drafts created, for the two weeks before and after.
