# UI / UX audit, 18 Nov 2026

## In short
- On the regular order page, when a customer puts a pickup-only item in the basket, the Pickup or Delivery choice disappears and a delivery they had already chosen quietly becomes a pickup, with no reason given; the address they typed is dropped. American Crust's own shop explains the same thing in one line; the regular order page should too.
- On American Crust, the new "Gift cards" link in the footer of every page leads to a gift card section that only appears once gift cards are switched on for the shop. If they are off, the link lands at the top of the shop with nothing about gift cards.

- Needs your decision: whether gift cards are switched on for American Crust (if not, take the footer link out or switch them on), and the sign-up headline from yesterday.

## New findings

1. **Order page: a pickup-only item switches the order to pickup without saying why** (Low, customers of any store that delivers and marks a product pickup only).
   `frontend/src/pages/OrderPage.jsx:212-213` sets the order to pickup as soon as a pickup-only product is in the basket; `:235` then makes `deliveryPossible` false, so `:484-487` shows only "Pickup only" in place of the Pickup/Delivery switch. A customer who picked Delivery and typed an address sees the switch and address vanish, with no mention of the product. Proof is the code path (no store in the local test data has a pickup-only product). The American Crust shop does it right: "The pickup bundle is for pickup only, so this order is a pickup." (`american-crust/shop.html`, `#h-bundle`, seen working in `shots/2026-11-18/crust-step2-375.png`).
   Fix: when the store delivers but the basket forces pickup, add one line under "Pickup only" naming the product, e.g. `t('order.pickupOnlyBecause', '{{name}} is pickup only, so this order is a pickup.')`, with a Hebrew line written fresh. Not fixed here: it is new order-flow copy, so it is yours to word.

2. **American Crust: "Gift cards" footer link can lead nowhere** (Low, visitors).
   All three pages' footers link to `/business/american-crust/shop#gift` (`index.html`, `shop.html`, `contact.html`). The `#gift` section is `hidden` and `gift.js` only shows it when `/api/marketplace/gift-cards/american-crust` returns amounts, which happens only when gift cards are on in the dashboard. Locally, with no gift cards, opening `shop.html#gift` leaves the section hidden and the page at the top (`{"giftHidden":true,"scrollY":0}`). With gift cards answered (stubbed), the section renders well on phone and laptop (`crust-gift-375.png`, `crust-gift-1280.png`). Whether gift cards are on for the live shop could not be checked here.
   Fix: switch gift cards on for American Crust, or have `gift.js` also hide the footer link when it hides the section.

3. **Business sign-up: category buttons under finger size on phones** (Low, business owners signing up).
   At 375 wide the 16 category buttons (`wizard-cat-*`) are 34px tall and "Fill in from these" 36px (measured on the rendered `/businesses/add`). Older than yesterday's change, but now the most crowded control on the cleaner form. Fix: `min-h-[44px]` on both in `CreateGig.jsx`.

4. **Join form: password placeholder cut off on phones** (Low, everyone signing up).
   At 375 wide the password box shows "6+ charact" (`shots/2026-11-18/join-step2-after-en-375.png`); Hebrew "6+ תווים" fits. Fix: a shorter English placeholder such as "6 or more", or let the password row stack on narrow phones.

## Part B. One improvement

**Mechanism: the concreteness effect** (a concrete, picturable outcome is judged and remembered more readily than an abstract one such as a price difference).
Surface: the pickup bundle card on the American Crust shop (`scrollcraft/builds/american-crust/shop.html`, the `.pk-deal` card, "Pickup bundle, 2 pizza + 2 bagel ₪105 ₪114").
Change: on the card itself, say what the bundle makes, "12 bagels and 4 pies", next to the price, using the numbers already in the page's own product data (`bagels: 12, pies: 4`). On a phone the card is the first thing seen; the "Makes 12 bagels and 4 personal pies" line sits further down, under the photo.
What would show it worked: the bundle's share of American Crust website orders, from the order lines the site already records (and the new admin "Orders through the website" count), two weeks before and after. Real numbers only; nothing about the saving changes.
