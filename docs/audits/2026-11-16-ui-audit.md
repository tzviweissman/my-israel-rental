## Part B. One improvement

**Mechanism: the fresh start effect** (people start a new routine more readily at a time landmark: a new week, a new month).
Surface: Clean Plus booking, step 2, "The first day you would like", `scrollcraft/builds/clean-plus/book.html:47` (a bare date field).
Change: above the date field, two real quick picks worked out from the chosen weekday: "This coming Monday, 23 Nov" and "From the start of December, Mon 1 Dec" (only when it is within three weeks). Tapping one fills the date; the field stays for any other day. No deadline, no scarcity, just the two nearest landmarks for the habit the customer is setting up.
Why: Clean Plus sells a weekly habit, and the empty date field is the one step that asks the customer to decide when the habit starts. A named landmark makes that decision easy and gives it a natural beginning.
What would show it worked: the share of Clean Plus booking requests that carry a `preferred_date` (rather than none) and the share of visitors to step 2 who send, two weeks before and after, from the bookings the site already saves.
