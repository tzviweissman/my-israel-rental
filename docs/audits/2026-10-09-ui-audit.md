## Improvement of the night
**Mechanism: the isolation effect (Von Restorff).** The distinctive item in a set is the one remembered and chosen. Kosher certainty is the visitor's main question, and the only fact on a card that answers it with evidence is "Certificate checked". Today it is the smallest element on the card: an `text-xs` pill at `components/restaurants/RestaurantCard.jsx:112-118`, set after the grey kashrut chip and the certifier line, and it is hidden on unchecked cards by design.
**Change:** move the checked mark to sit on the certifier line (`:104-109`), replacing the blue `BadgeCheck` with a filled check plus the words "Certificate checked" at `text-sm`, and leave unchecked cards as they are. Checked cards then differ from the rest by presence, not by a fourth chip. Do not add a badge to unchecked cards, and do not imply anything about them.
**Expect:** if restaurant call/directions clicks are already logged, the click-through share of checked versus unchecked cards at the same list position should rise. I did not find a counter for this in the files I read, so confirm one exists before relying on it. No new statistic is claimed.
