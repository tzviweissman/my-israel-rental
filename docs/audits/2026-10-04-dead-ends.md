## Broken: goes nowhere or errors

1. **Admin "Verified" toggle calls a path that does not exist.** HIGH. *Confirmed.*
   - `frontend/src/components/admin/ServicesTab.jsx:92` sends
     `PATCH ${API}/businesses/{id}/verified`.
   - `backend/routes/marketplace/businesses.py:965` serves it under
     `APIRouter(prefix="/marketplace")` (line 49), so the real path is
     `/api/marketplace/businesses/{id}/verified`. No un-prefixed route exists.
   - Result: the verified badge cannot be set from the admin console.
   - **Fix (build):** change the client to `${API}/marketplace/businesses/${id}/verified`
     and make one real call to confirm.

2. **Chat and payment deep links lose their destination when signed out.** MEDIUM-HIGH. *Confirmed.*
   - `App.js:435` (`/chat/:propertyId`) and `App.js:437` (`/payment/success`)
     use `<Navigate to="/auth/login" />`. `ToAuth` (App.js:70-77) exists to keep
     the return-to; these two were not converted.
   - Affected links: `backend/utils/email.py:514`, `:591` (new-message reply),
     `backend/routes/admin/chats_nudge.py:370` (nudge email),
     `backend/routes/marketplace/requests.py:922` (`chat_url`).
   - Result: expired session, click the email, sign in, land on `/dashboard`
     instead of the conversation.
   - **Fix (build):** `user ? <Chat/> : <ToAuth/>` on both. `ToAuth` keeps
     `pathname + search`, so `?with=` survives.

## Dead-end promises: destination lacks the affordance

**Root cause A (items 3-5, 12): the `my-businesses` pane is blank for non-providers.**
`Dashboard.js:532` renders it only when `showGigTabs` (`canPublishGigs(user)`
or gigs_count>0; `utils/providerTrial.js`). The tab id is still accepted, so
the page loads with no pane, no heading and no message.

3. **"Add your business, free"** (`deliveries.addBusinessCta`),
   `components/dashboard/DeliveriesTab.jsx:140-149`, plain `<a href>` to
   `/dashboard?tab=my-businesses`. Shown to a courier with no business, who is
   usually role renter/owner. Lands on a blank pane. HIGH. *Confirmed.*
4. **"Add your business"** (`network.addBusiness`),
   `components/dashboard/NetworkTab.jsx:131-134`, `navigate('/dashboard?tab=my-businesses')`.
   Reached by owner-role hosts via tour step `h.automations`
   (`tourSteps.js:54`) and anyone opening a network notification. Same blank
   pane. HIGH. *Confirmed.*
5. **Host automations tour step** `tour.step.h_automations` ("Let the upkeep run
   itself") and the host recipes: rules are owned by a business
   (`/businesses/{id}/automations`), and the Network tab is hidden for an
   owner with no business and no incoming requests. The promise is only
   keepable by a host who also has a business, and the way in dead-ends at
   item 4. MEDIUM. *Confirmed.*
   - **Fix for 3-5 (build, pick one):** send the CTAs to `/businesses/add`
     (a `ToAuth` route that works for any signed-in user, and `CreateGig`
     creates the business), or render an add-business empty state in the pane
     when `showGigTabs` is false. Do not just add an `else` without deciding
     what it shows. For 5, also say in the step copy that it needs a business.

6. **Setup checklist "Add your first service and its price" can never tick for a shop.**
   HIGH. *Confirmed.* Key `setup.item.biz_service` (`en.js:1262`), links to
   `/businesses/add`. `backend/routes/onboarding.py:125-135` is done only if
   `price`, `tiers.price` or `price_from` is >0. Store listings are saved with
   `tiers: []` and priced `products[]` (`CreateGig.jsx:518,530`), and no gig
   field `price`/`price_from` exists (`price_from` is computed only in
   `routes/admin/marketplace.py:88`).
   **Fix (bug in the check):** add `{"products.price": {"$gt": 0}}` to the `$or`.

7. **Wizard says "You can change it or add exceptions later from your dashboard."**
   MEDIUM. *Confirmed.* `CreateGig.jsx:1281` (`wizard.hoursIntro`; I did not
   locate the en.js key). `weekly_availability` and `slot_duration_minutes`
   are written only in `CreateGig.jsx`; `EditListingModal.jsx` has neither
   field. Backend PATCH accepts them (`gigs.py:753`). Only exceptions exist
   (`BlockTimePanel`, `MyBusinessesTab.jsx:219`).
   **Fix:** add a weekly-hours editor to `EditListingModal`, or change the
   sentence to "add days off later".

8. **Availability-expiry email: "Pause them in your dashboard settings."**
   MEDIUM. *Confirmed.* `utils/email.py:855-858` links to `/dashboard?tab=settings`.
   `availability_reminders_optout` is read at
   `routes/availability_reminders.py:216`; grep across backend and frontend

   **Fix:** add a switch to `EmailSwitches` (and the

10. **Jobs emails: stopping is a one-way door, and instant job emails have no stop.**
