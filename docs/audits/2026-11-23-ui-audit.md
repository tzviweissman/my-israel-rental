## Part B. One improvement

**Zero-price effect** (Shampanier, Mazar and Ariely, 2007: a price of exactly zero is chosen far more often than a small price, beyond what the saving explains).

- Surface: the Pickup / Delivery switch on the order page, `frontend/src/pages/OrderPage.jsx:514`. Today it reads "Delivery · ₪25" even after a free-delivery code is applied; the "Free" only appears down in the summary.
- Change: when a free-delivery code is applied and its minimum is met, label the switch "Delivery · Free" (Hebrew "משלוח · חינם"), with the ₪25 struck through beside it. It is true, and it puts the word at the moment of the pickup-or-delivery choice.
- What should move: among orders that carry a free-delivery code, the share sent as delivery rather than pickup, and fewer "choose delivery to use it" refusals. Both are already in the data: orders store `fulfilment` and `discount.free_delivery`.
- Honest: shows only a real, applied code; no countdown, no scarcity.
