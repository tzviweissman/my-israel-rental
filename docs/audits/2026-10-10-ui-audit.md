## Should fix
1. **Touch targets under 44px.** `frontend/src/components/showcase/ShowcaseStrip.jsx:62` `.scs-arrow { width: 40px; height: 40px }`. The two arrow buttons are the only way to move the row besides swiping. Fix: `width: 44px; height: 44px`.
2. **Touch targets under 44px in the page editor toolbar.** `frontend/src/pages/PageEditor.jsx:245` (`.pe-seg, .pe-icon`) and `:251` (`.pe-btn`) use `min-height: 40px`. Undo, redo, laptop/phone, Original design, View live and Save are all 40px. Fix: `min-height: 44px` in both rules; the `.pe-icon` buttons also need `min-width: 44px`.
3. **Layout shift on /businesses.** `frontend/src/pages/Services.jsx:423` renders `<ShowcaseStrip />`, which returns `null` until `/showcase/showcase.json` loads (`ShowcaseStrip.jsx:17`), then inserts a full section (heading plus tile row, roughly 350px+) above the business list. Everything below jumps. Fix: while `items === null`, render the section shell with a fixed-height placeholder (`min-height` equal to a tile row). Keep returning null only when the list loaded empty.
4. **Link accessible name drops the visible text.** `ShowcaseCard.jsx:64` sets `aria-label="Open the page of {name}"` on the anchor, which replaces the visible name and one-line description for screen readers, and breaks "label in name" for voice control (visible text "Bun Intended" is not in "Open the page of Bun Intended"... it is, but the line is lost). The video also carries `aria-label={name}` (`:47`), a second announcement of the same name inside the link. Fix: remove the anchor's `aria-label`, give the video `aria-hidden="true"` and `tabIndex={-1}`, and let the anchor name come from `.sc-name` plus `.sc-line`.

## Nits
1. **New hardcoded hex** in changed lines: `#F3F4F6` (ShowcaseCard.jsx:81, PageEditor.jsx group bg), `#ECEDF0` (ShowcaseCard.jsx:85), `#E3E3E3` (ShowcaseCard, ShowcaseStrip, Showcase, PageEditor), `#E9EAEE`, `#E5E7EB`, `#6B7280` (PageEditor). Fix: use `var(--surface-alt)` or the equivalent token from theme-flow.css, or record the extra greys as tokens.
2. **Disabled Save label contrast.** `PageEditor.jsx:255` `#6B7280` on `#E5E7EB` is 3.90:1 (WCAG formula on the token hexes). The label flips to "Saved" there, which is meaningful state, not decoration. Fix: `color: #4B5563` (about 5.9:1 on `#E5E7EB`; not computed here with the script, compute before adopting).
3. **Reduced motion autoplay.** `ShowcaseCard.jsx:36` removes `autoplay` in an effect, after the element has mounted with `autoPlay`, so a clip can start for a frame before pausing. Fix: compute the media query during render and omit `autoPlay` from the JSX.
4. **iframe title duplicates the page heading.** `PageEditor.jsx:224` reuses `pageEditor.title`. Fix: a distinct key, for example "Preview of your page".
5. **Raw `err.message` in a toast.** `PageEditor.jsx:135` shows whatever `uploadOneFile` throws, which may be English in the Hebrew UI. Fix: show the translated `uploadFailed` string and log the error.

## Improvement of the night
**Mechanism: foot-in-the-door (Freedman and Fraser, 1966) - a small first commitment raises the odds of agreeing to a larger one.**
Surface: `frontend/src/pages/Showcase.jsx:40-45`, the closing CTA "Want a page like these for your business?" which sends a visitor straight to `/join`, the full sign-up. A visitor who just watched five real pages has not yet done anything; the jump to an account is the large ask.
Change: put a low-cost first step in front of it on the same line, for example a secondary link "See how a page is made" to the existing `/why-list` or the first tile's page, and keep "Add your business, free" as the primary. Do not pre-select anything and do not add copy about scarcity.
Expectation: the share of `/showcase` visitors who reach `/join` should not fall, and the share who reach `/why-list` first and then `/join` later in the same session should be measurable from the existing page-view log. If both are flat, drop it. No new tracking needed.
