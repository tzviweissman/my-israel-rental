## Part B. Improvement idea
**Mechanism: endowed progress effect (Nunes and Dreze).** People finish a task more often when they can see it already started.
**Surface:** the Gift Boutique order page, the sticky bottom bar (`frontend/public/pages/the-gift-boutique/order.html:196`), which shows "Gifts, before delivery $0" and a muted send button until something is added.
**Change:** show the three steps (gifts, who they are for, where) as a small line above the bar, with step 1 ticked as soon as any gift is added and the line saying what is still missing ("Add a delivery city to send"). Real state only, no invented counts.
**Expected if it works:** a higher share of order-page visits ending in a tap on "Send on WhatsApp". The page already opens WhatsApp links, so count taps against page views in the existing page-view log.
