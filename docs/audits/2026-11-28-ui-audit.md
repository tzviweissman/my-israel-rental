## Part B. One improvement

- **Mechanism:** picture superiority effect (people remember and act on concrete pictures more than abstract marks). Used before only on the size ladder (25 Sep), never on this surface.
- **Surface:** the new switch in the listing editor, `EditListingModal.jsx:745` (`editListing.logoCoverHint`).
- **Change:** when the listing has photos, add one honest line under the switch: "A photo of your work usually gets more taps than a logo." / "תמונה של העבודה שלכם בדרך כלל מקבלת יותר לחיצות מלוגו." Leave the switch and its default alone. For a listing with no photos, say instead "Add a photo of your work to show here instead", linking to the photos field.
- **What would show it worked:** fewer listings with photos switched to the logo, and, on the board, more clicks per view on photo cards than logo cards. `marketplace_view_events` already records views per listing; split by `logo_cover` after two weeks. If logo cards do as well, drop the line.
