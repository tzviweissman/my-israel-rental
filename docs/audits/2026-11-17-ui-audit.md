## Part B. One improvement

**Mechanism: the self-reference effect** (people attend to and remember a line that is about them and what they get, more than one about the company).
Surface: sign-up card headline, `frontend/src/pages/SignupJoin.jsx:260` (`signupJoin.headline`).
Change: replace "Join My Israel Rental" with a line that names what each of the three choices below it gets, rentals first, for example "Book a stay, hire a local pro, or add your business, free" (Hebrew written fresh, not translated). Nothing else on the card changes.
Why: the card's job is to get a visitor to tap one of three roles, and the heading is the one line everyone reads first. Naming the three outcomes previews the choice and puts the free supply offer in the visitor's words.
What would show it worked: the share of `/signup` visits that go on to step 2 (a role tapped), from the page visits the site already records, two weeks before and after.
