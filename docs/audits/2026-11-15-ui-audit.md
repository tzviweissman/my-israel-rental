## Part B. One improvement

**Mechanism: the Zeigarnik effect (people remember, and come back to, a task they can see is unfinished).**
Surface: Sales summary, "Each item" panel, `frontend/src/components/dashboard/ItemSales.jsx:122` (the "Type what each item costs you..." line).
Change: once at least one cost is typed, replace the line with a count of what is left: "Costs added for 2 of 6 items. Add the rest to see your full profit." Counted from the items already loaded; nothing made up.
Why: the margin and profit columns, the point of the panel, only appear for items with a cost. Today the prompt disappears after the first cost, so an owner who adds one and leaves gets no reminder that the rest stay blank.
What would show it worked: the share of each business's listed products that have a row in `db.item_costs`, two weeks before and after.
