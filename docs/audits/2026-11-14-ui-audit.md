## Part B. One improvement

**Mechanism: recognition over recall (people pick faster and with fewer mistakes from options they can see than from ones they have to remember or guess).**
Surface: The Brown Box order form, the delivery date, `scrollcraft/builds/the-brown-box/order.html:234` (the date box) and `:709` (`dayWhy`).
Change: under the date box, show the next four days that can actually take a delivery as tap buttons, with their times ("Sun 15 Nov", "Mon 16 Nov"...), from the same `dayWhy` and `dayWins` the page already uses. The date box stays for anything further out.
Why: the page now knows which days are open, but it only says so after a wrong pick ("We do not deliver on that day. Pick another."). A customer has to guess, get told no, and guess again, on a phone date wheel.
What would show it worked: fewer Brown Box baskets that never become an order, in the basket reminders the site already records (`/order-form/{gig}/basket` and `.../ordered`), compared over the two weeks before and after.
