## Part B. One improvement

**Mechanism: choice visibility (making every option and its price visible at the point of choice, so nobody has to open a menu to find the cheaper one).**
Surface: L.A. Cholent order form, the Meat choice, `scrollcraft/builds/la-cholent/order.html:84`.
Change: replace the drop-down with two tap buttons that carry their price, "With our meat, ₪180" and "Without our meat, ₪85" (prices from the listing, as now). It also ends the cut-off text in finding 2.
Why: the cheaper "own meat or parve" option was the reason for yesterday's change ("la cholent doesnt see a 180 shekel order when really its 85"). Inside a closed drop-down, the second option and its price are invisible until opened, so some customers who want parve will still type it in the notes or order the meat price.
What would show it worked: in the orders already saved, the share of Medium and Large orders with an `-own` option rises, and the share whose notes mention "parve" or "own meat" falls to near zero.
