## Dead-end promises: destination lacks the affordance

1. **Property checklist "Set your availability" never ticks for iCal users.** `backend/routes/onboarding.py:205` (projection) and `:234` (done test) read singular `ical_url`; nothing writes it. The real field is `ical_urls` (list; `routes/ical.py:34`, `PropertyList.jsx:762`). A lister who connected iCal feeds and has no `available_from` stays at "not done" forever. Link: `/dashboard?tab=properties`. **Fix (code):** project `ical_urls`, test `bool(p.get("ical_urls"))`.
2. **Checklist "Add photos to your listing" / "Set your availability"** (`onboarding.py:229,235`) land on the property list; the controls are behind the pencil icon then the wizard (`AddPropertyModal.jsx:890`). Friction, not a hard dead end. **Fix (build):** with exactly one property append `&edit=<id>` (`Dashboard.js:227` handles it).
3. **Hardcoded English empty states** (do not translate, do not flip RTL): `components/dashboard/JobRequestsTab.jsx:79-82` ("No open jobs matching your gigs yet..."), `MyJobsTab.jsx:386-394` ("You haven't posted any jobs yet", "Post your first job"). Destinations work. **Fix:** `t()` plus he.js keys.
4. **Paying provider has no cancel/upgrade control.** `/marketplace/subscription/select-plan|upgrade|cancel` have no caller; `MyGigsTab.jsx:434` says the flows were handed off. **Unverified:** whether PayPal cancellation is handled elsewhere. Confirm before deleting the endpoints (change promise) or wiring cancel (build).
