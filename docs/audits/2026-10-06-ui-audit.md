## Improvement of the night
**Mechanism: the Fogg behavior model (ability: make the first step look small).** Behaviour happens when motivation, ability and a prompt meet; for someone who just clicked into an empty pane, motivation is already there and the only lever left is how big the first step looks. Not previously used on this surface (ambiguity aversion and endowed progress were applied to other flows).

**Surface:** the new empty state `EmptyPane` for `my-businesses` at `frontend/src/pages/Dashboard.js:560-568` (body key `dashboard.noBusinessBody`). Today the body repeats the pricing promise ("Free to list, free to be found, no commission") but says nothing about effort.

**Change:** keep the cost line and add the true size of the first step, taken from the real required fields of `CreateGig`, for example "Name and category are all you need to start; add photos and prices later." Only render it if CreateGig actually saves with just those fields; otherwise do not ship it. No timer, no invented minutes.

**Expected if it works:** a higher share of users who open `/dashboard?tab=my-businesses` with no business go on to create one within the same session. Checkable from existing data: businesses with `created_at` within 1 hour of the user's last dashboard load versus the 2 weeks before this change, without new tracking.
