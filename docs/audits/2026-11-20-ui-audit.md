## Part B. One improvement: operant reinforcement on the owner's order card
- **Mechanism:** behaviour that is followed quickly by a real, specific reward is repeated (operant reinforcement). The owner's reward for sending a "being made" photo is the customer's "Looks great", but today it shows only on that one card, after the fact, and only if they reopen it.
- **Surface:** the owner's order card, by the photo button (`OrderCard.jsx:308-311`).
- **Change:** under the button, one quiet line with real counts from the orders already stored: "Customers liked 3 of the 4 photos you sent this month". Shown only once at least one photo was sent; no line at zero likes, no invented numbers.
- **What would show it worked:** a higher share of orders with `making_photo` set, per business, in the 30 days after, and more `liked_at` with it (both already stored on the order).

Mechanism logged in `mechanisms-used.md`.
