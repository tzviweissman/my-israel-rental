## Part B. One improvement: the end of each row
- **Mechanism:** the marginal value theorem from information foraging (Charnov; Pirolli and Card). People leave a "patch" when it runs out, and they leave toward whatever is in front of them at that moment.
- **Surface:** the category rows on /businesses (`BrowseRows.jsx:126-128`, the strip). A visitor who swipes to the end of the Food row reaches a faded edge and nothing more. The only way into the full group is the "See all 11" button at the far top corner, back where they started.
- **Change:** a last tile in each row's strip, the same size as a card: "See all 11 in Food & Treats", using the group's real count. It opens the group exactly as "See all" does. Show it only when the group has more than the row shows, or always as the row's end. No new data, no invented numbers.
- **What would show it worked:** more `/businesses?group=` page views per `/businesses` view, from the page views the site already records. Also a smaller share of visitors who scroll the rows but never open a group or a listing.
