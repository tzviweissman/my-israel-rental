## Dead-end promises: destination lacks the affordance

**1. "Add your business" sends non-business accounts to a blank dashboard pane (medium, new)**
- `frontend/src/components/dashboard/NetworkTab.jsx:133`: text "Add your business, free, to connect with other businesses." / button "Add your business" -> `navigate('/dashboard?tab=my-businesses')`.
- Destination: `Dashboard.js:532` renders `my-businesses` only when `showGigTabs` (provider role, active trial, or at least one gig). A renter/owner/manager with no business lands on a dashboard with nothing under the tab strip. No error.
- Same destination, same problem: host walk step `h.automations` (`tourSteps.js:53-54`, "Do it now" clicks this button); `DeliveriesTab.jsx:146` ("Add your business, free", shown to couriers without a business); `ServicesHeroSearch.jsx:456-460` ("Become a provider", shown to everyone incl. signed-out); `data/featureLibrary.js:45,64,93,107` (feature-page CTAs to `my-businesses`, `orders`, `network` panes - probable, role filtering only partly checked).
- Fix (build): point these at `/businesses/add`, which any signed-in user can reach (one line each). Alternative: render the add-business form in the pane when `showGigTabs` is false.

**2. Email and notification links to `/chat/{id}` lose the destination when signed out (medium)**
- `App.js:435`: `user ? <Chat/> : <Navigate to="/auth/login" />` - no `?redirect=`. `App.js:71-77` documents that `ToAuth` was introduced for exactly this and Chat was missed.
- Linked from `backend/utils/email.py:514` and `:591` (new-message / mention emails, CTA "reply"), `routes/admin/chats_nudge.py:370`, `routes/marketplace/requests.py:922`, `Navigation.js:268`.
- What happens: after sign-in `Auth.js` falls back to `/dashboard`; the person never reaches the conversation they were emailed about. In-app callers pre-check the user, so only email/notification entry points are hit.
- Fix (build): `<ToAuth />` for `/chat/:propertyId`. Same one-liner for `/payment/success` (`App.js:437`, low severity).

**3. Walk step `b.page` promises a cover photo the target form cannot set (minor)**
- Text: "A logo and a cover photo are the difference...", button "Add my logo and cover" -> `?tab=my-businesses&details=1` -> `BusinessDetailsForm`, which has a logo control only. Cover lives under "Design your page" (`BusinessPageEditor`).
- Fix: change the button to "Add my logo", or point it at the `business-design` target.

**4. Email promises a pause switch that does not exist (minor)**
- `backend/utils/email.py:857`: "Don't want availability-expiry reminders? Pause them in your dashboard settings." -> `/dashboard?tab=settings`. `availability_reminders.py:216` reads `users.availability_reminders_optout`; nothing in backend or frontend ever writes it (confirmed by grep). `email.py:846` also says "or pause the listing"; no listing-pause control found.
- Fix: add a switch to `EmailSwitches` plus a PATCH handler (or a one-click opt-out link like the other emails), or remove both sentences.

**5. Disabled automation recipes give a reason but no way to fix it (minor)**
- `AutomationsPanel.jsx:582-592`: "Connect with a business first" is plain text under six disabled recipes. "Find partners" is one view away (`?view=find`) with no link.
- Fix: make the hint a button calling `setView('find')`.
