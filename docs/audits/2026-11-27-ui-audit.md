## Part B. One improvement

- **Mechanism:** recognition heuristic. When choosing between options, people lean towards the one they recognise, and a name they know is spotted far faster than one they have to read.
- **Surface:** service cards on the Services board and home (`ServiceCard.jsx:144`, used from `Services.jsx:435` and `:970`, `HomeNext.jsx:307`). Yesterday's change put the business name first, which is exactly what recognition works on.
- **Change:** on a card for a business the signed-in customer has already saved (Liked) or ordered from, add a small grey line under the name: "You saved this" / "You ordered here" (Hebrew "שמרת" / "הזמנת כאן"). Only from the customer's own real history; nothing shown to anyone else, nothing invented.
- **What would show it worked:** a higher share of board clicks going to businesses the visitor saved or ordered from, and more repeat orders started from the board rather than from a search, both from the view and order records the app already keeps.
