## Part B. One improvement

- **Mechanism:** Zeigarnik effect (unfinished tasks stay on the mind and pull people back when they are shown as unfinished).
- **Surface:** the business dashboard. Today the "Make your page stronger" checklist (`CreateGig.jsx:1328-1360`, `wizard-live-todo`) appears once, on the screen right after publishing. If the owner leaves, the open items (kashrut, delivery, how early to order, languages, logo) are never shown to them again; `ListingStrength.jsx` does this only for rental properties.
- **Change:** show the same list, with its real "2 of 7 done" count, at the top of the business dashboard until every item is done or the owner hides it. Same items, same tap-to-fill rows; no new claims.
- **What would show it worked:** for businesses created after the change, the share with hours, kashrut, delivery settings and languages filled within 7 days of publishing, compared with businesses created the week before (the fields and `created_at` are already on the business record).
