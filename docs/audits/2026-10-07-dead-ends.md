# Dead ends - 2026-10-07

Scheduled dead-ends audit following `.claude/skills/dead-ends/SKILL.md`. Four
parallel static passes (forward links, backward promises, orphaned fields and
enums, endpoints/components/schedulers) against the 62-route table in
`frontend/src/App.js`. Baseline: `2026-09-14-dead-ends.md`.

**Method limit:** static analysis only (grep, AST, code reading). Nothing was
clicked in a browser and no `scripts/check-*.mjs` was run. The four findings
marked *spot-checked* were re-read by hand after the passes returned; the
rest are as reported by the passes and cite file:line for re-checking.

**Headline:** the 09-14 gaps that mattered are fixed (admin request-reports
queue, courier leave, Auth.js redirect/rel, BlocksHero, `GigPatch.status`
`paused`, `/properties/featured`). New bugs of the "email/link goes somewhere
useless" kind landed with the automations and storefront work. No nonexistent
routes, no ignored query params, no no-op handlers.

---

## Broken: goes nowhere or errors

1. **Relative URLs in email buttons** (*spot-checked*). Mail clients cannot
   resolve `href="/dashboard?..."`.
   - `backend/routes/marketplace/price_watch.py:116` passes `action_url`
     (`/dashboard?tab=network&view=automations`, `/services/gig/<id>`) as the
     "Open" button.
   - `backend/routes/marketplace/automations.py:563-564` passes `_where(ctx)`
     (`/dashboard?tab=orders`, `...tab=appointments&highlight=...`).
   - `orders._email` (`orders.py:1114`) uses the string unchanged; every other
     caller prefixes `_frontend_url()`.
   - **Fix (build):** prefix `_frontend_url()` at both call sites, or in
     `orders._email` when `button[1]` starts with `/`. Keep the in-app bell
     `action_url` relative.

## Dead-end promises: destination lacks the affordance

2. **Dashboard tabs render a blank pane for the wrong role.** `Dashboard.js`
   gates `orders` (:572) and `job-requests` (:587) on `showGigTabs`,
   `properties` (:492) and `bulk-manager` (:516) on `isPropertyLister`,
   `subleases` (:462) on `isRenter`. Only `my-businesses` and `contracts` have
   an EmptyPane fallback. Public routes into them:
   - `featureLibrary.js:56`, "orders-and-delivery" CTA -> `/dashboard?tab=orders`
   - `featureLibrary.js:137`, "many-at-once" CTA -> `/dashboard?tab=bulk-manager`
   - `automations.py:537-546/589`, `message_customer` sends a *customer* to
     `/dashboard?tab=orders` (business-only). Their tab is `my-orders`.
   - **Fix (build):** EmptyPane fallbacks copying the `no-business-yet` pattern
     (`Dashboard.js:435`, `:562`), with "Add your business, free" for orders.
     For the automation, use `tab=my-orders`.

3. **Service reminder email points at a switch providers cannot see** (new,
   `f08584a`). `chats_nudge.py:~366`: "You can turn these reminders off from
   Dashboard -> Settings." The card is `SettingsTab.jsx:353`
   `{isPropertyLister && ...}`, so a provider-role recipient finds nothing,
   though the backend honours `auto_nudge_opt_out` for them
   (`chats_nudge.py:496-502`). **Fix (build):** show the card when
   `showGigTabs` or the user owns gigs.

4. **"Mark it unavailable" / "pause the listing" for rentals has no control.**
   - `backend/utils/email.py:~843-846` (availability expiry): "...clear the
     cap entirely, or pause the listing", links to `/dashboard?edit=<id>`.
   - `chats_nudge.py:~365`: "please mark it as unavailable in your dashboard".
   - `AddPropertyModal` has Available From/Until only; `PropertyUpdate` has no
     status and nothing sets `archived`/`paused` (services do have Pause,
     `MyGigsTab.jsx:402-415`). **Fix (change the promise, cheaper):** say "set
     an end date" or "delete the listing"; or build a hide toggle. Same stale
     wording in `autoNudgeHint`.

5. **Tour step "Add my logo and cover"** (`en.js:1111-1115`) -> `tourSteps.js:33`
   `?tab=my-businesses&details=1` opens `BusinessDetailsForm`, which has a logo
   field (:250-302) but **no cover**. Cover lives in `BusinessPageEditor`
   ("Design your page", `MyBusinessesTab.jsx:457`). **Fix:** point `doIt` at
   `business-design`, or relabel "Add my logo".

6. **Feature-library "Try it" CTAs land on the Overview**, which has none of
   the controls (`featureLibrary.js`, `cta: '/dashboard'`): `put-it-on-a-flyer`
   :79 "Get my link and code" (QR panel is in Businesses/Properties),
   `one-calendar` :116 "Connect my calendar" (iCal in `PropertyList`),
   `instant-or-ask` :130 "Set this on my listing" (inside the edit modal),
   `what-to-charge` :144 "Look at my pricing" (`PropertyList`). `take-payment`
   :107 is one click short. **Fix:** `?tab=properties` / `?tab=my-businesses`
   (role-aware for the flyer).

7. **Verify email: "Log in to resend"** (`VerifyEmail.jsx:28-35`) for
   expired/invalid links. Nothing after login can resend: `/verify-pending`
   (the only caller of `/auth/resend-verification`) is linked from nowhere, and
   `auth.py:139` marks every signup `email_verified: True` (rolled back
   2026-06). The emailed `?token=` success path works. **Fix (change the
   promise):** retire the expired/invalid copy while verification is off.

8. **Check-in/check-out times cannot be set per listing**, and an automation
   depends on them. `PropertyCreate.checkin_time/checkout_time`
   (`models.py:146-147`) are written only by `BulkEditModal.jsx:23`
   (*spot-checked*: `AddPropertyModal` has no reference); never displayed.
   `automations.py:390-396` times the checkout cleaning from them (`535146d`).
   **Fix (build):** two time inputs in the add/edit stay-rules section.

    return-to. No in-app link reaches it for non-admins. **Fix:** `<ToAuth/>`
    when `!user`. Low.
