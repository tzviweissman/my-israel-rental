## New findings
1. [Medium] Chicken Munch, all four pages at 375 and 390 wide (and 1280 too, 1349px wide): the page scrolls sideways. The opening word `.w1` ("Chicken", 97.5px font at 375) measures from -30px to 405px, wider than the screen; the page's width is 439px (456 at 390, 1349 at 1280). The hero section clips it (`frontend/public/pages/chicken-munch/site.css:46`), but `html` and `body` are `overflow-x: visible`, so the page itself still scrolls sideways. Proof: measured in a browser after 5 seconds of animation, screenshots `docs/audits/shots/2026-10-24/chicken-munch-hero-5s-375.png` and `chicken-munch-index-375.png`. Fix: cap the word's size to the screen (for example `font-size: min(97px, 24vw)`) and add `overflow-x: clip` on the page. Not done by this audit: it is a hand-built page generated from the build folder, and the word's size is a design choice. Not caused by the last 26 hours (`site.css` last changed in 82935a3d).
