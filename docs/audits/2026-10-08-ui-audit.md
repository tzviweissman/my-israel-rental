## Should fix

1. **`var(--muted)` used as a colour in the new page-v3 CSS (shadcn token collision).** `frontend/src/styles/page-v3.css:492, 500, 523` (added tonight; same pattern already at `:111, :172, :203, :215, :326, :336, :356`, so fix all ten). `--muted` is defined only in `index.css:71` as the HSL triplet `210 20% 98%`, and nothing in `page-v3*.css` redefines it. `color: var(--muted)` is therefore invalid at computed-value time and the text inherits instead of going muted. For `.pv3-proof` it is `!important` on `*`, so the whole proof line is affected. Fix: replace every `var(--muted)` in `page-v3.css` with `var(--brand-muted)`.
2. **Hand-rolled solid blue button with white text.** `frontend/src/components/dashboard/PageVersions.jsx:160`: `background: 'var(--brand-primary)', color: '#fff'`. Wrong style under the flow theme. White on `#167AB8` computes to 4.66:1 (WCAG luminance formula from the token hex), so it passes, but the publish action must be black. Fix: `style={{ background: 'var(--action)', color: 'var(--action-ink)' }}`.
3. **Same pattern on the step badges.** `frontend/src/pages/SignupJoin.jsx:237, 240`: `bg-[var(--brand-primary)] text-white`. 4.66:1 so readable, but it is a solid accent fill with white text. Fix: use `bg-[var(--action)] text-[var(--action-ink)]` for the active step.
4. **Password show/hide toggles are low contrast and tiny.** `frontend/src/pages/Auth.js:273` and `:378`: `text-gray-400` (`#9CA3AF`) on white is 2.54:1 (computed), under the 3:1 non-text minimum, and the button has no min size, so the target is the icon alone (well under 44px). Fix: `className="absolute end-1 top-1/2 -translate-y-1/2 min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px] inline-flex items-center justify-center text-gray-600 hover:text-gray-900"` (`#4B5563` is 7.56:1 on white).
5. **Close button under 44px in a new modal.** `frontend/src/components/dashboard/PhotoMatch.jsx:53`: `className="p-1"` around an 18px icon gives about 26px. Fix: `className="min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px] inline-flex items-center justify-center"`.
6. **Card action buttons 40px.** `frontend/src/components/marketplace/CardAction.jsx:142`: `min-h-[40px]`. Under the 44px rule at 375. Fix: `min-h-[44px]`.

## Improvement of the night

**Mechanism: foot-in-the-door (Freedman and Fraser, 1966).** A small first request that is easy to grant raises compliance with the larger second request, because people act consistently with what they have just done.

**Surface:** the review ask on the tracking pages, `frontend/src/pages/OrderTrackPage.jsx:173-180` and `frontend/src/pages/BookingTrackPage.jsx:105-112`, which lead to `WriteReview.jsx:107-150`.

**Today:** the ask is "How was your order?" with a "Write a review" button. The button opens a form with a star rating, then a required text box with a minimum character count (`WriteReview.jsx:140-150`). The first request is the whole job.

**Change:** render five tappable stars (the existing `StarInput`) inside the card in place of the button. Tapping one navigates to `WriteReview` with the rating carried in the query string and pre-set into `rating` state (`WriteReview.jsx:35`). The text box stays required, as it is today. No change to what is public, and no pre-checked values: the person chose the stars.

**Expect:** the existing review store already records both finished orders and reviews written. If it works, reviews per `done` order rise, and more people who open the form submit it (starts to submissions). Check both against the review counts the app already holds; no new tracking needed.
