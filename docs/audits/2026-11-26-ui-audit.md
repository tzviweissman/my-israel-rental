## Part B. One improvement

- **Mechanism:** ambiguity aversion (people hold back from a choice whose outcome they cannot see; not knowing how payment will work is a known reason checkouts are left).
- **Surface:** the order page form, before "Send". Today the business's payment links and payment note appear only on the "Order sent" screen (`frontend/src/pages/OrderPage.jsx:389-398`), after the customer has committed.
- **Change:** one quiet line above the Send button, from the same real data: "You pay {{business}} directly: PayBox, or cash on pickup" (the link labels plus the owner's own note), shown only when the business has set either. No new claims, nothing invented.
- **What would show it worked:** for businesses with payment links or a note, the share of started baskets that end in a sent order, in the two weeks before and after (the order page already records each basket at `/order-form/{gig}/basket` and marks it `/ordered` when sent, `OrderPage.jsx:174`, `:344`). Businesses without payment details are the comparison.
