# Dead ends - 2026-10-02

Scheduled dead-ends audit following `.claude/skills/dead-ends/SKILL.md`. Three passes (forward, backward, orphaned capabilities) against the 61-route table in `frontend/src/App.js`. Static analysis only: no browser click-through was run. Findings marked **spot-checked** were re-read by hand by the orchestrator; others are as reported by the pass and read from code.

**Headline:** the 09-14 open items are mostly closed (courier leave, admin request-reports queue, Auth.js redirect and rel, `paused` status, Requests filter params, jobs-digest scheduler, BlocksHero). New since then: two calls to URLs that do not exist, one systemic dead-end (`?tab=my-businesses` for non-business roles), two emails promising settings that do not exist, and a signed-out chat deep-link that loses its destination.

## Broken: goes nowhere or errors

1. **Admin "verify business" always 404s** (spot-checked). `frontend/src/components/admin/ServicesTab.jsx:92` calls `${API}/businesses/{id}/verified`. The route is `backend/routes/marketplace/businesses.py:965` under prefix `/marketplace`. Fix: `${API}/marketplace/businesses/...`.
2. **3D-tour upload failure report 404s silently** (spot-checked). `frontend/src/components/property/Tour3DUpload.jsx:191` posts `/upload-failure`; the route is `/client/upload-failure` (`backend/routes/misc.py:56`; `utils/fastUpload.js:278` is correct). The try/catch swallows it. Fix: correct the path.
3. **"Add your business" lands on a blank dashboard pane for non-business roles** (spot-checked gate: `Dashboard.js:532` renders the pane only when `showGigTabs`, i.e. role provider/admin, active trial, or `gigs_count > 0`; the tab id still passes `ALL_TAB_IDS`, so nothing redirects). Controls:
   - `components/marketplace/ServicesHeroSearch.jsx:456` "Become a provider →" (`<a href="/dashboard?tab=my-businesses">`, shown to every visitor of /services and /businesses).
   - `components/dashboard/DeliveriesTab.jsx:146` "Add your business, free" (couriers with no business).
   - `components/dashboard/NetworkTab.jsx:133` "Add your business" (Network empty state; also reached by the host walk step `h.automations`).
   Fix: point all three at `/businesses/add` (open to any signed-in user, guarded by `ToAuth`). Optionally render a stub in `Dashboard.js` when `activeTab` has no pane.
4. **Feature-page "Try it" CTAs are role-blind** (`pages/FeatureDetail.jsx:99`, targets in `data/featureLibrary.js`). Blank for the wrong role: `tab=my-businesses` (your-own-page, take-payment, block-your-time, `:45,:93,:107`), `tab=orders` (`:56`), `tab=contracts` (sign-without-printing, tagged for travellers, `:123`), `tab=bulk-manager` (`:137`), `tab=network` (`:64,:72`). Fix: choose CTA by role, or send others to `/businesses/add` / settings.
5. **Signed-out chat deep links lose their destination.** `App.js:435` `/chat/:propertyId` uses `<Navigate to="/auth/login" />` with no `?redirect=`; token is in sessionStorage (`App.js:229`) so an email click is always signed out; after login the user lands on `/dashboard`. Affects the reply email (`backend/utils/email.py:591`), mention email (`:514`), owner-nudge email (`routes/admin/chats_nudge.py:370`). Same pattern at `App.js:437` `/payment/success` (low). Fix: use `<ToAuth />`.
6. **Overview "Waiting on you" Open** (`OverviewTab.jsx:166`) goes to `job-requests` (gated on `showGigTabs`, `Dashboard.js:545`) for renters/hosts when no bookings are waiting: blank pane. Fix: only render when the destination exists.
7. **`/admin` for signed-out admin** (`App.js:427`) bounces to `/` with no sign-in prompt. Low. Fix: `ToAuth` when `!user`.

## Dead-end promises: destination lacks the affordance

1. **Availability-expiry email** (`backend/utils/email.py:855-858`): "Don't want availability-expiry reminders? Pause them in your dashboard settings." Links to `/dashboard?tab=settings`. No such control: `availability_reminders_optout` is read at `backend/routes/availability_reminders.py:216` and set nowhere. Spot-checked. Fix: add a switch in `EmailSwitches.jsx` + prefs endpoint, or a one-click opt-out page; or drop the sentence.
2. **Pricing Insights email** (`email.py:794-797`): "Manage your email preferences in your dashboard settings". Settings has no such switch; the only control is `pricing-insights-toggle` in `SmartPricingModal.jsx:538`. Fix: add to `EmailSwitches.jsx`, or reword and link to the Properties tab.
3. **Setup checklists link to hidden tabs.** `backend/routes/onboarding.py:312-317` picks the list by data (`has_business`/`has_property`), the dashboard hides tabs by role. A provider/renter with a property gets `tab=properties` items (pane needs `isPropertyLister`); an owner with a business gets `tab=my-businesses&details=1` items (pane needs `showGigTabs`). Uncommon (role is single-valued) but real. Related: Settings role switch is exclusive while `SignupJoin.jsx:440` says "You can always add another role later". Fix: one shared visibility rule for lists and tabs.
4. **"Choose your categories"** (`BusinessCompleteness.jsx:42-48`, `action: null`): no category control; categories derive from listings, so it never completes until a service exists. Fix: relabel "Add a service in a category" linking `?services=1`, or build a picker.
5. **Leads/Visitors cards** say "Add a listing to start counting" (`en.js:1738`, `OverviewTab.jsx:173,181`) to renters, with no link or way to add one. Fix: hide for renters.
6. **Store businesses can never finish "Add your first service and its price"** (`onboarding.py:125-136`): the `$or` checks `price`, `tiers.price`, `price_from`; stores store prices in `products[].price`. Spot-checked. Fix: add `{"products.price": {"$gt": 0}}`.
7. **Walk step b.page "Add my logo and cover"** (`en.js:1000`) opens `?details=1`; the form has a logo but no cover field (cover is in "Design your page", `MyBusinessesTab.jsx:459`). Fix: relabel or retarget.
8. **Renters get the business walk** (`TourProvider.jsx:190` `tourRoleFor` returns `'business'` for all but owner/manager; "Show me around" is shown to everyone, `Navigation.js:838`). Runtime not verified.
9. **Feature-library CTAs for hosts** (`featureLibrary.js:79,116,130,144`) land on Overview, not the Properties-tab controls. Fix: `?tab=properties`.
10. **"Choose how people reach you"** (`prop_contact`, `en.js:1269`) goes to a single WhatsApp-number field. Copy fix.
11. **"Set your availability"** (`onboarding.py:229-233`) auto-ticks: `AddPropertyModal.jsx:245-250` defaults `available_from` to today; `ical_url` it also tests is the dead singular field. Fix: test `ical_urls` / user-set window, or drop.
12. Host checklist items "Add photos"/"Set your availability" open the plain list, not an edit form (`?edit=<id>` exists, `Dashboard.js:217`). Improvement, not a defect.
