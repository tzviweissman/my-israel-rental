## Improvement of the night
**Mechanism: information scent (Pirolli and Card).** People follow a link when its label predicts what they will find behind it, and they judge that from specific words and quantities, not from a general promise.

**Surface:** the Restaurants tile on `/businesses`, `frontend/src/pages/Services.jsx:450-472`. The subtitle `restaurants.tileText` ("Each with its hechsher, meat or dairy, by city and neighbourhood") describes the page but gives no sense of how much is behind it, and the tile always links to the generic `/restaurants`.

**Change:** render the subtitle with a real count taken from the restaurants list endpoint the `/restaurants` page already calls (for example "412 kosher restaurants in 38 cities", both numbers from the query, no line if the query fails). When the visitor already has a `location` filter set on this page, link to `/restaurants/{city}` and name the city with its own count. Add the matching `he.js` strings in the same commit.

**Expected if it works:** more visits to `/restaurants` whose referrer is `/businesses`, and a lower bounce rate on that path. Both are readable from the existing page-view log; no new tracking needed.
