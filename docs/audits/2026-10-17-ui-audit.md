# UI audit, 17 Oct 2026

## In short
- The new business page for The Brown Box had a "Scroll" prompt on its opening film. The page rules ban scroll prompts. Customers saw it on laptops; it is removed.
- The new "Tell me when it's back" button on sold-out products is smaller than a thumb-sized target on a phone (36 and 40 pixels, the rule is 44). Not fixed yet; it is a quick follow-up.
- Fixed tonight: the "Scroll" prompt, on branch `claude/sweet-knuth-i0j1g7` (one commit, `c67e586`).
- Needs your decision: nothing. The new dashboard screens (birthday treat, restock list) and the Hebrew pages could not be looked at (see Not checked), so they have not had a visual review yet.

Scope: 16 Oct changes (The Brown Box pages, the strip of business pages, restock and birthday features, walkthrough tour, page rules).

## New findings

1. **[Low] Scroll cue on a business page.** `frontend/public/pages/the-brown-box/index.html:279` had `<div class="hint" id="hint">Scroll</div>` (shown at 1280 wide, screenshot `docs/audits/shots/2026-10-17/bb-index-en-1280.png`). Defect 9 bans scroll cues. FIXED, see below.
2. **[Low] Touch targets under 44px.** `frontend/src/components/forms/RestockNotify.jsx`: opener button `min-h-[36px]`, email field and "Email me" button `min-h-[40px]`. Defect 12 needs 44 at 375 wide. Fix: change the three values to `min-h-[44px]`. Not auto-fixed (touch sizes are not on the safe-fix list).
3. **[Low] Hardcoded colours in new lines.** `RestockNotify.jsx` uses `#1F5F3A` and `#B42318`, `RestockWaiting.jsx` uses `#F9FAFB`, and a switch in the same commit uses `#D1D5DB`. Defect 3. Contrast of the two text colours on white is above 6:1, so readable; it is only a token slip.

## Fixed this run
