# UI / UX audit, 2 Nov 2026

## In short
- Chicken Munch's home page can be dragged sideways by about 7px on a phone, because the closing "Order now" block is wider than the screen. Customers see the page wobble.
- Every one of the 17 business sites republished this morning has small footer and menu links (19 to 24px tall) on phones, under the 44px touch rule. Not new, but now on every site.
- Fixed tonight: nothing (the one bug is in a hand-built site, and those need the build edited and republished through the checks).
- Needs your decision: whether the footer links on the 17 sites should get the 44px treatment in the shared build template.

## New findings
1. [Low] Chicken Munch home, 375 wide: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` is 382 against 375. The unclipped elements reaching x=382 are the `.close.bigclose` columns and `.go-order` (`frontend/public/pages/chicken-munch/site.css:312-313`; markup `index.html:138`). The page can be dragged 7px sideways. Proof: measured in Chromium at 375, screenshot `shots/2026-11-02/chicken-munch-375.png`. Fix: in the build under `scrollcraft/builds/chicken-munch`, add `min-width:0` to the grid children or `max-width:100%` to `.go-order`, then republish with `scripts/publish-page.mjs`.
2. [Low] On phones the footer contact links and menu links on all 17 republished sites measure 19px (footer phone, email) and 24 to 32px (menu, "Order") tall, e.g. chicken-munch, bake-away, kashermybnb. Proof: bounding boxes at 375 in Chromium. Fix: padding on footer and menu links in the shared template.
