# Ad blueprint: myisraelrental-moving

Every ad rule, in making order (docs/ad-rules-checklist.md). Answer a stage before making it; tick [x] once made.

## 1. The idea

- [x] A0.1 One job: what you get, or a question, or a real offer. Never two.
  plan: what you get: a place to stay, and on the same site the local businesses you will want once you are there. One job, for someone looking for a stay; the supply offer ('Add your business, free') is left for its own ad.
- [x] A0.2 One-second test: a stranger knows what is offered and where. Clarity beats cleverness.
  plan: frame one already shows a real living room on a card under a huge A STAY., a ring of real business photos swinging round it in depth, and MyIsraelRental, 'Stays, local businesses and restaurants', myisraelrental.com and the QR at the foot: what (a stay) and where (the site, whose name says the country). The last frame says the rest in six words.
- [x] A9g.1 Starts from an ad that already works: name the ad in docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02 (or Meta's Ad Library, by reach and days running) it takes its idea from.
  plan: motion library (docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02/motion) n11, 5/5: chocolates stay fixed in the centre while everything round them swaps on each beat ('keep the product fixed and swap its surroundings to show range in one clip'); here the swap is one ring that turns. The colour flood on every beat is from t02 (5/5, Lay's) and p101 (5/5, each bottle gets its own colour screen, then all of them together). The last frame learns from the still library's type-and-photo-interlock 5s 88ee2d and 9b765f (Fuscas: a car 'passing in front of some letters and behind others' of one huge word on a flat colour), which is what the ring does to REST.
- [x] A9e.2 In one sentence: what the picture says that the words finish. Cover the words: the picture raises a question. Cover the picture: the words are not complete.
  plan: the picture says everything turns round the stay: the room sits still in the middle while dinner, a party and a fresh look swing round to the front of it; the words finish it: find a stay, find the rest. Cover the words and it asks why fried chicken, balloons and nails are orbiting a living room; cover the picture and 'the rest' means nothing.
- [x] A9e.3 The idea, by name: the product stands in for a familiar object, or sits in a familiar screen, or type and subject interlock, or surreal scale, or a pun. Not a good photo with a headline (scored 1.9 to 2.8).
  plan: type and subject interlock, in depth: one ring of twelve real photos orbits the stay, passing behind the letters of REST. on its far side and in front of the stay card on its near side, and the card stands in front of the letters' feet. In the film the ring weaves through every wave's word the same way.
- [x] A9f.1 The angle, by name: a real moment and why ours wins there, or the exact reader, or an honest trade-off, or the want behind the feature, or a moment in the day, or a real review.
  plan: the want behind the feature: nobody wants only a room; once the stay is found you want dinner, a party sorted, a fresh look. Ours wins at that moment because the stay and those businesses are on one site. Shown, never claimed in words.
- [x] A9g.9 Is this a different-looking ad from the last ones for this business, and not the same design with another headline?
  plan: yes. The earlier MyIsraelRental ads (burst, split, answer-less, series, automation, list, mir-sites-video) speak to businesses joining, on a navy wall or a walnut desk, with a phone or a made scene. This is the first for someone looking for a stay, the first made only of real listing photos, the first on a blue ground, and none of the others swaps what surrounds one fixed thing.
- [x] A9g.10 The gallery of one business looks varied at a glance: its designs do not share one colour or one layout, and each starts on its own product where there are several.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (variety)

## 2. The words

- [x] A2.1 The headline is the biggest words on the ad and stops the scroll on its own; it is what the eye lands on first (squint test).
  plan: 'REST.' in white Anton capitals, about 430px, the biggest thing on the last frame, with 'FIND THE' (about 150px) over it and 'Find a stay.' (serif italic, about 88px) above that. During the film each wave's one word stands in the same place at about 300 to 400px.
- [x] A2.2 At most about 8 words, never cut with a "…"; 2 to 6 is best. Rewrite, never shrink.
  plan: six words: 'Find a stay. / FIND THE / REST.' Nothing cut, no '…'.
- [x] A2.3 Says who it is for or where, plus what it is, most concrete fact first. Their situation, never a boast.
  plan: what it is first (a stay), then what else (the rest: the local businesses just shown). Where: the name MyIsraelRental and the link at the foot. No town is named, because the tiles are not filtered to the stay's own area.
- [x] A2.4 Says it the way a person would, in the reader's words; from a real review when there is one.
  plan: how a person would say it: 'find a place to stay, then find the rest'. No review is quoted.
- [x] A2.5 What it gets them, or the want behind it, not just the name again. A twisted known phrase or the reader's own world beats a description.
  plan: what it gets them: the stay and everything after it from one place. The twist is 'the rest': it is what is still left to find, and it is the twelve things on the screen.
- [x] A2.6 Words a ten-year-old follows, one idea a sentence. No power words, no "even if", no made-up mechanism.
  plan: six one-syllable words in two short sentences, one idea each. No power words, no mechanism.
- [x] A2.7 Copy rules: no long dash, no exclamation in the headline, no praise the listing never states.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (words)
- [x] A2.8 Hebrew speaks in the plural (אתם), never to one man or one woman.
  plan: n/a: an English ad. A Hebrew one would be written in the plural (מצאו) and laid right to left.
- [x] A2.9 The caption: who or where, what it is and one or two facts, one plain invitation. No hashtags, no signature.
  plan: caption: 'Stays, local businesses and restaurants across Israel, on one site. No booking fees, no commission. Find yours on myisraelrental.com' (the home page's own line and voice.md's free line; no hashtags, no signature).
- [x] A2.10 Short, intriguing and clear: the headline with its one small line says what the business gives and why you would want it, in plain words a stranger gets in one look (the product or service named, or unmistakable in the picture, plus what it does for them). A pun or a twist only on top of that, never instead of it: 'Set it to LOW.' told nobody what L.A. Cholent sells. The answer writes 'A stranger reads:' and what someone who has never heard of the business would take from the headline alone.
  plan: A stranger reads: I can find a place to stay there, and the other things I will want as well (food, party things, beauty and home services). The product is named ('a stay') and is the photo in the middle; 'the rest' is unmistakable because it is the twelve photos round it, and the foot says it plainly: stays, local businesses and restaurants.
- [x] A2.11 Five headlines, then cut: write at least five headlines, numbered 1) to 5), and strike each that fails one of these, naming the failure: (a) names the object without saying why you want it ('THE RING.'); (b) guesses at the reader's situation instead of the offer ('Ready to ask?'); (c) is a pun or twist only someone who already knows the business gets ('Set it to LOW.'); (d) sounds like something else, a film, a song or another brand's slogan; (e) claims what the listing does not; (f) is more than six words or needs its small line to make sense. Write 'Chosen: N' and why. When Tzvi is in the conversation, show him the best three before the ad is built and build his pick.
  plan: 1) Find a stay. Find the rest. 2) ~~Your stay, sorted.~~ (d) every travel app's line, and it fits any site. 3) ~~One site for the whole trip.~~ (b) guesses it is a trip; many stays here are long rentals. 4) ~~A stay, and everything near it.~~ (e) 'near' is true only if the tiles are filtered to the stay's own area, and they are not. 5) ~~Stay here. Eat there.~~ (a) leaves out the businesses, and (d) sounds like a slogan. 6) ~~Book the stay. Find the rest.~~ (e) not every stay is booked on the site; 'find' is always true. Chosen: 1, six words, it names the product and says what else you get. Tzvi's pick (17 Nov 2026).

Never shrunk with filled or pale sides. The answer names ad-fit-picture, or n/a when no picture is generated.
  plan: n/a: no picture is generated; the ground is a flat colour the page draws edge to edge.

## 9. The pro look (Tzvi, 8 Nov 2026: "use the new things you learnt... make it a rule for all future ad builds")

- [x] A11.1 One idea you get in a second: name the surprise, pun or visual question the picture asks; the words answer it. "A good photo with a headline" is not an idea.
  plan: the surprise: the room is the sun and the town orbits it. The picture asks why fried chicken, balloons and nails are circling a living room; the words answer: find a stay, find the rest.
- [x] A11.2 Words and picture interlock: the headline or one huge word sits behind the subject with parts of the subject in front, or the subject sits inside the letters. Name how.
  plan: in depth, as the Fuscas ads weave a car through a word: back to front the layers are the ground, the far half of the ring, the word, the stay card, the near half of the ring. So the four far tiles pass behind the letters of REST., the card stands on the letters' feet, and the near tiles overlap the card's lower edge. Every wave's word is woven the same way.
- [x] A11.3 One colour world: the ground takes the product's own colour or its set's, with one accent; no stock yellow sticker or white button that belongs to no picture.
  plan: the ground is the blue of the stay's own armchairs (our accent #167AB8) with white as the one accent; each wave's ground is a colour from that wave's own photos. No sticker, no button.
- [x] A11.4 A frozen moment: something is happening (falling seeds, steam, a pour, a splash, motion). Name it, and keep it true to the product.
  plan: acted out, then frozen mid-orbit: the last frame holds the ring where it stopped, near tiles large and over the card's lower edge, far tiles small behind the letters, the card still turned 3 degrees toward the last swing.
- [x] A11.5 Light that shapes: one strong side or back light, a fall to dark, real depth. Never flat even daylight on the hero.
  plan: the room is a real photo and is not relit. The depth round it is real perspective: tiles grow from about 105px at the back to about 230px at the front, far ones hazed with the ground's colour and slightly soft, near ones casting a soft shadow on the card and the ground; the card is glass with a layered shadow, turns a few degrees with each swing, and one glint crosses it per wave. Judged on the sheets.
- [x] A11.6 Three things carry the ad: the picture, the headline, the name. The link, code and price are small and quiet, and corners are left empty.
  plan: the stay with its ring, REST. and the name. Line, link and QR small at the foot; the top left is empty during the waves and the bottom 230px is empty throughout.
- [x] A11.7 The headline is 2 to 5 spoken words, set big, in two voices where it helps (bold capitals with a lighter or script line).
  plan: 'Find a stay.' in a serif italic over 'FIND THE REST.' in Anton capitals: two voices, six spoken words (one over this rule's five and inside A2.2's six, kept because 'the rest' needs both halves).
- [x] A11.8 The product is the hero: it fills about half the width or more, close, standing on its set like a product shoot, never pasted in front of one.
  plan: the stay card fills 59% of the width, centred and whole, in the same place in all 240 frames; only the ring, the ground and the word change.
