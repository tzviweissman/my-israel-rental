# Ad blueprint: myisraelrental-restaurants-status

Every ad rule, in making order (docs/ad-rules-checklist.md). Answer a stage before making it; tick [x] once made.

## 1. The idea

- [x] A0.1 One job: what you get, or a question, or a real offer. Never two.
  plan: what you get: one place to find a restaurant with a hechsher (a kosher certificate) anywhere in Israel, over 5,000 of them. One job; adding a business, rentals and services stay out.
- [x] A0.2 One-second test: a stranger knows what is offered and where. Clarity beats cleverness.
  plan: a huge white '5,000+' on our flat accent blue with a fork of spaghetti lifted through the digits, 'restaurants, each with a hechsher.' under it and 'Kosher across Israel, in one place.' above: a stranger gets 'one place with thousands of kosher restaurants in Israel' in a second.
- [x] A9g.1 Starts from an ad that already works: name the ad in docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02 (or Meta's Ad Library, by reach and days running) it takes its idea from. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design; A9g.11 says what must have been looked at first.
  plan: Pinterest pin 900d6a saved 25 Nov 2026 (docs/inspo/pinterest-restaurants-ad-2026-11-25/900d6ab6023f2e22ce0ebd58d0ffe758.jpg: a sandwich held up through huge red letters 'NYC' on plain cream, food in front of some letters and behind others), with the library 5 that does the same weave: 88ee2d (docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02/best/88ee2d1934949c4431944a02ac939eac.jpg, a car driving in and out of one huge word on flat yellow), and 02fbe9 from the same search for the number told with food. Three ideas were shown to Tzvi, each from a reference opened: (2) CHOSEN by Tzvi, 25 Nov 2026, 900d6a with 88ee2d: the number '5,000+' set huge, a fork twirling pasta lifted through the digits, in front of some and behind others. Not chosen: (1) 1761c9 with 8e34d1: many plates of food, from above, laid out into one map pin, an empty plate its hole. (3) 197420 with eb24c5: one place setting from above, the plate's face a street map dotted with blue pins, for 'One place.' The rest of this blueprint is for idea 2.
- [x] A9e.2 In one sentence: what the picture says that the words finish. Cover the words: the picture raises a question. Cover the picture: the words are not complete.
  plan: cover the words: why is a fork of pasta threaded through a giant number? Cover the picture: 'Kosher across Israel, in one place.' does not say how much is in that place. Together: the number is the restaurants, and it is something you eat.
- [x] A9e.3 The idea, by name: the product stands in for a familiar object, or sits in a familiar screen, or type and subject interlock, or surreal scale, or a pun. Not a good photo with a headline (scored 1.9 to 2.8).
  plan: type and subject interlock: the fork and its strands pass in front of some digits of '5,000+' and behind others, one strand through the hole of a zero, as the sandwich does in 900d6a and the car in 88ee2d.
- [x] A9f.1 The angle, by name: a real moment and why ours wins there, or the exact reader, or an honest trade-off, or the want behind the feature, or a moment in the day, or a real review.
  plan: a real moment and why ours wins there: choosing where to eat and having to check each restaurant's hechsher one at a time; here they are all in one place, each with its hechsher named, and you can filter by it.
- [x] A9g.11 Ideas come from references opened, not summaries: before any idea is written, at least five library ads (docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02, by their six-character codes) are opened at full size, and one Pinterest search is made for this ad (or its series) and saved under docs/inspo/pinterest-<subject>-<date>/ with its README. The answer names the folder and the five codes. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design from 25 Nov 2026; with A9g.1, the reference it names is one of these.
  plan: docs/inspo/pinterest-restaurants-ad-2026-11-25 (two Pinterest searches, 44 pins on sheet-0.jpg and sheet-1.jpg; 8e34d1, 900d6a and eb24c5 opened at full size, 639cf9 and 02fbe9 read on the sheet). - [x] A9g.9 Is this a different-looking ad from the last ones for this business, and not the same design with another headline?
  plan: yes: 'Be found.' is a 3D golden-hour street with a round glass; 'Be on the map.' - [x] A9g.10 The gallery of one business looks varied at a glance: its designs do not share one colour or one layout, and each starts on its own product where there are several.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (variety)
- [x] A27.1 The site's brand: for a business with a custom site, name its scrollcraft/builds/<folder>/BRAND.md and say which of its colours and fonts this ad uses and which of its bans it keeps; or "n/a: no custom site".

  plan: n/a: no product with a label; a plain fork and plain spaghetti carry no name or mark.

## 2. The words

- [x] A2.1 The headline is the biggest words on the ad and stops the scroll on its own; it is what the eye lands on first (squint test).
  plan: the headline 'Kosher across Israel, in one place.' is at the top, where reading starts: Playfair Display 100px, ink #111827, two lines ('Kosher across Israel,' / 'in one place.', 'one place.' in #0F5E8F). The only larger thing is the number '5,000+' (about 330px, #167AB8), which is the picture itself and the second thing read; 'restaurants,' sits right under it so the number reads as restaurants.
- [x] A2.2 At most about 8 words, never cut with a "…"; 2 to 6 is best. Rewrite, never shrink.
  plan: six words.
- [x] A2.3 Says who it is for or where, plus what it is, most concrete fact first. Their situation, never a boast.
  plan: what it is (kosher), where (across Israel) and what we are (one place); the number under it gives the concrete fact, '5,000+ restaurants, each with a hechsher.' A count, not a boast.
- [x] A2.4 Says it the way a person would, in the reader's words; from a real review when there is one.
  plan: Tzvi's own spoken line, 25 Nov 2026: 'kosher acoss israel in 1 place'.
- [x] A2.5 What it gets them, or the want behind it, not just the name again. A twisted known phrase or the reader's own world beats a description.
  plan: what it gets them: not checking place by place, town by town; everything kosher across the country in one place.
- [x] A2.6 Words a ten-year-old follows, one idea a sentence. No power words, no "even if", no made-up mechanism.
  plan: one sentence, one idea, words a child follows (hechsher is the readers' own word); no power words, no made-up mechanism.
- [x] A2.7 Copy rules: no long dash, no exclamation in the headline, no praise the listing never states.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (words)
- [x] A2.8 Hebrew speaks in the plural (אתם), never to one man or one woman.
  plan: Hebrew addresses nobody, so no singular: 'כשר בכל הארץ, במקום אחד.' with '5,000+' and 'מסעדות, כל אחת עם תעודת כשרות.' under it, written for Hebrew, not word for word.
- [x] A2.9 The caption: who or where, what it is and one or two facts, one plain invitation. No hashtags, no signature.
  plan: caption: 'Restaurants across Israel: over 5,000 on MyIsraelRental, each with the hechsher named on its own website. Search by city, neighbourhood, meat or dairy and hechsher, or look on the map. Free. myisraelrental.com/restaurants'
- [x] A2.10 Short, intriguing and clear: the headline with its one small line says what the business gives and why you would want it, in plain words a stranger gets in one look (the product or service named, or unmistakable in the picture, plus what it does for them). A pun or a twist only on top of that, never instead of it: 'Set it to LOW.' told nobody what L.A. Cholent sells. The answer writes 'A stranger reads:' and what someone who has never heard of the business would take from the headline alone.
  plan: A stranger reads: there is one place to find kosher food anywhere in Israel; the number and its line then say what: over 5,000 restaurants, each with a hechsher.
- [x] A2.11 Five headlines, then cut: write at least five headlines, numbered 1) to 5), and strike each that fails one of these, naming the failure: (a) names the object without saying why you want it ('THE RING.'); (b) guesses at the reader's situation instead of the offer ('Ready to ask?'); (c) is a pun or twist only someone who already knows the business gets ('Set it to LOW.'); (d) sounds like something else, a film, a song or another brand's slogan; (e) claims what the listing does not; (f) is more than six words or needs its small line to make sense. Write 'Chosen: N' and why. When Tzvi is in the conversation, show him the best three before the ad is built and build his pick.
  plan: 1) 5,000+ kosher restaurants. One place. 2) Kosher across Israel, in one place. 3) 5,000+ restaurants. All with a hechsher. 4) ~~5,000 places to eat. One place to look.~~ (f) eight words, and without its small line nothing says kosher. 5) ~~Hungry? We know a place.~~ (b) guesses at the reader's situation instead of the offer, and (d) a line many restaurant ads use. 6) ~~The kosher map of Israel.~~ (a) names the thing without saying what you get from it, and (e) not every kosher restaurant is on it. 7) ~~Every hechsher, one place.~~ (e) we list the hechsherim restaurants name on their own websites, not every one. Shown to Tzvi: 1, 2, 3. Tzvi, 25 Nov 2026: "kosher acoss israel in 1 place". Chosen: 2) Kosher across Israel, in one place. Six words; the number it leaves out is the picture, drawn huge with 'restaurants, each with a hechsher.' under it, so together they carry all four parts of his first line: one place, 5,000+, a hechsher, Israel. Nothing says 'verified' or 'certified': the listing shows the hechsher 'as shown on their website'.

## 3. The picture

- [x] A3.1 One huge subject on one quiet ground: it fills half the frame or more, two or three colours in all.
  plan: one huge subject, the number with the fork of pasta through it, the digits 87% of the width and the fork and pasta about half of it, on one flat ground of our accent blue #167AB8: blue, white, and the red of the sauce.
- [x] A3.2 A rental or a place: only the owner's real photos. A generated room does not exist. Say where each picture comes from.
  plan: no restaurant and no real place is shown: one generated photograph (through scripts/hf.py) of a plain steel fork, no hand, coming in from the lower right edge and lifted, twirled with a nest of spaghetti, a few long strands hanging and looping, on a plain, empty warm cream studio ground so the digits can sit on it. Nothing claims it is a listed restaurant's dish. Kosher-safe: spaghetti in plain tomato sauce with one basil leaf, pareve; no meat, no cheese, no shellfish, no pork.
- [x] A3.3 A generated picture of a product keeps the real product: rebuilt around its real photo, its traits unchanged, compared beside that photo. Describe the reference scene in full, never "swap the product".
  plan: n/a: no product of a business is generated.
- [x] A3.4 Another shape than the screen: the background is extended, never cropped or zoomed.
  plan: the picture is made in the story's own 9:16 shape; feed and square get their own layout on the flat blue ground, never a crop of the fork.
- [x] A3.5 Sharp and bright; one colour that stands out on a white and a dark feed, from our palette or the owner's own photo and logo.
  plan: sharp and bright; our accent blue #167AB8 on the digits is the one colour that stands out on a white and a dark feed, with the red sauce against it.
- [x] A3.6 A hand holding, offering or reaching in, where it helps. Real or native beats polished.
  plan: no hand: the fork comes in from the edge on its own. A generated hand is the likeliest thing to come out wrong, and it would read as a customer (A5.4).
- [x] A3.7 No words drawn into a generated picture. The picture is one layer, the words typed on top and editable.
  plan: no words or digits in the generated picture: fork, pasta and plain ground only; '5,000+', every word, the logo and the QR are typed in ad.html.
- [x] A3.8 Another brand's idea and composition only, never its marks, wording or product.
  plan: the idea and composition of 900d6a only (food woven through huge type on a plain ground); none of its words, marks, product or colours.
- [x] A3.9 The picture fills the ad edge to edge as it was made: made in the ad's own shape or the nearest shorter one the model takes, laid at full width with scripts/ad-fit-picture.py, which carries on only the plain wall above and table below in the picture's own colours and refuses to shrink. Never shrunk with filled or pale sides. The answer names ad-fit-picture, or n/a when no picture is generated.
  plan: the picture is made at 9:16 and laid at full width with scripts/ad-fit-picture.py, with --cut; the fork and pasta are cut out (front.png) and laid at full size on the flat blue, 60px lower than shot; never shrunk.
- [x] A3.10 A product shot is styled like a pro shoot: the product big and sharp on a rich surface (wood, stone, linen, never a bare white table), in warm shaped light, with two or three things from the business's own world at the sides (what it is served with, made from or sold beside). Props never in front of the product, never presented as the product, no cooked food passed off as theirs. Name each prop and why it belongs.
  plan: n/a: not a product shot of a business. The ground is plain on purpose, because the digits sit on it as the letters do in 900d6a; the care goes into the food: glossy sauce, each strand sharp, one basil leaf, warm shaped light and a soft shadow on the ground.
- [x] A3.11 Layout first, never numbers alone: before any generated picture, its layout is drawn with scripts/ad-layout-sketch.py (boxes where the product, props and moment go; the headline and foot areas kept empty) and given to the reference-image model (marketing-studio/image/sunburst) as an image, with "photograph this layout for real" and the real product photo when there is one. Told only in words, the model centred the phone and drew eight notes for thirty. Exact parts (a screen, a label) are laid on afterwards in code. The answer names the layout picture; the ad's gen script must use it. n/a only when nothing is generated.
  plan: docs/ads/gen/myisraelrental-restaurants-status/layout.png (from layout.json, drawn with scripts/ad-layout-sketch.py), given to the model as Image 1: ground #F4EBDD; the headline area kept empty from 220px to 500px; six outlined boxes where the digits '5 , 0 0 0 +' will be typed, x 6.5% to 93.5%, y 29% to 42%, marked 'keep clear, strands cross here'; the nest of pasta x 48% to 82%, y 40% to 60%, its top just over the feet of the last two zeros; the fork's handle from the nest down to the right edge at y 73%; two strands rising from the nest through the boxes of the last two zeros, three hanging and looping down and left to x 12%, y 72%; the label area x 6.5% to 48%, y 43% to 51% kept empty; the foot's band (77.5% to 87.5%) and the bottom 230px kept empty. gen.py gives it to marketing-studio/image/sunburst with 'photograph this layout for real'.
- [x] A3.12 A screen in an ad shows the real thing: our website or dashboard captured at the screen's own shape from the local test account (its sample data, never a real business or customer, no test-looking names), not an icon or a made-up mock.   plan: n/a: no screen in the ad.

  plan: n/a: no scarcity or urgency.
