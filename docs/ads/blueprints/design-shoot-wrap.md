# Ad blueprint: design-shoot-wrap

Every ad rule, in making order (docs/ad-rules-checklist.md). Answer a stage before making it; tick [x] once made.

## 1. The idea

- [x] A0.1 One job: what you get, or a question, or a real offer. Never two.
  plan: What you get: the owner's own dish, its noodles, cheese, sauce or cream tied round the one big word that names it. The price and link are the Product photo design's small foot, not a second job.
- [x] A0.2 One-second test: a stranger knows what is offered and where. Clarity beats cleverness.
  plan: The big word names the food and the food itself is wrapped through it, so what is sold is read and seen in the same look; the business's name is the eyebrow over it and the link and QR say where (drawShoot, unchanged).
- [x] A9g.1 Starts from an ad that already works: name the ad in docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02 (or Meta's Ad Library, by reach and days running) it takes its idea from. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design; A9g.11 says what must have been looked at first.
  plan: Tzvi's two posters, docs/inspo/pinterest-pasta-posters-2026-11-25/pasta-word.jpg ('PASTA': tagliatelle knotted through a heavy condensed word on cream) and docs/inspo/pinterest-pasta-posters-2026-11-25/twist-word.jpg ('TWIST': pasta in front of some letters and behind others, herbs in the air), with pin 900d6a (docs/inspo/pinterest-restaurants-ad-2026-11-25/900d6ab6023f2e22ce0ebd58d0ffe758.jpg, a sandwich through huge letters 'NYC' on cream) and the library 5 that does the same weave: 88ee2d (docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02/best/88ee2d1934949c4431944a02ac939eac.jpg, a car in and out of one huge word). The hand-made ad that proved it for us: docs/ads/myisraelrental-restaurants-status-ad.jpg (take 4).
- [x] A9e.2 In one sentence: what the picture says that the words finish. Cover the words: the picture raises a question. Cover the picture: the words are not complete.
  plan: The picture asks 'why is the food tied round the letters?'; the word it is tied round answers what it is, and the lead line and the name finish whose it is. Cover the word and the food curls round nothing; cover the food and it is one bare word.
- [x] A9e.3 The idea, by name: the product stands in for a familiar object, or sits in a familiar screen, or type and subject interlock, or surreal scale, or a pun. Not a good photo with a headline (scored 1.9 to 2.8).
  plan: type and subject interlock: the product's own soft or long parts pass in front of some letters of one huge word and behind the rest, where the model photographs them, shading them.
- [x] A9f.1 The angle, by name: a real moment and why ours wins there, or the exact reader, or an honest trade-off, or the want behind the feature, or a moment in the day, or a real review.
  plan: the want behind the product: the food shown close enough to see its strands, shine and crumbs, in the business's own word for it (the lead line gives the moment, as in the Product photo design).
- [x] A9g.11 Ideas come from references opened, not summaries: before any idea is written, at least five library ads (docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02, by their six-character codes) are opened at full size, and one Pinterest search is made for this ad (or its series) and saved under docs/inspo/pinterest-<subject>-<date>/ with its README. The answer names the folder and the five codes. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design from 25 Nov 2026; with A9g.1, the reference it names is one of these.
  plan: docs/inspo/pinterest-pasta-posters-2026-11-25 (Tzvi's own screenshots of a Pinterest search for 'pasta ads design', its README beside them; both opened at full size) and docs/inspo/pinterest-restaurants-ad-2026-11-25 (two searches, 44 pins; 900d6a opened at full size). Library ads opened at full size for this blueprint: 88ee2d (a car woven through one huge stacked word on flat yellow, in front of the S and A, behind the U and C), 9b765f (the same in red on blue: the weave holds on any ground when the word is one flat ink), 772fc1 (a chocolate cake floating in front of huge red words on cream, raspberries and curls in the air), 96a856 (a coffee bean in front of tall thin 'COFFEE', pouring into a cup: one word, the food hanging from it), 8d1dca (a burger tucked into white 'CHEESE' on yellow: the food set into the letters, but nothing passes behind and nothing moves, the calm one of the five).
- [x] A9g.9 Is this a different-looking ad from the last ones for this business, and not the same design with another headline?
  plan: yes: the only picture in the maker where the food crosses the letters; the plain shoot stands the product in front of a word on a dark or wooden set, the tiny people show a crew, this is a pale cream poster with the word in a deep warm ink. It is a third picture idea of the Product photo design, picked beside the other two.
- [x] A9g.10 The gallery of one business looks varied at a glance: its designs do not share one colour or one layout, and each starts on its own product where there are several.
  plan: built by AdMaker START_LOOK, FAMILIES and THEMED give each layout its own look; confirm: check-ad-designs measure variety
- [x] A27.1 The site's brand: for a business with a custom site, name its scrollcraft/builds/<folder>/BRAND.md and say which of its colours and fonts this ad uses and which of its bans it keeps; or "n/a: no custom site".
  plan: built by AdMaker for every food business, not for one site: the ground is the cream #F3EBDD, the word's ink is the kind's own (WRAP_INK) and the owner can recolour the word to their brand colour (PARTS.ink; weaveFood follows the new ink), in the design's fixed fonts. Nothing reads a site's BRAND.md, so a business with a custom site checks its bans by eye; to build: offer the BRAND.md accent as the ink.
- [x] A28.1 The look is chosen before anything is built: one cheap sample still of the same moment in each look that fits the idea (drawn flat, 3D animated film, real photo), shown to Tzvi side by side; the answer names the look he chose and the sample sheet.
  plan: a real photograph: the bar Tzvi set is two real food photographs (docs/inspo/pinterest-pasta-posters-2026-11-25/pasta-word.jpg, twist-word.jpg) and the remake he then asked to turn into this design is one too (docs/ads/myisraelrental-restaurants-status-ad.jpg, 25 and 26 Nov 2026). An owner's real dish has no drawn or 3D look to choose; no sample sheet of other looks was made.
- [x] A28.2 A product rebuilt in a generated scene keeps its own label: the real label or sticker, cut from their photo, is laid over the rebuilt one on the still ad and on frames where it holds still. When it is in a moving shot, the label is laid with scripts/label-fit.py onto the corners of the clip's own label, in the scene's light, and carried along with the pack's own surface so it never slides on it, never pasted flat into a fixed or hand-placed box. The answer names the label file, or n/a: the product is their own photo.
  plan: n/a: the product is a dish with soft or long parts and no pack or label; the prompt keeps it as in the owner's photo and allows no text. A labelled pack has nothing to wrap: the owner picks Product shoot for it. No label is laid by this design.
- [x] A28.3 When a generated still or clip shows their product: their real product photo is given to the model as a reference image, and the prompt asks for the product with a plain panel in the label's main colour, the label's shape and place, and no writing on it, so the real label lands on a clean panel that already turns and takes the light. The answer names the product photo and the panel colour, or n/a: no product in a generated shot.
  plan: the owner's product photo is image 1, taken by the server from the business's own listing, never from the request (short_links POST /ads/shots); no label panel is asked for, because a dish carries none (panel colour n/a).
- [x] A28.4 When a prompt names a thing the model may not know (a dreidel, a menorah, a sufganiya, a challah, any holiday or local thing): the prompt spells out its shape, parts and colours in plain words, not only its name, and the first picture is looked at before any more are made. The answer quotes the shape words, or n/a: nothing unusual is asked for.
  plan: n/a: the prompt names nothing unusual, only general parts ('noodles, ribbons of pasta, melted cheese, sauce, dough, cream, chocolate, herbs, whatever this product really has'); the product itself is shown to the model by its photo, and its name comes from the listing.

## 2. The words

- [x] A2.1 The headline is the biggest words on the ad and stops the scroll on its own; it is what the eye lands on first (squint test).
  plan: the big word is the largest thing on the ad: Anton (Secular One in Hebrew), 20% of a story's height and 22% of a square or feed's, shrunk only until it fits the width less 80px (wrapBox).
- [x] A2.2 At most about 8 words, never cut with a "…"; 2 to 6 is best. Rewrite, never shrink.
  plan: one big word (up to 24 characters, the AI writer's big_word, else its key word) and a lead of 2 to 5 words; never cut with dots.
- [x] A2.3 Says who it is for or where, plus what it is, most concrete fact first. Their situation, never a boast.
  plan: the big word is the thing itself from the listing; the eyebrow says the business; the price line names the item (unchanged from the Product photo design).
- [x] A2.4 Says it the way a person would, in the reader's words; from a real review when there is one.
  plan: unchanged from the Product photo design: the lead is written the way a person would say it (writer rules); the owner can write their own.
- [x] A2.5 What it gets them, or the want behind it, not just the name again. A twisted known phrase or the reader's own world beats a description.
  plan: unchanged: the lead names the moment or the want ('Bring the', 'Missing real American'); here the picture adds the want, the food close up.
- [x] A2.6 Words a ten-year-old follows, one idea a sentence. No power words, no "even if", no made-up mechanism.
  plan: unchanged: the AI words rules.
- [x] A2.7 Copy rules: no long dash, no exclamation in the headline, no praise the listing never states.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (words)
- [x] A2.8 Hebrew speaks in the plural (אתם), never to one man or one woman.
  plan: unchanged: the AI words speak in the plural in Hebrew.
- [x] A2.9 The caption: who or where, what it is and one or two facts, one plain invitation. No hashtags, no signature.
  plan: unchanged: the same caption as every design.
- [x] A2.10 Short, intriguing and clear: the headline with its one small line says what the business gives and why you would want it, in plain words a stranger gets in one look (the product or service named, or unmistakable in the picture, plus what it does for them). A pun or a twist only on top of that, never instead of it: 'Set it to LOW.' told nobody what L.A. Cholent sells. The answer writes 'A stranger reads:' and what someone who has never heard of the business would take from the headline alone.
  plan: A stranger reads: one word for a food ('PASTA', 'CHALLAH', 'CHEESECAKE') with that very food wound through it, the business's name over it and its price under it: what is sold is the word and the picture at once. No pun is needed.
- [x] A2.11 Five headlines, then cut: write at least five headlines, numbered 1) to 5), and strike each that fails one of these, naming the failure: (a) names the object without saying why you want it ('THE RING.'); (b) guesses at the reader's situation instead of the offer ('Ready to ask?'); (c) is a pun or twist only someone who already knows the business gets ('Set it to LOW.'); (d) sounds like something else, a film, a song or another brand's slogan; (e) claims what the listing does not; (f) is more than six words or needs its small line to make sense. Write 'Chosen: N' and why. When Tzvi is in the conversation, show him the best three before the ad is built and build his pick.
  plan: n/a: a design, not one ad; its words are the AI writer's lead and big word for each business, which the owner picks or rewrites (five-then-cut is for hand-made ads). The owner must have a big word before the picture is made (ads.shotWrapNoWord), and a new word is a new picture (A3.13).

## 3. The picture

- [x] A3.1 One huge subject on one quiet ground: it fills half the frame or more, two or three colours in all.
  plan: one subject, the dish with its word, on plain cream: the word fills the width, the dish sits against it, the rest is empty; three colours, the cream, the word's one ink and the food's own.
- [x] A3.2 A rental or a place: only the owner's real photos. A generated room does not exist. Say where each picture comes from.
  plan: n/a: businesses' products only; the endpoint refuses rentals and sites, and only food kinds get this idea (WRAP_KINDS: grill, bakery, cafe, kitchen; any other kind is refused with 'Wrapped round your word is for food businesses').
- [x] A3.3 A generated picture of a product keeps the real product: rebuilt around its real photo, its traits unchanged, compared beside that photo. Describe the reference scene in full, never "swap the product".
  plan: image 1 is the owner's own photo from their listing; prompt_for_wrap says the product is 'the same as in image 1, its shape, colours, toppings and texture unchanged', describes the whole scene (the word as solid painted letters on cream, the soft parts curling round them, daylight from the upper left) and never says swap. The owner sees the picture before using it; every picture is kept.
- [x] A3.4 Another shape than the screen: the background is extended, never cropped or zoomed.
  plan: made in the ad's own shape (1:1, 3:4, 9:16) with the plate drawn in that same shape, so nothing is cropped; another shape is photographed again with its own plate, not cut from this one.
- [x] A3.5 Sharp and bright; one colour that stands out on a white and a dark feed, from our palette or the owner's own photo and logo.
  plan: made at 2K; the one colour is the word's deep warm ink per kind (grill #7A2E12, bakery #8A3B12, cafe #4A2C1A, kitchen #2F5D2F) on cream, which stands off a white feed and a dark one.
- [x] A3.6 A hand holding, offering or reaching in, where it helps. Real or native beats polished.
  plan: n/a: 'No hands, no people' in the prompt; the food's own parts do the acting, and a generated hand could read as a customer (A5.4).
- [x] A3.7 No words drawn into a generated picture. The picture is one layer, the words typed on top and editable. The one exception is a word or number the picture is made around (A3.13, from 25 Nov 2026), which is still typed on top.
  plan: built by AdMaker draws every word as its own canvas layer over the picture; confirm: LOOK
- [x] A3.8 Another brand's idea and composition only, never its marks, wording or product.
  plan: only the idea of the posters and library ads (food woven through one huge word on a plain warm ground); none of their words, marks, dishes or layouts: the word is the business's own and the dish is their photo.
- [x] A3.9 The picture fills the ad edge to edge as it was made: made in the ad's own shape or the nearest shorter one the model takes, laid at full width with scripts/ad-fit-picture.py, which carries on only the plain wall above and table below in the picture's own colours and refuses to shrink. Never shrunk with filled or pale sides. The answer names ad-fit-picture, or n/a when no picture is generated.
  plan: made in the ad's own shape and laid by drawShoot's cover() at full width, edge to edge, never shrunk and no sides filled; scripts/ad-fit-picture.py is the same laying for a hand-made sheet and is not needed here because no shape is borrowed.
- [x] A3.10 A product shot is styled like a pro shoot: the product big and sharp on a rich surface (wood, stone, linen, never a bare white table), in warm shaped light, with two or three things from the business's own world at the sides (what it is served with, made from or sold beside). Props never in front of the product, never presented as the product, no cooked food passed off as theirs. Name each prop and why it belongs.
  plan: not a set: the plate is the whole set (no set picture is sent). The ground is plain warm cream paper, as on both of Tzvi's posters, the light soft and warm from the upper left, and there are no props at all: 'no plate unless image 1 shows one'; only the product's own small pieces near it. Nothing is in front of the product and nothing is passed off as theirs.
- [x] A3.11 Layout first, never numbers alone: before any generated picture, its layout is drawn with scripts/ad-layout-sketch.py (boxes where the product, props and moment go; the headline and foot areas kept empty) and given to the reference-image model (marketing-studio/image/sunburst) as an image, with "photograph this layout for real" and the real product photo when there is one. Told only in words, the model centred the phone and drew eight notes for thirty. Exact parts (a screen, a label) are laid on afterwards in code. The answer names the layout picture; the ad's gen script must use it. n/a only when nothing is generated.
  plan: the plate is the layout: wrapPlate draws the ad's word alone, in its final font, size and place, on a cream canvas of the ad's own shape, and it goes to marketing-studio/image/sunburst as image 2, so the model is shown where the word is, not told; the prompt keeps the top 25% (story) or 18% and the bottom 30% or 25% empty for the eyebrow, lead and foot. No scripts/ad-layout-sketch.py boxes are sent for the food's path. Worked example with the path drawn: docs/ads/gen/myisraelrental-restaurants-status/layout4.png.
- [x] A3.12 A screen in an ad shows the real thing: our website or dashboard captured at the screen's own shape from the local test account (its sample data, never a real business or customer, no test-looking names), not an icon or a made-up mock. Professional is in the details. Name the page captured.
  plan: n/a: no screen.
- [x] A3.13 Words the picture is made around: when the subject wraps, passes behind or shades a word or number (A11.2), the picture is made around our own typed word. The ad's page draws that word alone in its final font, size, place and ink on the ground (ad.html?plate=1), and that image goes to the model with 'keep these characters exactly'; then the page types our crisp word over the picture again and a script in docs/ads/gen/<name>/ cuts only the parts that pass in front of it. The one exception to A3.7, for that word only, which is still typed on top: changing it means a new picture. The answer names the plate picture, or n/a: nothing crosses the words. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design from 25 Nov 2026 whose words and picture interlock.
  plan: built by AdMaker: wrapPlate(format, word, rtl, ink) draws the word alone (Anton or Secular One, wrapBox's size and place, the kind's ink, cream #F3EBDD) and sends it as a PNG with POST /ads/shots; prompt_for_wrap says 'Keep that word EXACTLY as it is in image 2'; drawShoot types our crisp word at the same wrapBox place with a 3px ring of its own ink over the model's letters, and weaveFood puts the picture's food back over every other letter, 6px inside our strokes and only where the picture is not the letter's ink. A new word is a new picture (wrap_word is part of the saved picture's key). Worked example of a plate: docs/ads/gen/myisraelrental-restaurants-status/plate.png. Built 26 Nov 2026 after the American Crust test: wrapPlace finds the model's letters (columns and rows mostly the ink), fits our word to them, and when nearly all their ink lies inside our letters (box.own) the photo's own letters are kept with the food really round them; otherwise our crisp word is typed over it (3px ink ring) and weaveFood puts back only warm or dark food pixels inside our letters. Feed plates are drawn 3:4, the model's own shape.
- [x] A3.14 Food is shown on a warm ground: behind food or drink the ground is warm and appetising (cream, paper, wood, warm stone, or a warm colour such as yellow, orange or red), never a cold flat blue, grey or green as the main colour; the brand colour goes on the type instead. Applies to every hand-made food or drink ad and new design from 25 Nov 2026; n/a for any other subject.
  plan: the ground is warm cream #F3EBDD in the plate and in the prompt ('plain warm cream background'); the colour goes on the type (WRAP_INK), never a cold ground. The foot keeps the Product photo design's dark shade under the price and link.
- [x] A3.15 Food shows its texture and life: the dish's own garnish and seasoning on it and a few pieces in the air (herbs, pepper, cheese, crumbs, steam), light raking across so the surface reads, and the food big enough on a phone to see that texture. For a business's real product, only the bits that product really has (A9.6). Applies to every hand-made food or drink ad and new design from 25 Nov 2026; n/a for any other subject.
  plan: the prompt asks for 'crisp, mouth-watering, fine texture', the product's own soft parts round the letters casting soft real shadows, 'a few of the product's own small pieces (crumbs, herbs, seeds, flakes) float in the air near it, only ones this product really has', and soft warm daylight from the upper left; the word is a fifth of the ad's height, so what crosses it is big on a phone. Nothing the product does not have is asked for (A9.6).
- [x] A3.16 The bar is set before paying: before any paid picture, the layout sketch (A3.11) sits beside three or more top posters of the ad's own subject from a saved Pinterest search, on a sheet from python scripts/ad_vs_pros.py <sheet.jpg> <layout.png> --refs <pin>,<pin>,<pin>, and the answer names the sheet and what the prompt takes from those posters (how the subject meets the words, the ground, the garnish, the flow). The same posters are then used for A9.9. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design from 26 Nov 2026 with a generated picture; n/a when nothing is generated.
  plan: the bar was set from posters opened before the prompt was written: docs/inspo/pinterest-pasta-posters-2026-11-25/pasta-word.jpg, docs/inspo/pinterest-pasta-posters-2026-11-25/twist-word.jpg and docs/inspo/pinterest-restaurants-ad-2026-11-25/900d6ab6023f2e22ce0ebd58d0ffe758.jpg. The prompt takes from them: the food passes behind some letters and in front of others and shades them; one heavy word in one flat ink; a plain warm cream ground; the dish's own bits in the air; soft light from the upper left. Not taken: the diagonal from a fork (see A9.9, 6). No sheet of this design's plate beside them was made with scripts/ad_vs_pros.py (nothing was built or paid for with this blueprint); the worked example is the restaurants ad beside its posters, docs/ads/myisraelrental-restaurants-vs-kind.jpg.

## 4. Body and proof

- [x] A4.1 At most three points, each one fact; dropped first when the picture needs the room.
  plan: n/a: no points; the picture and the word carry it (unchanged from the Product photo design).
- [x] A4.2 One price, and it is the price of the thing in the picture. "From" only for the item that costs that.
  plan: built by AdMaker adIssues(words, item) flags any price in the words that is not the featured item's, shown live in the panel as the owner types; confirm: check-ad-designs measure price
- [x] A4.3 A struck-through price only when real: same thing bought separately at real prices, or really charged before.
  plan: n/a: no struck price.
- [x] A4.4 A review is real, shown on the public page, rated 4 or 5, the reviewer's words, shortened only by cutting. No hearts or counts.
  plan: n/a: no review.
- [x] A4.5 Press or partner logos only if the business really has them, four at most.
  plan: n/a: none.
- [x] A4.6 The business's own logo is on every ad it pays for or is shown in: their real logo file (the listing's logo, or cut cleanly out of a picture of theirs), never redrawn, retyped or generated, placed where it reads at phone size and clear of the phone's top bar (from 100px down on a 1920 status). The name in words may sit beside it, never in place of it. The answer names the logo file used; n/a only with "n/a: no logo" when the business has none, and then the name is set in their own style. Since 18 Nov 2026 its lettering is at least 30px tall at 1080 wide, measured: check-moving-ad.py does it on a moving ad's last frame, and a still ad's answer quotes backend/.venv/Scripts/python scripts/logo_check.py <ad.png> <x,y,w,h>. A badge, seal or flyer whose letters are small or sit on a busy picture is not shrunk into a corner: their wordmark is cut out clean (docs/ads/gen/la-cholent-chain-moving/logo-mark.png) and shown large.
  plan: built by AdMaker extras() places the listing's logo on every design (part 'logo', white tile for see-through logos); AdStudio uses a fixed site LOGO, to build: the same extras() in AdStudio; confirm: check-ad-designs measure logo
- [x] A1.3 The action: one thing to do, the short link and QR code, last on the eye's path.
  plan: built by AdMaker drawFooter, footBand, qrTile and linkPiece draw link and QR in every design; confirm: check-ad-designs measure link

## 5. Honesty

- [x] A5.1 Nothing invented: no number, feature, review, person, view or distance the listing does not state.
  plan: nothing invented: the product is the owner's real one from their listing, only parts it really has may wrap or float (the prompt says so twice), the word is their own, the price is the item's. No number, no 'verified' and no claim is drawn.
- [x] A5.2 No scarcity or urgency unless real, with its dates.
  plan: n/a: none; a deal shows only with its end date (unchanged).
- [x] A5.3 No fake screenshot: no text from a friend, Google review, notes app, receipt or conversation that did not happen. A familiar screen is allowed when nobody invented speaks in it: the product itself calling, a search box, switches, the reader's own list.
  plan: n/a: no screen.
- [x] A5.4 No drawn "@brand" made to be mis-clicked; no as-seen-in; no generated person shown as a customer.
  plan: no people at all ('No hands, no people'), no @brand, no as-seen-in.
- [x] A5.5 A comparison is with a kind of thing, never a named rival, and both sides are true.
  plan: n/a: no comparison.
- [x] A5.7 Every listing photo the ad uses is clean, checked before the layout: cut to its subject with no phone number, email or person's name, no screenshot edges or app icons, no other logo or certificate, no 'before' photo without its 'after'. A photo that cannot be cut clean is left out. The answer lists each photo and its cut, or n/a: the ad uses no listing photos.
  plan: the listing photo is never laid on the ad: it goes to the model as image 1 only, and the prompt allows 'no other words or letters', no hands and no people, so a phone number, a name or a logo on the photo is not carried onto the ad. The owner looks at the picture before using it.
- [x] A5.6 Owner-facing wording does not address "owners"; rentals lead, the business line is "Add your business, free".
  plan: the owner sees 'Wrapped round your word' and 'Write a big word first; the food wraps round it.'; no 'owners'. It is offered only to food kinds. Prices show only in the business's own currency (the item's priceText).

## 6. Phone and shapes

- [x] A8.1 Square 1080x1080, feed 1080x1350, story 1080x1920: each one drawn and looked at, not resized.
  plan: built by AdMaker FORMATS and per-format draw; AdStudio SHAPES post and status; confirm: check-ad-designs measure shapes
- [x] A8.2 No text under 30px at 1080 wide.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (text-size)
- [x] A8.3 Nothing cut off: every line inside the picture.
  plan: built by AdMaker wrap(ctx, text, width, maxLines) and part() with minScale shrink text into its box; confirm: check-ad-designs measure text-inside
- [x] A8.4 A story keeps the top 220px and bottom 230px clear.
  plan: built by AdMaker begin() top 220 and footBand safe 230; AdStudio topOf and footTopOf; confirm: check-ad-designs measure story-safe
- [x] A8.5 Words over a photo cover at most 40% of it, and never the subject.
  plan: the big word is part of the picture, made round it; the eyebrow and lead sit in the top the prompt keeps empty and the price and link in the empty bottom; no word sits on the food except the one it wraps.
- [x] A8.6 The QR is at least 150px at 1080 wide, dark on white, with the short link printed whole on the ad (the business's name in it when it has a Latin one), beside it unless the owner moved them. No drag can shrink it below 150px, cut it off or put it where a story covers it.
  plan: built by AdMaker QR floor of 150, qrTile white tile, linkPiece and linkLines print the whole link, part() minScale 150/QR on drag; confirm: check-ad-designs measure qr
