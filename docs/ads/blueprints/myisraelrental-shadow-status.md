# Ad blueprint: myisraelrental-shadow-status

Every ad rule, in making order (docs/ad-rules-checklist.md). Answer a stage before making it; tick [x] once made.

## 1. The idea

- [ ] A0.1 One job: what you get, or a question, or a real offer. Never two.
  plan: a real offer: add your business, free, and reach more customers than your own street brings. One job; rentals, orders, texts and ads stay out.
- [ ] A0.2 One-second test: a stranger knows what is offered and where. Clarity beats cleverness.
  plan: one small shop on an empty pale ground, its shadow a tall tower with lit windows, and the words 'more customers' and 'free': a stranger gets 'a free way for my small business to reach like a big one' in a second.
- [ ] A9g.1 Starts from an ad that already works: name the ad in docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02 (or Meta's Ad Library, by reach and days running) it takes its idea from. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design; A9g.11 says what must have been looked at first.
  plan: Pinterest pin f88747 saved 24 Nov 2026 in docs/inspo/pinterest-business-ad-2026-11-24/f88747f194c265aeedae8182f2a7ae43.jpg (a small shop casts the shadow of a tower, 'small business, big dreams'), with the library 5 it matches: docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02/creative 2c0115 (a cat walks in a door and a tiger walks out: the small thing and its big self, alone on an empty ground).
- [ ] A9e.2 In one sentence: what the picture says that the words finish. Cover the words: the picture raises a question. Cover the picture: the words are not complete.
  plan: cover the words: why does a little shop cast a tower's shadow? Cover the picture: 'Reach more customers' does not say how far. Together: add your business and it reaches as far as a big one.
- [ ] A9e.3 The idea, by name: the product stands in for a familiar object, or sits in a familiar screen, or type and subject interlock, or surreal scale, or a pun. Not a good photo with a headline (scored 1.9 to 2.8).
  plan: surreal scale as a visual pun: the shop's shadow is not its own but a tower's, many times its size.
- [ ] A9f.1 The angle, by name: a real moment and why ours wins there, or the exact reader, or an honest trade-off, or the want behind the feature, or a moment in the day, or a real review.
  plan: the want behind the feature: to be bigger than your own street and reach people who never walk past, not 'a listing page'.
- [ ] A9g.11 Ideas come from references opened, not summaries: before any idea is written, at least five library ads (docs/inspo/pinterest-ads-2026-11-02, by their six-character codes) are opened at full size, and one Pinterest search is made for this ad (or its series) and saved under docs/inspo/pinterest-<subject>-<date>/ with its README. The answer names the folder and the five codes. Applies to every hand-made ad and new design from 25 Nov 2026; with A9g.1, the reference it names is one of these.
  plan: docs/inspo/pinterest-business-ad-2026-11-24 (two Pinterest searches, 47 pins; f88747 and 16731f opened at full size, the rest on sheet-0.jpg and sheet-1.jpg). Library ads opened at full size before any idea: 2c0115 (cat in, tiger out: the small thing and its big self, nothing else on the ground), a10f1e (elephant foot over an ant: scale alone tells it, with the word set big under it), 0d757a (search bar as a bridge between 'your business' and 'your customers'), 622672 (binoculars through the page: an almost empty white ground makes one object a 5), 0a54ab (a tap pouring over a skyline: too many small lines round a good idea, so ours keeps one line), 1e6716 (Enter key as a door: a tiny figure and its long shadow give the scale), c38023 (a man on a ladder of search lines: a soft shadow on a pale ground makes it feel real).
- [ ] A9g.9 Is this a different-looking ad from the last ones for this business, and not the same design with another headline?
  plan: yes: 'Be found.' is a full golden-hour street with a round glass in the middle, and the map pin ad is a dark dusk stacked top to bottom; this one is almost empty, a pale wall and floor in hard morning light, the shop small and low on the right, its shadow running left and up the wall, type at the top left. Earlier MyIsraelRental ads are navy or wood desks with a phone as the stage.
- [ ] A9g.10 The gallery of one business looks varied at a glance: its designs do not share one colour or one layout, and each starts on its own product where there are several.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (variety)
- [ ] A27.1 The site's brand: for a business with a custom site, name its scrollcraft/builds/<folder>/BRAND.md and say which of its colours and fonts this ad uses and which of its bans it keeps; or "n/a: no custom site".
  plan: n/a: no custom site; our own brand (brand/design-tokens.css, theme-flow.css): accent #167AB8, Playfair Display headline and Manrope body, as on the series' first ad 'Be found.'.
- [ ] A28.1 The look is chosen before anything is built: one cheap sample still of the same moment in each look that fits the idea (drawn flat, 3D animated film, real photo), shown to Tzvi side by side; the answer names the look he chose and the sample sheet.
  plan: 3D animated, Tzvi's pick on 24 Nov 2026 for the whole series, from the magnifier's sheet docs/ads/gen/myisraelrental-magnifier-status/out/looks-sheet.jpg (real photo, 3D animated, flat drawing); this ad is made in the same look, so no new samples are paid for.
- [ ] A28.2 A product rebuilt in a generated scene keeps its own label: the real label or sticker, cut from their photo, is laid over the rebuilt one on the still ad and on frames where it holds still. The answer names the label file, or n/a: the product is their own photo.
  plan: n/a: no product with a label; the shop is generic and its sign is blank.

## 2. The words

- [ ] A2.1 The headline is the biggest words on the ad and stops the scroll on its own; it is what the eye lands on first (squint test).
  plan: 'Reach more customers.' is the largest type (Playfair Display, about 170px on two lines, 'more' in accent blue, the rest ink), top left on the pale wall, the first thing the eye lands on; the tower shadow's top floors and mast fall across the foot of 'customers.'.
- [ ] A2.2 At most about 8 words, never cut with a "…"; 2 to 6 is best. Rewrite, never shrink.
  plan: three words.
- [ ] A2.3 Says who it is for or where, plus what it is, most concrete fact first. Their situation, never a boast.
  plan: says what it gets them, most concrete first (more customers), and the small line above it names the offer and the price: 'Add your business, free.' Their situation, no boast.
- [ ] A2.4 Says it the way a person would, in the reader's words; from a real review when there is one.
  plan: spoken words, what every small business says it wants; it is the site's own standing line ('reach more customers', CLAUDE.md).
- [ ] A2.5 What it gets them, or the want behind it, not just the name again. A twisted known phrase or the reader's own world beats a description.
  plan: what it gets them, not the feature: more customers, with the tower shadow showing how much further a small shop can reach.
- [ ] A2.6 Words a ten-year-old follows, one idea a sentence. No power words, no "even if", no made-up mechanism.
  plan: one idea, words a child follows, no power words, no made-up mechanism.
- [ ] A2.7 Copy rules: no long dash, no exclamation in the headline, no praise the listing never states.
  plan: measured by check-ad-designs (words)
- [ ] A2.8 Hebrew speaks in the plural (אתם), never to one man or one woman.
  plan: Hebrew in the plural, written for Hebrew and not word for word: 'תגיעו ליותר לקוחות.' with 'מוסיפים את העסק, בחינם.' above it.
- [ ] A2.9 The caption: who or where, what it is and one or two facts, one plain invitation. No hashtags, no signature.
  plan: caption: 'Cleaners, caterers, movers, guides and shops: add your business to MyIsraelRental, free, and reach more customers. Your own page, found in search, no listing fee and no commission. myisraelrental.com/businesses/add'
- [ ] A2.10 Short, intriguing and clear: the headline with its one small line says what the business gives and why you would want it, in plain words a stranger gets in one look (the product or service named, or unmistakable in the picture, plus what it does for them). A pun or a twist only on top of that, never instead of it: 'Set it to LOW.' told nobody what L.A. Cholent sells. The answer writes 'A stranger reads:' and what someone who has never heard of the business would take from the headline alone.
  plan: A stranger reads: there is a free way for my small business to reach more customers.
- [ ] A2.11 Five headlines, then cut: write at least five headlines, numbered 1) to 5), and strike each that fails one of these, naming the failure: (a) names the object without saying why you want it ('THE RING.'); (b) guesses at the reader's situation instead of the offer ('Ready to ask?'); (c) is a pun or twist only someone who already knows the business gets ('Set it to LOW.'); (d) sounds like something else, a film, a song or another brand's slogan; (e) claims what the listing does not; (f) is more than six words or needs its small line to make sense. Write 'Chosen: N' and why. When Tzvi is in the conversation, show him the best three before the ad is built and build his pick.
  plan: 1) Reach more customers. 2) ~~Small business. Big dreams.~~ (d) the reference pin's own line, and (b) it talks of dreams, not the offer. 3) Small shop. Big reach. 4) ~~Cast a longer shadow.~~ (a) names the picture without saying what you get. 5) Bigger than your street. 6) ~~Look like the big guys.~~ (e) the site gives a page and customers, it does not make anyone look big. 7) ~~Be big.~~ (f) needs its small line to mean anything. Best three to show Tzvi: 1, 3, 5. Chosen: 1) Reach more customers. Three words, the offer in the site's own standing words, true of every kind of business and not only shops; the picture brings the surprise, and the small line above it says what and the price: 'Add your business, free.'

## 3. The picture

- [ ] A3.1 One huge subject on one quiet ground: it fills half the frame or more, two or three colours in all.
  plan: one subject, the small shop and the tower shadow it casts, together about 78% of the width and half the height, on one quiet pale ground: off-white, a blue-grey shadow tinted to our blue, and the shop's warm colour with an accent blue awning.
- [ ] A3.2 A rental or a place: only the owner's real photos. A generated room does not exist. Say where each picture comes from.
  plan: no rental and no real business's place is shown: one generic generated shop with a blank sign on an empty studio ground (no street, no flag, no landmark, and the tower is no building anyone knows); nothing claims it is a business on the site.
- [ ] A3.3 A generated picture of a product keeps the real product: rebuilt around its real photo, its traits unchanged, compared beside that photo. Describe the reference scene in full, never "swap the product".
  plan: n/a: no product of a business is generated.
- [ ] A3.4 Another shape than the screen: the background is extended, never cropped or zoomed.
  plan: the picture is made in the story's own 9:16 shape; feed and square get their own picture or more plain wall and floor, never a crop of the shop or its shadow.
- [ ] A3.5 Sharp and bright; one colour that stands out on a white and a dark feed, from our palette or the owner's own photo and logo.
  plan: sharp and bright; the shop's accent blue #167AB8 awning and warm walls stand out on a white feed, and the pale ground stands out on a dark one.
- [ ] A3.6 A hand holding, offering or reaching in, where it helps. Real or native beats polished.
  plan: n/a: the shop stands alone as in the reference; a hand would make it a toy and break the shadow.
- [ ] A3.7 No words drawn into a generated picture. The picture is one layer, the words typed on top and editable.
  plan: no words in the generated picture: every sign, window and awning is blank, no flag, no letters of any language; every word is typed on top in the page.
- [ ] A3.8 Another brand's idea and composition only, never its marks, wording or product.
  plan: the pin's idea and composition only: our shop, our colours and a 9:16 wall in place of its square; none of its words, its 'Small Business' sign or its marks.
- [ ] A3.9 The picture fills the ad edge to edge as it was made: made in the ad's own shape or the nearest shorter one the model takes, laid at full width with scripts/ad-fit-picture.py, which carries on only the plain wall above and table below in the picture's own colours and refuses to shrink. Never shrunk with filled or pale sides. The answer names ad-fit-picture, or n/a when no picture is generated.
  plan: the picture is made at 9:16 and laid at full width with scripts/ad-fit-picture.py; never shrunk.
- [ ] A3.10 A product shot is styled like a pro shoot: the product big and sharp on a rich surface (wood, stone, linen, never a bare white table), in warm shaped light, with two or three things from the business's own world at the sides (what it is served with, made from or sold beside). Props never in front of the product, never presented as the product, no cooked food passed off as theirs. Name each prop and why it belongs.
  plan: n/a: not a product shot.
- [ ] A3.11 Layout first, never numbers alone: before any generated picture, its layout is drawn with scripts/ad-layout-sketch.py (boxes where the product, props and moment go; the headline and foot areas kept empty) and given to the reference-image model (marketing-studio/image/sunburst) as an image, with "photograph this layout for real" and the real product photo when there is one. Told only in words, the model centred the phone and drew eight notes for thirty. Exact parts (a screen, a label) are laid on afterwards in code. The answer names the layout picture; the ad's gen script must use it. n/a only when nothing is generated.
  plan: docs/ads/gen/myisraelrental-shadow-status/layout.png (from layout.json, drawn with scripts/ad-layout-sketch.py): headline area kept empty from 220px to 575px; a pale wall down to y 66% and a pale floor below it (#e9eef4); the shop x 52% to 90%, y 55% to 73.5%; its shadow on the floor from the shop's foot left to the wall, x 14% to 56%, y 66% to 73.5%; the tower shadow standing up the wall x 12% to 50%, y 31.5% to 66%, mast to 30.5%, in blue-grey #8ea0b8 with paler window squares; the foot's band (77.5% to 87.5%) and the bottom 230px kept as empty floor. gen.py gives it to marketing-studio/image/sunburst with 'photograph this layout for real'.
- [ ] A3.12 A screen in an ad shows the real thing: our website or dashboard captured at the screen's own shape from the local test account (its sample data, never a real business or customer, no test-looking names), not an icon or a made-up mock. Professional is in the details. Name the page captured.
  plan: n/a: no screen in the ad.

  plan: n/a: no price.
- [ ] A4.4 A review is real, shown on the public page, rated 4 or 5, the reviewer's words, shortened only by cutting. No hearts or counts.
  plan: n/a: no review.
- [ ] A4.5 Press or partner logos only if the business really has them, four at most.

- [ ] A4.6 The business's own logo is on every ad it pays for or is shown in: their real logo file (the listing's logo, or cut cleanly out of a picture of theirs), never redrawn, retyped or generated, placed where it reads at phone size and clear of the phone's top bar (from 100px down on a 1920 status). The name in words may sit beside it, never in place of it. The answer names the logo file used; n/a only with "n/a: no logo" when the business has none, and then the name is set in their own style. ## 9. The pro look (Tzvi, 8 Nov 2026: "use the new things you learnt... make it a rule for all future ad builds")

- [ ] A11.1 One idea you get in a second: name the surprise, pun or visual question the picture asks; the words answer it. "A good photo with a headline" is not an idea.
  plan: the visual question: why does a tiny shop cast the shadow of a tower? The words answer it: reach more customers.
- [ ] A11.2 Words and picture interlock: the headline or one huge word sits behind the subject with parts of the subject in front, or the subject sits inside the letters. Name how. When the subject is generated and crosses the words, the overlap is planned in code from the start: the layout sketch keeps the subject clear of the headline, and the part in front is cut from the picture and laid over the words (docs/ads/gen/myisraelrental-magnifier-status/rim.py), never paid for again to move the subject.
  plan: planned in code from the start: layout.png keeps the shadow clear of the headline area; in ad.html the headline is set so the foot of 'customers.' reaches the tower shadow's top floors and mast, and shadow.py (as rim.py did for the glass) cuts that part of the shadow from the picture and lays it over the words as a darkening layer, so the words read as painted on the wall with the shadow falling across them. 'more' stays clear of the shadow. Nothing is paid for again to move the shop.
- [ ] A11.3 One colour world: the ground takes the product's own colour or its set's, with one accent; no stock yellow sticker or white button that belongs to no picture.
  plan: one colour world: an off-white wall and floor, the shadow in a blue-grey tinted to our accent, the accent blue on the awning and the word 'more', the shop's warm walls as the one accent; no sticker or button that belongs to no picture.
- [ ] A11.4 A frozen moment: something is happening (falling seeds, steam, a pour, a splash, motion). Name it, and keep it true to the product.
  plan: the moment the morning sun reaches the shop: its door open and awning out, light in its window, and its shadow standing up the wall as a tower with every window lit.
- [ ] A11.5 Light that shapes: one strong side or back light, a fall to dark, real depth. Never flat even daylight on the hero.
  plan: one hard low sun from the front right, so the shop is lit on one side and falls to shade on the other, and the long shadow gives the depth; no flat even light.
- [ ] A11.6 Three things carry the ad: the picture, the headline, the name. The link, code and price are small and quiet, and corners are left empty.
  plan: the picture, 'Reach more customers.' and the name carry it; the link and QR sit small in the foot, the corners and most of the wall empty.
- [ ] A11.7 The headline is 2 to 5 spoken words, set big, in two voices where it helps (bold capitals with a lighter or script line).
  plan: 'Reach more customers.', three spoken words, set big in ink Playfair with 'more' in accent blue, and the lighter italic line 'Add your business, free.' above it.
- [ ] A11.8 The product is the hero: it fills about half the width or more, close, standing on its set like a product shoot, never pasted in front of one.
  plan: the shop is about 38% of the width on purpose, because being small is the idea; with its shadow, made in the same picture, it spans about 78% of the width and half the height, standing on its own ground, not pasted.
- [ ] A11.9 Its own look: the ground is the business's own colour (from its logo, product or place, named), and the layout is one none of its earlier ads and none of our last five ads (docs/ads) used. List those ads and say how this one differs. The foot is the logo, one line, and the link with its QR, never a block of small lines.
  plan: ground: an off-white studio wall with shadow in our own blue (the brand's colour, greyed). Earlier MyIsraelRental ads (docs/ads/myisraelrental-*: answer-less, automation, burst, inbox, list, press, shop, split, moving) are navy or wood desks, a phone as the stage, tiny workers, sticky notes, a paper tear, an orbit of photos; 'Be found.' is a full golden-hour street and the map pin ad a dark dusk. None is an almost empty pale ground with one small object and its shadow on a diagonal. Foot: the logo, one line, the link and the QR.
- [ ] A11.10 The product takes another thing's place: name the familiar object whose place it takes (food in a ring box, tubs wrapped as a gift) and what the viewer works out. A paid ad without one says why.
  plan: a tower's shadow takes the place of the shop's own shadow; the viewer works out that the small business, once people can find it, reaches like a big one.
- [ ] A11.11 A hand or a person acts on the product (lifts, offers, sprays, bites): a real hand, a drawn figure or the owner's own photo, never a generated customer (A5.4). Name who does what, or n/a: why the ad is stronger without.
  plan: n/a: the shop stands alone as in the reference; a hand or a figure would give away the scale and break the shadow's clean shape.
