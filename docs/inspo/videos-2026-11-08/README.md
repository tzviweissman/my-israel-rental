## What Bramble does, step by step (frame-verified)

1. **Opening.** A blueberry branch on a deep plum ground breaks in from the top-right edge. Beside it is the
   headline "Good things grow *wild.*", with the one italic word in lime. There is a lime pill button. A tiny
   capitals line sits above the headline ("A JUICE BAR WITH A WILD SIDE"). Two tiny corner labels sit at the bottom:
   "SCROLL TO FOLLOW THE FRUIT" on the left and "01 — ROOTED IN THE GOOD STUFF" on the right.
2. **On scroll the berries come off the branch and fall.** The branch slides away. The headline does not vanish: it
   dims to about a third and fades out as the berries pass it.
3. **Strawberries and raspberries follow and fall into purple juice with a splash.** The corner label changes to
   "02 — LET THE GOOD STUFF FALL". The bottom-left label becomes "SKIP TO THE BLENDS".
4. **"A little wild. A lot of *berry.*"** The berries rest in the juice.
5. **The camera tips to look straight down, then dives into the swirl.**
6. **The bottle tumbles out of the swirl and lands upright.** Its label and cap are the same lime as the button.
7. **"Wildness, *bottled.*"** appears with the bottle and a button ("Meet your new favourite"). Corner label: "05 — WILDNESS, BOTTLED".
8. **A lime ticker strip ("GOOD FRUIT. ✱ GOOD MOOD. ✱ A LITTLE WILD.") carries the page from the dark film to a cream section.**
9. **"Big fruit energy."** Tabs for the three flavours sit under it (Wild Berry, Sun Chaser, Green Day). The bottle
   sits on a plum tile, with the facts beside it in small capitals ("THE FEELING: Rich + tangy").
10. **Back to plum with leaves: "Keep it fruity. Keep it *joyful.*"**
11. **The phone gets its own upright film with the same story.** It is not the laptop film cropped.

## Why it feels polished

- **Three colours, nothing else:** plum, lime and cream. The product itself (the label and cap) is in the site's
  accent colour, so the bottle, the buttons and the ticker look like one brand.
- **The film is shot on the page's own colour.** Every scene sits on the same plum, so the film and the page are one
  surface, with no box and no edge.
- **The opening still is the first frame of the film.** The branch in the opening is what moves when you scroll, so
  nothing gets swapped out.
- **The object breaks the edge of the screen** (the branch comes in from outside), so it feels bigger than the screen.
- **The same headline shape every time:** two short lines, one italic accent word in lime, and a tiny capitals line above.
  That repeats on every screen, and that sameness reads as design.
- **Small editorial corner labels:** a numbered chapter name bottom right, and "skip to the blends" bottom left. The
  visitor always knows where they are and can leave the film.
- **Colour changes happen on a strip** (the lime ticker), never on a hard edge.
- **Words dim and fade while the film moves.** They are never cut.
- **Real gravity:** the fruit speeds up as it falls, splashes, and the bottle tumbles and settles.

## The other sites, one line each

- **Veyr watch:** the watch comes apart into its parts, the camera goes into the gears, then it pulls back to the
  watch **on a wrist**. The film ends with the thing being yours (Bart's point).
- **Alto pizza:** dough thrown in the air, the pizza made and baked, then it **drops into a box** ready to take home.
  Same idea.
- **Offgrid bike:** the bike stands **in front of** the huge words "TAKE THE LONG WAY" and the photo is behind them.
  The layers shift slightly with the mouse. Nate's point: layering adds depth without being too much.
- **Stillpine cabin:** a film walks you through the cabin's front door. Then comes a booking card beside "Everything
  for your kind of escape". The photo collage shows the same place from different angles. The last video fades into
  the reviews column.

## Compared with our sites

Our **Fruit Platter** is already close to Bramble: one strong colour ground, the fruit drawn on it, and the board
filling up. The Brown Box, American Crust and Elisheva open on a photo with words in the corner (see the 7 Nov notes).

Bramble has these; most of our live sites do not (checked in the 25 published pages):

| Bramble does | Ours |
|---|---|
| A product or accent in the site's colour on buttons, ticker and label | Our buttons are mostly white or black, not tied to the product's colour |
| Numbered chapter labels and a "skip" link during the film | None of our sites |
| A ticker strip as the hinge between dark and light | Chicken Munch and Brown Box have a ticker |
| The same headline shape on every screen (one italic accent word) | It varies within a site |
| The object breaks the screen's edge in the opening | Usually the whole object sits inside the frame |
| Its own phone film | Some of ours ship separate phone pictures (`-m` files); not checked film by film |
| A film that ends with the customer having it (in the box, on the wrist, bottled) | Our 3h.2 ends on the product and the button, which is close |

## Options for a new site (not rules)

1. **Three colours and a matching product colour** (the button part became rule 3g.19 on 8 Nov 2026): pick plum/lime/cream-style trios from the business's own
   product or packaging, and use the product's colour for the buttons and the ticker.
2. **Chapter labels and a skip link** in the film's bottom corners (tiny capitals, numbered).
3. **One headline shape** repeated on every screen: two short lines, one italic accent word, a tiny capitals line above.
4. **Opening object breaking in from the edge**, and it is the thing that moves first on scroll.
5. **A ticker strip** where the page changes from the film's colour to the light section.
6. **A phone film of its own**, upright, with the same story.
7. **End the film with it being theirs:** in the box, on the table, in the hand.
8. **Words in front of and behind the object** (Offgrid). We already ask for overlap in 3i.4.
