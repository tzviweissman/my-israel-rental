# Pinterest and our own inspo: how the meat should arrive (Burnt Meatboards), 23 Nov 2026

Why: Tzvi says the raw cuts "randomly floating down" in the Burnt opening film do not look good, and wants a real wow for how the meat arrives or is revealed.

Searches run (Pinterest, public result pages, 736 px pictures; most pins are short videos and the picture is one frame): "steak reveal cinematic", "meat commercial motion cinematic", "steak slicing slow motion dark", "butcher board food styling dark moody", "bbq advertising smoke fire meat reveal", "smoke clearing reveal steak on wood board dark", "charcuterie board reveal". Not run: "food product reveal animation" (the commercial search already returned that genre, all generated).
Pin link: https://www.pinterest.com/pin/ID/ . A pin shows one frame, so the "wow moment" below is the move the pin shows or implies; judge the motion on the source pin.

Main finding: the Pinterest "meat commercial" results are almost all generated meat floating in the air. That is exactly the look Tzvi rejected, so they are kept only as two negative examples (09, 10). The real wow moments all have a visible CAUSE: a blade, smoke, fire, hands.

## Pins that work

- 01.jpg. Pin 157274211994289150, search "steak reveal cinematic". Four thick slices of a rare steak stacked on black, side lit, a board edge glowing below. Wow: the steak is revealed by being cut, the slices shown open and pink. Beats falling meat: the cut is the cause, the inside is the reward, nothing is in the air.
- 02.jpg. Pin 1077134435864938098 (0:04 video), searches "steak reveal cinematic" and "steak slicing slow motion dark". A big blade, motion blurred, pushes down through a seared steak on a board, a fork braced beside it. Wow: one knife stroke is the whole event. For Burnt: a knife or cleaver enters frame, one stroke, the cut meat falls open onto the walnut. Beats falling meat: real cause, one event, believable at once.
- 03.jpg. Pin 920915823821541546 (0:18 video), search "bbq advertising smoke fire meat reveal". A seared piece fills the frame inside a wall of flame, the flame lighting the crust. Wow: the meat is found inside the fire as the flame sweeps past. Matches our fire-first opening: the fire that burns the mark is the fire the meat comes out of.
- 04.jpg. Pin 917186280352308475, search "smoke clearing reveal steak on wood board dark". A sliced rib roast on a dark worn board, smoke rising behind, one side light. Wow: smoke clears and the board is already loaded. Same dark wood and side light as our gen/fire/hero-bare-noiron.png, so it reads as our board with meat on it.
- 05.jpg. Pin 1140255199403525837, same search. A charred joint with three slices cut away on a pale board, one thread of smoke. Wow: the burn and the cut together (black bark outside, red inside). A finished "settled" frame the film can end on.
- 06.jpg. Pin 26880929019363997, same search. A scored steak on a wooden board with tomatoes and herbs, smoke curling behind. Wow: warm smoke backlight over a board set like real service, the arrival of a finished board.
- 07.jpg and 08.jpg. Pins 660903314106637540 and 1056094181385329335 (0:12 and 0:15 videos; which id is which picture was not recorded), search "charcuterie board reveal". One is an empty round plate with the first piece being placed; the other an overhead of hands laying a board item by item. Wow: top-down build, placed by hands in order, the board fills. Believable (one cause, hands) and it sells the craft. Light backgrounds, so for us it needs the dark walnut ground.

## Negative examples (do not copy)

- 09.jpg. Pin 1005710160546420006 (0:05), search "meat commercial motion cinematic". Cubes of meat, olives and tomatoes in a column against red, sauce strings in the air. Generated floating assembly: no cause, nothing lands. This is the "randomly floating" Tzvi dislikes.
- 10.jpg. Pin 95631192083203476, search "steak reveal cinematic". A steak hung in mid air over flames with rosemary and sparks. A generated hero shot with no hand, no board, no cause.

## Ideas from our own inspo that fit (paths under docs/inspo/)

1. videos-2026-11-01/ai-food-realism.md and real-food-filming.md: the generator does rigid objects falling and hand-to-food contact well and fails at mid-air assembly ("can do rigid falling objects... fails on mid-air assembly"). Real filming: one simple action per shot, a rim light that also picks out smoke. Smoke only as thin threads, never a wall; a smoke wipe reads as a hidden cut (fake-floating-smoke-wipe.jpg is the failure). So the meat can be set down by hands or cut by a blade, and must not assemble in the air.
2. videos-2026-11-01/real-hands-one-event.jpg and bar-breaking-2026-11-02/README.md: hands cause it, one slow event (rice-treat-pull-apart.jpg, hands-break-soft-bar.jpg). The start frame already shows the hands or blade at the board.
3. bar-breaking-2026-11-02/ai-stretch-held-apart.jpg: the liked AI clip works because the two halves are already apart and held still while the camera pushes in. Same trick: show the already cut meat and push in; do not generate the cutting.
4. pinterest-burnt-2026-11-18/ (iron-lifted-beside-its-mark-walnut-wisp.jpg, glowing-iron-face-pressed-by-hand.jpg, thin-smoke-wisps-backlit-black.jpg): the iron lifted off its mark with one wisp is the hand-over from burn to board. The same hot iron could press a sear mark into a cut as the second beat.
5. pinterest-burnt-2026-11-16/ (hirschen-metzg-hero-knife-over-steak.jpg, steak-doneness-callouts-dark.jpg, board-numbered-callouts-overhead.jpg, dark-slate-board-overhead-long.jpg): a knife over one steak, stacked slices with labels (like 01 here), an overhead of the finished board with numbered names. Fits a top-down finish with callouts.
6. product-films-2026-10-21/README.md (platter-build-3 to 25.jpg, Olipop can film): an overhead build by hands that "sells the craft", and a product that clears and lands as a clean packshot. A generated person must not be shown as the owner's staff.
7. videos-ruben-stom-2026-11-22/README.md: the light reveal (line 89 and 94: near black, a warm band of light sweeps across and uncovers the table, camera locked, only the light moves) is a physically honest reveal: fire light sweeps the dark board and the meat is already there. Also the split-open intro (line 34, pure code) and "wipe, not push" where the panel rises and the image does not move (line 423); both are free, code-only.
8. videos-2026-11-22/README.md and videos-ruben-stom README line 132: write the motion with numbers (seconds, scale, stagger), which is what makes a reveal work.
9. awwwards-food-2026-10-18/ (crav-burgers-2-burger-inside-title, burrito-madre-2-type-behind-photo): the real food cut out and sitting across the giant letters. For Burnt: the meat across and in front of BURNT, a still plus a code move, not generated motion.
10. Inspo MCP (one recommend, one search): only generic dark marquee sites (Beard Meats Food, Death and Company, Dinner by Heston). Nothing with a meat arrival reveal, so no get_screen was spent. Beard Meats Food is a loud neon brutalist site, wrong for this film.
