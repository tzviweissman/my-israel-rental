# Pinterest: pasta posters with the food wrapped round the word (25 Nov 2026)

Tzvi's own screenshots from a Pinterest search for "pasta ads design", sent while the restaurants ad was being
remade ("can you see why this is better than ours? something about ours just looks bland"). The search itself would
not load for us that day, so these two are kept as he sent them.

- **pasta-word.jpg** "PASTA": tagliatelle knotted through a heavy condensed word, shading the letters, a bowl below,
  warm cream ground, small print set like a magazine.
- **twist-word.jpg** "TWIST": a fork at the top lifts tagliatelle that wraps the word, in front of some letters and
  behind others, herbs and pepper in the air, a diagonal from the fork, an italic note and a price.

What we took (ad rules A3.13 to A3.15, A9.9): the picture made round our own typed word, food on a warm ground, the
food's texture and life, a diagonal, magazine small print. Used for the restaurants ad and the ad maker's
"Wrapped round your word" picture (docs/ads/blueprints/design-shoot-wrap.md).
