What they all share and hers lacked:

3. Two grounds taking turns, cream and chocolate (hers: six).
4. The headline beside the product, nothing over the photo (hers: a cream panel over half the challah).
5. One headline size with a small label above (hers: 36, 36, 76 and 52 px).
