# Shops: fashion, luxury, e-commerce (Awwwards Sites of the Day, anatomy study, 22 Oct 2026)

Captured at 1440x900 and 390x844, home page plus one shop or booking page. Contact sheets: `<tag>-sheet.webp` (ec = e-commerce, fa = fashion, lx = luxury). Best single screens are the files without "sheet".

Sites studied (all marked SOTD on Awwwards): Aardvark Book Club, Vero New-York, Serotoninn, Decathlon Yestalgia (e-commerce); Outfit, Odd Ritual, Balmoral, Wolverine Worldwide (fashion); Odyssee Clinic, Tengile MalaMala, White Desert, Sobha Privy (luxury).

Skipped: Zeroz (maintenance page), Brunello Cucinelli (access denied), Cartier (loader only), Meech213 (a photographer's portfolio), Detroit Paris (an AI studio, not a shop), Indigo (a sound-gate "enter the experience" wall). Sobha showed only 3 screens (very long page that did not scroll in the robot).

## Anatomy per site

**Aardvark Book Club (book subscription).** Loud yellow, pink and turquoise grounds that change every section, hand-drawn blobs, one chunky rounded sans (huge, mixed case), stickers. Sequence: hero (big headline, floating book) / this month's books on a shelf of real covers / "how it works" as 4 colour cards / an open 3D box on pale blue / genres as a word list / reasons as stickers / FAQ / gift / members' choice / shop pages. Photos: real book covers and cut-out box art, no lifestyle photos. Button: small red pill in the top bar and hero. Dense in props, light in text. Trust: FAQ, shipping line, sale ribbon, awards.

**Vero New-York (custom wedding-dress sculpture).** Warm off-white throughout, broken by full photo screens (orange studio, grey studio). One serif with italic accent words, capitals for key words. Sequence: film hero / one big sentence on white / white dress on a stand, full height / "where innovation meets craftsmanship" / full-bleed studio photos / three-image collage with a named client quote / gallery of rooms with the sculpture / product page: photo left, "from" price, three tabs (description, process, care), one button "Start your commission". Airy, little text. Trust: named quote, credits, a real starting price.

**Serotoninn (Berlin fashion).** White ground, giant black condensed capitals, one red accent, torn-paper edges and a lips shape. Sequence: hero (model cut-out and "New drop") / new arrivals: 3 cut-out models on grey / campaign film / categories as a row of standing cut-outs with vertical words / "dresses" and "corsets" halves / mission line / film in a lips shape / dark footer film with newsletter. Shop page: 4-across grid of full-body cut-outs on white, name and price under each, filters on top. Dense, loud type, quiet colour.

**Decathlon Yestalgia (retro sports campaign).** Pink and lilac ground, neon-yellow buttons, 80s shapes, one condensed capital sans. Sequence: shapes opening / "by your side since 1976" / big title / product tiles: three items with price and colour dots / photos of people wearing them / illustrator collage / grandparents carousel / feature "OG2K". Products sit as small cards inside a story, not a catalogue.

**Outfit (small apparel store, Uruguay).** Beige ground, one red, one grotesque sans, giant red word "OUTFIT" as the hero. Sequence: name and a row of 4 product photos / grid of products with name and price below (3 to 4 across) / product page: big photo left, name, price, quantity, button. Almost no text. The whole home page is the shop. Footer: tagline "Made to be worn. Or judged. Or both." and links.

**Odd Ritual (golf apparel).** White, with big full-bleed photos between rows; small sans for UI, one big serif line ("More than just golf"). Sequence: lifestyle photo hero with "Shop now" / short brand paragraph / 4 featured products on white / three story tiles (products, story, community) / photo breaks / community partners / footer index. A newsletter pop-up with a photo on first visit. Shop page: 3-across grid, sold-out badge, price with crossed-out old price.

**Balmoral (running wear, Canada).** Black ground, big photo or film, one light serif for the name, tiny sans menu. Sequence: film hero "New arrivals" / "Shop all" strip over a photo / product grid with studio photos on grey, name and price in tiny type. Cookie and country boxes sit in front.

**Wolverine Worldwide (corporate shoe group).** Not a shop; a contrast. Black and white alternate, huge bold sans headlines, drifting photo tiles, share-price numbers, news row.

**Odyssee Clinic (medical aesthetics).** Warm cream throughout, soft grey, one thin serif. Sequence: skin close-ups as hero / one personal paragraph with a portrait / "why Odyssee" with droplet images / philosophy / treatments as image pairs / contact and hours / pearl closing film. "Book consultation" sits in the top bar all the way down. Very airy, small body text.

**Tengile MalaMala (safari lodges).** Cream ground, deep green accents, a light serif at huge size with tiny labels. Sequence: film / story paragraph / three lodge photos with names / quote / full-bleed wildlife / history / community portraits / green footer. Booking page: date picker and enquiry form together. Photos are about 70% of the page.

**White Desert (Antarctica trips).** White and ice-blue, giant light serif capitals over film, orange side tab "Enquire". Sequence: film / one sentence / photo / "our trips" slider / camps as cards over ice film / trip page with overview, itinerary, history tabs. Cookie bar at the bottom.

**Sobha Privy (Dubai homes).** Black opening film then white; thin capital serif with italic accents; one dark opening then a white gallery of three images.

## Category patterns

**E-commerce (4 sites).** Only Vero keeps one calm ground. The other three give each brand its own loud world (Aardvark yellow, pink and turquoise; Serotoninn black, red and white; Decathlon pink and yellow), and colour changes per section or per product, not between dark and white. Products are cut-outs or covers on a flat colour, in a grid with name and price under each, or inside a story with price and colour dots. The main button is small, one colour, near the top, and repeats at the product. All four use one condensed or chunky sans for huge, often capital, headlines. Text is short; a "how it works" strip or FAQ carries the explaining. Versus Moto Card: no full-bleed photo and white alternation, except Vero.

**Fashion (4 sites).** 3 of 4 (Outfit, Odd Ritual, Serotoninn) are white or beige throughout, with the product grid on or just under the first screen. Only Balmoral is dark. One accent colour at most. Photography is mixed: studio cut-outs on grey for the grid, one or two lifestyle photos for mood. Grids are 3 to 4 across, name and price in small type under each item, sold-out badges, filters. The brand paragraph is short, then back to products. Versus Moto Card: the product, not a film, is the hero, and photo breaks are used sparingly between product rows.

**Luxury (4 sites).** Warm cream or white ground throughout, thin serif at large size with tiny labels, film or photo hero. Photos are 60 to 70% of the page and show moments (skin, lodges, ice), not products. One paragraph per section. One quiet booking or enquire button, fixed in the top bar or a side tab, plus a booking page with dates or a form. Closest to Moto Card but lighter: cream instead of black, and photos sit inside the cream rather than filling alternating screens.

## Mapping to our businesses

**RS Modest Activewear (one shop, Jerusalem).** From Outfit, Odd Ritual and Balmoral: the shop grid on the first screen below one lifestyle photo, 3 to 4 across, each item with name and price underneath. Real photos of the skirts on a plain grey or sand ground for the grid, plus two lifestyle photos of women running or training (mood, not catalogue). Sizes, price and an in-stock or sold-out badge visible in the grid. One accent colour, small black button. A bar with the shop address and opening hours before the footer (a real-shop detail the online-only sites do not need).

**Nails by Elisheva (nail studio).** From Odyssee: warm cream ground, thin serif, close-up photos of hands, "Book" fixed in the top bar all the way down, treatments as image pairs with "from" price and duration. Booking page like Tengile: date picker and a short form on one page. A short personal paragraph with her portrait. Small text, airy page.

**Michal Simkin (wig salon and academy).** Two audiences, so two doors, "Book a fitting" and "Courses", both visible. From Vero: one big sentence, a client quote with a name, a product page with a "from" price, tabs for description, process and care, and one button. From Odyssee: a portrait of Michal beside her philosophy. Wigs as cut-outs on a plain ground in a grid (Serotoninn), plus two lifestyle photos. The academy as its own row with dates and price.

**The Gift Boutique (gift boxes and Judaica).** From Aardvark and Outfit: the box as the hero (cut-out on a flat colour), a 3 to 4 card "how it works" strip (choose, personalise, we deliver), a grid of boxes with price under each, and a gift-card or occasion row. Each occasion (Shabbat, wedding, new baby) can have its own colour room. A FAQ with delivery and returns; a sale ribbon only if a real price drop exists.

## Limits
Awwwards Sites of the Day skew toward brand experiences, so several are campaigns, not shops. The inner page per site is the first matching link the robot found (Decathlon's was legal notices; Wolverine's a report page), so product-page claims rest on Vero, Serotoninn, Outfit, Odd Ritual and Tengile only. Screens are what loaded in a robot browser; pop-ups were closed by script.
