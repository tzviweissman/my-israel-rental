# Food and restaurant site anatomy, 22 Oct 2026

How real sites in "Food & Drink" and "Hotel & Restaurant" on Awwwards are built (page layout, not effects).
Captured at 1440x900 and 390x844, plus the first two screens of one shop/menu/booking page each.
Each `<site>.webp` is a contact sheet: desktop screens in reading order, then the inner page, then the phone row.

## Sample and its limits
- The Awwwards filter `?award=sotd` does not filter. Only a handful of true Sites of the Day exist per category, so
  I took the SOTDs available (Tengile MalaMala 3 Oct, White Desert 11 Sep) and filled with the newest awarded real businesses.
- Skipped: Santioni Spirits (SOTD, 5 Oct: only a black loader with a logo, never opens), Fix Chocolatier (Cloudflare robot check).
  Replaced with Agrumea Farm and Best Bean Best Cup. La Joya needed a long wait (green splash first).
- Delta and Best Bean put a cookie bar / 15% popup over the shots; the layout underneath is still readable.
- Food and drink: Theobroma (chocolate), Delta Cafes (coffee), La Joya (tinned gourmet food), Agrumea Farm (jars), Best Bean Best Cup (coffee).
- Hotel and restaurant: Delice (Quebec restaurant), Qissa (Indian restaurant, Kent), Le Saint Georges (hotel and restaurant, Switzerland),
  Tengile MalaMala (safari lodges, SOTD), White Desert (Antarctica camps, SOTD).

## Anatomy per site

**Theobroma (chocolate shop).** Hero: product flat-lay photo with a loud headline and a "just in" product card. Then: short pitch on cream, "Our products" filter pills,
a product carousel on a purple ground, a dark brown "fairtrade / real ingredients" band, best sellers (3 cards with Add to cart), a cacao photo, a brown story band with the founder photo,
customer quotes on bright green tiles, "15% off your first order" email box, Instagram strip, giant name footer.
Type: one heavy chunky capital sans, big. Colour: dark brown, cream, lime green, plus a new bright colour per band (purple, blue). Product shown as cut-out wrappers on plain cards; prices and Add to cart sit on every card, quantity +/- too. Packed, playful, shop first.

**Delta Cafes (coffee roaster).** Dark film hero, then a row of coloured coffee bags, heritage photo collage, "journey of coffee" brown and green bands, coffee-personality quiz, a scattered field of bags on cream, journal, sustainability band, footer. Products page: bags on cream, grouped by origin. Type: condensed tall capitals for headlines plus a light sans. Colour: brown, cream, green, with each bag's own colour. Story-heavy, company-sized, few prices on the home page.

**La Joya (gourmet tins).** Opens on a flat acid green splash with a giant thin serif LAJOYA and two tins. Then a yellow "Our gourmet products" line and a mosaic of tin photos (each tin in a gloved hand on a blue or grey ground) mixed with plated-food photos; scrolling banner of product names; chef-endorsement rows; mixed ground (green, blue, yellow, red). Shop page: green ground, product cards with price and a small button. Type: thin elegant serif for the name, plain sans for the rest. Loud, 3 to 4 flat colours, picture heavy.

**Agrumea Farm (jars).** One screen for a long time: cream ground, six jars floating and tilting, each with its own label colour, a dark red arch with curved text "we grow, preserve and process" under them. Product page: huge red serif product name, a jar, short taste line, ingredients and allergens in small type. Type: one light serif, capitals. Colour: cream plus one deep red; the label colours come from the jars. Airy, almost no text.

**Best Bean Best Cup (coffee).** Yellow ground, headline in plain sans, then a stack of full-width coloured product panels (blue, yellow, green, teal, pink, orange); every panel is one coffee: its bag on its colour, name, flavour notes, a plus button. Giant blocky "PRODUCTS" and "BUNDLES" words between. Shop: white grid, bag cut-outs, price "from $x". 15% popup on arrival. Colour changes per product, not per section.

**Delice (restaurant).** Red hero with a wavy shape and a tilted plate photo; sections each get their own flat colour (red, teal, olive, navy, cream, forest green, maroon). Menu items are round plate photos on a ring, arched headline over them ("Plaisirs du jour"), with a name, one line and a price rule ("half-price wine"). Big circles hold facts (1970, 160 staff, 300 seats). Events list as cards with date. Menu page: sticky tabs (pizza, mains, desserts...) then a plain text list. Reserve and Order buttons in the bar. Type: light serif headings, small sans. Loud, 6 colours, but all from one family.

**Qissa (restaurant).** Dark navy throughout, gold small caps and gold button. Hero is the shopfront photo with name; sections: meaning of the name, a signature-dish plate on dark, a gold strip of words (Taste, Craft, Gathering), menu category list, reservations photo, signature dishes with dish name and one line, star rating 4.8 and counts, interior photos, a reviews quote, "two ways to taste": reserve or order online, giant faint wordmark, opening hours and address footer. Menu page: dark text list with name, short description and price in two columns. Main button: "Reserve a table" gold pill, top right, always there. One colour, serif plus small sans, restrained.

**Le Saint Georges (hotel and restaurant).** White throughout, brown buttons. Hero: building photo, "Reserve" button. Sections are text blocks on white with one photo each: rooms, restaurant, meetings, weddings, offers (slider), the village (with a hand-drawn map), a fondue chef photo, Instagram, footer with address, phone and trust badges. Rooms page: one card per room with size, amenities and Book. Type: small serif, a lot of white. Practical, trust first, phone and address always visible.

**Tengile MalaMala (SOTD).** Cream ground, wide serif, nature photos in loose rows, quote, a green band, enquiry form with dates. Type light serif, quiet. Colour: cream and olive green only.

**White Desert (SOTD).** White and ice blue, one orange "Enquire" tab on the edge, big serif over huge photos, trips with price in dollars listed in the picture ("US $115,500"). Camps shown one per screen over ice. Dates and prices page. Luxury, very airy.

## Category patterns

### Food and drink (Theobroma, Delta, La Joya, Agrumea, Best Bean)
- 5 of 5 are bright or warm, not dark. Only Delta's hero is dark and it returns to cream. None uses the Moto Card pattern of black full-bleed photo against white.
- 4 of 5 change colour with the product (each bag, jar or tin brings its own colour); Agrumea and Best Bean show it most clearly. Section grounds are one or two colours.
- 5 of 5 show the product itself as a clean cut-out or studio shot on a flat ground; real food in a lifestyle photo is used as a second layer (La Joya, Theobroma's story band).
- 4 of 5 have an actual way to buy near the top (Add to cart on the card, Shop in the bar). Prices sit on the card, not hidden.
- Type is one loud family, often heavy capitals; a single thin serif is the other style (La Joya, Agrumea).
- A story band (founder, farm, heritage) appears in 4 of 5, always mid page. Email discount or a popup appears in 2.
- Real-business signs: ingredients and allergens in small type, quantity and weight on the card, an Instagram strip, customer quotes, a big name in the footer.
- Versus Moto Card: lighter, more colourful, more shop and less cinema; picture share is moderate to high but the pictures are of the packed product, not an atmosphere.

### Hotel and restaurant (Delice, Qissa, Le Saint Georges, Tengile, White Desert)
- Two families: moody (Qissa dark navy and gold; Tengile and White Desert quiet luxury) and warm and friendly (Delice flat colours; Saint Georges white and brown). 3 of 5 keep one ground for the whole page.
- Photography is atmosphere: rooms, dishes in a restaurant setting, the people. The dish photo is round or framed, rarely full bleed, except at the hero.
- The main button is the same everywhere: "Reserve" or "Enquire" in the top bar (5 of 5), repeated at the end of sections. Menus are text lists with prices (Qissa, Delice); rooms and trips show price or "from".
- Trust is concrete: opening hours, address, phone, rating (4.8, counts), badges, a map, staff or chef photo.
- Text amount is higher than food shops (a paragraph per section) and serifs dominate (4 of 5).
- Versus Moto Card: Qissa is the only one near it (dark, photo against dark), and even Qissa keeps text and menu lists, not alternating full-bleed photo and white.

## Map to our businesses

**The Whipped Cup (whipped coffee jars).** Take from Agrumea and Best Bean: jar as the hero, one flavour per colour so each jar owns a colour and the page changes colour per flavour; keep the label design as the colour source. Price and size on the flavour card with a buy button. A short, plain "what is in it" line like Agrumea's ingredients. A clear way to order for gifts or events.

**Bun Intended (cinnamon buns).** Take from Theobroma and Delice: warm cream ground, loud friendly one-family headline, round or cut-out bun photos with name, one line and price, a "box of 4 or 6" card row with Add. A story band with the baker. Instagram strip, weight and allergens in small type.

**Lechem Emek (bakery).** Take from Le Saint Georges and Delice: trust first. Opening hours, address, phone and map above the fold. A menu as a text list with prices grouped by type (breads, pastries, cakes) and an order or "call" button. One photo per section on a calm ground; the baker and the oven as the story.

**Chicken Munch (chicken boxes and pans).** Take from Theobroma, Best Bean and La Joya: loud flat colours, a panel per box or pan on its own colour, big blocky name words, cut-out box photo, price on the card and the order button always visible. Keep the sizes (box, pan) as a row of cards, not a paragraph.

**L.A. Cholent (cholent kits).** Take from La Joya and Agrumea: the kit as the product on a flat colour; ingredients and allergens in small type like Agrumea; a "what you get" row; story of the recipe in a mid page band; Shabbat ordering days and pickup or delivery shown like opening hours (a restaurant-style trust block).

**The Brown Box (acai bowls and gift boxes).** Take from Best Bean and Theobroma: one panel per box with its own colour, cut-out of the box, price, and what is inside as short lines; gift-box occasions as cards (Qissa-style "two ways": order now or ask for a custom box); customer quotes on bright tiles.

**LS Fruit Platter (fruit platters).** Take from Delice and Best Bean: round platter photos as a size row (small to extra large) with servings and price on each; one calm ground with a fresh accent; occasion cards (birthday, event) with an order button; a clear delivery area and lead time block.

**Blazin' Boards (meat boards).** Take from Qissa and Delice: darker warm ground is allowed here because the category supports it (Qissa dark with gold); boards as round or framed photos with name, one line, who it serves and price; a text menu list for add-ons; reserve-style "order for your date" button in the top bar; reviews with a star rating and count.

Shared for all eight: a visible way to buy from the first screen, price on each product card, a trust block (hours, area, phone, rating from real customers only), and a story band with the owner's real photo. Claims like "fresh daily" go in only when the owner said so.
