pictures showing some other products near the coffee cup, the jellyfish is pulsing like a real jellyfish ... look
through pinterest ... in different industries and website styles". 21 video pins found by searching scroll and
animation websites for skincare, jewellery, drinks, food, furniture, real estate, travel, perfume and tech; downloaded

What they share: the product is never just placed; it is handled (a lid lifts, a tube turns, a grinder opens), it is
part of the words (a can as a letter), it lives (a jellyfish pulses, a flower opens, shadows of leaves move), and its
world is around it (fruit, aloe, moss, the other products). Added to the small-touches menu, rule 3bb.
