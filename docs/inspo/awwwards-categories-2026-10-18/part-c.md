## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Hero photo dissolves into a second room** (schlosshotel-kitzbuhel-1-sauna-crossfade)
The outside photo of the hotel fades into a warm interior shot while the logo and the Book and Enquire buttons stay fixed. A round badge with a seasonal offer floats at the bottom left.
Build: both (generated or real stills, a slow cross-fade in code)
Suits: hotel, rental apartment, restaurant, spa, tour guide.

**2. Close-up opening** (odyssee-2-close-up-opening)
The first screen is one huge face crop, with the page text sliding over the bottom edge as you scroll. There is no stock hero, just one confident close-up.
Build: code, with one strong photo
Suits: photographer, salon, bakery (a single food close-up), cleaner (a gleaming surface).

### Scroll scenes

**3. Pinned numbered timeline** (woonpact-1-numbered-timeline)
A heading stays still while a big number counts 01 to 05 and the text and drawing beside it swap. It explains a process in steps without a long page.
Build: code
Suits: mover, cleaner, caterer ("how it works"), tutor, rental apartment (booking steps).

**4. Stepped list with a photo that follows** (mejuma-tuscany-1-stepped-list)
Five words in a column, the active one turns dark while the others fade, and a photo shifts beside it.
Build: code
Suits: tour guide, mover, renovator, photographer.

**5. Full-bleed project panels** (lenval-properties-1-full-bleed-projects)
Each project is one photo that takes the whole screen with a small label, then the next slides over it. Only dark background, warm photos.
Build: code
Suits: rental apartment, hotel rooms, photographer, caterer menus.

**6. Colour block per service** (omni-1-colour-block-sections)
Each service gets a full-width block in its own colour and bold pattern, so the page reads like a stack of posters.
Build: code with simple shapes
Suits: cleaner, tutor, gift shop, handyman, any business with 3 to 5 services.

### Showing products or services

**7. Floating product card over a real photo** (armor-1-floating-product-card)
A shop photo scrolls slowly while a small white card with the product and a button rides over it. It places the product in real use.
Build: both (a generated or real scene, card in code)
Suits: gift shop, bakery, restaurant, any shop with one hero product.

**8. Dark glow grid of four** (armor-2-glow-grid)
After the light page the screen turns dark with an orange glow, and four numbered cells show line drawings of the products.
Build: code with a blurred gradient
Suits: caterer packages, cleaner packages, tutor levels.

**9. Illustrated topic cards in a swipe row** (kohen-avocats-1-topic-cards)
Each service is a tall pastel card with one object (a leaf, a flower, a vase) and the service name on a pill.
Build: generated (one object per service) plus code
Suits: tutor, cleaner, photographer, gift shop.

### Galleries

**10. Overlapping portrait collage** (odyssee-1-portrait-collage)
Photos of different sizes overlap and slide past each other at different speeds with a short line of text between. It feels edited rather than gridded.
Build: code
Suits: photographer, salon, hotel, restaurant.

**11. Wall of tilted tiles** (duda-works-1-tile-wall)
A field of small tilted squares in bright colours fills the screen, each a different photo or drawing, and they nudge near the pointer.
Build: code
Suits: gift shop, bakery, photographer, caterer (a wall of dishes).

### Type

**12. Giant statement line** (mejuma-tuscany-2-big-statement)
One light serif sentence at a very large size, centred, with plenty of empty space. It says the promise in one breath.
Build: code
Suits: any business, especially rental apartment, tour guide, hotel.

### Footer and close

**13. Giant brand footer** (mejuma-tuscany-3-giant-footer)
The brand name fills the width of the page at the bottom in huge type, with links and contact small above it.
Build: code
Suits: any business with a short name.

## Best five

1. Hero photo dissolves into a second room (hotel, rental): shows outside then inside with no click.
2. Pinned numbered timeline: explains how booking works in five steps.
3. Floating product card over a real photo: puts a product in real life.
4. Overlapping portrait collage: makes a plain photo set feel designed.
5. Giant brand footer: a cheap, memorable close for every page.
