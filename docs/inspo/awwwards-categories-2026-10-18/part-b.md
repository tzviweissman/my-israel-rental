## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Photo cycle behind one line** (offforum-1-photo-cycle-hero)
Full-screen event photos fade one after another while one centred line of text and a ticket button stay put. It feels busy and alive without heavy video.
Build: code (stacked images, timed fade, text fixed on top).
Suits: restaurant, caterer, tour guide, photographer, event hall, rental apartment.

**2. Split two-product opening** (deadly-ponies-1-split-product-hero)
The first screen is cut in two halves, each a different product photo with a small label at the bottom. Scrolling brings one large product at a time.
Build: code (two columns, each a link).
Suits: gift shop, bakery with two lines (cakes and breads), a cleaner offering home and office.

**3. Texture film behind a short headline** (preymaker-1-texture-film-hero)
A dark close-up film of feathers fills the screen and shifts as you scroll, with only one small headline. The material sets the mood, the words stay short.
Build: both (a short close-up loop of your own product, generated, plus code for scroll scrub).
Suits: bakery, caterer, florist, jeweller, cleaner (fabric or surface close-ups), photographer.

**4. Headline words slide apart over a film** (tengile-malamala-1-split-word-headline)
Two big words start stacked then slide to opposite corners as a river film comes in beneath them. A single button waits at the bottom.
Build: both (film generated, word slide in code).
Suits: tour guide, rental apartment, guesthouse, restaurant with a view.

### Scroll scenes

**5. Paragraph that spreads and snaps back** (the-design-society-1-spread-word-reveal)
On a flat blue screen a paragraph starts with words flung far apart and pulls into a clean block as you scroll. It makes people read the whole story.
Build: code (per-word offsets tied to scroll).
Suits: any business with a founder story: tutor, bakery, mover, cleaner, photographer.

**6. One object, many materials** (switchblade-1-material-morphing-object)
A single 3D star turns from chrome to glass to moss to metal while words appear beside it. One object carries the whole page.
Build: both (stills or a loop of your own product in different finishes, generated, swapped by scroll in code).
Suits: gift shop, bakery (one cake, many flavours), jeweller, caterer.

**7. Numbered steps with a turning object** (3d-discovery-1-numbered-3d-objects)
A big number 01, 02, 03, 04 sits beside one small turning object and one short title, so each step of the service gets a full screen.
Build: both (objects generated as short loops, number swap in code).
Suits: cleaner, mover, tutor, photographer (how it works, step by step).

**8. Pink statement with pictures sliding through** (psp-production-1-pink-statement-images)
A huge bold statement on hot pink with small photos sliding across and behind the words, mixing serif italic with heavy sans.
Build: code (colour block, text, moving thumbnails).
Suits: caterer, bakery, event business, photographer.

### Showing products or services

**9. Year cards strip** (tedxportland-1-year-card-strip)
Tall coloured cards, one per year, sit in a row; the active one grows and a click opens a video in a dark overlay.
Build: code (accordion cards, video modal).
Suits: restaurant seasonal menus, photographer yearly albums, tour guide seasons, mover or cleaner past jobs.

**10. Pill rows for shows or services** (aya-1-pill-show-list)
Each offer is a fat rounded pill with a title, an age or price chip and a round button, stacked on a flat saturated page. It reads like a menu and is easy to tap on a phone.
Build: code.
Suits: cleaner (service list), tutor (courses), tour guide (tours), mover (packages), restaurant.

**11. Project list with a poster on top** (east-64-films-1-poster-list-index)
A small table of projects, and the picked project shows a single image with its title below. The list stays calm while the picture changes.
Build: code.
Suits: photographer, caterer (past events), cleaner or mover (before and after jobs).

**12. Case-study slides with big numbers** (pier-gorelli-1-case-study-slides)
A large 01 of 03 counter, a short paragraph and one image per slide, with a thin progress line.
Build: code.
Suits: photographer, tutor, mover (three proof stories), rental apartment (three rooms).

### Galleries

**13. Horizontal project strip** (death-to-robots-1-horizontal-project-strip)
The page scrolls sideways through large project photos, each with a tiny name below, with plenty of dark space around each image.
Build: code (pinned section, horizontal translate).
Suits: photographer, restaurant dishes, bakery, rental apartment rooms, gift shop.

**14. Scattered photo cluster** (tengile-malamala-2-scattered-photo-cluster)
Small photos are placed loosely around a centred line of text, like prints on a table, and drift gently as you scroll.
Build: code.
Suits: restaurant, bakery, tour guide, guesthouse, photographer.

### Navigation and menus

**15. Hover highlight list** (stefano-girardelli-1-hover-pill-list)
A list of names turns one row into a bright pink full-width bar on hover, with a thumbnail appearing in the bar. It doubles as the menu of work.
Build: code.
Suits: restaurant menu sections, tutor subjects, cleaner services, photographer galleries.

### Mouse and hover

**16. Sticker tags around a headline** (studio-ping-pong-1-sticker-tag-headline)
Small label stickers sit on and around a big headline and move when the pointer comes close.
Build: code (absolute tags, pointer proximity).
Suits: bakery, gift shop, caterer, tutor.

### Type

**17. Rotating word in the headline** (stefano-girardelli-2-rotating-headline-word)
One word in the first sentence swaps every second ("I design interfaces / brands / games") in a bright colour.
Build: code.
Suits: any business with several jobs: cleaner (homes, offices, windows), mover, tutor, photographer.

**18. Object cutting through the headline** (easyseller-1-object-through-headline)
A large photo drops through a one-line serif headline so letters sit in front of and behind it.
Build: both (photo cut out, layered in code).
Suits: gift shop, bakery, florist, jeweller.

### Transitions

**19. Pink screen close** (death-to-robots-2-pink-close)
After dark sections, the page ends on a bright pink screen with a short line and an email. The colour jump tells you the page is over and now it is your turn.
Build: code.
Suits: any small business as the final contact screen.

### Forms

None worth borrowing. The pop-ups seen on several sites (newsletter, welcome) hid the page and are not recommended.

### Footer and close

**20. Giant wordmark footer** (offforum-2-giant-wordmark-footer)
The footer is the name of the business set so big it fills the width, with small links above it.
Build: code (text scaled to width).
Suits: every small business.

**21. Playful footnotes footer** (studio-ping-pong-2-footnotes-footer)
The page ends on a cheerful set of small objects (a record, photos, a thank-you note) with contact lines, under a script title.
Build: both (small objects drawn or generated).
Suits: bakery, gift shop, tutor, photographer, caterer.

**22. White "say hi" close** (psp-production-2-white-say-hi-close)
After a dark section the page flips to white with a huge friendly line next to a photo and the contact details.
Build: code.
Suits: cleaner, mover, tutor, rental apartment.

## Best five

1. Pill rows for services (aya-1): the clearest, most tappable way to list what a business does.
2. Giant wordmark footer (offforum-2): cheap, memorable, works on every business.
3. Texture film behind a short headline (preymaker-1): a close-up of our own product gives mood with almost no words.
4. Numbered steps with a turning object (3d-discovery-1): turns "how it works" into a full-screen story.
5. Paragraph that spreads and snaps back (the-design-society-1): makes people actually read the founder story.
