## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Full-screen case slider** (thirdway-1-fullscreen-case-slider)
Each project takes the whole screen with its name large on the photo and a short client quote on the right. Small dots step through them. One strong photo and one real sentence do the selling.
Build: code, with real photos and quotes.
Suits: photographer, cleaner, mover, rental apartment, caterer, any business with finished jobs.

**2. Thumbnail strip as the menu** (dontmatter-1-thumbnail-strip-nav)
A row of tiny pictures runs along the bottom while the hero swaps between full-screen scenes. Clicking a thumbnail jumps to that piece, so the page is its own menu.
Build: code.
Suits: restaurant (one thumbnail per dish), tour guide (one per stop), gift shop, bakery.

### Scroll scenes

**3. Torn section edges** (pigeons-mission-2-wavy-dividers)
Sections change colour with a zig-zag edge instead of a straight line, and small label stickers sit above each heading. It feels hand made and friendly.
Build: code (CSS clip-path).
Suits: bakery, kids activities, gift shop, tutor.

**4. Angled cut edges** (rogers-obrien-1-angled-cut-edges)
Dark and light bands meet on a slanted cut, with a numbers row below (staff, years in business). Scrolling feels like moving through layers.
Build: code (clip-path).
Suits: mover, cleaner, handyman, any firm with proof numbers.

**5. One illustrated town** (townhouse-1-isometric-town)
A small isometric picture of a town changes with each numbered section, the same world seen from new angles. One illustration style carries the whole story.
Build: generated pictures, code to swap them on scroll.
Suits: rental apartment (neighbourhood), tour guide, restaurant, cleaner.

### Showing products or services

**6. Services as priced rows** (kota-studio-1-service-price-rows)
Each service is a row: name, short line, "from $3,000", a small start button, and a picture on the right that changes as you scroll. Prices are visible up front.
Build: code.
Suits: cleaner, mover, photographer, tutor, caterer.

**7. Service rows with a small photo** (bello-2-services-rows-with-image)
Thin ruled rows list each service with a small photo tucked in the corner and details in tiny type. Quiet and easy to scan.
Build: code.
Suits: cleaner, tutor, photographer, handyman.

**8. Audience tiles with photos** (rogers-obrien-2-audience-tile-grid)
A grid of photo tiles titled by who you serve, each with a short label. A visitor finds their own case quickly.
Build: code with photos.
Suits: mover (home, office), cleaner, caterer (weddings, offices), rental apartment (families, students).

**9. Project card carousel** (mk-vision-studio-1-project-card-carousel)
Phone and desktop mockups of finished work slide sideways as large cards, and the last card says "see more".
Build: code, horizontal scroll.
Suits: photographer, bakery (cake gallery), gift shop.

### Galleries

**10. Loose case study grid with a dark close** (twks-1-particle-footer)
Work sits in an uneven grid of full-colour images with small captions, like a wall of posters. The page ends on black with drifting particles.
Build: code for the grid, generated clip or canvas for the particles.
Suits: gift shop, bakery, photographer, restaurant.

**11. Pastel testimonial cards** (eemonroy-1-pastel-testimonial-cards)
Colour-wash cards, each with a face, name and a few warm lines, laid out like sticky notes. Friendly proof instead of a plain quote block.
Build: code, a gradient per card.
Suits: tutor, cleaner, tour guide, bakery.

### Mouse and hover

**12. Hover preview follows the cursor** (mike-wilcock-1-hover-preview-list)
A plain list of names; hovering one fades the others and floats a picture of it beside the cursor with a round "view" dot. The list stays light and the picture appears on demand.
Build: code (mouse follow).
Suits: restaurant menu (dish preview), tour guide, rental apartment list, photographer.

### Type

**13. Cut-up giant letters** (creative-reference-library-1-cutup-giant-type)
Black slabs of oversize letters fill the screen and re-cut themselves, with small text sitting over them. Loud, best as a one-off opening.
Build: code or generated clip.
Suits: gift shop, bakery, event caterer.

### Transitions

**14. Pixel-block dissolve** (joshgrafik-1-pixel-dissolve)
Between sections a grid of black and grey squares flickers out and in like a screen change, followed by plain large type.
Build: code (canvas or CSS grid).
Suits: tutor, photographer, any studio.

### Forms

**15. Contact panel over a blurred page** (dontmatter-2-contact-panel-over-blur)
A white panel opens over the blurred site with name, email, tap chips for service and budget, a message and a black send button. The visitor never leaves the page.
Build: code.
Suits: cleaner, mover, photographer, caterer, tutor.

**16. Click to copy the email** (joshgrafik-2-copy-email-cta)
After a big "Let's talk" line, the email address is set huge and underlined; clicking copies it.
Build: code.
Suits: any small business that prefers email.

### Footer and close

**17. Giant sideways wordmark footer** (thirdway-2-giant-wordmark-footer)
A dark footer with the name set huge and turned sideways, contact details and links beside it.
Build: code.
Suits: restaurant, rental apartment, any brand with a short name.

**18. Giant red wordmark** (bello-1-giant-red-footer)
The page ends on a full-width red block with three large links and the brand name overflowing the bottom edge.
Build: code.
Suits: bakery, gift shop, restaurant.

## Best five

1. Full-screen case slider with a client quote (effect 1): one photo and one real sentence per job, easy for any trade.
2. Services as priced rows (effect 6): visible "from" prices and a start button convert quickly.
3. Contact panel over a blurred page (effect 15): choose service and size without leaving the page.
4. Hover preview follows the cursor (effect 12): menu, tours or listings that show a picture on hover.
5. Audience tiles with photos (effect 8): visitors pick the kind of job that matches them.
