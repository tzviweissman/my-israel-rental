# Awwwards, every category, 18 Oct 2026

Tzvi asked for inspiration from every Awwwards category. The top 5 sites from
each of the 26 subject categories and the top 4 from 12 style categories
(scrolling, transitions, storytelling, microinteractions, parallax, typography,
3d, gallery, header, footer, forms) were captured at 1440x900 with
`scripts/inspo-shoot.mjs` and read frame by frame: 170 sites, 161 effects, one
picture each. Sites that showed only a loader, a robot check or a cookie wall
are listed as not studied in each part.

The site list is `../awwwards-2026-10-18b-sites.txt` (lines 63 to 232).
