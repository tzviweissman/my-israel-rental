## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Giant wordmark that fills the screen** (undplus-1-giant-wordmark)
The business name is set wider than the screen and slides sideways, with one short line under it. A strong first impression with only type.
Build: code. Huge heading, slow sideways move.
Suits: bakery, caterer, gift shop, photographer, any bold name.

**2. Wordmark plus a mascot** (stash-1-wordmark-mascot)
A very wide wordmark with a little character moving in front of it. It feels friendly at once.
Build: both. Code for the type, a generated short clip of the character.
Suits: bakery, kids business, tutor, pet shop.

**3. Huge white name on black, then the offer** (montarville-1-huge-wordmark)
The name fills the top, a one line promise sits under it and a photo slides up beside it.
Build: code.
Suits: mover, contractor, cleaner, any trade.

**4. Film opening with a character** (atmospherik-1-robot-opening)
A dark cinematic scene fades in, a character walks into view, then a short headline appears over it.
Build: generated. Make the clip, add type in code.
Suits: tour guide, restaurant, event caterer.

**5. Hero object on a dark sky** (astro-1-planet-hero)
One large glowing object, wide spaced serif name and two buttons. Calm and premium.
Build: both. Generated hero image, code for the type.
Suits: gift shop, photographer, tour guide, jeweller.

**6. Pixel type opening** (1minus1-1-pixel-word-opening)
Two bright pixel words that change, on purple, with a short line below.
Build: code.
Suits: tutor, kids business, event planner.

### Scroll scenes

**7. Image windows in soft shapes** (belgrade-arbor-1-shaped-image-windows)
Photos sit in rounded, odd shaped frames that drift as you scroll while the text stays calm.
Build: code. CSS masks and a slow scroll offset.

**8. Huge words that change, chapter by chapter** (belgrade-arbor-2-big-type-chapter)
A large headline reveals line by line, then a new one takes over, each chapter on its own colour.
Build: code.
Suits: rental apartment, restaurant, tour guide.

**9. Word cut by a photo** (belgrade-arbor-3-location-word-over-photo)
A giant section word sits half behind a photo so the picture seems to pass through it.
Build: code. Layered type with the image above.
Suits: rental apartment, tour guide, restaurant.

**10. Story that changes as you scroll** (atmospherik-3-swirl-story)
One moving background and a new line of text for every scroll step, so the page reads like a short film.
Build: both. A generated looping background, code for the steps.
Suits: tour guide, caterer, any business with a story.

**11. Words that turn into particles** (atmospherik-2-particles-words)
Dots gather as a sentence arrives and spread as it leaves.
Build: code.
Suits: cleaner or mover (mess becomes order).

**12. A fixed centre piece while cards change** (orizon-2-orb-coverage-card)
One graphic stays in the middle while small cards appear around it for each point.
Build: code.
Suits: any service business explaining its steps.

## Best five (updated)

1. Tilted colour service cards (1minus1-2-tilted-service-cards): turns a service list into something people remember.
2. Each product its own coloured room (flux-theory-2-coloured-room-product): one master scene per flavour or package, easy to generate.
3. Three arched doors as the menu (april-the-lantern-1-three-arch-doors): one scene that doubles as a three choice menu.
4. Journey along a map (the-ride-to-rio-2-map-story-cards): ideal for tour guides and movers.
5. Hover list that shows a picture (signal-a-studio-1-hover-image-list): works for dishes, services and listings.
