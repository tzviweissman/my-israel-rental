# Subtle touches, 12 Nov 2026

Tzvi: "its these small subtle additions that change a website from good to great". These are small details seen in pictures we saved, checked against `docs/page-generation-rules.md` (including 3aa.15 to 19, which already hold the first batch). All paths are under `docs/inspo/`. Each is an option for new sites, not a rule, until Tzvi picks it.

## Motion

1. **The ground changes colour with the product.** The whole background slides from purple to orange to green as the juice changes, so the colour itself tells you the scroll is moving. Seen in `videos-2026-11-12/chroma-juice-chameleon.jpg`. Not in our rules (3aa.3 asks for one ground; this is one ground that travels).

2. **A thin progress line with a live number on the edge.** A fine line at the right edge fills as you scroll, with a small figure at its top that counts. It shows where you are without a bar across the page. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/mode-tabs-numbers.jpg` (right edge). Not in our rules (rule 3c.2 progress is about steps, not scroll position).

3. **A line that draws itself.** A route traces round a track as you scroll, with three figures under it, so a fact is shown as a journey. Seen in `videos-2026-11-07/f1-site-sections.jpg` ("This season, so far"). Not in our rules.

4. **A graph line climbs beside the counting number.** Two figures count while a stepped line rises between them, with the product ghosted behind. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/count-up-chart.jpg`. Partly: rule 3x.2 covers numbers that count, not the line.

5. **The glow under the product changes with each choice.** Pick a mode and the light on the floor under the bike changes colour as well as the number. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/mode-tabs-numbers.jpg` and `turn-it-over-hotspots.jpg`. Partly: rule 3x.3 covers tabs changing one panel, not the light.

6. **One section flips from light to dark.** The page is pale, then a single section turns black with large white words, which resets the eye. Seen in `videos-2026-11-12/find-real-estate-layout-motion.jpg` (rows 1 to 2). Not in our rules.

## Words

7. **The footer headline fills in.** A big sentence sits in the footer as a faint outline and becomes solid as you reach it, so the last screen has its own small event. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/footer-faded-headline.jpg`. Not in our rules.

8. **Options as big words with an arrow.** "Buy, Sell, Rent" are huge rows, each with a thin arrow, that slide as you scroll past. The choice is read in one second. Seen in `videos-2026-11-12/find-real-estate-layout-motion.jpg` (rows 2 to 3). Not in our rules.

9. **A quote shown as a sound wave.** A customer's sentence sits beside a red wave and a play dot, so it feels spoken. Use only a real recording or leave it out. Seen in `videos-2026-11-07/f1-site-sections.jpg` ("On the radio"). Not in our rules.

10. **Tiny readouts at the edges.** A small vertical label on the left and coordinates at the bottom (depth, place, number) make a site feel made for that world. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/flashlight-hero-sheet.jpg`. Not in our rules (3u covers small unique touches only in general).

23. **Chapter counter in the corner.** Bottom right of each screen a small line reads "02 - Let the good stuff fall", bottom left "Skip to the blends", so the scroll has numbered chapters. Seen in `videos-2026-11-08/bramble-chain.jpg`. Partly: rule 3x asks for chapter labels and the skip link (check-web-basics), not the numbered, named counter.

## Buttons and feedback

11. **A highlight paints behind a price on hover.** Hover a price and a block of colour wipes behind the figure while a ring follows the cursor. The price you are looking at is the one that lights. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/prices-beside-booking.jpg`. Partly: rule 3x.5 covers a light that follows the visitor, not the price highlight.

12. **The cursor becomes a round "View" bubble.** Over a row in a list the pointer turns into a labelled circle and a preview picture appears beside it. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/mike-wilcock-1-hover-preview-list.webp`. Partly: 8a.4 allows one cursor effect per site, so this would be that one.

13. **One signature button shape.** The main button has one clipped corner, the same on the menu button and the form button, so it reads as the brand's own. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/prices-beside-booking.jpg`. Not in our rules (rules say colour and state, not shape).

14. **A mode switch in the menu.** A small Auto, Morning, Day, Evening, Night control changes how the page looks and says "00:05 Night" beside the logo. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/halcyon-lighting-1-lights-off-toggle.webp`. Partly: the rules list "the time of day" as a unique-touch idea (section 9), not as a menu control.

15. **Cookie wording in the brand's voice.** The notice says "Cookies, not umbrellas", with the two buttons the same size. A required box becomes a small smile. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/blunt-umbrellas-1-product-hotspots.webp`. Not in our rules.

24. **A start button as the gate.** One round red button reading "Start engine" sits between the two headline words and the screen waits for a press, so the first action is a small event. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/bmw-f3-1-start-button.webp`. Not in our rules.

## Layout and edges

16. **The product ghosted behind the figures.** The bike sits faint and dark behind the big numbers, so the numbers never float on nothing. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/count-up-chart.jpg`. Not in our rules.

17. **A coloured top edge on the form card.** The booking form is a dark panel with one thin bright line along its top, which pulls the eye to where the action is. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/prices-beside-booking.jpg`. Not in our rules.

18. **A live local time and place in the menu bar.** "Manchester, UK / 21:50" sits centred in the header and ticks. For a local business it says someone is there now (use "Open now" only from real hours). Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/mike-wilcock-1-hover-preview-list.webp`. Not in our rules.

19. **One section peeks over the next.** A strip of products shows its tops above a full-bleed photo section, so the page feels layered, not stacked. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/agnestoth-2-colour-dot-product.webp`. Partly: 3s peek-row covers a cut-off card row, not a section laid over another.

25. **A small tag on the product picture.** A tiny pill ("Rich + tangy") sits in the corner of the bottle picture and the same words repeat in a "The feeling" row. Seen in `videos-2026-11-08/bramble-chain.jpg` (bottom row). Not in our rules.

26. **A still ticker strip as a section divider.** A lime band of short phrases with stars separates the dark film from the pale shop. Seen in `videos-2026-11-08/bramble-chain.jpg`. Partly: 3g.19 colours it; no rule asks for the divider, and the moving version is banned (section 9.4).

27. **Live depth and count readouts.** Bottom right, small grey lines ("Depth 2,840 m, Particles 290,863") sit beside a number made of dots. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/junkbranding-1-particle-counter.webp`. Partly: touch 10 above, with values that change.

28. **A small join card in the corner.** A white card with underlined fields, an arrow "Sign up" and a close mark sits bottom right over the photo, never a full-screen box. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/estudio-niksen-1-shop-the-look.webp`. Not in our rules.

## The shop

20. **Colour versions peek in a row.** The same bag in three colourways sits as a row of cut-off tops at the page edge, so you see there is a choice before you reach it. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/agnestoth-2-colour-dot-product.webp`. Partly: rule 3l covers saying each difference on the item, not showing the colours early.

21. **The next section's picture grows out of the last.** In the coffee site, the shop closes with the cup in the middle and the bags floating round it, so buying feels like the end of the story. Seen in `pinterest-coffee-2026-11-11/pour-play-full-sheet.jpg` (last row). Partly: rule 3aa.11 covers it.

22. **The price sits beside the booking.** Two prices as thin rows on the left and the form on the right, so nobody asks the price after filling in. Seen in `videos-2026-11-04/prices-beside-booking.jpg`. Partly: rule 3j covers showing the price, not this pairing.

29. **Say who models the size.** "Mateo is 5'11 and is wearing size 1" sits under a Shop the look block, with four items each showing price and a Bestseller tag. Seen in `awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/estudio-niksen-1-shop-the-look.webp`. Not in our rules (3l is about differences between items).
