## What theirs do that ours do not (seen in the frames; how many videos show it)

1. **The headline is the picture's biggest thing, set as type to look at** (4 videos). Condensed capitals, often
   each line on its own white label box or with key words reversed out (L'Oreal, socks, the bag set, North
   Face). Ours: a serif or bold headline of ordinary size beside the product.
2. **The product sits among what it is made of or used with** (4). Protein jar with coconut and pineapple on a
   table; perfume in lilac and gooseberries; L'Oreal tube on a ribbon; the bag opened with its contents. Ours:
   a cut-out alone on a flat ground with a shadow.
3. **The action is a button that names the thing** (3). "SHOP PINEAPPLE COCONUT FLAVOUR", a black "SHOP NOW"
   bar, "Book a free trial lesson". Ours: small "See more and order" text beside the QR.
4. **Proof inside the ad** (3). A review bubble with a face and a heart, stars and a named buyer. Ours has a
   Review design, but no other design ever carries a review.
5. **The offer or promise is the headline, with its stake** (3). "Buy 3 pairs, get 3 free", "gone in 15 minutes
   or your money back", "-20% until Sunday". Ours: the price on a sticker, never a deal or a promise.
6. **Labels on the product** (2). Lines and words pointing at the bag's pockets, a diagram of the foot. Ours: none.
7. **A hand holding it, or a person** (3). Needs the owner's own photo or generation; our rules allow a real
   hand only (A3.6).
8. **Words aimed at one person or one problem** (2). "I beg you, if you have any of these symptoms",
   "Can your child solve this?". Text-heavy is fine when the reader is narrow.

What we already match: one product centred on a quiet ground, a round price sticker, the palette taken from the
product, several looks per business, story-box "native" design, question and versus designs.
What we do that they do not, on purpose: the QR code and short link (our ads also go on paper and WhatsApp).

## Free, in code (no paid picture per ad)

- **Label-box headline**: each line on its own box, key word reversed; a choice on the words, every design.
- **Action button**: a solid pill with a verb and the item ("Order the bagels"), the short link and QR beside it.
- **Review chip**: when the business has a real public review, a small bubble with the reviewer's first name and
  stars can sit on any design (A4.4 still applies: real, 4 or 5 stars, their words).
- **Callouts**: two or three thin lines from the cut-out to short facts the listing states.
- **Deal headline**: only when the owner enters a real deal with its end date (A4.3, A5.2).

## Needs a picture made (paid) or the owner's photo

- The product among its ingredients or props, a hand holding it, a person using it, a scene. Our rule A3.3
  allows a generated scene only rebuilt around the real product photo, compared beside it.
