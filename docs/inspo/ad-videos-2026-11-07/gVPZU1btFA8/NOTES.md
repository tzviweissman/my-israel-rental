## The one transferable idea
Don't invent the ad; copy the structure of ads that have run longest with the widest reach in your niche (Meta Ad Library), keep it SIMPLE, and make a few labelled formats per product: (1) callout diagram, (2) review card with real quote, (3) offer card with struck-through price. Then test angles ("reasons to buy"), changing one element at a time.

## Comparison with our ads (american-crust-ads.jpg, six-new-designs-en.jpg)
Ours: concept-led square cards (incoming call, notifications, search bar, switches, checklist, before/after), strong colour grounds, big Hebrew/English headline, brand strip with logo + link at the bottom.
Differences:
- Pros show the product's USE (flat-lay of what goes in it, open bag, features with lines) so the buyer pictures themselves. Ours show a metaphor screen, often not the owner's product.
- Pros put proof in the ad: star rating, a named quote, "Verified buyer". We have none.
- Pros strike a real price ($79 -> $61 "until Sunday") with a "-20% off" pill and a deadline. Our yellow sticker "from NIS 30" has no comparison or deadline.
- Pros end on one big action button ("SHOP NOW", "Swipe to shop", "BOOK A FREE TRIAL LESSON") with a micro-reassurance ("FREE RETURNS"). Our footer is a link + QR, with no verb button.
- Pros run 3 formats per product and test them; ours is one look per product.
- Pros use a question hook ("NEED A BAG FOR WORK?") and a 3-second challenge; ours are mostly statements.
- Pros have a plain white/flat ground, simple, much white space, one product; our grounds are busier.

## What we can adopt in free code-drawn ads
- Review card design: stars + one real quote from the owner + name (owner supplies it; never invent).
- Strike-through price + "-20%" pill + "until <date>" (owner supplies real old/new price; ties into our "show the deal in money" rule).
- Callout diagram with lines/numbers/dimension lines over the cut-out photo, using owner-written feature text (all drawable on canvas).
- Offer card with a question headline, one subhead, a CTA pill ("Order now / Book free") and a micro-reassurance line (free delivery, free quote).
- Lead-magnet CTA for services ("Free quote", "Free first visit") as a button; "same ad, one element changed" variant button (hook A/B) in the maker.
- Hook styles: question, number ("1 bag, 3 outfits"), big single word.
- Resize safety: keep text and product inside a safe zone for feed/story crops.
Needs generation or a photographer: flat-lay "what's inside" compositions, feature close-up inserts, UGC/lifestyle people, flame/frost product dramas, motion clips. For small owners, a phone photo of their own flat-lay is the cheap route.
