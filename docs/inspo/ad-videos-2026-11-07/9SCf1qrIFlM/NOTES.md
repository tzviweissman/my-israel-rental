## The one transferable idea
Do not make more versions of one winning ad. Make many ads that are each a different angle for a different specific person and stage (cold, middle, ready-to-buy), and visibly different from each other. Winners die in weeks; new angles are what keep an account alive.

## Techniques
1. Ads are grouped by funnel stage: top (TOF), middle (MOF), bottom (BOF) -- frame-verified: board-tof-mof-bof-overview.jpg (Figma board, 4 ads in each of three boxes).
2. Bottom ads are plain offers. Examples: "THIS DEAL IS INSANE" with "BUY 3 PAIRS, GET 3 FREE" (second line has the word FREE in yellow); "WAREHOUSE SALE / BUY 3 PAIRS, GET 3 FREE" on a blue-grey banner; "ANNUAL SPRING SALE UP TO 50% OFF" on a small red-orange tag. Frame-verified: bof-insane-deal-and-warehouse-sale.jpg, bof-ugc-sale-pile-and-spring-sale.jpg. Huge white condensed headline over a dark photo; brand logo small, centred above the headline; offer line at the bottom.
3. Benefit checklist panel on the product photo: three white rounded rows, each a bold one-word title ("BREATHABLE", "SOFT", "FITS") with a tiny fabric swatch and a 3-word explanation; headline words get a white highlight box ("COMPRESSION SOCKS"). Frame-verified: same file.
4. Middle ads explain the product: a cream "diagram" ad with labelled arrows (cutaway, zones, "firmest at ankle"), headline "SWOLLEN ANKLES MEAN FLUID IS STUCK. HOLLOW MOVES IT UP." with the brand word in dark red. Frame-verified: mof-diagram-ad-and-runner-ad.jpg.
5. Lifestyle photo with a circular close-up inset: runner photo, headline "STOP HOT SPOTS BEFORE THEY FORM" right-aligned over empty sky, round inset of the sock texture with curved text around it. Frame-verified: same file.
6. Very narrow audience, written as a list of symptoms: "I BEG YOU, START WEARING THESE COMPRESSION SOCKS IF YOU HAVE ANY OF THESE PLANTAR FASCIITIS SYMPTOMS", five bullets, week-by-week promise, "TREAT IT NOW!". Dark green ground, yellow first words, anatomy pictures. Frame-verified: mof-symptom-list-and-hot-vs-cool-split.jpg. Narration: "if you don't have this, you will not connect with the brand, which is perfect."
7. Split-screen comparison with a problem headline: orange hot side vs blue cool side, cheap sock vs theirs, thermometer-gun hands, text card "100 degree heat and edema? YOUR NYLON SOCKS MAKE IT WORSE." Frame-verified: mof-symptom-list-and-hot-vs-cool-split.jpg.
8. Top ads show a real problem and a before/after: UGC-style photo collage of real swollen legs with a dark-blue headline band "THIS IS WHAT GRADUATED COMPRESSION DOES TO LEGS WITH EDEMA"; and an engraving/cartoon style illustration of heart, legs and red cells. Frame-verified: tof-illustrated-legs-and-ugc-collage.jpg. Narration: AI used "as a style, like this cartoon style", not only people and backgrounds.
9. Visual variety is deliberate: each ad has a different ground colour (cream, green, orange/blue, black, forest photo), type style and image type. Frame-verified across the board overview.
10. Cadence: new angles every week; refresh before the winner dies. Narration only.
11. Spend is concentrated: 3-5 ads carry an account (one $25,000, then $12,000, $12,000, $9,000, $7,000 in 30 days of about $200,000). Frame-verified as an ad-manager table: meta-account-spend-concentration.jpg ($196,302 total, 1,351 ads). Narration: judge by spend, not ROAS/CPA.
12. Unit economics: some brands run at 0.3 ROAS because customers return for months (Ryze). Narration plus a lifetime-value chart (-$80, -$25, -$45...). Not an ad technique.

## Compared with our ads
Ours: one short headline, one cut-out product on a single colour, round yellow price sticker, footer band with link and QR, same structure every time.
Differences:
- Their ads each have a different structure (diagram, checklist, split, collage, illustration, offer). Ours are one structure in different colours.
- Their headlines state a problem or a very specific person ("if you have morning stabbing heel pain"), or an offer with a number ("Buy 3, get 3 free"). Ours name the product.
- They show proof or explanation (before/after, labelled parts, comparison) -- ours show only the product.
- They use a real scene or hands/people; ours use only a cut-out.
- Offer is in the headline in huge type; ours is a small sticker. No footer link/QR because the click is in the platform.
- Text-heavy ads are allowed (bullets, timelines) when the audience is narrow.
