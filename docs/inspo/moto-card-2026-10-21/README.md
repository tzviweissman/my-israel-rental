# Moto Card (moto-card.com), 21 Oct 2026

Tzvi: "go through this website and you'll see that there are different sections and it changes to pictures, not everything is just one colour ... this looks like a real website, ours do not." An Awwwards Site of the Day for a premium card. Studied screen by screen at 1440x900 (sheet0 to sheet2 show every screen in order).

## The page, section by section
1. **Opening** (hero-2500): black, a stone wall, the product (the card) on a lit plinth, one huge line in capitals, one pill button. The product is the picture.
2. **Globe film** (l02, l03): the earth at night fills the screen; small status chips float over it ("Transaction Successful", "Hotel Booked", "Jet Booked"): the product in use, shown, not described.
3. **The card in a wall of photos** (l05, l07): the card turns in the middle of a tunnel of floating photographs (hotels, dining, interiors) at different depths; the next headline rises from below.
4. **A white section** (l09): the page suddenly turns pale grey: currency amounts in many currencies drift and fade as a texture behind "SPEND GLOBALLY. 0 FX FEES BUILT IN." The subject's own vernacular (prices in many currencies) is the decoration.
5. **Full-bleed cinematic photo** (l11): "24/7 CONCIERGE. ANYTHING. ANYTIME." over a dark film still, three short facts bottom left, a ticker of world clocks along the bottom (proof by detail).
6. **Pinned photo with the benefits stacking up** (l13): huge right-aligned lines over a moody photo.
7. **White editorial list** (l14): "MADE FOR THE PLACES YOU ALREADY MOVE THROUGH", numbered rows 01 to 05 (Hotels, Dining, Travel, Experiences, Wellness), each with one line, hairline dividers.
8. **Full-bleed interior photo** (l16): "ACCESS OUR NETWORK" centred over a curtain and a lamp, short line, the same pill.
9. **White FAQ**: category labels on the left, questions on the right, accordion.
10. **Black closing** (l19): "A QUIETER WAY TO MOVE THROUGH THE WORLD", the pill, tidy link columns, app badges.

## What makes it feel like a real website
- **Every section is a different world.** Dark film, product in a photo tunnel, a white page, a cinematic photo, white again, a photo again, black. The ground changes with each section; most sections are a picture or film edge to edge, and the plain ones are deliberate pauses between them. Ours keep one ground colour for the whole page.
- **Pictures carry the page.** Seven of ten screens are full-bleed imagery. Text sits on the picture, never in a box beside it.
- **One loud type treatment, used the same way everywhere:** a single grotesque, huge capitals for each section's one line, tiny calm body text, lots of empty space.
- **One action, repeated identically:** the same "Apply for Access" pill in the bar, on every section and in the footer.
- **The subject's own details as texture:** currencies drifting, world clocks ticking, status chips over the globe. Each is a true detail of what the product does.
- **Light editorial sections as rests:** numbered rows and the FAQ are on white with hairlines, so the photos hit harder.
- **A real footer:** a final statement, the button, link columns, contact, legal.

## For our pages
- Alternate worlds: after the opening, every section changes ground or picture; no two sections in a row share the same plain colour; at least half the sections are a full-bleed photo or film of the owner's own work (rule 3t, checked by check-site WORLDS).
- One headline per section, big, with one short line under it.
- The same button, the same words, in every section.
- One true detail of the business as texture (prices, sizes, hours, the shop's street) instead of decoration.
