# Our website references, by look

Every website picture saved under `docs/inspo/`, grouped by the look it has, not the day it was saved. Use it at
step 2 of a build: pick the family that fits the business and its category (rule 3t), open the pictures, and copy
the look words into REFERENCES.md so builds share one vocabulary. Filter it in a browser: `by-look.html`.

Each line: the picture, the site, 3 to 5 words that name the look, and the kinds of our businesses it suits
(rule 3t: food-shop, place, fashion-shop, beauty, events, trainer, service, rental). Borrow how a look works,
never a site's exact look (README). Idea D4 in `videos-2026-11-25/README.md`.

Made by `node scripts/build-inspo-by-look.mjs` from `by-look.tsv`. After saving new site pictures, add their
lines to the tsv and rerun; `--check` lists any picture not yet in it. Ads are in `pinterest-ads-2026-11-02/`,
not here.
