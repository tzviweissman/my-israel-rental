hechsherim, over 5000"; 5,325 listed on the live site that day, each with a named hechsher). Two searches, 44 pins,
saved by their Pinterest image id; sheet-0.jpg and sheet-1.jpg show them all with their codes.

- "creative restaurant directory ad find restaurants"

Most results are phone-in-hand delivery-app posters (c7b16f, e28d3d, c0dd4d, 1d8e3f): the look to stay away from.

- **8e34d1** a steak whose tip becomes the map pin on a small folded map: the food is the location.
- **900d6a** a hand holds a sandwich in front of huge red letters "NYC": the food woven through big type.
- **639cf9** a burger with a phone's coiled cord: an object built from the food.
- **eb24c5** a plate of food in hand beside a phone map with one pin: found, then eaten.
- **02fbe9** a chalk pie chart, one slice a real chocolate cake: the number drawn with the food.
