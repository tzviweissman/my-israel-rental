# Matt Penny, "10 GitHub Repos to 10x Your AI Motion Graphics"

youtu.be/aD9X6Lc3dVk, 13:03, 207K views.

How it was read: the transcript panel in Chrome, plus 4 frames (3 saved; the Three.js bar chart frame came out blank
between animations).

## The one idea

Code-made motion looks less like AI when it borrows a finished style or a real tool instead of being invented from
scratch: "the human has done the creative work and AI is just selecting which one of those templates to use."

## The ten, and what each is

1. **Lemo Opuscar:** 66 film styles for code-made motion (paper cut, 80s anime, ink wash, retro terminal, isometric
   explainer). Frame: a layered paper-cut lighthouse scene with real depth and moonlight (`aD9X6Lc3dVk-paper-cut-style.png`).
2. **Three.js:** 3D in the browser (bar charts made of stacks of cash, particles, camera fly-throughs).
3. **pixelfx:** pixel-art effects (fire, lightning, explosions).
4. **Blender MCP:** full 3D scenes; an exploded pair of headphones with each part labelled
   (`aD9X6Lc3dVk-exploded-headphones-blender.png`).
5. **Two prompt packs** for Opus-made videos (mosaic, flat explainer, ready UI pieces).
6. **Claude animation base:** a mascot with emotions.
7. **React Rapier:** real physics (dominoes falling to spell a word, `aD9X6Lc3dVk-physics-dominoes-word.png`; a
   Galton board).
8. **React postprocessing:** glow, depth blur, film grain, glitch, run as filters on each frame.
9. **librosa:** finds the beat in music so cuts land on it.
10. **Video ShotCraft:** 124 product-launch video recipes.

## What to ignore

- The "skill selector" and the mastermind with "$400,000 of savings".
- Everything here is for explainer and launch videos, not small-business sites, and most is shown one-shot.
- The mascot, pixel art and physics toys are gimmicks for a bakery or a cleaner.

## What it means here

- A **labelled exploded view** (shared with video 1) suits a few of our kinds: what is inside a sandwich or a layer
  cake, the parts a repair shop replaces, the stones of a ring. Labels must be real facts from the owner.
- A **paper-cut scene drawn in code** is a cousin of 3ak.1 (live art drawn in code): free, light, and unusual for a
  business with a story (a tour guide, a family bakery). It would join the menu of 3ai.2, never be required.
- **Beat-matched cuts** belong to the moving ads if they ever get music; today the ad rules have no sound, so nothing
  to do.
- Blender appears again (video 1 too): two videos out of eight, so worth the first trial.
