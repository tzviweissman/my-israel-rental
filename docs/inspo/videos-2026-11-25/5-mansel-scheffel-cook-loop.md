## The one idea

The agent "stops the moment something works". Give it reference pictures as the target, a bar per dimension, a
time floor, and judges who never saw it being built.

## Anything not taken on 22 Nov

- **A time floor as well as a cap:** "run for at least 8 hours... if you're done after 2 hours, something is wrong."
  His own view is that a website needs about 2 hours at most. Our builds are not time-boxed this way and should not
  be: the design board's four yeses (3aj.8) set the pace.
- **"Avoid never or always" in rules for Fable**, "because it's extremely literal": a rule that says never, when it
  means usually, can wreck a build. This matches our "each rule says when it applies" step.

## What to ignore

- The 45-hour, $1,700 to $2,200 showcase and the "Ultra Code" army of agents.
- His own one-hour results were "not overly impressive".
- The Skool community.
