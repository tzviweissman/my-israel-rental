# Awwwards food and drink sites, 18 Oct 2026

Tzvi asked for every site in the Awwwards food and drink category to be studied
for Chicken Munch and future food pages. The 62 newest sites (two pages) were
captured at 1440x900 with `scripts/inspo-shoot.mjs` (the first 5 seconds, then
24 steps top to bottom) and read frame by frame. 52 gave usable frames; the rest
were age gates, robot checks, cookie walls or blank. 113 effects, one picture each.

The site list is `../awwwards-2026-10-18b-sites.txt`.

Each part ends with "For Chicken Munch": effects that keep the owner's real
photos untouched, after generated food motion looked fake on every try
(page rules 3h.13 and the film-opening memory). Lines in those sections that
suggest claims ("fresh every day", "fresh oil") are examples only: a claim
goes on a page only when the owner said it.

**The strongest single reference for a fried-chicken or burger shop:**
crav-burgers-2-burger-inside-title. A giant red word, the real food cut out and
sitting across the letters, a yellow wordmark under it and small sticker labels.
