## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Burger inside the title** (crav-burgers-2-burger-inside-title)
The words THE BURGER are set huge in red and a real burger photo sits between the letters, with the yellow CRAV word below it. A small loader before it shows a bun then a drawn burger. The photo and the type share one frame, so the food looks like part of the headline.
Build: code. A cut-out photo layered between two lines of type.
Suits: restaurant, bakery, food truck, caterer.

**2. Bun loader** (crav-burgers-1-bun-loader)
A solid red screen with one drawn bun that grows into a full burger and a line "preparing to serve". It is short and sets the mood before the page.
Build: code. A few drawn frames and a text line.
Suits: restaurant, bakery, cafe.

**3. Bottle hero with flying fruit** (bucks-sauce-1-hero-bottle)
A dark page with a bold headline, the bottle in the middle and real fruit pieces drifting around it at different depths. The cut-outs move as solid pieces, nothing breaks.
Build: both. Photo cut-outs moved by code on scroll or time.
Suits: sauce maker, bakery, gift shop, caterer.

**4. Split hero with the product on colour** (terry-ho-1-split-hero-bottle)
Half the screen is a red block with the bottle and the brand name repeated behind it, the other half cycles photos of the sauce on real meals under the line "Good On Everything".
Build: code. Two columns and a slow photo swap.
Suits: restaurant with one hero dish, bakery, sauce or food seller.

**5. Giant type marquee on colour** (kfc-rewards-1-giant-type-marquee)
Huge white capitals slide across a deep red field, one word filling the screen height. It is simple and loud.
Build: code.
Suits: restaurant, takeaway, any brand with a bold colour.

**6. Neon street scene** (oreo-bts-1-neon-street-scene)
After a glowing logo the whole screen becomes one rich street scene full of signs and objects with a short thank you line over it.
Build: generated. A single made image or film. Not for food.
Suits: event, brand campaign (low fit for Chicken Munch).

**7. Tilted photo and heavy type** (imperiale-bolgheri-1-tilted-photo-giant-type)
One portrait photo is turned a few degrees on black, and the headline THE IDEA overlaps its bottom edge in a cream colour.
Build: code. A rotated image and big type that overlaps it.
Suits: restaurant, tour guide, gift shop, cleaner with a strong photo.

### Scroll scenes

**8. Timeline takeover** (banh-mi-viet-nam-1-timeline-takeover)
Each year (1859, 1958, 2011) takes over the screen on a tan panel with a big photo, then the next panel wipes in. It tells a story in short steps as you scroll.
Build: code. Pinned sections with wipe transitions.
Suits: bakery with history, tour guide, restaurant, caterer.

**9. Stacked claim cards** (bucks-sauce-2-stacked-claim-cards)
Cards such as No corn syrup, No seed oils, No additives drop one on top of the other at small tilts while fruit floats behind. Each card is a solid object.
Build: code. Cards that stack as you scroll.
Suits: any business with three to five promises (cleaner, caterer, bakery).

**10. Wavy colour sections** (crav-burgers-3-wavy-colour-sections)
Red, cream and yellow sections are separated by a wavy edge instead of a straight line. A burger photo, then a hand holding a burger, slide up inside each wave.
Build: code. SVG wave edges and pinned photo.
Suits: restaurant, bakery, food truck, gift shop.

**11. Travel route with photos** (crav-burgers-4-travel-route-photos)
On yellow, a dotted line with a small plane crosses the screen and customer photos pop up in different cities.
Build: code. A path drawn on scroll with photo cards.
Suits: shop that ships, caterer, tour guide.

**12. Photo that holds while text changes** (ballena-fine-dining-1-floating-video-window)
A small window with photo or video stays in the centre while colour blocks, food and landscape slide behind it and the headline changes.
Build: both. Pinned element and sliding layers.
Suits: restaurant, event venue, tour guide.

**13. Can tilts with feature text** (ciao-energy-1-can-carousel)
A product floats in a dark room. Scrolling tilts it and the feature (less sugar, natural aromas) appears in text that wraps around it.
Build: code. Works with a flat cut-out tilted in 2D, no 3D needed.
Suits: bakery, gift shop, any single hero product.

**14. Row of cans in a ribbon** (ciao-energy-3-can-ribbon)
All the flavours line up in a diagonal ribbon that slides across the screen.
Build: code. A row of cut-outs on a diagonal track.
Suits: bakery with many items, gift shop, caterer.

**15. Zero cloud behind the can** (ciao-energy-2-zero-cloud-can)
A purple cloud background fills the screen and a faint huge phrase sits behind the can.
Build: both. Type behind a cut-out.
Suits: bakery, restaurant, gift shop.

## For Chicken Munch

1. **Burger inside the title** (crav-burgers-2-burger-inside-title). Set CHICKEN MUNCH huge in red and put his real poppers in the kraft bowl as a cut-out sitting between the letters, with the gold word below. The photo is untouched, only the layers matter.
2. **Photo with headline text behind it** (burrito-madre-2-type-behind-photo). A giant red word runs behind a cut-out of the cornflake chicken box so the real box sits in front of the type. It works on a black ground with the gold accent.
3. **Cut-out menu grid** (burrito-madre-1-cutout-menu-grid). Each item (poppers, cornflake chicken, boxes, pans) is cut from his photos and shown on black in a clean grid with one red order button. It doubles as an ordering entry.
4. **Stacked claim cards** (bucks-sauce-2-stacked-claim-cards). Cards such as Kosher, Fresh every day, Cornflake crunch drop one on another at a tilt next to the real photos. Real food floats behind as solid cut-outs that drift slowly.
5. **Speech bubble quotes** (terry-ho-2-speech-bubble-quotes). Real customer quotes appear as gold bubbles over a red block with a bold line about the chicken. No effect on the food itself.
