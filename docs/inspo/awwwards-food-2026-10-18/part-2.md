## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Giant mark behind the headline** (soufflet-malt-1-giant-mark-opening)
A dark navy screen with four gold leaves forming a flower, the headline sitting beside it in bold caps. The mark is large enough to be the picture on its own.
Build: code, one SVG shape that scales in.
Suits: bakery, caterer, any shop with a simple logo shape.

**2. Photo window inside the logo** (zeroz-brand-site-1-photo-window-in-logo)
A huge grey wordmark across the bottom, and a small photo box above it that changes between shots. The photo is the only bright thing, so the eye goes straight to it.
Build: code, a photo box that swaps frames over a fixed word.
Suits: restaurant, gift shop, any business with a short name.

**3. Shrimp on the plate, sea wave edge** (pier88-coast-1-shrimp-on-plate-wave)
The hero is a real food photo cut out and placed over a sea photo, with a white wavy edge at the bottom that leads into the plate section. The cut out dish sits on top of the scene as one solid piece.
Build: code, the dish cut out as a PNG over a background photo.
Suits: restaurant, caterer, fish shop.

**4. Can in the wordmark** (still-1-can-in-wordmark)
A giant black wordmark, and a round window cut through it where the can slides across. As the can moves, the window shows it half in light, half in shadow.
Build: code, a circular mask over the product photo.
Suits: shop with one hero product, drinks, bakery with one signature item.

**5. Bottle rises from the vine** (klimt-wine-1-bottle-rises-from-vine)
Letters of the headline appear one by one, then a vine shape drifts in and the bottle comes up in front of it. Dark ground, one photo, very little else.
Build: code, a cut out product rising over a still.
Suits: wine, oil, honey, any bottled product.

**6. Map world opening** (meatopia-1-map-world-opening)
A drawn map of a whole world, faded behind the logo, with one button to explore. The loader counts up before it opens.
Build: generated art plus code.
Suits: festival, market, tour guide.

**7. Photo circle inside the name** (do-you-g-1-photo-circle-in-name)
The brand name is split in two and a round photo sits in the gap between the words.
Build: code, a circle mask between two words.
Suits: cafe, juice bar, small brand.

### Scroll scenes

**8. Circle of light opens the story** (zeroz-brand-site-2-light-circle-wipe)
A white circle grows from the middle of a dark screen, and the first sentence sits inside it. The circle then becomes the whole screen.
Build: code, a growing circle mask tied to scroll.
Suits: any business with a story section, tour guide, cleaner.

**9. Ring of cards** (zeroz-brand-site-3-ring-of-cards)
Cards arranged in a ring turn slowly as you scroll, with the section title in the middle.
Build: code, cards placed on a circle and rotated by scroll.
Suits: catering menus, tour stops, product ranges.

**10. Aerial wave wipe** (pier88-coast-3-aerial-wave-wipe)
A top down photo of the sea with foam fills the screen and the page scrolls through it. It works as a pause between sections.
Build: code, a full screen photo with a slow scroll.
Suits: restaurant, tour guide, anything near water or a landmark.

**11. Day flips to night** (pier88-coast-4-day-lingers-dark-flip)
The page flips from a white section to a black one, with the words "Day lingers" changing to "Night lingers" as you pass. The change in light tells the story.
Build: code, a colour flip tied to scroll.
Suits: restaurant, bar, event hall, anything with day and evening.

### Product and menu

**12. The plate as the menu** (pier88-coast-2-plate-as-menu)
One big plate in the centre. As you scroll, the word on the plate changes from Breakfast to Lunch to Dinner and small photos float in around it.
Build: code, with the plate as a PNG and the labels swapped by scroll.
Suits: restaurant, caterer, bakery with morning and evening ranges.

**13. Flavour glow swap** (still-2-flavour-glow-swap)
One can stays put while the name changes (Clear, Dawn, Dusk) and the soft glow behind it changes colour with each.
Build: code, a coloured radial gradient behind a fixed product photo.
Suits: bakery flavours, juice bar, sauces, any range of three to five.

**14. Inside, dark ingredient tabs** (still-3-inside-dark-tabs)
A black section with the product in the middle and small tabs for each ingredient. Pressing a tab changes the label beside the product.
Build: code, tabs over a fixed photo.
Suits: sauces, bakery, spice shop.

**15. One colour panel per product** (best-bean-best-cup-1-colour-panel-products)
Each product gets its own tall colour panel (blue, yellow, green, orange) side by side, each with the name and a bag photo. The row scrolls sideways.
Build: code, a horizontal row of coloured cards.
Suits: shop with packaged goods, bakery, gift shop.

**16. Huge arrow type for a section** (best-bean-best-cup-2-big-arrow-type)
A section title set enormous with an arrow, letters in boxes, with photos popping between the letters.
Build: code.
Suits: any menu or product section that needs a loud label.

**17. Sliding product card strip** (partake-foods-1-product-card-strip)
Product cards in different pastel colours sit in a strip that slides under a short headline.
Build: code, a draggable strip.
Suits: bakery, gift shop, any shop with several products.

**18. Floating cards row** (soufflet-malt-2-floating-cards-row)
Product cards sit at slightly different heights and slide across the screen as you scroll, like items on a shelf.
Build: code, cards moved by scroll at different speeds.
Suits: gift shop, bakery, deli.

**19. Tall photo cards on a tinted ground** (caffe-gilli-1-coloured-product-cards)
Three tall product photos side by side on a slate blue ground under a short serif title.
Build: code.
Suits: bakery, pastry shop, cafe.

**20. Product on a red circle** (kervan-1-red-blob-product)
A product photo sits on a bold red circle that stays in place while the page text moves. The circle is the brand colour, so the whole thing feels branded.
Build: code, a coloured circle behind a cut out photo.
Suits: shop with a strong brand colour, a fried chicken shop included.

**21. Cards fill with photos** (cozy-eating-village-1-cards-fill-with-photos)
A wall of blank cream cards where photos land one by one. It makes a loading moment look intentional.
Build: code, staggered image reveal.
Suits: restaurant menu grid, gift shop.

## For Chicken Munch

Black page, red #EF2B2B, gold #FAA519, real photos only.

1. **Plate as the menu** (pier88-coast-2-plate-as-menu). One of his real kraft bowls or trays sits in the middle. As you scroll, the label on it changes between popper bowl, cornflake chicken and boxes, with his other photos sliding in around it. Real photos, only moved as flat pieces.
2. **Can in the wordmark** (still-1-can-in-wordmark). A giant CHICKEN MUNCH wordmark in red, with a round window across it where his kraft bowl photo slides through. Only the window moves, the food photo is untouched.
3. **Product on a red circle** (kervan-1-red-blob-product). A cut out of the bowl of poppers sits on a big #EF2B2B circle, the circle stays put and the text moves past. It reads as a brand mark and keeps the food real.
4. **Glow orb call to action** (monarque-evenements-3-glow-orb-call). The closing screen is black with a red to gold glowing circle and "Hungry?" and the order button, taking the photo out of the equation.
5. **Photo inside the heading / portrait pops in a line** (monarque-evenements-2-photo-inside-heading). A headline like "Crunch [popper photo] Kosher" with his real photo inline as a word, so type and photo work together.
