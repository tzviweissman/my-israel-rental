## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Biscuit opening** (kindi-land-1-biscuit-opening)
One real biscuit sits alone on a blue screen, then the page grows out of it and the headline appears. A single product as the first picture is calm and clear.
Build: code. A product cut-out on a plain colour, then a fade into the page.
Suits: bakery, gift shop, caterer, any shop with one hero item.

**2. Film hero with story lines** (delta-cafes-1-film-hero-chapter-lines)
A full screen film of real people with one short line in the corner that changes with each shot. The page says who the business is before any menu.
Build: both. A short real clip, lines timed in code.
Suits: restaurant, bakery, tour guide, family run shops.

**3. Fog title hero** (haunted-boulder-city-1-fog-title-hero)
A night photo of the town with slow fog and a huge two part title, one line scribbled and one in block capitals. It sets the mood in one look.
Build: both. A still with a slow drift in code, fog from a looped clip.
Suits: tour guide, event business, restaurant with a strong theme.

**4. Real shop front opening** (qissa-1-shopfront-opening)
The first picture is the actual shop front, which then fades to black and the name appears in gold. Customers see the real door they will walk to.
Build: code. One photo and a fade.
Suits: restaurant, bakery, any shop people visit in person.

**5. Counter loader** (agrumea-farm-2-counter-loader)
A big percent counter with an orange shape runs while the page loads, then the first scene opens. The wait becomes part of the show.
Build: code.
Suits: any small business with a few strong photos.

### Scroll scenes

**6. Cards slide over a fixed photo** (bettr-academy-1-cards-over-photo)
A wide landscape photo stays still while paper-like cards slide up and over it, each with one benefit. The photo gives depth and the cards stay readable.
Build: code. Sticky background and cards that scroll over it.
Suits: cleaner, caterer, tour guide, trades with a few selling points.

**7. Stacked card deck** (kindi-land-3-stacked-card-deck)
Reasons to choose the business arrive as cards that pile on top of each other as you scroll, one message at a time.
Build: code. Sticky cards with a slight rotation.
Suits: cleaner, mover, caterer, any service with three or four promises.

**8. Spotlight chapter** (haunted-boulder-city-2-spotlight-chapter)
Each chapter is one big line over a different moody photo, and the photo changes with the line. It reads like a poster per scroll.
Build: code. Full screen photos with big type.
Suits: tour guide, restaurant with several stories.

**9. Film chapters with big type** (morpho-water-1-film-chapters-big-type)
Short real clips, each with a two line statement in light serif: waterfall, sea, surfer, and each line fits what you see.
Build: both. Real clips, type in code.
Suits: tour guide, restaurant, anything about place and mood.

**10. Chapter break with mascot truck** (foodnia-1-chapter-mascot-truck)
Between sections the screen goes white and a small illustrated truck drives across under a chapter name. A playful reset.
Build: both. A drawn character moved in code.
Suits: caterer, delivery, any friendly local business.

**11. Vegetables in a round window** (foodnia-3-tomato-porthole)
A large product sits inside a round opening in a teal scene, and the next scroll swaps the product while the opening stays. The frame is constant, the food changes.
Build: both. A set of cut-outs swapped inside one fixed frame.
Suits: bakery, restaurant, gift shop.

**12. Coconut that scales over the type** (palmo-1-coconut-over-type)
A 3D coconut grows between the words of the headline so the type wraps around it, then it cracks open on later scrolls.
Build: generated. A 3D object rendered as frames, type in code. Not suited to real food photos.
Suits: shop with one iconic product.

**13. Lab chapter with arch** (foodnia-2-veg-in-arch-lab)
A teal arch with one vegetable inside under a giant LAB title, a short chapter about how things are made.
Build: both.
Suits: bakery, caterer with a kitchen story.

### Product and menu

**14. Colour swap product slider** (theobroma-1-colour-swap-slider)
Each product sits on its own bold colour, and the whole stage turns blue, orange or green as you move to the next one.
Build: code. Cut-out product shots and a colour change per slide.
Suits: bakery, gift shop, any menu with a few hero items.

**15. Category cards with product pop** (kindi-land-2-category-cards-product-pop)
A row of rounded cards, one per category, with the product breaking out over the edge of its card. They swipe sideways.
Build: code. Cut-outs placed to overlap the card edge.
Suits: bakery, restaurant menu groups, gift shop.

**16. Jars with hover tags** (agrumea-farm-1-jars-hover-tags)
Three jars float and tilt over a dark red shape with a curved line of type, and a small tag appears beside each as you move near.
Build: code. Cut-outs, tilt and small tags.
Suits: gift shop, bakery, caterer with packaged goods.

**17. Stat bubbles on the product** (palmo-2-stat-bubbles)
Round yellow and brown bubbles with one fact each ("500mg", "0g sugar") float around the open product.
Build: code. Circles with text moving gently.
Suits: bakery, restaurant, any shop with facts to show (kosher, fresh daily, size).

**18. Line drawing cutaway** (acme-1-1-line-drawing-cutaway)
Each cup is shown as a thin technical drawing first, then the real photo takes its place. It feels crafted.
Build: both. Outlines traced from the real photo.
Suits: bakery, gift shop, trades that make things.

**19. Colour swatch row** (acme-1-2-colour-swatch-row)
Round colour dots for each option in one calm row; tapping a dot changes the product.
Build: code.
Suits: gift shop, caterer with options, bakery with flavours.

**20. Circular tray diagram** (mam-com-1-circular-tray-diagram)
A round tray drawn as a diagram, each dish a named circle that opens a short text beside it.
Build: code. A drawn circle with clickable parts.
Suits: restaurant, caterer with platters, bakery with boxes.

**21. Recipe with serving switch** (mam-com-3-recipe-servings)
Ingredients and method in two calm columns, with a serving size switch that changes the amounts.
Build: code.
Suits: restaurant, bakery, caterer that shares recipes.

**22. Plates carousel with gold band** (qissa-2-gold-marquee-band)
Round plates photographed from above slide sideways, with a slow gold band of words (taste, craft, gathering) beneath.
Build: code. Photos of plates from above, a slow text band.
Suits: restaurant, caterer.

**23. Flavour space** (3dcc-1-flavour-space)
Flavours as coloured balls floating on labelled axes (sweet, crisp, creamy). You drag to turn the space and tap a ball.
Build: code. A simple 3D scene.
Suits: restaurant or bakery with a big menu and a quiz style guide.

**24. Promo tile mosaic** (proteinbolaget-1-promo-tile-mosaic)
Four promo tiles of different sizes in a clean mosaic, each with a photo and one line. It works as a menu of offers.
Build: code.
Suits: restaurant with specials, bakery, shop.

## For Chicken Munch

All five use his real photos only.

1. Real shop front opening (qissa-1-shopfront-opening): open on a photo of the actual shop on the black page, fade to black and bring the name up in red and gold. The shop is real and people find it easily.
2. Cards slide over a fixed photo (bettr-academy-1-cards-over-photo): keep one wide photo of the poppers in the kraft bowl still while black cards with red type slide over it, one promise each (kosher, fresh, Jerusalem).
3. Colour swap product slider (theobroma-1-colour-swap-slider): each real cut-out (poppers bowl, cornflake chicken, box) on its own red, black or gold stage that changes as you move; the photo itself is not animated.
4. Plates carousel with gold band (qissa-2-gold-marquee-band): his top-down photos of pans and boxes slide sideways with a slow gold band of words (crispy, kosher, hot) under them.
5. Stat bubbles on the product (palmo-2-stat-bubbles): small round red and gold bubbles with one fact each ("kosher", "made to order", "fresh oil") float around the real bowl photo; only the bubbles move.
