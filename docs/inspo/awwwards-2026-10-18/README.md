# Awwwards Sites of the Day, 18 Oct 2026

Tzvi sent five award-winning sites (from awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_day)
"to see different affects and wow ideas to save in our database". Each was
captured at 1440x900: the first 5 seconds, then a slow scroll top to bottom
(wheel ticks for the one that takes over the scroll), and studied frame by
frame. One picture per effect is saved beside this file.

**How to use this:** these are ideas to borrow the way a site works, never its
look. Colours, type and copy stay the business's own (page rules 3c.1). Every
effect here still has to pass our rules: one signature film per page (3h),
one cause, one motion (3h.13), words and picture take turns (3h.17), measured
readability and cut-off checks. "Build" says how we would make it: **code**
(scroll-tied CSS/canvas, exact and cheap), **generated** (a Higgsfield
still or clip), or **both**.

## The five sites in one line each

| Site | What it sells | The big idea |
|---|---|---|
| drone.riotters.com | A lidar drone (a studio's tech demo) | One product held in view the whole page, turning to show each feature, on a clean white ground |
| butter.video | A video editor | Playful objects (a keyring of 3D charms, app icons) that fly into the product |
| usavionix.com | Autonomous military drones | The page is a film: scene after scene, the real world turning into a map |
| unitedcarriers.com | Freight and logistics | One container travels the whole page: crane, truck, road, ship, plane |
| seasats.com | Ocean drones | The page's colour changes with the story (sea grey to green to sunset orange) |

## The effects

### Opening and first screen

**1. Keyring of charms above the headline** (butter-1-keychain-hero)
A bunch of 3D objects hangs on a ring over the headline and sways gently with
the mouse, as if real. Build: both (generated objects cut out, swing in code).
Suits: gift shops, keys and locks, kids' activities, anything with several
small products.

**2. Diagonal wipe from the loader into the film** (usav-1-diagonal-wipe)
A dark loading screen with the product's outline is cut open by a diagonal
slash that reveals the full scene behind it. Build: code (a clip-path).
Suits: any page with a film opening; a cleaner reveal than a fade.

**3. Glowing globe edge with live routes** (united-1-globe-routes)
The top of a dark globe glows at the rim; routes light up between cities
while the headline sits over the dark half. Build: code (canvas or SVG).
Suits: movers, shipping, tour operators, anything that covers areas.

**4. Calm film hero with a coloured side menu** (seasats-1-hero)
A still sea with the product on it, a short serif headline, and the menu as a
column of colour bands down the right edge, one per section. Build: generated
film, code menu. Suits: premium services that sell calm (spas, holiday stays).

### Words

**5. Words fill in as you read** (drone-1-word-reveal, butter-2-inline-icons-text)
A paragraph starts pale and each word turns dark, one after another, as the
scroll moves. Butter adds small pictures inside the sentence (an icon between
"Butter" and "is") and colours the words in pastels. Build: code. Suits:
every page's "about" line; the inline pictures suit food menus (a tiny photo
of each dish inside the sentence).

**6. Lines light up one at a time, each with its own picture** (seasats-2-line-by-line-insets)
A list of short lines over a scene; as each line lights up, a small framed
photo of that thing appears beside it (a submarine, an algae bloom), then
fades for the next. Build: code, with their photos or generated ones.
Suits: "what we do" lists (a caterer's events, a cleaner's rooms).

**7. Giant outlined word behind the action** (united-3-outline-text-behind)
A huge outlined word ("OUR SERVICES") slides slowly behind a truck as it
drives across. Build: code. Suits: section openers on bold, loud brands
(Chicken Munch style).

**8. Giant section words** (drone-3-sticky-tabs)
Each section opens with one enormous plain word ("Gear", "Solutions",
"Capabilities"). Build: code. Suits: clean, technical services.

### The product on stage

**9. One product held on screen, turning to show each feature** (drone-2-stat-readout, drone-3-sticky-tabs)
The drone stays in the same spot while the page scrolls; it rotates and tilts
to face each feature as that feature's number lights up underneath
("5 returns", "±2 cm", "450 m"), one at a time. Build: generated turntable
frames, code for the readout. Suits: any single hero product (a cake, a
board, a machine for hire).

**10. Sticky tabs that swap the picture** (drone-3-sticky-tabs)
A short list on the left (Scan, Connection, Compactness); the active one is
dark, the picture beside it changes with it. Build: code. Suits: services
with three or four parts.

**11. Product switcher that recolours the page** (seasats-3-switcher-colour)
Three products in a small pill at the bottom; choosing one swaps the 3D model
AND turns the whole section that product's colour (orange for Quickfish).
Build: code plus one picture per product (one look per product, 3h.12).
Suits: a menu of styles or flavours (Chicken Munch's picker does this already).

**12. Hotspots on the product** (seasats-4-hotspots)
Small square markers on the product; each opens a label and a spec. Build:
code over a still. Suits: a box or tray with several items, a room, a vehicle.

**13. Icons fly in and land in the product** (butter-3-icons-fly-in, butter-4-icons-land)
Scattered app icons tumble toward the editor window and settle into its
timeline in a row. Build: code (cut-outs on paths). Suits: a gift box being
filled, ingredients landing in a bowl, items packed into a moving box.

### Travel and transitions

**14. One object travels the whole page** (united-2-crane-lift, united-4-road-path, united-5-from-above-clouds)
A container is lifted by a crane, set on a truck, the truck drives along a
road that bends across the page (seen from above), and later the containers
are seen from the sky through clouds. Each section is the next leg of the
same journey. Build: both (generated scenes, code for the path). Suits:
movers, delivery, catering ("from our kitchen to your table").

**15. Speed dive between scenes** (usav-2-speed-dive)
The camera rushes forward with a radial blur and lands in the next scene.
Build: code (blur and scale) or generated. Suits: a hand-off between two
chapters of a film; use once.

**16. Real photo turns into a map** (usav-3-hud-boxes, usav-4-photo-to-map)
Over a real aerial scene, small labelled boxes pop onto things; then the photo
fades into a dark map of the same place with glowing zones. Build: both.
Suits: anyone who serves areas (which neighbourhoods we deliver to, a tour
route, a service map).

**17. Terrain wireframe** (drone-4-terrain-wireframe)
A pale 3D landscape drawn with glowing contour lines, with a marker on it.
Build: generated or code. Suits: tours, hiking, real estate by area.

### The close

**18. Giant wordmark with a glow** (butter-5-glow-wordmark)
The footer is the brand name huge, with a soft coloured glow under it.
Build: code. (Same family as 3i.10's cropped wordmark.)

**19. Footer landscape with live readouts** (seasats-5-footer-readouts)
The brand name huge at the floor of a warm landscape, with small live numbers
above it (speed, distance). Build: generated landscape, code readouts. Only
with true numbers (rule 3e: no invented counts).

## Patterns all five share

1. **The page is one story told in scenes**, each scene with a small title
   top-left, one short caption bottom-left, and small technical details
   bottom-right (usavionix does this on every scene). Our chapter lines are
   the same idea.
2. **One object carries the page**: the drone, the container, the vessel.
   It stays recognisable from the first screen to the last (our 3h.14).
3. **Colour changes with the story**, never at random: seasats moves from grey
   sea to green to sunset; butter keeps white and lets the objects bring the
   colour.
4. **Calm type, loud picture**: four of five use plain sans-serif headlines
   and let the image do the work.
5. **The close is the brand name, huge.**
