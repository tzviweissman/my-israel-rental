## Patterns in this batch

**Openings (14 sites)**
- Straight onto the real picture or product with no loader (counting "first sheet shows it in frame 1"): the-vineyard-hotel (film, fountain), myfrida (product tub), house-of-giants (mascot, ~0.5 s). 3 sites.
- A plain ground first, with the picture fading or growing in underneath: odyssee (grey ground, film at 1 s), pragen-hatlen (gradient, watch at 2.5 s), esr-bespoke (black + giant ESR, then headline typed, film strip at 2 s), huxley-saga (black 1 s then film, giant letters assemble), polpharma (white with green dot ~1.5 s, then teal scene), lpas (dark grey, letters stack, photo box grows to full screen at ~4.5 s). 6 sites.
- A long coloured loader or gate (8 s or more) where you wait: sweet-tape (flat green 4+ s, 8 s before first screen), la-boite-a-slide (violet spinner about 10 s), spyker-cars (white or black 3-7 s plus a cookie card), forge-automotive (consent gate: never usable until clicked, "Accept and enter"). 4 sites.
- A counter loader with flashing photos: realevate (counter 27 to 99 and a photo changing every 0.5 s, wipe at ~3.5 s). 1 site.
- Seconds until usable (first nav or button visible): about 1 s: vineyard, house-of-giants (~1.5), huxley (~1.5, but the title is built by 3 s), odyssee (~1.5-2), esr (~2), polpharma (~2), myfrida (0). About 3-4 s: pragen-hatlen, realevate, spyker (best case), lpas (~6). 8-13 s: sweet-tape, la-boite-a-slide. Never: forge-automotive (gate).
- What arrives first: words and a brand name before any picture (esr, lpas, odyssee tagline, huxley giant letters, forge): 5. A picture or product first: vineyard, myfrida, house-of-giants, realevate (flashing photos): 4. Nothing but a colour: sweet-tape, spyker, la-boite, pragen, polpharma (white): 5.
- Words that type or write in letter by letter or line by line: esr, spyker, huxley (letters), polpharma, la-boite, lpas (blurred rise), house-of-giants (blur to sharp). 7 sites.

**Follow-along techniques**

- Sticky list where the active line is highlighted and a picture beside it swaps: esr-bespoke (marques), polpharma (DNA tab list), lpas (focus areas). 3 sites.
- Picture that shrinks to a small card or frame as you scroll: vineyard (hero to portrait frame), realevate (first screen shrinks into a card among four tall panels), spyker (heritage photo grows to centre). 3 sites.
- Giant sideways text or marquee (time-driven or scroll-driven): realevate (headline loop on the first screen), sweet-tape (pinned headline sideways), myfrida (word marquee, sideways plane), la-boite (Depuis 2002 marquee). 4 sites.
- Things travelling with you or trails: house-of-giants (footprints), sweet-tape (sound button, side tab), vineyard (rating bar sticks to the bottom). 3 sites.
- Parallax (things at their own speed): measured only on polpharma (page and main move at about 7.5x the scroll, pinned layers) and sweet-tape (main 4.4x); by eye spyker (overlapping photos), odyssee. 4 sites; most sites use pin and swap, not classic parallax.
- Sideways strips as a rail with arrows: vineyard (offers), house-of-giants (case studies), huxley (character picker). 3 sites.
- Counters and ticking numbers: huxley (12+, 40M+, 221K), realevate (loader count), lpas (1/5 panel count). 3 sites.

**Mouse reactions**
- Visible pointer reaction in the 2 s test: 0 of 14. Where something moved (polpharma glass bubble, vineyard film clips, odyssee film, myfrida berries, realevate marquee, huxley intro) it also moves without the mouse, so it is time-driven. The vineyard summary lists a custom cursor (div.cursor) that I could not see in the sheet; can't tell. Mouse effects are not what makes this batch win; the loader, the scroll story and the type are.

**Section hand-overs**
- Colour-block change with a curved or torn edge wiping up: la-boite (violet, white, green), myfrida (torn black/pink/yellow/purple/green), sweet-tape (wavy ribbon, orange to green), vineyard (cream/sage/navy). 4 sites.
- Next section slides over a pinned one (overlap): huxley, odyssee, polpharma (teal to white to mint, plus grey cover), esr (white sheet over film), lpas. 5 sites.
- Hard edge / plain colour change: house-of-giants (olive, cream, dark green), spyker (white to black). 2 sites.
- Shared object carrying across: vineyard (the photo frame), realevate (hero card into panels), esr (the giant ESR opening and ending), polpharma (the glass object).

**Endings**
- Page lifts or ends on a dark footer with a giant name or object: huxley (fixed footer with giant HUXLEY), esr (giant ESR on white), lpas (dark footer revealed under a photo), odyssee (metal sphere with logo), myfrida (giant "my moment" cut off at the bottom), house-of-giants (giant head and logo), vineyard (giant Sleep/Eat/Meet/Play links over a garden photo). 7 sites.
- Plain link footer: spyker (big links), polpharma, sweet-tape, realevate, forge, pragen, la-boite — can't tell (page not reached) for 6 of those.

**Takeaways for a small local business (ranked by cost to build)**
1. A flat brand colour with only the name and a line for about 1 s, then the film or photo fades in (odyssee, pragen, esr). Keep first useful content under 2 s.
2. Hero that shrinks into a small photo card on scroll while the story text writes in (vineyard, realevate).
3. One panel per product line, each with its own colour, wiped in by a curved or torn edge (la-boite, myfrida, lpas).
4. Giant footer name that the page lifts off (huxley, esr, house-of-giants).
5. Never use a gate or an 8-10 s loader for a small shop (sweet-tape, la-boite, forge): the three slowest sites lose the first 4-10 s of attention.

## Patterns in this batch

**Openings (14 sites)**
- Opens on a plain ground, then builds its own first screen from type and an object (no loader counter): mecha (grey, the wordmark shrinks to the corner at 2.5 s), two26-studio (pink, logo pops in, usable at about 4 s), format (charcoal, shapes drop, usable at about 3 s), neutomni (red, scissors cut the page, usable at about 4.5 s), extrafazant (a blue flat screen for 1.5 s, then the page, usable at about 2 s), signal-a-studio (usable from the first frame; text decodes in the first second), viv (colour blocks that turn into photos at 1.5 s, usable at about 3 s).
