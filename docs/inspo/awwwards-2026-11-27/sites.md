## Patterns in this batch
- Smooth scroll library (Lenis): 9 of 10 (all except realevate, a single-screen site). Everything scrolls with inertia.
- Full-screen video or cinematic imagery as the first screen: 6 (vineyard, odyssee, huxley, esr, spyker, pragen after its intro). The other four open with a colour or black screen: loader/gate (sweet-tape lime, realevate navy counter, forge black gate, myfrida black brand hero).
- Preloader / intro moment before the hero: 5 (sweet-tape, realevate counter, forge gate, spyker black + logo flash, pragen wireframe drawing).
- Opening has NO big H1 over the photo: 5 (vineyard, odyssee, huxley besides logo, pragen later, forge): the image or the wordmark does the talking; headlines are tiny or short.
- Giant wordmark / oversized word as footer or finale: 6 (huxley, esr, pragen, myfrida cropped tagline, sweet-tape "STICK BY YOU", odyssee pearl with logo; spyker uses a big text menu).
- Big type scale used as decoration: giant words/letters (sweet-tape, huxley, esr, realevate marquee, myfrida footer) = 5.
- Custom cursor in signals: 7 (vineyard, odyssee, sweet-tape, pragen, esr, spyker; note it is invisible in nav, almost never affects layout).
- Tiny, letter-spaced / monospace secondary type with large simple headings: 6 (huxley, pragen, esr, forge, odyssee, spyker). Labels like [01] or 01 numbering: pragen, esr, huxley, odyssee (1/4) = 4.
- A stated scroll-scrubbed list or step sequence where one item is "active": 5 (esr marques, odyssee 1-4, pragen step copy, sweet-tape TO CREATE/FIX/PROTECT, vineyard Sleep/Eat/Meet/Play slider).
- Two-or-three-colour disciplined palettes: 9 of 10 (everyone but myfrida's 4-colour pop); dark-led palettes: 5 (huxley, pragen, esr hero, forge, myfrida hero), neutral cream/white-led: 4 (vineyard, odyssee, spyker, realevate).
- Section colour changes (light to dark blocks) as the main divider, not lines: 7 (vineyard sage/navy, esr, spyker, pragen, myfrida, sweet-tape, huxley).
- WebGL/three or heavy canvas: 5 (sweet-tape, huxley, pragen, esr, forge).
- Persistent CTA: vineyard "Book Now" pill, pragen bottom "Place your interest" bar, esr "Contact", realevate "Our Selection" = 4.
- Hamburger or tiny nav, logo centred: 4 (vineyard, spyker, pragen, huxley).
- Collage / overlapping asymmetrical image placement (images at offset positions, overlapped by text): 4 (odyssee, spyker, sweet-tape, vineyard parallax trio).
- Cookie box overlapping the page in captures: myfrida, spyker, forge (gate). Phone breakage: sweet-tape blank on phone (WebGL/loader risk); else phones work.
- Absent: no stock-photo grids; no testimonials carousel except vineyard's 3-column quotes and huxley press quote slider; no pricing tables; no "features in 3 icons" row (vineyard's 7 line icons are the only icon row); no chat widgets; no sticky phone number; no dark-mode toggle (sweet-tape has a small toggle bottom right only); no gradient-blob/glass SaaS looks; very few sites show the actual price (only realevate's "from 100,000E"); no map except vineyard's illustrated resort map; and no site has a standard hero with H1 + subtext + two buttons on the left.
