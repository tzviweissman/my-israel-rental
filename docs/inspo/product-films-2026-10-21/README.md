## Olipop Banana Cream (can-*.jpg)
A 6 s product film on the product's own colour (soft banana yellow, light from above). The can turns slowly in mid-air while the flavour's own ingredients (banana slices, vanilla pods, vanilla flowers) float around it at different depths, a splash of the drink wraps the can, then everything clears and the can lands upright on a yellow ledge, label to camera: the end frame is a clean packshot. One cause, one motion: the product shows what it is made of, then presents itself.
For a food business: their product in the middle, its real ingredients orbiting it on a ground in its colour, settling into their own real photo as the last frame.
