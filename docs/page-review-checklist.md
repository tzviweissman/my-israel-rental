# Page review checklist (page rules 3r)

Read by a reviewer that did NOT build the page (a Sonnet subagent), before
Tzvi or the owner sees it. The builder fixes every finding, then the reviewer
looks again. Written 20 Oct 2026 after Tzvi found five things on The Whipped
Cup that the rules already covered or should have, and asked why the rules
did not prevent them: the builder was grading its own work, and none of them
was measured.

The scripts catch what can be measured (cut off, contrast, fits the screen,
photos blown up, effects alike, concept sheet). This list is for what a
person notices. Use the page for real: click, hover, tap, order.

For each item answer PASS or FAIL with where and what you saw.

## Consistency (Tzvi: "part of a great website is consistency")
1. Items of the same kind are shown the same way: every flavour, size or
   service thumbnail shows the same kind of picture (one jar each, not one
   jar for one item and four for another), cropped alike, the same size.
2. Prices, units and buttons sit in the same place on every item.
3. One wording per purpose: the same action says the same word everywhere.

## Things respond the way they look
4. Anything that looks pressable responds (cursor, hover, press state).
5. A list beside a big photo changes the photo when you point at or tap an
   item (if the photo shows one item, it shows the one you are on).
6. Every choice the visitor must make appears right under the thing it
   belongs to, the moment they pick it (rules 3l). Order each package or
   option once and check you can choose everything it allows.
7. The order or booking message comes out complete and correct.
7a. Every saving shown is true from the owner's prices (work it out), and
    a real one is not missing: packs against singles, bigger sizes against
    smaller, combined services against their parts (rules 3j).

## Photos
8. No photo looks soft, stretched or zoomed in; each shows its subject whole
   (the jar, the nails, the food), not a slice of it.
9. Photos of one product look like the same product everywhere (3o, one
   look per product).

## The screen
10. Wherever you stop scrolling, what you see makes sense on its own: a
    photo with its words and price, never half of one.
11. Nothing blank: no empty band where a reveal did not run, on laptop or
    phone.
12. The phone order bar never covers the thing you need to press.

## Their business
13. Every fact on the page is theirs. Read their own sources first (the build's
    `src/` folder: record, listing, menu card or flyer pictures) so their own
    words are not flagged; then flag anything not in them, and any of their
    own claims the page's prices contradict (ask the owner, don't print it).
14. The page has at least one small touch only this business would have
    (rules 3p.5), and it works.

Report: a numbered list of FAILs only, each with page, screen size, what you
saw and what would fix it. Say "all pass" if none.
