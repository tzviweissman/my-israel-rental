We never touch the money.

There is no checkout on this page, no basket, no escrow account and nothing called buyer protection, because we do not hold your money and we are not going to pretend otherwise. You agree a price in the chat and you settle it between yourselves, in cash or by whatever transfer you both use.

What we can do is make the other person legible before you go. The counts above come from this site and cannot be edited by the seller.
