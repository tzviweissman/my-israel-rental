## Grammar: gallery / catalog

The kitchen wall is the collection; each station is an object with the same
label schema (name, what is included, which service). The nav is the index of
stations and it jumps. The close is an inquiry plate set like a label.
Others lost: filmic one-shot and continuous world need travel through a place
(this is one wall); split stage is L.A. Cholent's; chaptered editorial needs
long copy; typographic poster wastes the one thing they sell (a whole
kitchen); live surface needs software; cutlist is the wrong pulse for a
careful service.

## Feeling curve

1. Arrival: an empty, unfamiliar kitchen, the headline and proof.
2. Assurance, six times: the camera visits each station; it is marked done and
   ticked in the index.
3. **Peak, relief:** the camera steps back, all six marks lit, "Ready to use."
4. Practical: their pots and cutlery, what can and cannot be kashered.
5. Belonging: rentals and Pesach, all of Israel, English and Hebrew, since 2019.
6. Resolution: the inquiry plate, one button.
