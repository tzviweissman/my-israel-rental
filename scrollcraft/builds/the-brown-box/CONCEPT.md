# The Brown Box, concept

Category: food-shop

Built 15-16 Oct 2026 as one film; reworked 22 Oct 2026 to the food-shop pattern of page rules 3t (light ground, one colour panel per bowl or box, price and Add on every card).

## Ideas
- **One colour panel per product** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/bestbean-colour-per-product.webp): Best Bean gives every coffee its own colour and shows its bag on it. For them: each of the five bowls on its own deep colour (acai purple, mango amber, apple green, kiwi blue, their brown for the one you build), with its own photo, toppings, price and an Add button.
- **A shop card that carries the price and Add** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/theobroma-shop-grid.webp): Theobroma puts the price and Add to cart on every card. For them: bowls, gift boxes and every menu row carry the price and an Add control.
- **A mosaic of the real product** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/lajoya-product-mosaic.webp): La Joya shows each tin as the picture. For them: the toppings drift as round tiles beside the menu heading.
- Own: their kraft brown box with a window, and the acai berry on the branch. The page opens on one unbroken film from the berries on the branch, into the blend, into their clear bowl, to the toppings falling and the bowl sitting in the box.
- Mobbin search 25 Oct, after the build, "dessert shop product menu section with boxes": David (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/97861b6d-985e-448c-9c60-6db7feba6c00: a grid of boxed products, one button each) and Corgi (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9df7bc10-f0b2-4ad1-99b1-2dbab807ab6b: a menu as price-list panels). Compared with the chosen opening: both are menu sections, not openings; our colour panels with a button each are the same grammar as David, and our opening film has no Mobbin counterpart (it comes from the business's own berries-to-box story).
- 21st search 25 Oct, after the build, "product card with add to cart button": Add to Cart Button by bidyut10 (https://21st.dev/@bidyut10/components/add-to-cart-button), a raised button that sinks while adding, then shows an "added" state. Compared with ours: ours sends the bowl to the order page; the added-state idea was not built in. Nothing copied.

## Chosen
The opening film and the colour panels together: the film is the event (page rules 3h) and everything after it follows the food-shop row of 3t, a light ground with the colour changing per bowl and per box, not per section. Every fact and price is theirs (listing, printed menus, WhatsApp catalogue).

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real picture: the bowl in its kraft box at the end of the film, full screen, then the five bowls on their own colours.
2. Signature moment from their own thing: the berries on the branch fall into the blend, it folds into their bowl, the toppings land, and the camera rises as the bowl sits in the box.
3. The choice changes the page: each Add goes to the order page with that bowl or box already in the order, which opens on WhatsApp.
4. Side by side with Best Bean: one product per colour panel with its name, price and an add control; ours holds a clear bowl photo on each. Sources (recorded 25 Oct, after the build): closest Mobbin reference https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/97861b6d-985e-448c-9c60-6db7feba6c00; Inspo docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/bestbean-colour-per-product.webp.
5. One sentence: you scroll a film of an acai bowl being made, then pick your bowl from five colour panels and add it.
