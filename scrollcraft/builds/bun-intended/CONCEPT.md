# Bun Intended, concept

Category: food-shop

## Ideas
- **Box as the product, colour per flavour** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/bestbean-colour-per-product.webp): Best Bean Best Cup gives every coffee its own colour panel with name, notes and a plus button. For them: four flavour cards (Classic cinnamon, Espresso, Lotus, Chocolate), each its own colour with the cut-out bun, the price of a box of 4 and an Add control.
- **Warm cream shop, loud friendly type, price on every card** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/theobroma.webp): Theobroma sells from the first screens with cut-out products on plain cards and Add to cart on each. For them: cream ground, the headline beside photos, flavour cards before the builder.
- **Colour per section from one family** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/delice-colour-per-section.webp): Delice changes flat colour band by band but stays in one family. For them: cream, caramel dough and cocoa only, taken from the logo and price list.
- Own: "Build your box" as a real box, slot by slot, in their kraft tray (their own photo of the box), with the icing poured over the bun they chose.

## Chosen
The first two together with their own box builder. Since 23 Oct 2026 the home page opens on the swirl of words (see Wow gate) instead of the rising photo panels, then shows the four flavours as colour cards that start a box, then the builder where the box fills slot by slot. It is theirs because the box and the bun are their own photo, and the saving shown is their own price list (8 for 100 instead of 110).

## Wow gate
Chosen by Tzvi on 23 Oct 2026: "The swirl of words". Their glazed classic cinnamon bun from above (a sharp top-down photo made from a crop of their own bun, cut round, unretouched), filling the screen on a kraft ground taken from their own tray, with "Bun Intended . sweet things ahead ." lying in the grooves of the bun's own spiral (a spiral fitted to the photo's layer lines, not a perfect curve). Scrolling turns the bun and moves it to the right (to the top on a phone) where it stays big; the words peel off the swirl one strand at a time, outer end first, straighten, drift out and fade, never touching each other; the headline, the line and their box of four with its price and Add arrive beside the bun. On a phone every strand stays on screen. It runs both ways (scrolling back winds it up again).
1. Strongest real photo, big: their glazed classic bun from above, filling about 72 percent of the screen height on a laptop and 92 percent of the width on a phone, with the words in its grooves.
2. Signature moment from their own thing: the words of their name and line wound in the bun's own swirl, then peeled off one strand at a time by the scroll; built in code from their own photo, no generated media.
3. The choice changes the page: the box of four on the card starts the order (Add goes to the builder with Classic cinnamon chosen); the flavour cards and the builder after it are unchanged.
4. Side by side: Monte on Mobbin, slogan running in a circle round a drawing (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2b8ad772-2f97-44a9-bea1-29d14f646b83), and Pizza Amici, ring text round a plate (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/pizza-amici-3-plate-ring-text.webp); ours is the same ring of words round the food, but it is a spiral like the bun and it unwinds on scroll into the headline.
5. One sentence: you land on their bun wrapped in its own words, scroll and watch the words unwind into the headline and their box arrive with the price and Add.
Sources: Mobbin (link above, and a Mobbin food-shop search on 23 Oct); 21st search failed 23 Oct, so the ring and spiral are their own code; Inspo picture above.

## Effects
- button: B-send, from the 21st "Interactive Hover Button"
- link: none
- tile: T-choice, from the 21st "Choice Cards"; flavour cards invert their own colours for the Add control
- reveal: scroll-driven swirl of words, from Monte (Mobbin) and Pizza Amici (docs/inspo ring text); the spiral unwinds into the headline
- steps: none
- price: the box-of-4 price huge and faint behind each bun, from their price list

- touch: the kraft tray drawn from their own box photo, which fills slot by slot

## Not alike

## Style sheet
accent caramel #B07A4E and caramel-deep #8A5532 (button outline and the script line); ink #3A2216; grounds paper #FBF5F0, paper-2 #F3E6DC, dough #E8C08E, white for the price list; flavour colours Classic #F3E6DC, Espresso #6B4430, Lotus #C8894B, Chocolate #4A2A1D; faces Cormorant Garamond (headings), Jost (body), Allura (one script line per heading); radius 22 px cards, 18 px controls, 999 px buttons; motion: hover under 300 ms.

## Effect budget
signature: the box builds bun by bun with the icing pour; accents: the swirl of words unwinding on scroll into the headline with the bun turning and settling on the box card, the card hover with the Add outline filling, the button fill from the 21st hover button, the faint price numeral behind each bun.
