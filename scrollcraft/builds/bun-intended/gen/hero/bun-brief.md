# Bun shot brief (rule 8, right the first time)

Reference: ref-bun-crop.jpg (their glazed classic bun, cropped from src/box4.jpg), plus src/box4.jpg for the tray.
Traits kept: hand-rolled uneven, slightly lopsided spiral; tall puffy dough, deep golden-brown edges, pale cream ridges; thick glossy messy white glaze in wide irregular ribbons, dripping over the edge; cinnamon between the coils; long narrow kraft tray with a silver-foil inner edge.
Changed only: camera to directly above; crisp, well lit.
Model: nano_banana_pro (Higgsfield). Result: bun-top-2.png (24 Oct), approved by Tzvi. Missed: the silver foil edge; a second bun at the top (cropped out).
First try (bun-top.png) rejected: whole box photo as reference and design words ("perfectly centred", "clearly readable spiral") gave a paler, flatter, perfect-spiral bun in a square tray.

Prompt used:
The SAME cinnamon bun as in the first reference photo, keep it exactly as it is: the same hand-rolled, uneven, slightly lopsided spiral; the same tall puffy dough with deep golden-brown edges and pale cream ridges; the same thick, glossy, messy white glaze that pools and drips in wide irregular ribbons over the swirl; cinnamon visible between the coils. Only change the camera: look at it from directly above so the whole spiral is seen, and make it a crisp, sharp, well-lit photograph. It sits in their long, narrow brown kraft paper tray with a silver-foil inner edge (as in the second reference), the tray running top to bottom, the bun filling most of the tray width and most of the frame, centred. Soft warm natural daylight from one side, like their photo. Keep their real colours, do not make it paler, flatter, neater or more symmetrical. No text, no hands, no other buns, no added props.
