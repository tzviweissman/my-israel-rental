# Michal Balloons, concept

Category: events

Business: balloon decor for events in Jerusalem and the area (arches, table centrepieces, photo stations, custom printed balloons). Hebrew first, English switch. Facts are hers only (record, listing, WhatsApp catalogue). No prices on the page (Michal asked for none).

## Ideas
- **Monarque: floating small photos round one line** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/events-sports-homes/monarque-sheet.webp): a short page, ground changes per section, small photos, little text, giant name footer.
- **Boom Ball: loud colour grounds, one button** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/events-sports-homes/boomball-sheet.webp): cream, navy, mustard blocks; the same button all the way down.
- **OFFF Forum: one film screen and a giant logo** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/events-sports-homes/offforum-sheet.webp): one opening, then almost nothing.
- Own: the arch. Her balloons rise into an empty cream room and gather into an arch around her line (the opening film, kept), and the page ends on her name drawn in small balloons that scatter and pop (the touch).
- Mobbin search 25 Oct, after the build, "event decoration balloons hero section": bella (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1a1af3a8-4165-4918-a511-71e3745b0886: balloons lifting the product in a sky) and Eventbrite (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/461338aa-4933-442c-884a-6cc590e932bf: an event photo under a big headline). Compared with the chosen opening: bella also uses balloons as the main picture; ours gathers them into an arch round her line, and Eventbrite leads with a photo and headline where ours is an event.

## Chosen
The own idea for the opening and the close, Monarque's structure between them: after the film a strip of her real events, four occasions as text columns with a small photo each, what she makes, the photo station, her work by occasion, the quote card, good to know. Ground changes in her own balloon colours (cream, sage, blush, gold, white).

## Wow gate

2. Signature: balloons gather into an arch round "Balloon design that lifts your event"; the closing name is made of balloons you can pop.
3. The visitor's choice changes the page: an occasion chip rearranges her work; the quote card writes the WhatsApp message.
