# Finest Cuts, concept

Category: food-shop

The owner's look wins over the category's bright ground: their logo and their board photo are black and amber. One direction chosen from the research, because it needs nothing but their own photo and numbers.

## Ideas
- **Light finds the subject in the dark** (docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-04/flashlight-hero.jpg): a dark world where only what is lit is seen. For them: their board on black stone with one part lit at a time.
- **A chapter line that changes with the picture** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/haunted-boulder-city-2-spotlight-chapter.webp): for them, "1 of 4 types of chicken" with the picture moving to that part.
- **Anatomy labels** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/banh-mi-viet-nam-2-anatomy-labels.webp): dropped until the owner names the four types.
- **One product, one object lit on dark under one line** (docs/inspo/moto-card-2026-10-21/hero-2500.webp): the first screen's mood.
- Own: their listing's "4 types of chicken and 3 Sauces" and their flyer's "The crunch you crave": the opening counts them out on the real board.

References kept: sweetgreen https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a7dbacd8-c85d-459f-b06f-635450c43365 (words beside the dish on dark stone), David https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/16443a75-7287-480c-aca4-7a85e4fd4239 (stepper, button with the price), MANA https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/397dea4b-1996-40fc-9527-88406c00385b (one product page), Blue Apron https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/034ae094-4059-4b93-8055-af18da44b565 (price above the button), Beard Meats Food https://www.beardmeatsfood.co.uk (chunky type on charcoal). Picked the light on the real board because a dish this full sells itself once you are made to look at each part.

## Chosen
F, Golden mountains (Tzvi, 6 Nov 2026, after "it just focuses on different chicken its not wow"). A tiny camera flies low over their chicken as if it were a mountain range, thin steam drifting past like cloud; their three sauce cups pass like lakes; it climbs until the mountains are one board on black, landing exactly on their real photo, with the price and the button. The film follows the scroll.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real photo, big: the film lands on their board photo, whole, filling the screen on laptop and phone.
2. Signature moment from their own thing: their own board and their own logo cups become the landscape, and the visitor's scroll flies over it.
3. The visitor's choice changes the page: "Your table" or "Someone else's table" changes the section's picture, ground, words and button, and carries into the order form (who it is for).
4. Side by side: docs/inspo/awwwards-2026-10-18/united-5-from-above-clouds.webp is a camera high above a subject through cloud; ours flies below the peaks in steam, on real food. The ending comes from docs/inspo/moto-card-2026-10-21/hero-2500.webp, one object lit on a dark ground under one line. The first screen's composition comes from sweetgreen on Mobbin (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a7dbacd8-c85d-459f-b06f-635450c43365).
5. One sentence: you land inside a landscape of golden crust, and your scroll flies you over it, past the sauce cups like lakes, then up until it is one board with the price.

## Effects
- button: B11, one light pass, the same warm light as the opening crossing the button on hover, from the effects library entry and a fresh 21st search "button add to cart" (https://21st.dev/@bidyut10/components/add-to-cart-button) for the sink on press
- link: L2, line grows from the middle, from the effects library
- tile: none, the contact tiles are plain facts and do not respond, because they press nothing; the plain tile comes from a fresh Mobbin search (MANA and David product sections)
- reveal: R5, scale up into place, each block settles in like a board set down, after the film climbs out to the whole board; fresh from 21st search "scale in reveal on scroll" (unlumen Scroll Reveal Image, 8 Nov 2026), replacing R3 blur, which Hadassah Prys also uses
- steps: S5, a plain numbered list, from the effects library, because the three order steps need no effect; the order step bar carries the sauce cups below
- price: P1, big numerals with a small raised sign, from the Blue Apron section on Mobbin (price right above the button)
- heading: none, the headings stay still so the light is the one moving idea
- touch: X38, the three order steps are their three sauce cups from their own photo: a cup lights up per step, because the board has three sauces and the order has three steps
- other: X37, the opening: a tiny camera flies over their board as a landscape, a generated film on the scroll, from docs/inspo/awwwards-2026-10-18/united-5-from-above-clouds.webp; X18, the your table or someone else's choice tints the section, from https://21st.dev/@halaska-studio/components/segmented-control

## Style sheet
accent: crust gold #CE8D09 from their board photo (3g.19) on the main button only (black on the amber band); ink #FBF4E8; ground #14110E; surface #211B16; line #3F352B; faces Secular One (headings) and Rubik (body); radius 14 px, pills for buttons; motion: opening tied to the scroll, hover under 300 ms, nothing loops, no linear easing, reduced motion shows the whole board still and the count as words. Effect budget: signature the flight over the board; accents: the arriving opening, a scroll-tied detail mid-page, the closing name; quiet touches: blur-in reveals, hover and press.
