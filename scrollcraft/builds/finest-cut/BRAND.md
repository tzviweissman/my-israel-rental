# Finest Cuts brand (from their logo and flyer)

Logo: their round mark, black with an amber flame and rooster, script "Finest Cuts", "Israel" under it. Taken from the lid of their sauce cup in their own board photo. It reads on dark grounds only; on the amber band the name is set in type.

## Colours and uses
- Ground #14110E: every page, black from the logo and the photo's stone
- Surface #211B16: cards and form panels
- Ink #FBF4E8: main text and prices
- Soft #CDBFAC: quieter text
- Line #3F352B: thin rules, field edges
- Crust gold #CE8D09, the golden crust of their chicken in assets/board.webp (rule 3g.19; was amber #F6A313 before 8 Nov 2026): the main button only, with #14110E words; and the one statement band as a ground, where the button turns black

## Fonts
- Secular One: headings (one weight, from the approved list, heavy like their flyer's headline)
- Rubik 400, 500, 700: everything else

## Type and spacing
The starter's scale, nothing in between: sizes --t-1 to --t5, gaps --s1 to --s10. One radius: 14 px for pictures and cards; pills for buttons.

## Textures
Their dark stone from the board photo, extended to the screen's shape. No drawn textures.

## Design bans
- No stickers, giant word opening or red: Chicken Munch owns those.
- No wood backgrounds as a page ground: the flyer has them, but only their photos may show wood.
- No generated food on the page; no generated motion without Tzvi's yes.
- No flame graphics or fire effects: nothing says the food is grilled over fire.
- No names for the four types until the owner sends them.
- No servings count, delivery fee, lead time or hours until the owner gives them.
