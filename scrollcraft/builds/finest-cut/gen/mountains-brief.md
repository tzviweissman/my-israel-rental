# Finest Cuts: the Golden mountains film (6 Nov 2026)

Tzvi, on the board tour: "it just focuses on different chicken its not wow generate a wow moment which is memorable".
Picked row F on gen/storyboard.png with its steps and price.

## Steps
1. A tiny camera skims low over ridges of their crust as if they were mountains, thin steam drifting past like cloud. Words: "The crunch you crave".
2. Their three sauce cups pass underneath like lakes, their logo going by.
3. The camera climbs and climbs until the mountains are one board on black.
4. The board, whole, with the price and the button. It lands on their real photo.

## Every object
- The chicken: their board (src/owner/p02.jpg), the same pieces throughout; it never changes shape, count or colour. Only the camera moves.
- The three sauce cups with their logo: where they are in the photo, passed from above; they do not move.
- The wooden board under the food: seen only as the camera climbs, as in the photo.
- The black stone ground: their photo's own, extended; it does not change.
- Thin steam: rises from the food, drifts one way, thins out over it; never a wall of smoke, never crossing the end frame.
- No hands, no knife, no text in the picture, nothing added to the board. Words and the button are the page's own, over the film.

## How it really happens
Only a camera moves over still food, so nothing has to break or flow. From the commercial food shoots watched for docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-01/real-food-filming.md
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqB5tmUHCkk): "open very close on texture and pull out to reveal", one smooth motion-control move, never handheld, the product sharp.
Steam is thin wisps lit from behind and above, drifting one way (the Baileys shoot in the same notes, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sjvmIEKauGI). Light: warm on the food, cooler behind.

## Key moment
The climb: from low over the cups to the whole board. It gets its own clip, between its own before still (S2, low past the cups) and after still (S3, their real photo on the black stone), never shared with the skim.
