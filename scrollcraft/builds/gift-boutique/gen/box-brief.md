# Brief: the Gift Boutique opening box (rule 8, R8.7)

Recorded after the fact (25 Oct). Made on 14 Oct (commit cfb29f5d); the brief rule came on 24 Oct, so no brief existed before.

## What was generated
Keyframe stills from Higgsfield soul/standard (gen/ribbon/still.py, text only) and a MiniMax H3 image-to-video clip between the first and last frame (gen/ribbon/clip.py): a ribboned box that unties, the lid lifts, light pours out, then their six real gift photographs rise out of it. Files: assets/open-first.webp, open-last.webp, open.mp4 and the phone set open-m-*.
The prompt texts were not kept in any file or commit (only the two scripts, which take the prompt as an argument), so they are not reproduced here. On 23 Oct a second phone clip (5 s MiniMax H3, 0c9aff22, 10 credits) came out darker and was not used (hero-shots.md).

## Reference crop and traits kept
None: the box is not their item. They have no photograph of a box, so the box is invented and shown as a box, not as their premises. Their six gifts on the page are their own photographs, unedited.

## Result compared with their photo
`box-compare.jpg`: one of their own gift photos (left, a baby-boy gift tray) beside the last film frame (right, a black box with gold ribbon and light). Different worlds on purpose: the box is a cinematic container, the gifts are theirs. Looked at 25 Oct: the black box and gold ribbon do not claim to be their packaging; their gifts keep their own look when they rise.
