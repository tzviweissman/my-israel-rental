# The Gift Boutique, concept

Category: fashion-shop (rule 3t, 22 Oct 2026): gifts to browse and buy. The opening is the one dark screen; after it the page is ivory throughout, the collection comes straight after the opening, how to order is a strip of three cards, no full-screen photo bands. Reworked 22 Oct from the dark Moto-style version of 21 Oct (kept in gen/index-before-category.html).

Business: hand-wrapped gift boxes from Jerusalem, delivered across Israel, ordered by choosing gifts on the order page and sending the order on WhatsApp. Prices in US dollars as in their own catalogue. Some items are out of stock and are shown as such.

## Ideas
- **A black box that opens onto the gifts** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/aardvark-1-books-shelf.webp): Aardvark Book Club opens on the product itself, with the real covers on a shelf right below. For her: the box opens and the real gifts rise out of it, then the collection sits directly under it.
- **The whole home page is the shop** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/outfit-1-shop-grid-home.webp): Outfit puts name and price under every product on the first screens. For her: the grid of gifts with price and an Add chip comes straight after the opening.
- **Shop grid with sold-out shown honestly** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/oddritual-1-all-products-grid.webp): Odd Ritual shows a sold-out badge in the grid. For her: "Out of stock" items stay on show, greyed.
- Own: the gold ribbon. Their logo is gold foil on black and every gift is tied with ribbon, so the opening is the ribbon untying and the lid lifting, and the name at the end is cropped like a label on a box.

## Chosen
The first and the last: the untying box as the one dark event, then the shop grid at once, in ivory, the way Outfit and Odd Ritual sell (grid first, a short how-it-works strip, then the details). It is hers because the box and ribbon are her logo's gold foil made into the first screen.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real material, big: the generated box filling the whole first screen, their own six gift photographs rising out of it.
2. Signature moment only they could have: the black gift box unties, the lid lifts and their six gifts rise out of the light, driven by the scroll.
3. The visitor's choice changes the page: the scroll opens the box; in the collection a group pill changes the grid, and an Add chip puts the gift in the order.
4. Side by side: Aardvark Book Club (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/aardvark-1-books-shelf.webp). Theirs is a flat shelf of covers; ours opens a box onto the real gifts.
5. One sentence: you land on a closed black gift box, and as you scroll the ribbon unties, the lid lifts and their gifts rise out of it.
