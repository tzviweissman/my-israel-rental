# Gifted Israel: brand kit (rules 3x.7)

Written before any picture. No picture is generated for this site: every product photo is their own (the
Waterdale studio shots on GiftedIsrael.com), the opening is their Diamond Menorah photo on its own white, and the
clip is their own Chanukah video. Source of the colours: their flyer and logo (src/owner/p01.jpg).

## Colours
- Ground `#FFFFFF`: the page, and the white their product photos stand on, so a piece never sits in a box.
- Stone `#F5F1EC`: the one second surface (cards, the order steps, the "who we are" room).
- Ink `#1F1B1A`: all text, prices included (3g.3), the charcoal of GIFTED on their logo.
- Soft `#5F5752`: secondary text (7.1:1 on white).
- Gift red `#6F0D0C`: their logo's red, and the red of the painted pomegranates on their own Rosh Hashanah Painted Pomegranate Honey Dish (`assets/g/gia0015d7c59.webp`), a shade of the product's colour too: the pomegranate red #7A351B on that dish is within 16 of it (rules 3g.19, product-colour on the home page). The main button only, white text on it (12.6:1), and the closing band.
- Line `#E6DFD7`: hairlines.
- Engraving gold `#5A4415` on their gold base: only inside the engraving preview, as letters cut into the metal.

## Fonts
- Headings: **David Libre** (approved list, traditional and warm, has Hebrew), one weight. It replaced Bellefair on 8 Nov 2026 after Hadassah Prys went live with Bellefair first; Tzvi chose Bona Nova, but The Brown Box already has it, so David Libre is the one approved serif no live site uses.
- Text: **Alef** (approved list, plain and readable, has Hebrew), 400 and 700. The ₪ is set in Alef (3o.5).

## Type and spacing scale (3x.13)
The starter's scales, unchanged: text `--t-1` .8125rem, `--t0` 1.0625rem, `--t1` 1.3125rem, `--t2` 1.625rem,
`--t3` to `--t5` growing with the screen. Gaps `--s1` 4px to `--s10` 128px. Nothing is sized in between.

## Logo
- On light: the red gift-box G from the flyer, keyed off its grey square (`assets/mark.png`), beside "Gifted Israel"
  in David Libre. The flyer's mark is small; a sharp file is asked for (OWNER-QUESTIONS.md is about the business only,
  so this goes in the report, not to the owner as a design question).
- On dark (the red closing band and the footer): the name in white David Libre, no mark (the red mark disappears on red).

## Textures and grounds
- None generated. Their own white is the texture: the pieces' reflections on it. One stone room and one red band
  are the only other grounds.

## Design bans (from 3c, grown with every look turned down on this site)
- No cream with serif and one italic word, no gradients, no frosted glass, no three identical cards.
- No gift ribbons, bows or unwrapping (The Gift Boutique's), no gold glint across words (X19, The Gift Boutique).
- No generated Lucite, glass or flame: see-through things read fake when generated (docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-01).
- No product in a frame. Since 6 Nov 2026 each shop picture sets the piece in a generated set of its category's colour (Tzvi, from the Pinterest product pictures; gen/backdrops-brief.md); the opening's pile stays on its own white.
- No red anywhere but the main button and the closing band.
- No "luxury", "exclusive", "expert" or "curated" in our words; their own lines only.
