# Gifted Israel: opening ideas (scripts/check-ideas.mjs)

## A. Engraved for them
- motion: engrave, words are cut into their Diamond Menorah's gold base letter by letter, then the visitor types their own
- sources: docs/inspo/moto-card-2026-10-21/hero-2500.webp, docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-04/brief-form-live-summary.jpg
- steps: 1 the menorah stands whole on its own white beside the headline. 2 a thin point of light runs along the gold base and "Chanukah 5787" is cut in behind it, letter by letter, in about two seconds. 3 a field under the piece says "Try your own engraving"; what the visitor types is cut into the same base as they type. 4 the Add button carries the menorah, its price and the words to engrave into the basket.

## B. Through the year
- motion: calendar, the Jewish year turns card by card (Shabbos, Havdalah, Chanukah, Rosh Hashana) and each card holds its own piece
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/tedxportland-1-year-card-strip.webp, docs/inspo/moto-card-2026-10-21/l14.webp
- steps: 1 tall cards in a row, one per occasion they sell for. 2 the card for the coming one (Chanukah) is open, wider, with its piece and from-price. 3 tapping another card opens it and the shop link under it changes to that occasion.

## C. Through the Lucite
- motion: refract, a clear Lucite block slides across the headline and the words behind it bend and magnify like through their pieces
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/flux-theory-1-steamed-glass-opening.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/serotoninn-2-all-products-grid.webp
- steps: 1 the headline on white. 2 the scroll slides a clear block across it. 3 the words under the block bend. 4 the block settles as their brachos block. Dropped: a drawn clear block is a generated look-alike of their piece, and see-through things read fake (BRAND.md bans).

## D. Own: Set your Shabbos table
- motion: candlelight, their own line "Set your Shabbos table": the opening is dim and a switch lights their candle-lighting tray
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/halcyon-lighting-1-lights-off-toggle.webp, docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-04/flashlight-hero.jpg
- steps: 1 their Resin Curve Hadlokas Neiros Tray on a dim ground. 2 a switch reads "Light the candles". 3 the light comes up on the tray and the line "Set your Shabbos table" (their site's own heading) arrives. Their photos have no lit candles, so the flames are generated (Tzvi, 5 Nov 2026: the engraving opening "isnt so wow"); this idea moves from their Shabbos tray to the Diamond Menorah, lit candle by candle for the eight nights, since Chanukah is the season they sell for now.

## E. Own: Painted by Batya
- motion: paint, a watercolour wash blooms across clear Lucite and becomes their painted menorah, as their own Chanukah clip shows the finished piece
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/morpho-water-1-film-chapters-big-type.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/flux-theory-1-steamed-glass-opening.webp
- steps: 1 a clear Lucite sheet stands empty on a white table. 2 scrolling, peach paint spreads through it from the bottom and the menorah's branches form. 3 the gold letters settle onto the sheet. 4 the camera pulls back to the finished Painted by Batya tray with the shop button.

## F. Rip it open
- motion: tear, the whole screen is wrapping paper in their red and the scroll rips it across, the way a gift gets opened, with the shop behind it
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/glowing-cheetah-1-torn-edge-hero.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/bonito-flakes-for-pets-1-torn-edge-photo-stickers.webp
- steps: 1 the screen is one sheet of red wrapping paper with "For someone special" on it. 2 scrolling, a tear starts at one edge and runs across, the two sides curling back. 3 behind it, on their white, their real photos from every part of the shop. 4 the paper is gone, the headline and the shop button stay.

## G. Own: Lift the lid
- motion: lid, the G box of their own logo lifts its lid, the tissue folds back and the camera sinks in to find the shop
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-2026-10-18/butter-4-icons-land.webp, docs/inspo/moto-card-2026-10-21/hero-2500.webp
- steps: 1 one closed box with their G stands on white. 2 scrolling, the lid lifts straight up and away. 3 tissue paper folds back. 4 the camera sinks in and the inside of the box opens out into a tray of their pieces and the shop button.

## H. What is the occasion
- motion: finder, the opening asks the occasion, and from a pile of their pieces on the floor those for the answer rise and flow into a line
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/delta-cafes-3-floating-packs-quiz.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/itsua-1-intent-picker.webp
- steps: 1 their pieces float around "Who is the gift for?". 2 the visitor taps Host, New home, Wedding, Thank you or Me. 3 most pieces drift away and five for that answer come forward with their prices. 4 the button takes those five to the shop.
