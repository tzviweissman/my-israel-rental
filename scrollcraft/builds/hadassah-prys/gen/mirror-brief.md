# The mirror still (rule 8.7, generate once, compare beside her flyer)

**What is generated:** one still, wide (16:9) and tall (9:16), through the Higgsfield API. Two images per shape at most.
No film: the bulbs coming on is light drawn in code over the still (IDEAS.md, chosen idea A). If a film is wanted later,
its own brief goes to Tzvi first.

**Close crop of the one item:** `gen/brush-crop.jpg`, cut from her flyer `src/owner/p02.jpg`.

**The brush's traits, written from the crop and kept:** full domed bristles, deep rose at the tip fading paler at the
root; a tapered polished copper-rose band with a darker rim; a smooth pale pink handle. No lettering on it.

**What changes:** the setting and the light only. The brush lies on a table instead of floating in powder.

**The setting (generated, never presented as her studio):** a round-bulbed makeup mirror on a table against a plain soft
pink wall in even daylight. Bulbs are off, frosted white globes. The mirror reflects only the plain wall. No person, no
hands, no face, no reflection of anyone, no words, no logos, no bottles with labels.

**Frame.** Wide: mirror on the right two thirds, its whole frame and all bulbs inside the picture, the left third plain
wall for the words. Tall: mirror whole in the upper part, the lower third plain for the words. Nothing cut by an edge.

**Light (3h.11):** bright, soft light from the side, ground mid to light, the mirror the brightest and sharpest thing.

**Checks after generating:** beside the flyer crop (same brush?), `scripts/brightness.py`, bulbs countable, no text, no
person in the glass. Bulb centres are measured on the picture and put in the page by fractions.

Price asked first through get_cost: nano_banana_2 at 2k is 2 credits per image. The API does not report per-call charges.
