# Brand: Hadassah Prys, makeup artist (hadassah-prys)

Written 6 Nov 2026 before any picture was made (rule 3x.7). Taken from her own logo (a rose script on pink, with a heart
in the double s) and her flyer (rose bristles, a copper band, pink powder, soft light).

## Colours

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ground` | `#FFF9F8` | page ground, a warm white so the logo pink reads as colour |
| `--surface` | `#F8D3DB` | her logo's own pink: cards, the statement band, the booking summary |
| `--ink` | `#35121C` | all text, prices, outline buttons, the one dark band |
| `--soft` | `#6B4450` | secondary text |
| `--accent` | `#A9334D` | the main button only (her logo's deepest rose, white text 6.4:1) |
| `--accent-ink` | `#FFFFFF` | text on the main button |
| `--line` | `#EBCBD2` | dividers, field borders |
| `--bulb` | `#FFE9C7` | the warm light of a bulb, only on lit things |

## Fonts (both on Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License, Hebrew subset listed; approved list, design rules)

- Headings: **Bellefair**, thin and high contrast, one weight. It sits beside her script logo without competing with it.
- Text: **Assistant** 400, 600 and 700.
- The ₪ is set in the text face.

## Type scale and spacing scale (rule 3x.13)

The starter's scales, nothing in between: text `--t-1` to `--t5`, gaps `--s1` to `--s10`. One radius, `--r: 14px`, the
corner of a powder compact's pan seen from above; the main button uses the same radius.

## Logo

Her script logo, cut from its pink ground: rose on light grounds, pale pink on the dark band. Never redrawn or recoloured
on a light ground. Her name appears in type beside it where a reader needs it.

## Textures

- A generated makeup mirror with round bulbs in a pink room, once with bulbs off and once on (gen/mirror-brief.md). Not presented as her studio: nothing in the record says she has one.
- Her flyer's brush (rose bristles, copper band, pink handle), redrawn by the generator from a crop of the flyer, on the table and beside the price.

## Design bans

- No generated person, face, lips or eyes, ever, shown as her or a client.
- No flyer crops or flyer text on the page as pictures (rule 3g.15).
- No before and after, no skin claims beyond her own words (natural glam, radiant skin).
- No sparkle stars as decoration, no two-colour gradients, no frosted glass, no cream with one italic word.
- No lips, lipstick kisses or eyelash icons as ornaments.
- No stars, review counts or "brides love" lines: she has no reviews and her listing does not name brides.
- No words "studio", "salon", "certified" or "bridal": none is in her record.
