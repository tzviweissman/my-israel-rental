## Style sheet (3g.1)
- Ground warm white #FFF9F8; rooms of her logo pink #F8D3DB; ink #35121C; soft #6B4450; line #EBCBD2; one dark band in ink.
- Accent, main button only (3g.3): her logo's deepest rose #A9334D, white text 6.4:1.
- Faces: Bellefair for headings at weight 400, Assistant 400, 600 and 700 for text (both approved, both with Hebrew); the ₪ in the text face.
- One radius 14px; one soft shadow 0 3px 30px tinted rose.
- Type and spacing: the starter scales only (BRAND.md).
- Motion: under 0.7 s for anything but the bulbs, eased, never linear; the bulbs are the only thing that moves by itself; with reduced motion the room is simply lit.
