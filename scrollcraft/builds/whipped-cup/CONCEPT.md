# The Whipped Cup, concept

## Ideas
- **Film with one big line** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/morpho-water-1-film-chapters-big-type.webp): Morpho opens on a full-screen film with one huge light serif line ("Follow the feeling"). For her: a sea of clouds at sunrise, with "Ever wanted to take a walk in the clouds?"
- **A window that holds while the world moves behind it** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/ballena-fine-dining-1-floating-video-window.webp): Ballena keeps one window still in the centre while scenes slide behind it. For her: a cloud-shaped window opens in the middle of the sky film, holding her real photo of the whipped cloud on her iced coffee, while the sky keeps drifting around it.
- **Calm sky, very little on screen** (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/wafer-1-sky-hero.webp): Wafer's sky photo with one short line and one small button. Rejected alone: too quiet, no event.
- **Product floating in a coloured room** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/ciao-energy-2-zero-cloud-can.webp): Ciao's can floats with words around it. Rejected: her jars are only in screenshots, too soft to cut out cleanly.
- Own: her tagline "your cup, your cloud" and her own "how do you use it" card (ice, cold milk, whipped coffee). Tzvi's idea from it: a walk on the clouds that becomes the cloud in her cup.

## Chosen
The first two together, driven by her own tagline. The page opens full screen on the generated sea of clouds (scenery only, never her coffee) with a small figure walking and the line "Ever wanted to take a walk in the clouds?" As you scroll, a cloud-shaped window opens in the middle holding her REAL photo of the whipped cloud on her iced coffee, the line changes to "Your cup, your cloud.", and the window grows until her photo fills the screen with the Order button. Her three steps then fill the drawn glass further down. It is hers because it is her tagline made into a scene, and every coffee shown is her own photo.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real photo, big: her iced coffee photo grows from the cloud window to fill the screen.
2. Signature moment from her own thing: her tagline "your cup, your cloud" as a walk on the clouds that becomes the cloud on her coffee; her own three steps as the glass filling.
3. The choice changes the page: the visitor's scroll opens the window and changes the line; in the order form each package shows its own choices.
4. Side by side: Morpho and Ballena openings (pictures above); ours is a film with one big line and a window, like theirs, with her own photo inside.
5. One sentence: you land on a walk above the clouds, and as you scroll a cloud opens onto her whipped coffee.
