# Nails by Elisheva: concept

Category: beauty

Rule 3t (22 Oct 2026). Pattern from Odyssee Clinic in docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/odyssee-1-intro-and-booking.webp: one warm cream ground after the opening, her own close-up photos framed inside the cream beside her words, treatments as rows with price and duration, "Book a time" fixed in the top bar all the way down, a booking page with day, time and details.

## Style sheet
Ground cream #FFF8F6, blush #F6E1DE for photo frames, ink #3A2523 for text and the closing grounds, accent #84423C on the main button only. Bodoni Moda headings, Outfit body, Parisienne for her name only. One radius: 18px images, pill buttons. Motion: the shade reveal 1.35 s ease-out, scroll reveals under 0.5 s, nothing loops.

## Ideas
- Close-up first, from docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/odyssee-1-intro-and-booking.webp: a full-screen close-up of her nails, the page otherwise quiet cream.
- Product stage in the shade you pick, from docs/inspo/product-pages-2026-10-20 and docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/vero-1-product-page-commission.webp: her photo big beside the service, price and time.
- Plan and date together, from docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/tengile-1-plan-your-stay.webp: day, time and details on one booking page.
- Own: tap a polish shade and her photo in that colour opens out from the chip over the whole opening, because a client chooses a colour first.

## Chosen
The Own idea, shade first, because choosing the colour is the first thing a client does at her table, and every shade is her own photo. Odyssee's cream ground and framed close-ups carry the rest of the page, as the beauty row of rule 3t asks.

## Wow gate
1. Clients land on her nails full screen and a shade opens from the chip; it is about nails, not decoration.
2. The picture changes with the visitor's tap and the page takes the shade.
3. Close-up first, cream after: it opens loud and settles quiet.
4. The tapped shade spreads her photo of that colour from the chip across the screen.
5. Cream ground with her own photos, not a salon template of dark gold and stock hands.
