# Gremier Coffee: concept (rules 3q)

Category: food-shop (rule 3t, drinks shop with an events side): bottled cold brew, mini packs, jugs and syrups for
home and office, and self-serve coffee bars for events. Their own site (gremiercoffee.co.il) sells like the shops in
docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22: each product big in its own sunlit photo, a grid with filters on top, price
and Add on every card. All references: REFERENCES.md. Ideas in full: IDEAS.md. Brand: BRAND.md. Facts: src/facts.md.

## Style sheet (3g.1)
- Ground #F6EEE3 (their sunlit wall, lighter); one oak surface #ECDFCC; one dark cold brew band #3B0A02 to close.
- Ink #1C1410, soft #5E5148, line #DCCFBC.
- Accent, main button only (3g.3, 3g.19): cold brew #3B0A02 with white text. Prices bold in ink.
- Faces: Noto Serif (+ Noto Serif Hebrew) for headings, IBM Plex Sans (+ Hebrew) for text; ₪ in IBM Plex Sans.
- One radius 14px; one soft warm shadow; no gradients over photos.
- Motion under 0.5 s, eased; the steeping is the one loud moment.

## Effect budget (3f.2)
- Signature: the cold brew journey film (GC-F1).
- Accents: the coffee bar's guest pills change the photo; each coffee's card carries its label stripe.
- Quiet touches: GC-B1 on buttons, L4 links, GC-T1 tiles, GC-R1 reveals, GC-P1 prices.

## Wow gate
1. Their strongest thing, big: their cold brew being made, full screen, ending on their own sunlit coffee bar photo.
2. The signature only they could have: their own process (beans chosen for cold brew, brewed for hours, not minutes) as one shot down to their real bar.
3. The visitor's choice changes the page: the guest count in the coffee bar section picks the photo of that size of bar and the button's words.
4. Side by side with a reference: the Pinterest coffee site Tzvi sent (docs/inspo/pinterest-coffee-2026-11-11/grind-pour-sip-journey.png) makes hot pour-over; ours makes cold brew and ends on their real bar. PULP's smoothie chain (docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-07/pulp-smoothie-chain.jpg) changes one product per chapter by cutting; ours changes one jar slowly in place, which is how cold brew is made. Mooala on Inspo (mooala-com) shows bottle, glass and ingredients still; ours moves. Mobbin's Assembly Coffee (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/747e1e51-77c1-4dd5-a556-a67199054387) lists its coffees beside a still box; ours lists them under a jar that is brewing. The 21st search "button liquid fill hover coffee" gave the button's rising coffee level.
5. One sentence: you land as beans pour into a grinder, and as you scroll they fall out as fine powder into cold water that steeps dark, run over ice, milk swirls in, and the camera pulls back to their coffee bar.
