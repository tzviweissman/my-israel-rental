# Gremier Coffee Co.: opening ideas (scripts/check-ideas.mjs)

Every idea is built in code from their own photos (no paid generation), and the first screen shows both bottles and a coffee bar before any scroll.

## A. Own: Brewed for hours, not minutes
- motion: steep, a clock hand sweeps round and round while a pale watery tint drains out of the dispenser jar until it is dark coffee
- sources: docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-07/pulp-smoothie-chain.jpg, docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/still-2-flavour-glow-swap.webp
- steps: 1 their 01-sunlit-closeup photo fills the screen: the dispenser jar with its tap, the three bottles and the iced glass on the oak table, the jar covered by a pale amber tint so it reads as water. 2 a thin clock ring beside the words sweeps round several times (no number of hours: their site gives none) while the tint drains downward out of the jar and it darkens to cold brew; their line "Brewed for hours, not minutes" sits on the wall. 3 the ring stops, and their line "We brew it slowly, in the cold, so it comes out smooth and never bitter" and one button, Shop cold brew, settle under it (3g.13: one button on the first screen).

## B. Swirl
- motion: swirl, milk spirals into the iced coffee in their tall glass as the screen's one moving thing
- sources: docs/inspo/product-films-2026-10-21/can-4.6.jpg, docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-07/pulp-smoothie-chain.jpg
- steps: 1 their 10-bright-minimal-v3 photo (jar, three bottles, glass, cups and straws on a table in a bright hall) fills the screen. 2 over the glass a code-drawn displacement (an SVG turbulence filter) makes the milk pattern already in their glass turn slowly like cream in coffee, and a few ice cubes bob. 3 the scroll widens the swirl until it is the page's cream ground and the headline "Elevate every day" lands over it with the shop button.

## C. Frost
- motion: frost, cold creeps in from the screen edges as a pale film, then thaws in rings back to a clear cold photo
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/flux-theory-1-steamed-glass-opening.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/delta-cafes-1-film-hero-chapter-lines.webp
- steps: 1 the screen is their 11-burst-classic photo (a mini 4-pack on ice, beans) seen through a condensation film that is mostly opaque. 2 as the scroll starts, frost grows in at the corners then thaws in rings from the middle, droplets sliding in code. 3 the thaw shows the photo and three chapter lines one after another: bottles, mini 4-packs, coffee bars. 4 the film is gone and the headline "Cold brew delivered" and the button stay.

## D. Own: Make it a coffee bar
- motion: guests, a "how many guests" switch fills the table with more dispensers, cups and a rising price
- sources: docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-04/mode-tabs-numbers.jpg, docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/itsua-1-intent-picker.webp
- steps: 1 their 01-sunlit-closeup (one dispenser with bottles) fills the screen, with four pills 25, 50, 75 and 100+ guests under the headline "Make it a coffee bar". 2 tapping 75 crossfades to their 09-coffee-bar photo (several dispensers on a long table) while the number of cups drawn in a row and the "from" price (from 349) change. 3 the button writes the chosen guest count into the WhatsApp order message. Prices for each guest count are an open question for the owner, so only "from 349" is shown.

## E. Own: Order by Wednesday

- steps: 1 tall day cards in a row, each with a crop of their own photos (bottle, 4-pack, jug, dispenser, cups); the open card is Wednesday, "Order by Wednesday". 2 the scroll moves the open card day by day, a small bottle marker travelling with it, to Friday where the card reads "Receive by Shabbat". 3 below the strip the headline "Stock your fridge" and the order button appear; pickup and event delivery on a chosen date sit as two more small cards.


## F. Own: Brewed for hours, from bean to your coffee bar
- motion: steep, the whole making of cold brew as one scroll film whose key moment is the steep: beans, fine powder, cold water turning dark, over ice, milk, out to their coffee bar
- sources: docs/inspo/pinterest-coffee-2026-11-11/grind-pour-sip-journey.png, docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-07/pulp-smoothie-chain.jpg
- steps: 1 beans pour into a black grinder under the headline. 2 the camera falls with the fine powder out of its outlet. 3 the powder clouds into a jar of cold water, which turns to deep cold brew ("Brewed for hours, not minutes"). 4 cold brew runs from a glass dispenser over ice. 5 milk swirls in and the camera pulls back to their own coffee bar photo with Shop cold brew.
