## Business
- Name: Gremier Coffee Co. Logo: round badge "GREMIER · COFFEE CO. · EST. 2024", iced cup in the middle. Tagline on their site: "Elevate Every Day."
- Based in Jerusalem, delivers across Israel (record: serves nationwide).
- Kosher: Rabbi Shmuel Weiner.
- WhatsApp 055-270-3413 (+972552703413), "WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us". Email gremiercoffee@gmail.com.
- Story: founder loved coffee, couldn't find coffee in Israel up to his standards, started brewing in his basement; today brings cold brew all around the country.
- How: brewed slowly in the cold, so smooth, never bitter. Brewed for hours, not minutes. Small batches, bottled fresh. Beans from Colombia, Honduras, Costa Rica and beyond; dark, medium, light roasts.
- Use: pour over ice, add milk if you like.
- Delivery: order by Wednesday, receive by Shabbat (regular delivery to Jerusalem and Beit Shemesh). Regular 2-5 days, expedited 1-2 days, event delivery on a chosen date/time, pickup free.
- Events (record): weddings, bar mitzvahs, kiddushim, corporate events, private parties.
