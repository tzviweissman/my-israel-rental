# Copy pass (rules 3u.14): every line over 20 words, and its shorter version

The shorter one is used unless it drops a fact.

1. "Your favourite cold brew, ready whenever you are. Pour it over ice, add milk if you like, and it is ready." (21)
   → "Pour it over ice, add milk if you like, and it is ready." USED (the first sentence said nothing new)
2. "Three jugs is our best price per litre: ₪32.60 a litre, against ₪33.80 for one Classic jug. Made for offices, shops and big events." (24)
   → "Three jugs is our best price per litre: ₪32.60, against ₪33.80 for one Classic jug." USED (who it is for is in the heading)
3. "The dispenser, cold brew, milk, cups, straws and three syrups in one box, for events across Israel. We confirm the price for your guest count on WhatsApp." (27)
   → "Dispenser, cold brew, milk, cups, straws and three syrups in one box. We confirm the price for your guest count on WhatsApp." USED ("events across Israel" is in the lede)
4. The founder story (39) → "Our founder loved coffee but could not find any in Israel that met his standards, so he started brewing it in his basement. Today our cold brew goes all around the country." (33) USED: the three facts stay, nothing shorter keeps them.
5. "Cold water and time bring out the sweet, chocolatey side of the coffee and leave the bitterness behind. Brewed in small batches and bottled fresh." (25)
   → kept: both facts are theirs and a shorter one drops one; the small batches line also sits in the kosher band, so the second sentence is dropped here. USED: "Cold water and time bring out the sweet, chocolatey side of the coffee and leave the bitterness behind." (18)
6. Delivery card (23) → "Regular delivery to Jerusalem and Beit Shemesh; across Israel in 2 to 5 days, or 1 to 2 expedited. Pickup is free." (21) USED; nothing shorter keeps the facts.
7. Shop lede (26) → "Litre bottles, mini packs, jugs, syrups and coffee bars. Tap one to see it, or Add it straight to your basket." USED (the count was not needed)
8. Contact delivery (38) → split into two short lines: "Order by Wednesday, receive by Shabbat, with regular delivery to Jerusalem and Beit Shemesh." and "Regular 2 to 5 days, expedited 1 to 2 days, events on the date and time you choose. Pickup is free." USED
9. The three jug descriptions (23 to 24) → "Five litres of Classic for the office, a busy home or an event. Keep it in the fridge." USED
10. Premium Coffee Bar (31) → "Everything for a coffee bar in one box: the dispenser, cold brew, milk, cups, straws and caramel, vanilla and sugar syrups." (21) USED; the syrups are what it holds, kept.
11. Sugar syrup and cups (21) → "Twenty-five 500 ml cups with domed lids and straws, and sugar syrup that mixes easily into cold coffee." USED
