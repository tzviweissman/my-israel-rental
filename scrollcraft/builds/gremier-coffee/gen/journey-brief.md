# Journey stills (11 Nov 2026, written before generating)

Tzvi said yes to the five-step journey (gen/journey-sketch.png) and asked to see the details like the Pinterest coffee
site (docs/inspo/pinterest-coffee-2026-11-11), and that the grinder and steeping must not look fake. These are the
concept stills for steps 1 to 4, made to look photographed; step 5 is their own coffee bar photo.

## How it really happens (rules 3h.18, the possible story)
- Beans are ground in a burr grinder (beans in the top, grounds out of the bottom).
- Cold brew is ground coffee steeped in cold water for hours: grounds fall into the water, the coffee bleeds out of
  them as dark plumes, and the water turns dark.
- The brewed coffee is drawn from the jar's tap; it is served over ice, and milk poured in swirls through it.
Every object starts and ends on screen: beans from above, into the grinder; grounds out of it, into the water jar;
the coffee out of the tap, into the glass; milk from above, into the glass; then the camera pulls back to their bar.

## Key moment per still
1. GRIND: beans mid-air, falling into a clear hopper on a matte black burr grinder.
1b. POWDER (added 11 Nov, Tzvi: "shows the coffee being ground into like a fine powder"): close on the grinder's outlet, fine powder streaming out and down.
2. STEEP: a stream of grounds hitting clear iced water in a tall glass jar; dark plumes bloom downward.
3. TAP: cold brew running from a white dispenser tap in a smooth stream into a glass of ice.
4. POUR: milk pouring into iced cold brew, curling in marbled ribbons, like their own glass photos.

## One look (rules 3g.14, 3w.15 places look photographed)
Their own shoot's world: warm cream plaster wall with soft leaf shadows, low golden sun from the right, light oak
table, shallow depth of field, side and back light, the camera side a little darker (3w.3). Coffee opaque and deep
brown, never glassy; ice real, with frost; no neon. No text, no logos, no labels, no hands, no people. Their bottles
and badge are never generated: steps 3 to 5 end on their own photos.
