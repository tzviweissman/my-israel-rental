# Gremier Coffee Co.: build report (10 Nov 2026)

Three pages: Home, Shop and Contact. Every product, price, photo and line comes from their own shop at
gremiercoffee.co.il and their MyIsraelRental listing.

- **Opening:** their own sunlit coffee bar photo fills the screen. As you scroll, the dispenser starts as pale water
  and steeps to dark cold brew while a small clock ring sweeps round. It is built in code from their photo.
- **Home:** the kosher band, then the five coffees with Add buttons, the mini packs and the 5 litre jugs. Then the
  coffee bar, where you choose home, garden, office or evening and see their bar in that setting. Then their story,
  how ordering works and the delivery promise.
- **Shop:** all 19 products in five groups, a product sheet for each, and a caramel syrup offer in the basket. The
  order goes in three steps and opens in WhatsApp to 055-270-3413, written out with the total.
- **Spend:** no credits. The paid API has no photo-extending model today. The two tries to call one returned
  "model not found" and cost nothing, so the opening photo was widened in code.
