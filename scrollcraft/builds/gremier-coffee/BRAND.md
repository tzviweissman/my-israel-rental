# Gremier Coffee: brand kit (rules 3x.7)

Written before any picture. No picture is generated for this site: every product and bar photo is their own, from
their own shop at gremiercoffee.co.il (src/site, read 10 Nov 2026). The colours come from those photos and their labels.

## Colours
- Ground `#F6EEE3`: a lighter tone of the sunlit wall behind their product photos (their walls measure #D3BDA6 to #E5DBCC), so the photos sit on it as warm windows.
- Oak `#ECDFCC`: the one second surface (cards, order steps), the light oak table their bottles stand on.
- Ink `#1C1410`: all text, prices included (3g.3); the black of their labels.
- Soft `#5E5148`: secondary text.
- Cold brew `#3B0A02`: the coffee itself in their bottles (sampled #3D0300 and #2D0800 from classic-liter-lifestyle-v2). The main button only, white text on it, and the dark closing band (rules 3g.19).
- Label stripes, only as the small mark of each coffee, never as a fill: Classic `#F2C531`, House Blend `#2FA8E0`, Colombia `#2DBA7A`, Decaf `#8A5A3C`.
- Line `#DCCFBC`: hairlines.

## Fonts
- Headings: **Noto Serif** with **Noto Serif Hebrew** (approved list, a serif like the lettering of their badge; no live site uses either), weight 600.
- Text: **IBM Plex Sans** with **IBM Plex Sans Hebrew** (approved list, no live site uses it), 400 and 600. The ₪ is set in IBM Plex Sans (3o.5).

## Type and spacing scale (3x.13)
The starter's scales, unchanged: text `--t-1` .8125rem, `--t0` 1.0625rem, `--t1` 1.3125rem, `--t2` 1.625rem,
`--t3` to `--t5` growing with the screen. Gaps `--s1` 4px to `--s10` 128px. Nothing sized in between.

## Logo
- On light: their round badge (assets/logo.webp, from their site) in ink, beside "Gremier Coffee Co." in Noto Serif.
- On dark (the cold brew band and footer): the badge in its own black and white, the name in white.

## Textures and grounds
- None generated. Their sunlit wall and the leaf shadow in their own photos are the texture.

## Design bans
- No italic accent word, no gradients over photos, no frosted glass, no three identical cards in a row.
- No coffee splash, steam, beans flying or pour generated: their photos are the coffee (rules 3o.1).
- No label colour as a background block; the stripes stay small, like on their bottles.
- No "artisan", "premium", "curated" or "world-class" in our words; their own lines only.
- No points club, subscriptions or card payment copied from their own site: orders here go by WhatsApp.
