# M and N Jewelry: opening ideas (scripts/check-ideas.mjs)

7 Nov 2026. They have twenty real photos of their pieces and a flyer, and say in their own words that "every piece is
carefully selected for quality, sparkle, and comfort". Every picture below was opened by the builder.

## Own: A. Look closer (a jeweler's loupe, from "carefully selected for quality, sparkle")
- motion: loupe, a jeweler's loupe glides onto the diamond of their own ring and shows it magnified, and the visitor moves it over the ring themselves
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/kinoria-1-viewfinder-reveal.webp, docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-04/turn-it-over-hotspots.jpg, docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-04/flashlight-hero.jpg
- steps: 1 you land on their twist solitaire full screen on its white linen, the headline at the left. 2 A round loupe with a thin rim glides in from the right edge and settles over the diamond, and inside it the stone and its prongs are shown twice as large, sharp, from their own photo. 3 Moving the pointer (or dragging on a phone) moves the loupe over the band and the stone; left alone it returns to the diamond.

## B. Light across the stone
- motion: glint, a band of light sweeps across the ring as you scroll and the diamond flares
- sources: docs/inspo/moto-card-2026-10-21/hero-2500.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/preymaker-1-texture-film-hero.webp
- steps: 1 their twist ring on a dark ground beside the headline. 2 A soft band of light crosses it with the scroll and the stone flashes, as the gold turns white, yellow, rose. 3 Four more of their pieces drift by in boxes and the ring settles in the middle above their three words. Chosen, see below.

## C. Through the headline
- motion: through, their tennis bracelet lies across the words, some letters in front of it and some behind
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/easyseller-1-object-through-headline.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/outfit-1-shop-grid-home.webp
- steps: 1 a giant line "Tennis bracelets" on white. 2 Their bracelet, cut out, slides in and settles across the letters. 3 The letters it covers sit behind it, the rest in front. Not chosen: a cut-out of a fine bracelet from a phone photo frays at every stone, and the bracelet photos are 630 px tall.

## Chosen: B (Tzvi, 8 Nov 2026)
A was built first. After comparing the site with 22 high-end jewelry sites and videos, Tzvi chose B. It was first a
generated clip, which he rejected on 9 Nov ("it looks so fake"). On 10 Nov B was remade in code from the pieces' studio
pictures (gen/scenes.html): the stone is the one in their photo, and the light is one soft band and one brief flash laid over it.
