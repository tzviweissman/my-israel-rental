# Clean Plus brand kit (rules 3x.7)

Written 15 Nov 2026, before any picture is generated, from the owner's own flyer (src/owner/p01.jpg):
a blue house logo with a gold sparkle, the royal blue "your way.", navy words, pale blue option tiles and
a navy WhatsApp bar. Every page and every picture follows this.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | #FFFFFF | page ground |
| surface | #EFF3FE | the flyer's pale blue tiles, sampled: option tiles, the plan summary, the price card's inside |
| ink | #1B2137 | the flyer's navy, sampled: words, headings, the close band and footer ground |
| soft | #4B5368 | second lines and labels (7.6:1 on white) |
| line | #D9E1F5 | hairlines and tile edges |
| accent | #3062E5 | the flyer's royal blue, sampled: the main button (white words, 5.1:1), the one blue word in the headline, a chosen tile's edge |
| accent-deep | #2149B8 | the button pressed and hovered, links on white (7.4:1) |
| gold | #E8A03A | the flyer's sparkle, sampled: the plan board's marks for a booked visit only, never under words, never a button |

Blue is the business's own colour; the page is white, pale blue and navy, as the flyer is. Gold is used
only as small filled marks on the plan board, the flyer's sparkle in its job of "this is booked".

## Fonts

- Headings, the name, prices: Plus Jakarta Sans (700 and 800, SIL OFL), round and firm like the flyer's
  heavy sans.
- Body, labels, buttons: Public Sans (400 and 600, SIL OFL).
- Two families only (3x.8); neither is used by another site. The ₪ sign renders in Public Sans.
- English only, so no Hebrew face is needed; a Hebrew page would add Assistant under [dir=rtl].

## Type and spacing scale (3x.13)

Every text size is one of the starter's steps, nothing in between: --t-1 (labels), --t0 (body, buttons),
--t1 (tile titles), --t2 (the plan summary, the price on its card), --t3 (kickers on a phone), --t4
(section headings), --t5 (the opening headline). Every gap is one of --s1 4px to --s10 128px.

## Logo

- On light: the house mark drawn as one line icon in the accent blue (lucide "house"), then "Clean" in
  Plus Jakarta Sans 800 navy and "Plus" in the accent blue. No file was uploaded; the flyer's logo is
  inside a poster with text baked in, so it is not cropped.
- On dark (footer, navy): the mark and "Plus" in #8FAEFF (7.0:1 on navy), "Clean" in white.

## Textures and pictures

- One opening picture, generated: a bright, tidy Jerusalem apartment living room, Jerusalem-stone wall
  outside a large window, morning light, white and pale blue tones, no people, no text, no brand
  names. Wide and tall from one shoot. It is never presented as a customer's or our own home.
- Icons: lucide line icons in navy, 1.8 px stroke, on the pale blue tiles as on the flyer.

## Design bans

- No sparkles, stars or shine bursts as decoration (the flyer has them; on a site they read as clip art).
- No bubbles, mops, gloves or spray bottles as decoration; no stock photo of a smiling cleaner.
- No people in generated pictures (never shown as our staff or customers).
- No squeegee, window wipe, haze or light pass (the other cleaning site, RBS, has them).
- No "insured", "background-checked", "eco products", "guaranteed", "24/7" unless the owner says so.
- No cream + serif + one italic word; no purple; no two-colour gradients; no frosted glass panels.
- No three identical cards in a row.
