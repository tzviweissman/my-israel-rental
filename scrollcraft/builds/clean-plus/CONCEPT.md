## Chosen
A, the visit that repeats: the event is the service itself, a 4-hour visit coming back on the same day every week, and the first screen shows the weekly visit and its real price before any scroll.

## Style sheet
Accent #3062E5 (the flyer's royal blue) on the main button, the one blue headline phrase and the plan's visit blocks; ink #1B2137 navy; ground white with the flyer's pale blue #EFF3FE; gold #E8A03A only as the small booked mark; Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and prices, Public Sans for text (BRAND.md); one radius 18 px; one soft shadow, only under the plan board; motion: the visits landing and repeating, the pills' highlight gliding, nothing else moves.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest photo, big: there is no real photo yet (a flyer and a receipt), so the opening is one generated, bright Jerusalem apartment, full screen at every size, never shown as a customer's home; the owner is asked for real photos (OWNER-QUESTIONS.md 10).
2. Signature moment from their own thing: their flyer's "we'll build the right plan" becomes a plan board where a 4-hour visit lands on Tuesday and repeats down four weeks as the page arrives.
3. The choice changes the page: tapping a day moves every visit to it; "Every two weeks" lifts two visits off; "Just once" leaves one; the options below add their own lines to the board; the sentence and the price line follow, and the same plan is carried to Build your plan.
4. Side by side: the saved Pinterest plan cards (docs/inspo/pinterest-clean-plus-2026-11-15/plan-cards-blue-light.jpg) only sit still; ours is a calendar that books itself and answers each tap. Closest Mobbin reference: Blue Apron "Personalize your meal kit", options with a live plan (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/01f8c42d-5c3a-443d-8ea9-d12969567b8d); Inspo: glideapps-com (one pale card in the opening). 21st search "schedule picker": Schedule Picker (https://21st.dev/@redrob-labs/components/schedule-picker).
5. One sentence: you land on a bright Jerusalem living room and a plan board where a 4-hour cleaning visit drops onto Tuesday and repeats down the weeks; tap another day and every visit moves to it.
