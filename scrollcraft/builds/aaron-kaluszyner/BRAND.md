# Brand: Aaron Dovid, kumzits and guitar lessons (aaron-kaluszyner)

Written 4 Nov 2026 before any picture was made (rule 3x.7). Taken from his own two flyers: the Kumzits flyer
(an acoustic guitar against a Jerusalem-stone wall, warm sand light) and the lessons flyer (black poster
capitals, burnt orange, "Learn. Play. Grow."). No logo exists; the name is set in type.

## Fonts (both on Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License, Hebrew subset listed; approved list, design rules)

- Headings: **Suez One** (display serif, poster weight, one weight 400). Poster capitals like his flyers, but a
  serif so it is not the lessons flyer's grotesque.
- Text: **Alef** 400 and 700. Plain, readable, Hebrew and Latin from one family.

## Type scale and spacing scale (rule 3x.13)

The starter's scales, nothing in between: text `--t-1` (14 px) `--t0` (17 px body) `--t1` (21) `--t2` (26)
`--t3` (28 to 36) `--t4` (34 to 52) `--t5` (42 to 76); the opening headline uses `--t5`. Gaps `--s1` 4 px,
`--s2` 8, `--s3` 12, `--s4` 16, `--s5` 24, `--s6` 32, `--s7` 48, `--s8` 64, `--s9` 96, `--s10` 128.
One radius, `--r: 6px` (a guitar pick's small corner, never a pill except the phone bar's button).

## Logo

No logo. The mark is the name "Aaron Dovid" in Suez One with a small drawn guitar pick before it: ink on the
light ground, limestone on the dark band. The owner is asked whether he has a logo.

## Textures

- A close view of a guitar like the one in his flyer, honey top and black fretboard, generated with no strings and no words (gen/guitar-brief.md), behind the strings. Tzvi, 5 Nov 2026: the strings looked out of place on the wall.
- Six guitar strings (three plain, three wound) drawn in code across the opening.

## Design bans

- No cream + serif + one italic word.
- No flyer crops or flyer text on the page as pictures (rule 3g.15).
- No fire, flames or night-campfire stock look: nothing in the record says his kumzitzes are round a fire.
- No generated people shown as Aaron, his students or his guests.
- No music notes, treble clefs or equaliser bars as decoration.
- No two-colour gradients, no glow, no frosted glass.
- No pill buttons (the radius is the pick's 6 px).
- No "stars", review counts or "families love" lines: there are no reviews.
- Nothing from the lessons flyer's icon set (guitar, note, bar-chart circles).
