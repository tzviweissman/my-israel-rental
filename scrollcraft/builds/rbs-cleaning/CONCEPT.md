## Ideas
- **A misted window over a scene** (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/flux-theory-1-steamed-glass-opening.webp): Flux Theory opens on fogged, beaded glass with the scene soft behind it. For RBS: the screen is a window with soap on it.
- **A hard edge sweeping across a veil** (docs/inspo/awwwards-2026-10-18/usav-1-diagonal-wipe.webp): USAV reveals the real scene behind a dark layer along one moving edge. For RBS: the edge is a squeegee blade and the veil is soap film.
- **Light that changes one room** (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/halcyon-lighting-1-lights-off-toggle.webp): Halcyon's day, evening and off over one photo. Rejected as the opening: light alone does not show cleaning.
- **A request that writes itself** (docs/inspo/videos-2026-11-04/brief-form-live-summary.jpg): the brief form whose summary builds beside it, for the Get a free quote page.
- Own: Daniel's flyer says "Window cleaning: streak-free, crystal clear". The scroll is the squeegee: three strokes, top, middle, bottom, as windows are done, and the room comes out clear. Full list in IDEAS.md (four ideas, A chosen).

## Chosen
A, the squeegee pass: his own words, his own craft, the cause on screen for the whole stroke. It was first drawn in code; that did not look real, so it is a filmed clip (gen/wipe-brief.md), a wide blade pulled down one soapy pane, then the bright room comes in. The room is a generated home, never shown as a customer's or our own.

## Style sheet
Accent #27622A (the RBS green) on the main button only; ink #0D2241 navy; ground white with pale sky #EEF6FB; Fredoka for headings (Secular One until 10 Nov 2026, when The Finest Cut went live with it), Alef for text (BRAND.md); one radius 14 px; one soft shadow 3 px down, 30 px blur, tinted; motion: the wipe, short rises once, nothing else moves.
