# Wipe film brief (R3h.18, 19, 23): a squeegee clears a soapy window

Replaces the drawn blade and soap, which did not look real over the room picture.

Approved by Tzvi: 5 Nov 2026, "i thought we made a rule that you dont need my permission to generate if code wont do a good job"; and on 4 Nov, relayed by the rules session: "if the squeegee clip doesnt look good or real then generate a better one".

## Steps (second story, 5 Nov: the first take, gen/wipe/a.mp4, was not used: an arm filled the frame, the lather dripped in arches, the blade crossed the window frame)
1. Close on one pane of glass with no frame in shot. All of it is white soap lather, the view only a soft glow.
2. A very wide squeegee blade on a pole enters past the top edge, level, as wide as the pane.
3. It is pulled straight down in one slow, steady stroke. Above the rubber the glass is clear with a hard edge; below it, lather. The lather gathers in a wet ridge along the blade.
4. The blade and the ridge leave past the bottom edge. The glass is clear: olive trees, a stone wall, hills.
5. The camera backs into the room: its own film, gen/pull-brief.md, since Tzvi's note of 5 Nov replaced the dissolve.

## Every object
- Glass: fills the frame in every frame.
- Soap lather: running down the glass at the start of the cut, moved only by the blade, ends as the ridge that leaves past the bottom edge.
- Squeegee blade and its pole: the blade enters past the top edge, the pole comes up from the bottom edge behind it; both leave past the bottom edge. No person in shot.
- The view: always behind the glass, seen as the lather goes.
- The room: seen only in the pull-back film (gen/pull-brief.md).

## How it really happens
From a real video: "How to Squeegee (fan) a Window Like The Pros!", Torch Window Cleaning, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1CWHINnUH-w (looked at from 0:45 to 1:50). The glass is wet with streaky white lather. The blade is about a third of the glass wide, held at an angle by a hand on a short handle, and never leaves the glass; it moves in a continuous S from the top, each pass overlapping the last. Directly behind the rubber the glass is completely clear, with a hard clean edge. Water gathers along the blade and runs down. Nothing fades: the lather only goes where the blade has been.

## Key moment
The hard level edge right behind the rubber during the stroke. It has its own clip between its own stills: before (gen/wipe/start2.png, all lather) and after (gen/wipe/end2.png, all clear). Frame 1: full lather, no blade. Middle: blade across the middle, upper half clear, lower half lather. Last: clear glass, no blade.

## How it is made
- End still: one generated photograph of the view through clean glass. It came with a window frame, which the clip kept, so the clip is cropped to the pane alone and starts at 0.7 s, after the first second where the frame and runs of lather formed; the blade leaves at about 5.3 s and the film ends at 6.1 s.
- Start still: that same still under the lather photograph, blended free in code, so nothing moves between them but the lather.
- Clip: start and end frames, one take, price from get_cost first (16 credits for 8 s on 5 Nov). This is the second and last try.
- check-clip on the clip, then watched at 8 fps against the steps. Phone uses a centre crop: the blade is level and full width, so nothing is lost.
