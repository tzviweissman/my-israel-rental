One continuous smooth camera move, no cuts, no fades, no dissolves. The camera starts right at a window, looking out at a green city park and cream stone apartment buildings, and dollies straight backward, level and steady, into a furnished living room. The window frame enters from all edges of the screen and shrinks as the camera moves back. The ceiling, walls, stone floor, then the vase of olive branches, wooden coffee table, woven rug, grey sofa, floor lamp, armchair, plant and sideboard enter from the edges of the screen. The landscape outside stays still. The window is one single pane of clear glass with no bars or dark lines across it. Nothing in the room moves or changes shape; straight lines stay straight. Slow ease in, slow ease out, stopping on the whole bright tidy room. Natural daylight, real photography. No people.
