# RBS Cleaning: opening ideas (scripts/check-ideas.mjs)

## A. Own: the squeegee pass
- motion: wipe, a squeegee pulls a soapy window clear and the bright room comes in
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/flux-theory-1-steamed-glass-opening.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-2026-10-18/usav-1-diagonal-wipe.webp
- why: the owner's own flyer words, "Window cleaning: streak-free, crystal clear". The glass is the screen.
- steps: 1 the screen is a window misted with soap, the room soft behind it, the headline on the left. 2 scrolling, a squeegee blade pulls across the top third left to right and the glass behind it is clear. 3 it comes back across the middle, right to left, leaving a thin water line that dries. 4 the bottom third, and the room is sharp and bright; the button and the ₪400 fact stay.

## B. Morning light comes in
- motion: light, a shaft of morning light sweeps across a dim room and every surface it touches is clean
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/halcyon-lighting-1-lights-off-toggle.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/zeroz-brand-site-2-light-circle-wipe.webp
- steps: 1 a dim, cool room with the blinds half shut. 2 scrolling, a band of morning light crosses the floor and up the sofa. 3 the room is fully lit and clean.

## C. The haze lifts
- motion: lift, a pale film of dust lifts off the whole room like breath off a mirror, from the floor up
- sources: docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/flux-theory-1-steamed-glass-opening.webp, docs/inspo/product-pages-2026-10-20/buds-3-before-after-split-backgrounds.webp
- steps: 1 the room under a grey, dusty film. 2 scrolling, the film thins from the floor upward. 3 the room is clear.

## D. The line between before and after
- motion: edge, one thin vertical line crosses the room; behind it the room is in disorder, ahead of it the same room is clean
- sources: docs/inspo/product-pages-2026-10-20/buds-3-before-after-split-backgrounds.webp, docs/inspo/awwwards-2026-10-18/usav-1-diagonal-wipe.webp
- steps: 1 a lived-in room, cups and a throw on the sofa. 2 a line crosses with the scroll, the clean room behind it. 3 the whole room clean. Needs a real before and after from Daniel to be true; held until he sends one.

## Chosen: A
A, because it is the owner's own words and craft, and the cause, the squeegee, is on screen for the whole stroke. First drawn in code, then filmed because the drawing did not look real (gen/wipe-brief.md). The room is a generated picture of a home like a customer's, never shown as our work.
