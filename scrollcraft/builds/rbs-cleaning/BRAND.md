# RBS Cleaning brand kit (rules 3x.7)

Written 4 Nov 2026, before any picture is generated, from the owner's own flyer (src/owner/p01.jpg):
the green RBS wordmark with a broom, the charcoal "CLEANING", the sky-blue ground and the navy lines.
Every page and every picture follows this.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | #FFFFFF | page ground |
| surface | #EEF6FB | the pale sky of the flyer: cards, chips, the quote summary |
| sky | #6CC2E3 | the flyer's sky, sampled: thin rules, the wiped-glass highlight, never under words |
| ink | #0D2241 | the flyer's navy, sampled: words, headings, the dark band and footer ground |
| soft | #4A5A6E | second lines and labels (7:1 on white) |
| line | #D6E4EE | hairlines and card edges |
| accent | #27622A | the RBS green, sampled: the main button only, with white words on it (6.9:1) |
| accent-ink | #FFFFFF | words on the green button |

Green is the business's own colour and appears only on the one main action; the rest of the page is
white, pale sky and navy, as the flyer is.

## Fonts

- Headings and the name: Fredoka (600 and 700; Latin and Hebrew, SIL OFL), heavy and rounded like the RBS wordmark. It replaced Secular One on 10 Nov 2026, when The Finest Cut went live with Secular One first.
- Body, labels, buttons and prices: Alef (400 and 700; Latin and Hebrew, SIL OFL).
- Two families only (3x.8). The ₪ sign is set in Alef (kit.price).

## Type and spacing scale (3x.13)

Every text size is one of the starter's steps, nothing in between: --t-1 .8125rem (labels), --t0 1.0625rem
(body, buttons), --t1 1.3125rem (card titles, prices on cards), --t2 1.625rem (the quote summary,
big prices), --t3 (section kickers on a phone), --t4 (section headings), --t5 (the opening headline).
Every gap is one of --s1 4px to --s10 128px.

## Logo

- On light: the word "RBS" in Fredoka in the RBS green, then "Cleaning" in Fredoka in navy, with
  the broom mark drawn as a single-line icon in navy (no file was uploaded; the flyer's logo is part of a
  generated poster with text baked in, so it is not cropped).
- On dark (footer, navy): "RBS" in #8FD18F (light green, 8.6:1 on navy) and "Cleaning" in white.

## Textures and pictures

- One opening picture, generated (cost approved by Tzvi first): a bright, clean living room in the
  flyer's style, a large window over a park and Jerusalem-stone buildings, morning light from the
  windows, white and pale sky tones, no people, no text, no brand names. Wide (16:9) and tall (9:16)
  from one shoot. It is never presented as a customer's or our own home ("a home like yours").
- The haze over it, which the scroll wipes clean, is drawn in code (soap film and streaks, in white at
  low strength), not a picture.
- Icons: lucide line icons in navy, 1.8 px stroke.

## Design bans

- No cream + serif + one italic word; no purple, no two-colour gradients, no frosted glass panels.
- No sparkles, stars or shine bursts (the flyer has them; they read as clip art on a site).
- No bubbles, mops or spray bottles as decoration; no stock photo of a smiling cleaner.
- No people in generated pictures (never shown as our staff or customers).
- No "insured", "background-checked", "eco products", "guaranteed", "24/7" unless Daniel says so.
- Green never used for anything but the main button; no green text links.
- No three identical cards in a row.
