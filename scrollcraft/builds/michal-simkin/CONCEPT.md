# Michal Simkin: concept

Category: beauty

Rule 3t (22 Oct 2026). Pattern from Odyssee Clinic (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/odyssee-1-intro-and-booking.webp) and Vero New-York (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/vero-1-product-page-commission.webp): a short dark opening, then warm ivory and blush throughout, her clients' own photos framed inside it, her portrait beside her own words, courses as rows with price, "Book a consultation" fixed in the bar all the way down. Two doors, both visible: Book a consultation and Courses.

## Style sheet
Ivory #FBF7F4, blush #F3E4E0, navy #0E1F39 for ink and the main button, rose-gold #E9C4B0 as the main button on navy only. Emphasised words stand out by italic and weight in the ink colour. Playfair Display headings, Manrope-style body as built. One radius: 18px photos, pill buttons. Motion: clients dealt on one per scroll notch, struck words tied to scroll, nothing loops.

## Ideas
- Skin close-ups and one portrait, from docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/odyssee-1-intro-and-booking.webp: her clients' faces as the opening, the page cream after.
- A named client quote beside the product, from docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/vero-1-product-page-commission.webp: eight named reviews, each a before and after.
- Plan and date together, from docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/shops/tengile-1-plan-your-stay.webp: the booking page asks what, when and details on one page.
- Own: the words her clients used about their old wig are struck through and the words they used after take their place, because all eight reviews contain both halves.

## Chosen
The Own idea, because it is true to every review she has, and nobody else can do it with these quotations. The cream ground, framed photos and rows come from the beauty pattern of rule 3t.

## Wow gate
1. Visitors land on her clients dealt one by one onto a stack, each a real woman in her chair.
2. The picture changes with the scroll: the before-words are struck and the after-words rise.
3. Navy opening, ivory and blush after: loud once, then quiet.
4. Each struck line is one reviewer's own before and after.
5. Real clients and their words, never a template salon with stock models.
