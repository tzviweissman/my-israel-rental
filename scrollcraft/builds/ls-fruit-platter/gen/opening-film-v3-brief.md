# Brief: the opening film v3, "fruit rain" (written before generating, 26 Oct 2026)

Tzvi chose this idea on 26 Oct ("go with 1, fruit rain"). Kept from before (his words): the fruit and the red background.

Approved by Tzvi: 26 Oct 2026, "yes" to the seven steps and the object list below; "go" after seeing the empty-board start picture

## Steps (for Tzvi's yes)
1. Her empty long wooden board with its handle on the red ground, the same warm light. A short beat.
2. Watermelon wedges fall in from the top of the screen and land along the back of the board with a small bounce and a few juice droplets.
3. Red grapes and green grapes fall, bounce once and settle in the middle and at the left end.
4. Pineapple chunks and kiwi halves rain down at the front left; lychees and the pomegranate halves land at the right end.
5. A neat stack of pale melon slices drops onto the front of the board, tips over and fans out like a deck of cards.
6. Last, the pineapple crown drops into the middle and settles.
7. The finished platter, as in her own photo, holds. On the page, when it repeats, it fades back to the empty board.
