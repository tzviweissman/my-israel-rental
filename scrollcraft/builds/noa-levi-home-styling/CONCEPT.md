# Noa Levi, Home Styling & Organizing, concept

## Ideas
- **Scattered photos that settle** (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/tengile-malamala-2-scattered-photo-cluster.webp): Tengile MalaMala scatters photos like prints on a table. For her: pieces of her tidy drawer are strewn at angles over her messy drawer.
- **A wall of tiles forming a picture** (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/duda-works-1-tile-wall.webp): a grid of equal tiles that reads as one image. For her: the tiles are the compartments of the finished drawer.
- **Before and after as the hero** (docs/inspo/product-pages-2026-10-20/buds-3-before-after-split-backgrounds.webp): buds shows two states of one thing side by side. Rejected alone: a static split is a still, not an event.
- Own: her trade is turning a jumble into order, and her flyer says "We organize your home. You gain peace of mind." The scroll does the organizing: scattered pieces of her real "after" photo slide into a grid on top of her real "before" photo.

## Chosen
The first two, driven by her own line. The page opens full screen on her real messy drawer with pieces of her tidy drawer thrown over it; as you scroll they slide into place, the "Before" label becomes "After", and the headline turns from "We organize your home." to "You gain peace of mind." It is hers because both halves are her own photos and the motion is the job she does.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real photo, big: her drawer before photo is the first screen's picture at the largest size her 565 px file allows (up to 1.2 times its size), beside the headline; the pantry, linen and drawer pairs follow.
2. Signature moment from her own thing: the messy drawer becomes her tidy one, piece by piece, as the visitor scrolls.
3. The choice changes the page: choosing a room swaps both before and after photos; on the Book page the service changes her work shown and the hours fill a storage tray and set the total.
4. Side by side: Tengile MalaMala's scattered photos and Duda's tile wall (pictures above); ours uses the same scatter and grid with her real work, and ends on a finished picture.
5. One sentence: you land on a messy drawer with pieces of a tidy one thrown over it, and as you scroll they slide into place and the drawer is sorted.
