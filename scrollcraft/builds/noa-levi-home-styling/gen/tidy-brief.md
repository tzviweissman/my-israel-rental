# Opening film: the drawer tidies itself (Tzvi approved 26 Oct 2026, MiniMax H3, 16 credits)

Tzvi: "instead of the random pictures that get realigned it can be real items that look messy that
rearrange when you scroll to make it orderly and neat".

Start frame: assets/drawer-before.webp (her real messy drawer). End frame: assets/drawer-after.webp
(the same drawer, organised, with her own privacy blur on one box). Both real; only the motion between
them is generated.

Motion, in order (agreed with Tzvi before generating):
1. Opens on the messy drawer, still.
2. Pieces lift one at a time, fold into neat rolls and squares, and slide into fabric organiser boxes
   that settle into the drawer; socks and underwear top, folded clothes down the sides.
3. One piece moving at a time, never a blur of everything at once.
4. Ends exactly on the after photo.
Camera fixed overhead (a slight settle to match the after photo's framing is fine).
Never: new clothes or colours that are not in the photos, hands, people, text, a different drawer.

Use: scroll-scrubbed in the opening (frames on a canvas, like The Brown Box), scrolling back reverses it.
Check the result beside both photos before it goes in the page.
