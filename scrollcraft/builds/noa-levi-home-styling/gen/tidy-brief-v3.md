Tzvi: "write the object list first". Rules 3h.18 (physically possible) and 3h.19 (generate once).

## Why v1 and v2 failed
Her before photo has no boxes and her after photo has eleven boxes full of folded rolls. One clip
from one to the other has no physical path: v1 dropped boxes from nowhere, v2 turned the pile into
the rolls (a morph). The fix is the story, not the prompt: what really happens in a session is that
everything comes out, gets folded, and goes back in boxes. The folding happens off screen.

## Watch-outs, checked at 8 fps before Tzvi sees it
- The blurred box must never show its contents clearly in any frame (her privacy). If a frame does,
  that clip is rejected.
- No object may change colour, shape or count between its entry and its place.
- Scroll: clip 1 over the first 40%, clip 2 over 40 to 70%, clip 3 over 70 to 100%; scrolling back
  plays it all backwards (boxes lifted out, clothes dropped back in a heap), which is still possible.
