## Ideas
- **Giant type that lands word by word** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/banh-mi-viet-nam-3-tilted-type-swing.webp): Banh Mi Viet Nam sets one huge line of type and moves it as the visitor arrives. For them: their own three-word line, "Boil. Bake. Brag.", set at display size and landing one word at a time.
- **Colour panel per product, price and Add on each** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/best-bean-best-cup-1-colour-panel-products.webp and docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/bestbean-colour-per-product.webp): Best Bean Best Cup gives each coffee its own colour panel with the buy control on it. For them: the bagel dough card in mustard, the pizza dough card in black, each with photo, what it makes, price and Add, directly under the opening.
- **Product pop on category cards** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/kindi-land-2-category-cards-product-pop.webp): Kindi Land's cards lift the product toward you. For them: the two dough cards tilt a few degrees toward the pointer.
- **Giant name footer** (docs/inspo/awwwards-food-2026-10-18/crav-burgers-5-giant-footer-logo.webp): Crav Burgers ends on a huge cropped name. For them: American Crust in condensed capitals cropped at the bottom of a black band.
- Own: their line "Boil. Bake. Brag." is their process in three words, and they have a photo of the raw dough pack and a photo of the baked everything bagels. Raw dough becoming the baked bagel, inside a ring that echoes their round logo, as the three words land.

A fresh Inspo `recommend` on 22 Oct 2026 for a mustard and black food brand returned a dark marquee-hero genre (type-only hero, no imagery); not taken, because the owner's flyer look is bright and the buyer must see photo and price first. A 21st search for the button, the card tilt and the marker link failed with a catalog error ("catalog search failed") three times that day, so those pieces come from the effects library and our inspo pictures, named above.

## Chosen
The first idea driven by the Own one. The page opens on mustard with Boil. Bake. Brag. landing one word at a time. A round ring on the right drops in holding their raw bagel dough (their flyer's pack) as "Boil." lands, ripples go out like a pot, then their real photo of baked everything bagels wipes over it in a circle as "Bake." lands, and the ring settles with a small stamp as "Brag." lands. Both doughs are already on the first screen as colour cards with price and Add, so nobody has to scroll to buy (rule 3t). It is theirs because the three words are their line and both pictures are their own product, before and after the oven. The owner's own look (mustard and black from the flyers and logo) wins over a different ground.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real photo, big: their everything-bagels photo fills the 450 px ring on the first screen and the left half of the "kitchen" section; the dough pack is in the ring first.
2. Signature moment from their own thing: Boil. Bake. Brag. as raw dough becoming baked bagel in one ring, then the basket that counts what you will bake (6 bagels per pack, 2 personal pies per tub).
3. The visitor's choice changes the page: choosing the pizza dough turns the shop stage from mustard to black with its tub photo, name, price and makes line; the basket text and the bar change with what is added.
4. Side by side: Best Bean Best Cup (colour panel per product, buy on the card) in the pictures above; ours has the same buy-from-the-top pattern in mustard and black with a landing-words opening where theirs is a flat panel.
5. One sentence: you land and Boil. Bake. Brag. drop in word by word while their dough becomes a baked bagel in a ring.
