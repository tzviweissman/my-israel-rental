# American Crust brand kit (rules 3x.7, read by its ads: ad step A27.1)

Written 22 Nov 2026 for the Chanukah ad, from the site as built (CONCEPT.md) and the owner's own logo and pack photos
(assets/logo.webp, assets/bagel-dough.webp). The site was made before this file; it records what the site already is
and changes nothing on it.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| mustard | #FDB225 | their label and logo yellow: the opening, cards, the delivery band |
| light mustard | #FFD267 | soft fills |
| cream | #FEF7E5 | ground |
| black | #141414 | words, the logo's letters |

## Fonts

- Headings: League Gothic (tall condensed capitals, like their label's BAGEL DOUGH).
- Body: Barlow.

## Logo

- assets/logo.webp: a round mustard badge, AMERICAN CRUST in black capitals round a cream mountain, ESTD 5786.

## For ads

- Mustard and black, their own label and logo as printed; tall condensed capitals for the headline.
- Their real pack and bagels are their own photos, or rebuilt from them and checked beside them; the label is always
  their real label laid on (A28.2).
- Kosher wording exactly theirs: "Pareve, hechsher of Rabbi Akiva Dershowitz".

## Design bans

- No invented claims (taste, freshness, delivery beyond Ramat Eshkol and around); no second brand's colours.
