## Ideas
- **A colour room per product** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/bestbean-colour-per-product.webp): Best Bean Best Cup stacks full-height panels, one per coffee, each on its own colour with name, notes and a plus button. For them: each box and each pan is a panel on its own colour (gold, red, navy, orange, black) with feeds, price and Add.
- **Shop first, price on every card** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/theobroma-shop-grid.webp): Theobroma puts the packed product on a plain card with price and Add to cart straight under the opening. For them: "Pick your box" is the second screen, three cards side by side, no text before the prices.
- **A mosaic of the product on flat colours** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/food/lajoya-product-mosaic.webp): La Joya tiles each tin on a flat colour. For them: the eight kinds of chicken as a 4 by 2 mosaic, each on its own colour, with their own bowl photo where they have one and a plain colour tile where they do not.
- Own: his logo is red and golden yellow on black, so the opening stays black with the giant CHICKEN MUNCH letters and the real bowl of Hot Poppers dropping across them, and then the page turns warm and bright like his menu card.

- Fresh search 22 Oct: Mobbin "product cards row for a food shop, each card on its own colour with name, size and price and an add button" returned Touchy Coffee (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a935259d-7fd8-4c7d-ab87-089e2cee1e5c: product row on a flat colour, name, size and price pill, an ADD pill under each) and re_ (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/73d10ac2-8d3b-4bac-a470-913e42e4d6f3: Add to Cart bar on the card); Inspo "food brand shop with a coloured panel per product" returned Graza, Mooala and Grass Roots. 21st search failed twice ("catalog search failed"), nothing taken from it.

## Chosen
The first two together, with his own opening kept as the event. A visitor lands on the dropped bowl, scrolls one screen and is looking at three priced boxes with an Add button each; the kinds of chicken, the pans and Shabbos orders follow on their own colours. It is his because the colours are the ones on his logo and menu card and every picture is his own photo.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real photo, big: his bowl of Hot Poppers in the opening, half the screen, on his own cornflake photo.
2. Signature moment from his own thing: the bowl drops across CHICKEN and the stickers stick on; scrolling spreads the letters apart.
3. The choice changes the page: Add on a box or pan goes into the basket on every page and opens the order with it in; the order page builds the message with his total.
4. Side by side: Best Bean Best Cup and Theobroma (pictures above); ours is the same colour-room row with price and Add, with his own boxes.
5. One sentence: you land on a bowl of poppers dropping onto the name, scroll once and pick a box.
