# Chicken Munch brand kit (rules 3x.7, read by its ads: ad step A27.1)

Written 22 Nov 2026 for the Chanukah ad, from the site as built (site.css) and the owner's own logo and box photos
(docs/ads/gen/chicken-munch-moving/logo-mark.png, assets/box-yellow.webp). The site was made before this file; it
records what the site already is and changes nothing on it.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | #0E0D0C | the site's near-black ground |
| card | #1A1817 | panels on the ground |
| ink | #FFFFFF | words on the ground |
| soft | #CFC8BD | second lines |
| red | #EF2B2B | the logo's red CHICKEN |
| gold | #FAA519 | the logo's golden yellow MUNCH, the main accent |
| yellow | #FFD34E | the bright yellow of their box photos' ground |

## Fonts

- Headings and the name: Anton.
- Body: Archivo (Heebo under Hebrew).

## Logo

- logo-mark.png: CHICKEN in red over MUNCH in golden yellow, cut clean from their flyer. On a yellow ground it
  sits on a black rounded tile, or the yellow MUNCH disappears.

## For ads

- Dark ground (#0E0D0C) or their box photos' own yellow (#FFD34E); black or white Anton words; red and gold as in
  the logo.
- Their real food and boxes are their own photos, never redrawn.
- No price unless one currency is chosen: boxes are priced in $ and pans in ₪ on the listing.

## Design bans

- No second brand's colours; no purple; no gradient text.
- No invented claims (taste, freshness, delivery times); kosher wording exactly "Hechsher of Rav Akiva Dershowitz".
