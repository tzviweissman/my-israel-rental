# Brief: the skirt hero shot and the sharper doors photo (rule 8, R8.7)

Written on 24 Oct, after the shot (23 Oct) and with the upscale (24 Oct); the brief rule came on 24 Oct. It records what was done so the next shot for her reuses it.

## Reference
`skirt-ref.jpg`: her flyer photo (assets/skirts.webp, 1023 x 1209): a black and a white skirt over a travertine block, her RS bag behind.

## Traits kept (read from the black skirt)
Black sports skirt with built-in leggings; wide smooth waistband; long, soft A-line drape; small "RS" mark near the hem.

## Changed (only these)
The black skirt alone (no white skirt, no bag), hung over a tall travertine block, plain warm stone gradient, one warm light from the left.

## Prompt (Higgsfield nano_banana_2, image_references, 2k, 4:5, job b1dcd497)
"Take only the black skirt from the reference photo and keep it exactly: the same black sports skirt with built-in leggings, its wide smooth waistband, its length, its soft drape and fabric, and the small 'RS' logo near the hem exactly as it is. Nothing added to it, no white skirt, no bag. Re-shoot it as a fashion product hero shot: the skirt hangs alone, full length, draped over the edge of a tall block of pale travertine stone, exactly the way it hangs in the photo, turned slightly. Background: a soft smooth gradient of warm stone beige, softly out of focus, nothing else in it. One warm key light from the left, a soft sheen along the fabric folds, a soft shadow on the stone. Shallow depth of field. Photorealistic advertising product photography, sharp product, no text added."
