# RS Modest Activewear, concept

Business: women's modest activewear store at King George 31, Jerusalem (listing owner name: Ravit Hen). One listed item: Premium Modest Sports Skirt, ₪320, "with built-in leggings". Photos: the flyer with two skirts (black, white) and the RS bag, the shop interior, the RS sign on the shopfront, the bag, a LinkedIn screenshot (not used).

## Ideas
- **Split two-product opening** (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/deadly-ponies-1-split-product-hero.webp): Deadly Ponies cuts the first screen into two halves, each a photo, with the shop one scroll away. For her: the first screen is her skirts photo cut down the middle. Rejected as a still: two halves that do nothing are only a layout.
- **One product per screen, in its place** (docs/inspo/product-pages-2026-10-20/closca-1-one-product-per-screen-in-its-place.webp): the product framed in a window with its name and price beside it. For her: the doorway frame in her shopfront green holding the photo, the one skirt and its price beside it.
- **Shop the look, editorial pairs** (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/estudio-niksen-1-shop-the-look.webp): a fashion shop that pairs a photo with its product. For her: the skirt photo paired with the store it hangs in. Rejected alone: no event.
- Own: her sign, her door, her address. The real photos include the green-framed shopfront, the open door and the rail inside. "Visit our store at 31 King George Street" is her own line, so the opening is the walk in: her skirts photo is the pair of shop doors, and they open onto her store. Her rail of hanging skirts became the hang tags and the way the page reveals things (everything hangs in and settles).

## Chosen
The first and the last: a split photo that becomes doors. The page opens on a framed doorway holding the skirts photo, split down the middle, with "Modest activewear in Jerusalem", one line and one button. Scrolling swings the two halves open (each turning on its outer edge, the room behind scaling from close to normal) onto her real store, and the words change to her own line, "Visit our store at 31 King George Street, Jerusalem", with the shop button and directions. Everything is her own photos, moved in code. It is hers because the opening is her address made into a door.

## Wow gate
1. Strongest real photo, big: the flyer photo of the black and white skirts fills the framed doorway (over 60% of the first screen on a laptop, the width of the phone on a phone), shown at no more than its own size; the shop interior fills the same frame at the end.
2. Signature moment only she could have: two shop doors made of her skirts photo swing open onto her store at King George 31, driven by the scroll, ending on her own words and the button.
3. The visitor's choice changes the page: the scroll opens the doors and changes the words; in the shop, tapping a photo changes the stage photo, the order changes the basket, the total and the phone bar, and choosing collect or delivery changes the step's fields and the message.
4. Side by side: Deadly Ponies' split opening (docs/inspo/awwwards-categories-2026-10-18/deadly-ponies-1-split-product-hero.webp). Theirs is two still halves; ours splits one photo and opens it onto a second scene.
5. One sentence: you land on a pair of shop doors made from her skirts photo, and as you scroll they swing open into her store at King George 31.
