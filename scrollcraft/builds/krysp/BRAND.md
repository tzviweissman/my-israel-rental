# Krysp brand kit (rules 3x.7)

Written 4 Nov 2026, after the pictures and film were made (the rule came later), from the values the
site already uses in gen/krysp.css and site.css. Anything new for Krysp follows this.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | #FBF1E6 | page ground, and the words on dark (footer) |
| peach | #F3D5BB | the film's ground, the menu bar, product tiles, the steps band |
| ink | #392615 | words, headings, the footer ground |
| soft | #6B5440 | small labels and second lines |
| line | #E3C7AC | hairlines and card edges |
| accent | #8A5A1E | buttons (Order, Add), with accent-ink #FFF8EF words on them |

The peach is the colour of the opening film's backdrop, so the film and the page are one ground.

## Fonts

- Headings, the name and prices: Nunito 900 (800 for smaller heads), upper case for the name.
- Body, labels and buttons: Mulish 400, 700 and 800.

## Logo

- On light: her own logo photo (src/owner/p01.jpg) as assets/mark.webp (40 px) beside "Krysp" in Nunito 900, upper case, ink.
- On dark (footer): the word "Krysp" in Nunito 900, in the ground colour; the mark is not used on dark.
- Her logo is a photo with its own ground, so it is not put on dark.

## Textures and pictures

- assets/ground.webp: the plain peach ground, made from her photos (gen/assets.py).
- Her own board photo (assets/luxe.webp) is the product's one look (one look per product); the film's
