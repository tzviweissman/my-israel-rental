# Krysp: the advert film, from the storyboard (2 Nov 2026)

Tzvi saw how the AI chocolate adverts he liked were made (docs/inspo/bar-breaking-2026-11-02, "How those adverts were
made") and asked for one made their way: a storyboard first (gen/film/SB-a.png, nine pictures), then one 12 s film from it
with the tool most likely to get it right first time. Seedance 2.5 was refused ("Requires plus plan or higher"; the account
is on the free plan), so MiniMax H3 at 2K, references: the storyboard and W0. Advert style is
allowed in the opening film since 2 Nov 2026 (rules 3w.1, exception).

## Every object
- The chew: her half-dipped peanut chew (src/owner/p02.jpg, as W0.png), the same in every shot. Floats; cracks once where
  the chocolate begins; the halves part; at the end both halves lie on a bamboo board. Never changes shape, size or colour.
- Advert style (3w.1 exception): a ribbon of peanut butter, dark chocolate chunks, puffed rice crisps. They come in past the
  left and right edges in shot 1, circle the chew in shots 2 and 3, drift soft behind it in shot 5, and leave past the edges
  or settle on the board at the end. None pops in or out in the open.
- The web: peanut butter with rice crisps in it, only between the torn faces, only after the crack; it stretches, thins,
  opens holes and its strands sag; it never goes back into the faces.
- The board and the other chews: seen only in the last shot, as in her photo.
- No hands, no text.

## How it really happens
The break follows the real references in docs/inspo/bar-breaking-2026-11-02 (rice-treat-pull-apart.jpg,
caramel-strand-sags.jpg): the halves part slowly, sticky strands are uneven, thin, sag and stay. The advert motion
(ingredients flying in, orbiting, settling) follows the liked adverts there and their published prompts at
https://adprompts.stunprompt.com (timed beats of 1.5 to 2 s, Phantom high-speed look, a long "do not" list).

## Key moment
The web (storyboard pictures 7 and 8): it gets the longest beat, 3 s, after the crack. If this film gets the web wrong, the
web clip already made (gen/film/wB.mp4) is cut in for that beat instead of a new generation (rules 3w.10.4).

## Steps (12 s)
1. 0 to 2 s, impact: the ribbon sweeps in from the left, chunks and rice crisps fly in from the right; the chew floats.
2. 2 to 4 s, orbit: everything circles the chew in a spiral, the camera moving a quarter round.
3. 4 to 5.5 s, macro: close on the rice and the white drizzle.
4. 5.5 to 7 s, crack: the chew still, one crack runs down where the chocolate begins; the swirl slows behind.
5. 7 to 10 s, the web: the halves part, the peanut-butter web stretches, holes open, strands sag.
6. 10 to 12 s, settle: the two halves come to rest on a bamboo board beside more chews, a few rice crisps land.
