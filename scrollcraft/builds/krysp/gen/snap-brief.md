# Krysp: the snap film (idea F), plan before any generation (29 Oct 2026)

Tzvi picked F and asked for it bigger, then "do it without hands if you can". No hands are seen: the
chew is framed so tight that both its ends run past the edges of the screen. It is held and bent from
outside the frame, so what the visitor sees is only the middle of the chew bending and breaking.
Physically true, and nothing appears from nowhere (rules 3h.18, 3h.19).

Waiting on: Chaya's photo of a chew broken in half (OWNER-QUESTIONS 10). The inside on screen must be
hers (3h.12); no inside is generated before it.

## Every object
- The chew: one half-dipped peanut chew from her board (src/owner/p02.jpg, the dark-chocolate half-dipped
  pieces in the second column), lying across the screen, both ends beyond the left and right edges the
  whole time. Starts whole; bends; cracks across the middle; breaks into two halves. The halves stay in
  frame until the camera leaves them in clip 3. Nothing is added to it.
- Crisp rice grains and chocolate flakes: come only from the break line at the moment it breaks (clip 1),
  hang in the air while the moment is frozen (clip 2), and fall away below the frame when time runs again
  (clip 3). They never appear before the break and never vanish in the air.
- The giant word KRYSP: page lettering in code behind the film, not in the film.
- Her board: below the chew, out of frame, on the same grey stone as her photo. It is seen only when the
  camera tilts down in clip 3, and ends as her real photo (the last frame is p02.jpg extended to the screen
  shape, rules 3c.12).
- Ground: warm peach from her logo (#F3D7BF family) behind the chew, lit bright and warm (3h.11).
- Nothing else is on screen. No hands, no fingers, no text in the film.

## Steps and clips (one cause, one motion: 3h.13)
1. Clip 1, about 6 s, start frame S0, end frame S1. The whole chew lies across the screen on peach. It bends
   slowly upward in the middle; a fine crack runs across the chocolate; it snaps into two halves a finger's
   width apart, grains and flakes leaving the break. End frame pinned at the instant after the snap (3h.15).
2. Clip 2, about 6 s, start S1, end S2. The moment stays frozen; only the camera moves half way round the break
   (from the front to looking into the broken face), showing the inside. Code pins three labels to the inside:
   crisp rice, peanut butter, dark and white chocolate (placed by measurement on the frames, rule 5).
3. Clip 3, about 6 s, start S2, end S3. Time runs again: the halves move apart and out past the screen edges, the
   grains fall below the frame, and the camera tilts down onto her board. S3 is her real photo, extended.
4. The page, in code: the scroll drives the three clips as one film (canvas frames, 3h.4); a thin crack line
   carries on down the page from the film's end; a "sound on" switch plays a recorded snap only after a tap;
   the order button cracks slightly when pressed.
