Category: trainer

Business: personal training for women in Jerusalem (home, her gym, online, small group circuits, her private gym). 60 shekels for 30 minutes at home; the rest "message for a price". Women only, English. Facts are hers only (listing and flyer).

## Ideas
- **Performance Lab: black and white alternate, numbers on black** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/events-sports-homes/perflab-sheet.webp): a person's service, offer cards, a proof block of big numbers, FAQ.
- **Reel Motion: prices side by side as cards** (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/events-sports-homes/reelmotion-sheet.webp).

## Chosen
The own idea in the bar, the Performance Lab order below it: photo opening with the button on the first screen, four offer cards with a photo and a price each (plus the circuits row), her private gym as a full picture, what she works on in her pink, the numbers on black (30 minutes, 60 shekels, 5 ways, women only), a short FAQ, the booking picture, the footer.

3. The visitor's choice changes the page: the scroll runs the clock and turns the bar pink; the FAQ opens.
4. Side by side: The Performance Lab (docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/events-sports-homes/perflab-sheet.webp). Sources (recorded 25 Oct): closest Mobbin reference https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/8adaef9a-d647-4e38-9541-c886bdb6742b; Inspo docs/inspo/awwwards-anatomy-2026-10-22/events-sports-homes/perflab-sheet.webp.
