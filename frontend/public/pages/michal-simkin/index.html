Michal Simkin, boutique wig salon and academy

A wig you love.

We create wigs that are a joy to wear, because when your wig feels right, you feel right.

This isn't about hair. This is about you.

That's why we begin by listening: to your needs, your lifestyle, how you want to look and feel. Then we design your wig around that vision.

Already have a wig that never sat right?

Maybe the cut feels heavy, the balance is off, or it never really fit from the start. With creative cutting, advanced styling and careful attention to detail, we rework the wig you had to make do with into one that feels authentically you.

  1. We listen.Your past wig experiences, your lifestyle, what you're looking for and what you want to avoid.
  2. We make a blueprint.We explore different options together and draw up the plan for your wig.
  3. We source the materials.And refine the design, keeping you involved as it takes shape.
  4. The cut, the colour, the fit.Cuts and transformations, wash and sets, handwork, repairs, cap adjustments, baby hairs, colour.
  5. You try it on.And feel like it was always meant to be yours.
